11.1 危害与可操作性研究 · HAZOP — Hazard and Operability Study☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.36 (System Safety) · 29 CFR 1910.119 (Process Safety Management)

HAZOP is a structured, systematic team-based technique for identifying hazards and operability problems in process systems. It was developed by ICI（帝国化学工业公司）ICI in the 1960s and is now the most widely used 工艺危害分析process hazard analysis (PHA)☆ technique in the chemical and process industries. HAZOP是一种结构化、系统化的团队技术，用于识别工艺系统中的危害和可操作性问题。它由ICI公司在20世纪60年代开发，现在是化工和过程工业中使用最广泛的工艺危害分析（PHA）技术。

HAZOP examines deviations from design intent. It systematically divides the process into nodes (sections of equipment/piping), applies guidewords to process parameters, and for each resulting deviation identifies causes, consequences, safeguards, and recommendations. HAZOP检查偏离设计意图的偏差。它系统地将工艺划分为节点（设备/管道区段），将引导词应用于工艺参数，并对每个产生的偏差识别原因、后果、防护措施和建议。

The HAZOP "Ask" Cycle / HAZOP提问循环

Node (节点) → Parameter (参数) + Guideword (引导词) → Deviation (偏差) → Causes (原因) → Consequences (后果) → Safeguards (防护) → Recommendations (建议)

Example: Node = transfer line, Parameter = Pressure, Guideword = More → Deviation = "More Pressure" (overpressure). Causes: blocked outlet, thermal expansion, regulator failure. Consequences: line rupture, release. Safeguards: PSV, pressure alarm. Recommendation: verify PSV sizing. 示例：节点 = 输送管线，参数 = 压力，引导词 = 更多 → 偏差 = "压力过高"（超压）。原因：出口堵塞、热膨胀、调节器失效。后果：管线破裂、泄漏。防护：安全阀、压力报警。建议：核实安全阀选型。

⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
HAZOP examines DEVIATIONS from design intent, not the design itself. Every deviation is a "guideword + parameter" pair. "More Flow," "No Level," "Reverse Temperature" (unusual but valid) are all HAZOP deviations. HAZOP检查偏离设计意图的偏差，而非设计本身。每个偏差都是"引导词 + 参数"对。"流量过多"、"无液位"、"温度反向"（不常见但有效）都是HAZOP偏差。

Choosing a PHA Method — Comparative Effort / 选择PHA方法——相对工作量

Process hazard analysis (PHA) methods form a spectrum — some are less rigorous and less resource-intensive, while HAZOP is the most detailed and structured PHA technique. Central to PHA, and to HAZOP in particular, is the team approach: a multi-discipline group (facilitator, scribe, process, operations, and instrument specialists) generates and evaluates deviations together. Comparing effort from lightest to heaviest: Checklist < What-If < SWIFT < HAZOP ≈ FMEA < FTA. 工艺危害分析（PHA）方法构成一个谱系——有些严谨度和资源需求较低，而HAZOP是最详细、最结构化的PHA技术。PHA（尤其是HAZOP）的核心是团队方法：由多专业小组（主持人、记录员、工艺、操作和仪表专家）共同生成并评估偏差。按工作量从轻到重：检查表 < What-If < SWIFT < HAZOP ≈ FMEA < FTA。

🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "What is the deviation when guideword 'More' is applied to parameter 'Pressure'?"
答题要点 / Key point: HAZOP=引导词×参数=偏差，检查的是偏离设计意图而非设计本身。链路：节点→参数+引导词→偏差→原因→后果→防护→建议。偏差是"引导词+参数"对（如More+Pressure=超压）。是PSM PHA最常用/最严格的方法。

11.2 HAZOP Guidewords and ParametersHAZOP引导词与参数

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.36 (System Safety) · 29 CFR 1910.119 (PHA element)

The Seven Standard Guidewords / 七个标准引导词

Guideword / 引导词Meaning / 含义Example (applied to "Flow")示例（应用于"流量"）
无No / None☆ Complete negation of the design intent / 设计意图的完全否定 No flow (blockage, pump failure) / 无流量（堵塞、泵故障）
更多More☆ Quantitative increase / 定量增加 More flow than intended (surge, valve stuck open) / 流量超出预期（冲击、阀门卡在开启位置）
更少Less☆ Quantitative decrease / 定量减少 Less flow than intended (partial blockage, leak) / 流量低于预期（部分堵塞、泄漏）
以及As Well As☆ Qualitative increase (additional component) / 定性增加（额外组分） Contaminant present in flow (impurity, corrosion product) / 流体中有污染物（杂质、腐蚀产物）
部分Part Of☆ Qualitative decrease (missing component) / 定性减少（缺少组分） Missing component in flow (incomplete reaction mixture) / 流体中缺少组分（反应混合物不完整）
反向Reverse☆ Opposite of design intent / 与设计意图相反 Reverse flow (check valve failure, back-siphoning) / 反向流动（止回阀失效、倒吸）
其他Other Than☆ Complete substitution / 完全替代 Wrong material flowing (incorrect feed, maintenance error) / 错误物料流动（进料错误、维修失误）

Process Parameters / 工艺参数

Guidewords are combined with parameters such as: Flow, Temperature, Pressure, Level, Composition, Viscosity, pH, Reaction, Phase, Speed, Voltage, Time. 引导词与以下参数结合使用：流量、温度、压力、液位、组成、粘度、pH值、反应、相态、速度、电压、时间。

⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
Know the two most commonly confused guideword pairs: More (quantitative increase) vs. As Well As (qualitative increase — an extra substance), and Less (quantitative decrease) vs. Part Of (qualitative decrease — a missing substance). 掌握最易混淆的两对引导词：更多（定量增加）vs.以及（定性增加——额外物质），以及更少（定量减少）vs.部分（定性减少——缺少物质）。
⚖️ 易混淆对比 / Common Confusions:
More 更多As Well As 以及关键区别 / Key Difference
定量增加（流量更大、温度更高）定性增加——混入额外物质More=数量更多；As Well As=多了一种东西（组分改变）
Less 更少Part Of 部分关键区别 / Key Difference
定量减少（流量更低）定性减少——缺少某组分Less=数量变少；Part Of=缺了某种物质（组成不完整）
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "Which guideword means a qualitative increase (an extra substance in the flow)?"
答题要点 / Key point: 两对易混：More(定量增)vs As Well As(定性增=额外物质)；Less(定量减)vs Part Of(定性减=缺组分)。Reverse=反向流、Other Than=完全替代(错料)。参数：流量/温度/压力/液位/组成/反应等。

11.3 HAZOP Team, Worksheet, and TimingHAZOP团队、工作表与时机

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.36 (System Safety) · 29 CFR 1910.119 (PHA element)

HAZOP Team Composition / HAZOP团队组成

  • Team Leader / 团队负责人: Facilitates the study, ensures methodology is followed (not necessarily the most senior technical person).主持研究，确保方法得当（不一定是最高级的技术人员）。
  • Scribe (Recorder) / 记录员: Documents all discussions, findings, and recommendations in real time.实时记录所有讨论、发现和建议。
  • Process Engineer / 工艺工程师: Understands the process design and operating conditions.了解工艺设计和操作条件。
  • Operations Representative / 操作代表: Knows how the plant is actually operated (often differs from design).了解工厂实际操作方式（通常与设计不同）。
  • Instrument/Control Engineer / 仪表/控制工程师: Understands control systems, alarms, and interlocks.了解控制系统、报警和联锁装置。
  • Safety Specialist / 安全专家: Advises on safety standards, regulations, and risk assessment.就安全标准、法规和风险评估提供建议。
  • Maintenance Representative (optional) / 维修代表（可选）: Provides insight into equipment reliability and maintenance.提供设备可靠性和维修实践方面的见解。

HAZOP Worksheet Columns / HAZOP工作表列

Node/Line Number | Guideword | Deviation | Possible Causes | Consequences | Safeguards | Risk Rating (L × S) | Recommendations | Responsibility | Status 节点/管线编号 | 引导词 | 偏差 | 可能原因 | 后果 | 安全措施 | 风险等级（L × S） | 建议 | 责任 | 状态

When to Conduct HAZOP / 何时进行HAZOP

  • Design stage / 设计阶段: Best time — problems found here are cheapest to fix.最佳时机——此阶段发现问题修复成本最低。
  • Before startup / 投产前: Verify the built system matches safe design.验证已建系统符合安全设计。
  • After significant modification / 重大改造后: Re-study changed sections.重新研究变更部分。
  • Periodic revalidation / 定期再验证: Process Safety Management (PSM) requires revalidation of PHA every 5 years.工艺安全管理（PSM）要求每5年再验证PHA。
⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
Under OSHA Process Safety Management (29 CFR 1910.119), a Process Hazard Analysis (which may be HAZOP) is required for processes above threshold quantities, and must be updated and revalidated at least every 5 years. 根据OSHA工艺安全管理（29 CFR 1910.119），超过临界量的工艺必须进行工艺危害分析（可以是HAZOP），并且必须至少每5年更新和再验证一次。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "How often must a PHA (e.g., HAZOP) be revalidated under PSM?"
答题要点 / Key point: PHA每5年再验证（1910.119(e)）。团队：负责人(主持)、记录员、工艺工程师、操作代表、仪表控制工程师、安全专家。HAZOP最佳时机=设计阶段（修复成本最低）。工作表列：节点|引导词|偏差|原因|后果|防护|L×S|建议|责任|状态。

11.4 蝴蝶结分析 · Bow-Tie Analysis☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.34 (Risk, Risk Assessment) · Brauer Ch.36 (System Safety)

The Bow-Tie model visually combines elements of FTA (left side) and ETA (right side) into a single diagram shaped like a bow-tie. It provides a clear, communicable picture of risk management for a specific hazardous event, centered on a single "top event." 蝴蝶结模型将FTA（左侧）和ETA（右侧）的元素直观地组合成一张蝴蝶结形状的图表。它为特定危险事件提供清晰、易于沟通的风险管理图像，以单一"顶事件"为中心。

Key Components / 关键组成部分

Component / 组成Position / 位置Role / 作用
Hazard / 危害Top of diagram / 图顶The source of potential harm (e.g., hydrocarbon under pressure)潜在伤害的来源（如：带压的碳氢化合物）
Top Event / 顶事件Center "knot" / 中心"结"The moment when control over the hazard is lost (e.g., loss of containment)对危害失去控制的时刻（如：围堵失效）
Threats / 威胁Left / 左侧Potential causes that could release the hazard可能释放危害的潜在原因
Preventive Barriers / 预防性屏障Left, between threats and top event / 左侧，威胁与顶事件之间Controls that PREVENT the threat from causing the top event防止威胁导致顶事件的控制
Consequences / 后果Right / 右侧Potential outcomes if the top event occurs (fire, explosion, toxic release)顶事件发生后可能的后果（火灾、爆炸、有毒物质泄漏）
Mitigative Barriers / 减缓性屏障Right, between top event and consequences / 右侧，顶事件与后果之间Controls that REDUCE the severity of consequences after the top event在顶事件后减轻后果严重性的控制

Barrier Effectiveness Criteria / 屏障有效性标准

  • Adequacy / 充分性: Does it address the specific threat or consequence?是否针对特定的威胁或后果？
  • Reliability / 可靠性: How likely is it to work when needed?需要时其工作可能性有多大？
  • Independence / 独立性: Is it independent of other barriers (no common-mode failure)?是否独立于其他屏障（无共因失效）？
  • Auditability / 可审核性: Can its effectiveness be verified through testing?其有效性是否可以通过测试来验证？
⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
Barriers on the LEFT are PREVENTIVE (they stop the event from happening). Barriers on the RIGHT are MITIGATIVE/RECOVERY (they reduce consequences after it happens). The top event is the loss-of-control moment, NOT a consequence. 左侧屏障是预防性的（阻止事件发生）。右侧屏障是减缓/恢复性的（发生后减轻后果）。顶事件是失控时刻，不是后果。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MEDIUM
典型问法 / Typical question: "Which barrier is on the LEFT of a bow-tie diagram?"
答题要点 / Key point: 蝴蝶结：中心顶事件=失控时刻（非后果）；左侧威胁+预防性屏障（防发生）；右侧后果+减缓性屏障（减后果）。屏障有效性四标准：充分性/可靠性/独立性（防共因失效）/可审核性。结合FTA（左）+ETA（右）。
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: "左防右缓" — 左侧Preventive预防（阻止发生）、右侧Mitigative减缓（减轻后果）；中心=顶事件（失控时刻）。屏障四性"充-靠-独-审"（Adequacy/Reliability/Independence/Auditability）。

11.5 SWIFT, What-If, and ChecklistsSWIFT、What-If与检查表

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.36 (System Safety) · 29 CFR 1910.119 (PHA element)

结构化What-If技术 · SWIFT — Structured What-If Technique☆

SWIFT is a brainstorming-based hazard identification method that is faster and less resource-intensive than HAZOP. It uses structured "What if...?" questions applied to process steps, guided by checklists and prompt words. SWIFT is suitable for simpler processes, early design stages, or when HAZOP would be prohibitively time-consuming. However, SWIFT is less systematic than HAZOP and may miss some deviations that HAZOP's rigorous guideword approach would catch. SWIFT是一种基于头脑风暴的危害识别方法，比HAZOP更快、所需资源更少。它使用结构化的"如果……会怎样？"问题，应用在工艺步骤上，由检查表和提示词引导。SWIFT适用于较简单的工艺、早期设计阶段，或当HAZOP耗时过长时。然而，SWIFT不如HAZOP系统化，可能会遗漏HAZOP严格的引导词方法能捕获的某些偏差。

What-If Analysis / What-If分析

The "What-If" method asks open-ended questions about the process ("What if the cooling water fails?", "What if a line is connected to the wrong tank?"). It is unstructured brainstorming — valuable for identifying novel scenarios but depends heavily on team experience and can be incomplete. "What-If"方法就工艺提出开放式问题（"如果冷却水失效会怎样？"、"如果管线接错罐会怎样？"）。这是非结构化头脑风暴——对识别新颖情景有价值，但严重依赖团队经验，且可能不完整。

Checklist Analysis / 检查表分析

Checklists use pre-compiled lists of known hazards to verify compliance and completeness. They are fast, cheap, and repeatable, and excel at routine inspections and compliance audits. Their limitation: they only find hazards that are ON the list — novel or site-specific hazards may be missed. 检查表使用预先编制的已知危害清单来验证合规性和完整性。它们快速、廉价、可重复，擅长例行检查和合规审计。局限：只能找到清单上的危害——新颖或场地特有的危害可能被遗漏。

SWIFT vs. What-If vs. Checklist vs. HAZOP / 四者对比

Technique / 技术Approach / 方法Best Application / 最佳应用Resource Level / 资源需求
ChecklistsPre-compiled list of known hazards / 预先编制清单Routine inspections, compliance audits / 例行检查、合规审计Low / 低
What-IfOpen-ended brainstorming / 开放式头脑风暴Simple processes, novel scenario identification / 简单工艺、新颖情景识别Low-Med / 低-中
SWIFTStructured "What if" with prompts / 带提示的结构化"What if"Moderately simple processes, early design / 中等简单工艺、早期设计Medium / 中
HAZOPGuideword + parameter, systematic / 引导词+参数，系统化Complex continuous processes / 复杂连续工艺High / 高
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MEDIUM
典型问法 / Typical question: "Which technique is BEST for a routine compliance inspection?"
答题要点 / Key point: 选型阶梯：Checklist（例行检查/合规，快速廉价，局限=只找清单上的）→What-If（简单工艺/新颖情景，非结构化）→SWIFT（带提示的结构化What-If，中等简单工艺/早期设计）→HAZOP（复杂连续工艺，资源最高）。PHA方法由简到繁。

11.6 风险财务——五大策略 · Risk Financing — The Five Strategies☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.34 (Risk, Risk Assessment) · Yates Ch.24 (Risk Assessment and Management)

CSP11 Domain 3 explicitly lists five distinct financial risk mitigation strategies. They must be understood as separate categories: CSP11领域3明确列出五种不同的财务风险缓解策略。它们必须作为独立类别理解：

Strategy / 策略Description / 描述Examples / 示例
1. 规避Avoidance☆ Eliminate the risk entirely by stopping the activity that generates it. The most definitive strategy.通过停止产生风险的活动来完全消除风险。最彻底的战略。 Discontinuing a hazardous product line; not entering a high-risk market; choosing a non-toxic alternative.停止危险产品线；不进入高风险市场；选择无毒替代品。
2. 保留Retention☆ Accept the financial consequences of the risk internally. Appropriate for low-severity, high-frequency risks.内部承担风险的财务后果。适用于低严重性、高频率的风险。 Self-insurance; deductibles; captive insurance companies; unfunded retention (paying losses from operating budget).自保；免赔额；专属保险公司；无资金支持的保留（从运营预算中支付损失）。
3. 分担Sharing☆ Distribute the financial burden of risk across multiple parties through contractual or organizational arrangements. Each party bears a portion of the loss.通过合同或组织安排将风险的财务负担分配给多个当事方。各方承担一部分损失。 Joint ventures; risk pools; mutual insurance companies; consortium arrangements; group self-insurance pools.合资企业；风险池；相互保险公司；联合体安排；团体自保池。
4. 转移Transfer☆ Shift the financial burden to another party, typically an insurer. Does NOT eliminate the hazard — only transfers financial responsibility for losses.将财务负担转移给另一方，通常是保险公司。不消除危害——只转移损失的财务责任。 Insurance policies; hold-harmless agreements; contract indemnification clauses; surety bonds; hedging.保险单；免责协议；合同赔偿条款；履约保证保险；对冲。
5. 损失预防与减少Loss Prevention & Reduction☆ Implement controls to reduce either the likelihood (loss prevention) or the severity (loss reduction) of losses — or both. Also called "loss control."实施控制措施以降低损失的可能性（损失预防）或严重性（损失减少）——或两者兼顾。也称"损失控制"。 Engineering controls; safety training; preventive maintenance; emergency response planning; fire protection systems.工程控制；安全培训；预防性维护；应急响应规划；消防系统。
⚠ 重要区别 / Important distinction:
Risk transfer (insurance) shifts financial responsibility but does NOT shift legal liability or moral responsibility for safety. The organization still bears reputational and regulatory consequences even if insured. Also distinguish RETENTION (keep the risk yourself) from TRANSFER (pay someone else to carry it). 风险转移（保险）转移了财务责任，但不转移安全方面的法律责任或道德责任。即使投保了，组织仍需承担声誉和监管后果。还要区分保留（自己承担风险）和转移（付钱让别人承担）。
⚖️ 易混淆对比 / Common Confusions:
Retention 保留Transfer 转移关键区别 / Key Difference
自己承担损失（自保、免赔额、专属保险）付钱让别人承担（保险、免责协议、赔偿条款）保留=自己扛；转移=转给他人。转移只转财务责任，不转法律责任/道德责任
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "Purchasing insurance is an example of which risk financing strategy?"
答题要点 / Key point: 五大策略：规避(停止活动)/保留(自担,低严重高频)/分担(多方共担,合资/风险池)/转移(保险/免责协议)/损失预防与减少(降可能性/严重性)。保险=转移；转移不除危害、不转法律责任。自保/免赔额=保留。

11.7 Insurance Types for Safety Professionals安全专业人员需了解的保险类型

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.34 (Risk, Risk Assessment) · Yates Ch.24 (Risk Assessment and Management)
Insurance Type / 保险类型Covers / 承保范围
工伤保险Workers' Compensation☆ Employee injuries/illnesses arising from employment. Mandatory in most jurisdictions. No-fault system — employee does not need to prove employer negligence.因雇佣关系产生的员工伤害/疾病。在大多数管辖区是强制性的。无过错制度——员工无需证明雇主疏忽。
一般责任险General Liability☆ Third-party bodily injury, property damage, and personal injury claims arising from business operations.因商业运营产生的第三方人身伤害、财产损失和个人伤害索赔。
职业责任险Professional Liability (E&O)☆ Claims of negligence or failure to perform professional duties. Relevant for safety consultants, engineers, and design professionals.关于疏忽或未能履行专业职责的索赔。与安全顾问、工程师和设计专业人员相关。
财产保险Property Insurance☆ Damage to buildings, equipment, and inventory from fire, natural disasters, theft, etc. Often includes business interruption coverage.因火灾、自然灾害、盗窃等造成的建筑物、设备和库存损坏。通常包括业务中断保险。
业务中断险Business Interruption☆ Lost income and continuing expenses when operations are suspended due to a covered event. Critical for business continuity planning.因承保事件导致运营中断时的收入损失和持续费用。对业务连续性规划至关重要。
汽车责任险Automobile Liability☆ Vehicle accidents involving company vehicles or employees driving on company business — third-party and often first-party (collision).涉及公司车辆或员工因公驾车的交通事故——第三方，通常含第一方（碰撞）。
⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
Workers' Compensation covers EMPLOYEES (first party). General Liability covers THIRD PARTIES (customers, public). Professional Liability covers professional negligence (errors & omissions). Do not confuse them. 工伤保险承保员工（第一方）。一般责任险承保第三方（客户、公众）。职业责任险承保专业疏忽（错误与遗漏）。不要混淆。

答题要点 / Key point: 保险配对：员工伤害=工伤保险（第一方、无过错、多数强制）；第三方/公众=一般责任险；专业疏忽=职业责任险(E&O)；设备/库存损毁=财产险（含业务中断）；运营中断损失=业务中断险。业务中断险对BCP至关重要。

11.8 Insurance Concepts — Deductible, Premium, Captive保险概念——免赔额、保费、专属保险

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.34 (Risk, Risk Assessment) · Yates Ch.24 (Risk Assessment and Management)

免赔额 · Deductible☆

The amount the insured pays out of pocket before the insurer pays a claim. Higher deductibles = lower premiums, because the insured retains more risk. The deductible is a form of risk retention within an otherwise transferred (insured) risk. 被保险人自付后才由保险人赔付的金额。免赔额越高 = 保费越低，因为被保险人保留了更多风险。免赔额是在其他方面被转移（投保）的风险中的一种风险保留形式。

保费 · Premium☆

The amount paid for an insurance policy. Premiums are based on expected losses, loss history (experience rating / 经验费率), exposure, industry classification, and administrative costs. A poor loss history raises premiums — linking insurance cost to safety performance. 为保险单支付的金额。保费基于预期损失、损失历史（经验费率）、暴露程度、行业分类和行政成本。不良的损失历史会提高保费——将保险成本与安全绩效联系起来。

专属（自保）保险公司 · Captive Insurance Company☆

A wholly owned subsidiary insurance company formed by a parent organization to insure its own risks. A captive is a sophisticated form of risk retention (self-insurance) that allows the parent to keep premiums (and investment income) within the organization while gaining tax and coverage-flexibility benefits. 由母公司成立的全资子公司保险公司，用于承保自身风险。专属保险是风险保留（自保）的高级形式，允许母公司将保费（及投资收益）保留在组织内部，同时获得税收和承保灵活性优势。

Annual Premium (年保费) ≈ Expected Losses (预期损失) + Risk Loading/Administration (风险附加/行政费用)
⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
A captive insurance company = RETENTION (self-insurance), NOT transfer. It is the organization insuring itself through a subsidiary. High-frequency/low-severity risks are best retained; low-frequency/high-severity risks are best transferred. 专属保险公司 = 保留（自保），不是转移。它是组织通过子公司为自己保险。高频率/低严重性风险最适合保留；低频率/高严重性风险最适合转移。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MEDIUM
典型问法 / Typical question: "A captive insurance company is BEST described as what form of risk financing?"
答题要点 / Key point: 专属保险=组织通过全资子公司自保=保留(Retention)，不是转移。免赔额=保留形式（高免赔=低保费）；保费=预期损失+风险附加/行政。高频低严重→保留；低频高严重→转移。保费与损失历史（经验费率）挂钩。
⚖️ 易混淆对比 / Common Confusions:
Retain 保留Transfer 转移关键区别 / Key Difference
自保/专属保险/免赔额；适合高频低严重商业保险/免责协议；适合低频高严重专属保险公司是保留的高级形式（常被误认为转移）。免赔额=保留成分。转移只转财务、不转法律责任
