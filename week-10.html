10.1 失效模式与影响分析 · FMEA — Failure Mode and Effects Analysis☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.36 (System Safety) · Yates Ch.20 (Accident Causation and Investigation)

FMEA is a systematic, bottom-up (inductive) method for identifying potential failure modes within a system, product, or process, and assessing their effects. It answers: What can fail? What is the effect? How serious is it? FMEA是一种系统性、自下而上（归纳法）的方法，用于识别系统、产品或工艺中潜在的失效模式并评估其影响。它回答的问题是：什么可能失效？影响是什么？有多严重？

Failure Mode vs. Failure Cause vs. Failure Effect / 失效模式 vs. 失效原因 vs. 失效影响

Concept / 概念Definition / 定义Example / 示例
失效模式Failure Mode☆ The way in which something fails某物失效的方式 Bearing seizes; valve sticks open; weld cracks轴承卡死；阀门卡在开启位置；焊缝开裂
失效原因Failure Cause☆ Why the failure happens失效发生的原因 Lack of lubrication; excessive temperature; fatigue load缺乏润滑；温度过高；疲劳载荷
失效影响Failure Effect☆ The consequence of the failure on the system/user失效对系统/用户造成的后果 Machine stops; product defect; injury to operator机器停机；产品缺陷；操作员受伤

DFMEA vs. PFMEA: 设计FMEADesign FMEA (DFMEA)☆ analyzes product design failures; 工艺FMEAProcess FMEA (PFMEA)☆ analyzes manufacturing/assembly process failures. The methodology is identical; only the scope differs. 设计FMEA分析产品设计失效；工艺FMEA分析制造/装配工艺失效。方法相同；仅范围不同。

🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MEDIUM
典型问法 / Typical question: "FMEA is BEST described as which type of analysis?"
答题要点 / Key point: FMEA=自下而上(bottom-up)、归纳(inductive)、单点失效(single-failure)分析——逐个失效模式评估影响。DFMEA=设计、PFMEA=工艺（方法相同、范围不同）。与FTA（自上而下、多点组合）区分。

10.2 风险优先数 · RPN — Risk Priority Number☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.36 (System Safety) · Yates Ch.20 (Accident Causation and Investigation)

The RPN is the product of three independently scored factors, each rated on a 1-10 scale. It is used to rank failure modes and prioritize corrective actions. RPN是三个独立评分因子的乘积，每个因子按1-10分制评分。用于对失效模式排序并确定纠正措施优先级。

RPN = Severity (S) × Occurrence (O) × Detection (D)   —   each 1–10, so RPN ranges 1–1000
Factor / 因子Scale / 范围Meaning / 含义
严重性Severity (S)☆ 1 (none / 无) to 10 (hazardous without warning / 无预警危险) How serious is the effect of the failure on the customer/user/system?失效对客户/用户/系统的影响有多严重？
发生频率Occurrence (O)☆ 1 (almost never / 几乎从不) to 10 (almost inevitable / 几乎不可避免) What is the probability or frequency of the failure cause occurring?失效原因发生的概率或频率是多少？
检测度Detection (D)☆ 1 (certain to detect / 肯定能检测到) to 10 (cannot detect / 无法检测) How likely are current controls to detect the failure cause or failure mode before it reaches the customer? HIGH D = POOR detection.当前控制措施在失效原因或模式到达客户之前将其检测到的可能性有多大？D高=检测差。

Worked Examples / 计算示例

Example 1: S=7, O=6, D=5. RPN = 7 × 6 × 5 = 210. This exceeds the common action threshold of 100, so corrective action is recommended. 示例1：S=7, O=6, D=5。RPN = 7 × 6 × 5 = 210。这超过常见的行动阈值100，因此建议采取纠正措施。

Example 2: S=9, O=2, D=8. RPN = 9 × 2 × 8 = 144. Despite the low occurrence, the high severity and poor detection still push it above the threshold. Note: high S alone often justifies action regardless of RPN. 示例2：S=9, O=2, D=8。RPN = 9 × 2 × 8 = 144。尽管发生频率低，高严重性和差检测仍将其推高到阈值之上。注意：单是高的S往往就足以证明需要行动，不论RPN如何。

⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
RPN is a RELATIVE ranking tool — the absolute threshold (100, 125, etc.) matters less than the ranking of failure modes. Also remember the counter-intuitive point: a HIGH Detection rating means the failure is HARD to detect (poor detection), so high D raises the RPN. RPN是相对排序工具——绝对阈值（100、125等）不如失效模式的排序重要。还要记住反直觉点：高检测度评分意味着失效难以检测（检测差），因此高D会提高RPN。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MEDIUM
典型问法 / Typical question: "What is the RPN for S=7, O=6, D=5?"
答题要点 / Key point: RPN = S × O × D（各1-10，范围1-1000）。常见行动阈值100。陷阱：D高=检测差（反直觉）会抬高RPN；S高本身常足以触发行动（无论RPN）。RPN是相对排序工具，阈值绝对值次之。例：7×6×5=210；9×2×8=144。
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: "RPN=S×O×D" — 严重度(Severity)×发生度(Occurrence)×检测度(Detection)。中文"严-发-检"。检测度反直觉：D分越高=越难检测。行动阈值≈100。

10.3 FMEA工作表 · FMEA Worksheet and Action Process☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.36 (System Safety) · Yates Ch.20 (Accident Causation and Investigation)

Worksheet Components / 工作表组成部分

  1. Process step / component / function工艺步骤 / 组件 / 功能
  2. Potential failure mode (how it could fail)潜在失效模式（可能如何失效）
  3. Potential effect(s) of failure (consequences)失效的潜在影响（后果）
  4. Severity rating (S)严重性评分（S）
  5. Potential cause(s) of failure失效的潜在原因
  6. Occurrence rating (O)发生频率评分（O）
  7. Current controls (prevention and detection)当前控制措施（预防和检测）
  8. Detection rating (D)检测度评分（D）
  9. RPN (S x O x D)RPN（S x O x D）
  10. Recommended action(s)建议措施
  11. Responsibility and target date负责人和目标日期
  12. Actions taken and revised RPN已采取措施和修订后的RPN

Action priority guidance: (1) First address failure modes with the HIGHEST RPN. (2) Pay special attention to any mode with S = 9 or 10, regardless of RPN — a "hazardous" severity warrants action by itself. (3) Recommended actions typically aim to reduce O (prevent the cause) or reduce D (improve detection), since S is usually a design property that is hard to change. 行动优先级指导：(1) 首先处理RPN最高的失效模式。(2) 特别关注任何S=9或10的模式，不论RPN如何——"危险"严重性本身就需要行动。(3) 建议措施通常旨在降低O（预防原因）或降低D（改进检测），因为S通常是难以改变的设计属性。

⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
After a corrective action is implemented, the team must recalculate the RPN to verify the new (lower) value — the "revised RPN." A common exam scenario asks what should happen after action implementation: verify effectiveness by recomputing RPN. 纠正措施实施后，团队必须重新计算RPN以验证新的（更低的）值——"修订后的RPN"。常见考试情景询问措施实施后应做什么：通过重新计算RPN验证有效性。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MEDIUM
典型问法 / Typical question: "What must be done AFTER a corrective action is implemented in FMEA?"
答题要点 / Key point: 实施后必须重新计算RPN（revised RPN）验证有效性。行动优先级：先处理RPN最高者；S=9/10本身即触发行动（不论RPN）。措施通常降O（预防原因）或降D（改进检测）；S难改变。

最佳答案 / Best answer: Act on it despite the modest RPN — severity of 10 (hazardous) warrants action on its own. 解析：S=9或10是"危险"级别，无论RPN高低都必须处理；行动还应改进检测（降D）。RPN是相对排序，不能覆盖高严重度。

10.4 故障树分析 · FTA — Fault Tree Analysis☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.36 (System Safety) · Yates Ch.20 (Accident Causation and Investigation)

FTA is a top-down, deductive analysis technique. It starts with an undesired event (the "top event") and works backward to identify all possible causes. FTA answers: What combinations of events could lead to this failure? It uses Boolean logic (AND/OR gates) and basic events with probabilities. FTA是一种自上而下、演绎法分析技术。它从不期望发生的事件（"顶事件"）开始，向后追溯识别所有可能的原因。FTA回答：什么事件组合可能导致此失效？它使用布尔逻辑（与/或门）和带概率的基本事件。

Boolean Logic Gates / 布尔逻辑门

Gate / 门Symbol / 符号Logic / 逻辑Probability Calculation / 概率计算
与门AND Gate☆ Output occurs only if ALL inputs occur仅当所有输入都发生时输出才发生 P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B) (independent / 独立事件)
或门OR Gate☆ Output occurs if ANY input occurs如果任一输入发生，输出就发生 P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A)×P(B) (union / 并集公式)

Worked Probability Examples / 概率计算示例

AND gate example: Two independent basic events with P=0.1 and P=0.2. Output = 0.1 × 0.2 = 0.02. AND gates reduce probability because ALL inputs must occur. (Redundancy: parallel components give higher reliability.) 与门示例：两个独立基本事件P=0.1和P=0.2。输出 = 0.1 × 0.2 = 0.02。与门降低概率，因为所有输入都必须发生。（冗余：并联组件可靠性更高。）

OR gate example: P(A)=0.1, P(B)=0.2. Output = 0.1 + 0.2 − (0.1 × 0.2) = 0.3 − 0.02 = 0.28. The intersection term is subtracted to avoid double-counting events where both occur. 或门示例：P(A)=0.1, P(B)=0.2。输出 = 0.1 + 0.2 − (0.1 × 0.2) = 0.3 − 0.02 = 0.28。减去交集项以避免重复计算两者同时发生的情况。

Small-probability approximation: When probabilities are small, P(A or B) ≈ P(A) + P(B), because the intersection term becomes negligible. This "rare event approximation" is commonly used in fault tree evaluation. 小概率近似：当概率很小时，P(A或B) ≈ P(A) + P(B)，因为交集项可忽略。这种"罕见事件近似"常用于故障树评估。

Cut Sets and Minimal Cut Sets / 割集与最小割集

  • Cut Set / 割集: Any combination of basic events that, if they all occur, causes the top event. 任何基本事件的组合，如果它们全部发生，就会导致顶事件。
  • Minimal Cut Set / 最小割集: A cut set containing no subset that is also a cut set. Remove any event from a minimal cut set and the top event will NOT occur. Minimal cut sets are the "critical failure paths." 不包含任何也是割集的子集的割集。从最小割集移除任何事件，顶事件将不会发生。最小割集是"关键失效路径"。
  • Top Event Probability / 顶事件概率: For small probabilities, approximated as the sum of the probabilities of each minimal cut set (rare-event approximation). Precise calculation uses inclusion-exclusion. 对于小概率，近似为各最小割集概率之和（罕见事件近似）。精确计算使用容斥原理。

FTA Symbols / FTA符号

  • Rectangle / 矩形: Intermediate event (output of a gate)中间事件（逻辑门的输出）
  • Circle / 圆形: Basic event (root cause, not further developed)基本事件（根本原因，不再展开）
  • Diamond / 菱形: Undeveloped event (not analyzed further)未展开事件（不进一步分析）
  • House / 房形: Normal/expected event (always present)正常/预期事件（始终存在）
  • Triangle / 三角形: Transfer in / out symbol转移输入/输出符号
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MEDIUM
典型问法 / Typical question: "Two independent failures with P=0.1 and P=0.2 feed an OR gate. What is the output probability?"
答题要点 / Key point: AND门=P(A)×P(B)（并联冗余、可靠性高）；OR门=P(A)+P(B)−P(A×B)（小概率可近似为相加）。割集=致顶事件的组合；最小割集=去除任一个就不再发生的"关键失效路径"。符号：矩形=中间事件、圆=基本事件、菱形=未展开、房形=正常事件、三角=转移。
⚖️ 易混淆对比 / Common Confusions:
AND gate 与门OR gate 或门关键区别 / Key Difference
所有输入须同时发生；概率相乘（0.1×0.2=0.02）任一输入发生即触发；概率相加（0.1+0.2−0.02=0.28）AND=冗余/并联、降低概率；OR=单点失效即触发。小概率下OR≈相加（罕见事件近似）

10.5 组合故障树计算 · Combined FTA Probability Calculation☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.36 (System Safety) · Yates Ch.20 (Accident Causation and Investigation)

Scenario: A tank overpressure (TOP event) occurs if the relief valve fails (A) OR the control system fails (B) AND the high-pressure alarm fails (C). Gates: TOP = A OR (B AND C). Given P(A)=0.01, P(B)=0.05, P(C)=0.02. 情景：储罐超压（顶事件）发生在：泄压阀失效（A）或（控制系统失效（B）且高压报警失效（C））时。逻辑：顶事件 = A 或（B 与 C）。已知P(A)=0.01, P(B)=0.05, P(C)=0.02。

P(TOP) = P(A) + P(B)×P(C) − P(A)×P(B)×P(C)
= 0.01 + (0.05 × 0.02) − (0.01 × 0.05 × 0.02)
= 0.01 + 0.001 − 0.00001 = 0.01099 ≈ 0.011

Step-by-step: (1) Compute the AND branch first: P(B AND C) = 0.05 × 0.02 = 0.001. (2) Compute the OR at the top: P(A OR 0.001) = 0.01 + 0.001 − (0.01 × 0.001) = 0.01099. The rare-event approximation would give 0.01 + 0.001 = 0.011 — a negligible difference because the intersection term is tiny. 分步计算：(1) 先计算与门分支：P(B与C) = 0.05 × 0.02 = 0.001。(2) 再计算顶部的或门：P(A或0.001) = 0.01 + 0.001 − (0.01 × 0.001) = 0.01099。罕见事件近似给出0.01 + 0.001 = 0.011——因交集项极小，差异可忽略。

Minimal cut sets: {A} and {B, C}. The {A} cut set dominates because it is a single-event path — it contributes 0.01 of the 0.01099 total. This tells us the relief valve failure is the single most important weakness: adding redundancy to it (or improving its reliability) is the highest-value improvement. 最小割集：{A}和{B, C}。{A}割集占主导，因为它是单事件路径——贡献了0.01099总量中的0.01。这告诉我们泄压阀失效是最关键的弱点：为其增加冗余（或提高其可靠性）是价值最高的改进。

⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
When evaluating a fault tree: (1) always reduce AND branches first (multiply), then OR branches (add − subtract intersection). (2) Single-event (single-basic-event) minimal cut sets dominate the top event probability and are the first targets for improvement. 评估故障树时：(1) 总是先化简与门分支（相乘），再化简或门分支（相加−减交集）。(2) 单事件（单一基本事件）最小割集主导顶事件概率，是改进的首选目标。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MEDIUM
典型问法 / Typical question: "For TOP = A OR (B AND C) with P(A)=0.01, P(B)=0.05, P(C)=0.02, what is P(TOP)?"
答题要点 / Key point: 计算顺序：先算与门(B×C=0.001)，再算或门(A+B·C−A·B·C=0.01099≈0.011)。最小割集{A}和{B,C}——单事件割集{A}主导（贡献0.01/0.01099），是最优先改进目标。先乘后加减。

10.6 事件树分析 · ETA — Event Tree Analysis☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.36 (System Safety)

ETA is a bottom-up, inductive analysis technique. It starts with an initiating event and works forward through a series of safeguards/barriers to determine all possible outcomes. ETA answers: Given this initiating event, what are all the possible outcomes? ETA是一种自下而上、归纳法分析技术。它从初始事件开始，正向通过一系列安全屏障/防护层，以确定所有可能的结果。ETA回答：给定此初始事件，所有可能的结果是什么？

Event Tree Construction / 事件树构建

  1. Define the initiating event (e.g., pipe rupture, control system failure).定义初始事件（如管道破裂、控制系统失效）。
  2. List safeguards/barriers in chronological order (e.g., alarm, automatic shutdown, operator intervention, containment).按时间顺序列出安全防护/屏障（如报警、自动停车、操作员干预、围堵）。
  3. For each safeguard, draw an upward branch (success) and downward branch (failure).对每个防护，画出向上分支（成功）和向下分支（失败）。
  4. At the end of each path, identify the outcome (safe state, minor release, major release, etc.).在每条路径的末端，确定结果（安全状态、轻微泄漏、重大泄漏等）。
  5. Calculate each outcome probability by multiplying probabilities along the path. The sum of all outcome probabilities equals the initiating event probability.通过沿路径相乘概率计算每个结果的概率。所有结果概率之和等于初始事件概率。

Worked Example / 计算示例

Initiating event (P=0.01). Safeguard 1 (S1) succeeds with P=0.9. If S1 fails, Safeguard 2 (S2) succeeds with P=0.8. If both fail, a major release occurs. 初始事件（P=0.01）。防护1（S1）成功概率P=0.9。如果S1失败，防护2（S2）成功概率P=0.8。如果两者都失败，发生重大泄漏。

  • Path 1 (S1 succeeds / S1成功): P = 0.01 × 0.9 = 0.009 → Minor outcome / 轻微结果
  • Path 2 (S1 fails, S2 succeeds / S1失败，S2成功): P = 0.01 × 0.1 × 0.8 = 0.0008 → Moderate outcome / 中等结果
  • Path 3 (S1 fails, S2 fails / S1失败，S2失败): P = 0.01 × 0.1 × 0.2 = 0.0002 → Major outcome / 重大结果

Sum check: 0.009 + 0.0008 + 0.0002 = 0.01 = initiating event probability. Note that a "safe" path still has a probability — the event tree must account for ALL outcomes. 求和校验：0.009 + 0.0008 + 0.0002 = 0.01 = 初始事件概率。注意"安全"路径仍有概率——事件树必须计入所有结果。

⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
The sum of all branch probabilities must equal the initiating event probability — this is an excellent check for your calculations. On a two-barrier tree there are 2² = 4 paths; with n barriers there are 2ⁿ paths. 所有分支概率之和必须等于初始事件概率——这是极好的计算校验。两屏障树有2² = 4条路径；n个屏障有2ⁿ条路径。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MEDIUM
典型问法 / Typical question: "In an event tree with initiating event P=0.01, S1 succeeds P=0.9, S2 succeeds P=0.8, what is P of major release?"
答题要点 / Key point: ETA=自下而上、归纳，从初始事件正向经防护层。各路径概率=沿路径相乘；所有结果概率之和=初始事件概率（求和校验）。n个屏障=2ⁿ条路径。例：0.009+0.0008+0.0002=0.01；重大=0.01×0.1×0.2=0.0002。

10.8 安全案例与风险汇总 · Safety Case Approach & Risk Summation☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.34 (Risk, Risk Assessment) · Brauer Ch.36 (System Safety)

安全案例方法 · The Safety Case Approach☆

A Safety Case is a structured argument, supported by a body of evidence, that provides a compelling, comprehensible, and valid case that a system is safe for a given application in a given operating environment. Unlike single-technique analyses (FMEA, FTA), a Safety Case integrates multiple risk analysis methods into one coherent justification of safety. 安全案例是一种结构化论证，由一系列证据支撑，提供一个令人信服、可理解且有效的案例，证明系统在给定的运行环境中对特定的应用是安全的。与单一技术分析（FMEA、FTA）不同，安全案例将多种风险分析方法整合为一个连贯的安全论证。

  • Core components / 核心组成部分: (1) Claims — statements about safety; (2) Evidence — test results, analysis data, operational records; (3) Arguments — logical reasoning linking evidence to claims. (1) 声明——关于安全的陈述；(2) 证据——测试结果、分析数据、运行记录；(3) 论证——将证据与声明连接起来的逻辑推理。
  • Goal Structuring Notation (GSN) / 目标结构符号: A graphical notation representing Safety Cases, showing how goals break down into sub-goals supported by evidence (solutions). 一种用于表示安全案例的图形符号，展示目标如何分解为子目标并由证据（解决方案）支撑。
  • Application / 应用: Mandatory in high-hazard industries — nuclear, offshore oil & gas (UK Safety Case Regulations), rail, aerospace, and increasingly in process safety. 在高风险行业是强制性的——核能、海上油气（英国安全案例法规）、铁路、航空航天，以及工艺安全领域。

风险汇总 · Risk Summation☆

Risk Summation aggregates individual risk analysis results (from FMEA, FTA, HAZOP, etc.) into a comprehensive risk profile. Methods include: (1) simple summation of risk scores (conservative, may overestimate); (2) risk matrix consolidation onto a common matrix; (3) probabilistic aggregation using Monte Carlo simulation. 风险汇总是将各单独风险分析结果（来自FMEA、FTA、HAZOP等）汇总为一个全面的风险概况。方法包括：(1) 简单汇总风险评分（保守，可能高估）；(2) 将风险映射到共通矩阵整合；(3) 使用蒙特卡洛模拟的概率汇总。

🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MEDIUM
典型问法 / Typical question: "What are the three components of a safety case?"
答题要点 / Key point: 安全案例三要素：声明(Claims)+证据(Evidence)+论证(Arguments)。整合多方法而非单一技术；高风险行业（核、海上油气、铁路）强制。风险汇总三法：简单求和（保守高估）/共通矩阵/蒙特卡洛模拟。GSN=目标结构符号。
