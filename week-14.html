14.1 1. 泄漏预防、控制与对策 · SPCC — Spill Prevention, Control & Countermeasure☆

📖 来源 / Sources: 40 CFR 112 (SPCC) · Clean Water Act · Brauer Ch.27 (Hazardous Waste)

The SPCC rule (40 CFR 112) is an EPA regulation under the Clean Water Act designed to prevent oil discharges into navigable waters or adjoining shorelines. SPCC规则（40 CFR 112）是EPA根据《清洁水法》制定的法规，旨在防止油类排入可通航水域或邻近岸线。

适用阈值 / Applicability Thresholds

标准 / Criterion阈值 / Threshold
地上储油容量 / Aboveground oil storage capacity> 1,320 加仑 gallons（总计，单个容器≥55 加仑）
地下储油容量 / Underground oil storage capacity> 42,000 加仑 gallons
涵盖的油类 / Oil types covered石油、燃料油、油泥、油渣、动物油脂、植物油 / Petroleum, fuel oil, sludge, oil refuse, animal fats, vegetable oils
⚠ CSP考试笔记 / CSP Exam Note:
SPCC is primarily an OIL spill rule. The two key gallon thresholds (1,320 aboveground / 42,000 underground) apply to oil. However, the rule ALSO covers certain non-oil hazardous substances: under 40 CFR 112.9(c), a facility storing a hazardous substance (e.g., sulfuric acid) in a container larger than 42,000 gallons must provide secondary containment — so SPCC-like requirements can apply to chemicals too, not oil only. SPCC主要是油类泄漏法规。两个关键加仑阈值（地上1,320 / 地下42,000）适用于油类。但该规则也涵盖部分非油类危险物质：依据40 CFR 112.9(c)，储存危险物质（如硫酸）且容器大于42,000加仑的设施必须提供二次围堵——因此SPCC式要求也可适用于化学品，并非只管油类。

SPCC计划要求 / SPCC Plan Requirements

  • Must be certified by a Professional Engineer (PE) unless the facility qualifies for self-certification (≤10,000 gal aggregate, no single discharge >1,000 gal to navigable waters, no discharge to navigable waters in the 3 years prior).必须由注册专业工程师（PE）认证，除非设施符合自认证条件（总容量≤10,000加仑，无单次排放>1,000加仑至通航水域，此前3年内无排放至通航水域）。
  • Must describe the facility, oil storage, spill history, discharge prevention measures, and countermeasures.必须描述设施、储油情况、泄漏历史、排放预防措施和对策。
  • Secondary containment is required for all bulk storage containers (dikes, berms, retaining walls, curbing). Must hold the capacity of the largest single container plus sufficient freeboard for precipitation.所有散装储存容器均需二次围堵（堤坝、护堤、挡土墙、路缘石）。必须容纳最大单个容器的容量加上足够的降水超高。
  • Plan must be reviewed and recertified every 5 years, or whenever there is a change in facility design, construction, operation, or maintenance that affects the potential for a discharge.计划必须每5年审查和重新认证一次，或在设施设计、施工、运营或维护发生变化影响排放可能性时进行。
  • Facility must conduct monthly visual inspections of containers and piping, and maintain records for 3 years.设施必须进行每月容器和管道的目视检查，并保存记录3年。

答题要点 / Key point: Aggregate aboveground capacity >1,320 gal (or underground >42,000 gal) triggers SPCC. Total = 4,400 gal → yes. These thresholds are for oil; SPCC also covers certain hazardous substances under 40 CFR 112.9. 地上总容量超过1,320加仑即触发（油类阈值），SPCC也涵盖部分危险物质。

14.2 2. 全球化学品统一分类和标签制度 · GHS — Globally Harmonized System☆

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1910.1200 (HazCom GHS) · Brauer Ch.24 (Chemicals) · Yates Ch.2 (Regulations)

The GHS is an international system created by the UN to standardize chemical hazard classification and communication. OSHA adopted GHS in the 2012 revision of the Hazard Communication Standard (29 CFR 1910.1200). GHS是联合国创建的国际体系，旨在标准化化学品危害分类和通报。OSHA在2012年修订《危害沟通标准》（29 CFR 1910.1200）时采用了GHS。

GHS标签要素 / GHS Label Elements

  • Signal Words / 信号词: "Danger" / "危险" for more severe hazards; "Warning" / "警告" for less severe hazards. Only one signal word per label (the more severe one)."危险"用于更严重的危害；"警告"用于较轻的危害。每个标签只有一个信号词（使用更严重的那个）。
  • Hazard Statements (H-statements) / 危害说明: Describe the nature and degree of hazard (e.g., H301 — Toxic if swallowed).描述危害的性质和程度（如H301——吞咽会中毒）。
  • Precautionary Statements (P-statements) / 防范说明: Describe recommended measures to minimize adverse effects (e.g., P264 — Wash hands thoroughly after handling).描述建议的措施以尽量减少不良影响（如P264——操作后彻底洗手）。
  • Pictograms / 象形图: Red diamond border with black symbol on white background. Product identifier, supplier information.红色菱形边框，白色背景上的黑色符号。产品标识符、供应商信息。

答题要点 / Key point: Health Hazard pictogram (human silhouette with star) = carcinogen/mutagen/reproductive toxin. Skull & Crossbones = acute toxicity (fatal/toxic). 健康危害象形图对应致癌物；骷髅对应急性毒性。

NFPA 704 菱形颜色含义 / NFPA 704 Diamond — Color Meanings

The NFPA 704 "fire diamond" is an emergency-response marking, not a workplace label. Each quadrant has a fixed color and a 0-4 severity rating (4 = most severe): Blue = Health hazard, Red = Flammability, Yellow = Instability/Reactivity, White = Special hazard (e.g., OX = oxidizer, W = water-reactive, ACID, COR). Mnemonic: "Blue is bad for your health, Red is on fire, Yellow is unstable, White is weird (special)." NFPA 704"消防菱形"是应急响应标识，不是工作场所标签。每个象限有固定颜色和0-4严重度（4=最严重）：蓝=健康危害、红=易燃性、黄=不稳定性/反应性、白=特殊危害（如OX=氧化剂、W=遇水反应、ACID、COR）。口诀：蓝伤身、红起火、黄不稳、白特殊。

HazCom 培训时机 / When HazCom Training Must Occur

Under 29 CFR 1910.1200(h), employers must train employees on hazardous chemicals at the time of their initial assignment to a work area with hazardous chemicals, and whenever a new chemical hazard is introduced into the work area (new physical or health hazard). A refresher that repeats the same content is not required, but retraining is triggered by a new hazard or a change in the program. 依据29 CFR 1910.1200(h)，雇主必须在员工初次分配到有危险化学品的工作区域时，以及工作区域引入新的化学品危害（新的物理或健康危害）时进行培训。不要求重复相同内容的定期复训，但新危害或计划变更会触发再培训。

14.3 3. 安全数据表——16个部分 · Safety Data Sheets (SDS) — 16 Sections☆

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1910.1200 (HazCom GHS/SDS) · Brauer Ch.24 (Chemicals) · Yates Ch.2 (Regulations)

Formerly called MSDS (Material Safety Data Sheets). The GHS-aligned SDS format has 16 sections in a prescribed order: 前称MSDS（材料安全数据表）。符合GHS的SDS格式有16个部分，按指定顺序排列：

部分 / Section内容 / Content
1标识 / Identification — 产品标识符、制造商联系信息、推荐用途、限制 / Product identifier, manufacturer contact, recommended use, restrictions
2危害标识 / Hazard(s) Identification — GHS分类、信号词、危害说明、象形图、防范说明 / GHS classification, signal word, hazard statements, pictograms, precautionary statements
3成分/组成信息 / Composition / Ingredients — 化学品名称、CAS号、浓度、商业秘密声明 / Chemical name, CAS number, concentrations, trade secret claims

10稳定性和反应性 / Stability and Reactivity — 化学稳定性、可能的危险反应、不相容材料、危险分解产物 / Chemical stability, possibility of hazardous reactions, incompatible materials

⚠ CSP考试笔记 / CSP Exam Note:
Sections 12-15 are considered "non-mandatory" under OSHA (they fall under other agencies' jurisdictions), but they must appear in the GHS 16-section format. The most commonly tested sections are 2 (Hazard ID), 8 (Exposure Controls), and 9 (Physical/Chemical Properties). 第12-15部分在OSHA下被视为"非强制"（属于其他机构管辖），但它们必须出现在GHS 16部分格式中。最常考的部分是第2节（危害标识）、第8节（暴露控制）和第9节（物理/化学性质）。

14.4 4. 危险物质储存 · Hazardous Materials Storage☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.24 (Chemicals) · Yates Ch.25 (Hazardous Materials Management) · NFPA 30 (Flammable/Combustible Liquids)

化学相容性 / Chemical Compatibility

Store chemicals by hazard class, not alphabetically. Incompatible materials must be segregated: 按危险类别而非字母顺序储存化学品。不相容的物质必须隔离：

  • Flammables away from oxidizers易燃品远离氧化剂
  • Acids away from bases酸远离碱
  • Acids away from cyanides (generates HCN gas)酸远离氰化物（会产生HCN气体）
  • Water-reactive chemicals away from aqueous solutions and moisture sources遇水反应的化学品远离水溶液和水源
  • Organic peroxides stored separately (temperature-controlled); never with flammables有机过氧化物单独储存（温控）；绝不可与易燃品同放

易燃品储存柜 / Flammable Storage Cabinets

  • Maximum capacity: 60 gallons per cabinet (Class I/II liquids)最大容量：每柜60加仑（Ⅰ/Ⅱ类液体）
  • Maximum 3 cabinets per fire area (unless in a facility protected by an automatic sprinkler system, then additional cabinets may be allowed per AHJ)每个防火分区最多3个柜子（除非设施有自动喷淋保护，此时可依AHJ允许增加）
  • Cabinet must be clearly labeled: "FLAMMABLE — KEEP FIRE AWAY"柜体必须清晰标注："易燃——远离火源"
  • Construction: double-walled 18-gauge steel with 1.5" air space, 3-point latch, self-closing doors option结构：双壁18号钢，1.5英寸空气层，三点锁，可选自闭门
  • Vents (if provided) must be plugged with bungs or piped outside通风口（如有）必须用塞子堵住或通到室外
关键数字要牢记 / Key numbers to memorize:
60 gallons max per flammable cabinet. Maximum 3 cabinets per fire area (without sprinkler allowance). These are CSP favorites. 每柜最大60加仑易燃液体。每个防火分区最多3个柜子（无喷淋许可）。这些是CSP最爱的考点。

一般储存要求 / General Storage Requirements

  • All containers must be labeled with the chemical name and hazard warnings所有容器必须标注化学品名称和危害警告
  • Secondary containment for liquid chemicals stored on shelving架子上存放的液体化学品需要二次围堵
  • Heavy/large containers stored at eye level or below (never above shoulder height)重/大容器存放在视线高度或以下（绝不超过肩膀高度）
  • No chemicals stored on the floor化学品不得直接放在地面上
  • Adequate ventilation (at least 6-10 air changes per hour in chemical storage areas)充足的通风（化学品储存区域至少每小时6-10次换气）
  • Eyewash and safety shower within 10 seconds travel (ANSI Z358.1 — within 55 ft, ~10 seconds, unobstructed path)洗眼器和安全淋浴器在10秒行程内（ANSI Z358.1——55英尺内，约10秒，无障碍通道）
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: What is the maximum capacity of a flammable liquid storage cabinet, and how many cabinets per fire area? 易燃品储存柜最大容量与每防火区柜数？
答题要点 / Key point: 60 gallons per cabinet; maximum 3 cabinets per fire area (without sprinkler allowance). 每柜60加仑，每防火分区最多3柜。Store by hazard class, never alphabetically. 按危险类别储存。

14.5 5. 资源保护与回收法案 · RCRA — Resource Conservation and Recovery Act☆

📖 来源 / Sources: RCRA 40 CFR 261 · Brauer Ch.27 (Hazardous Waste) · Yates Ch.25 (Hazardous Materials Management)

Enacted in 1976, RCRA gives EPA the authority to control hazardous waste from "cradle-to-grave" (generation → transportation → treatment → storage → disposal). Subtitle C governs hazardous waste; Subtitle D governs non-hazardous solid waste; Subtitle I governs underground storage tanks (USTs). RCRA于1976年颁布，赋予EPA从"摇篮到坟墓"（产生→运输→处理→储存→处置）全过程控制危险废物的权力。C子章管辖危险废物；D子章管辖非危险固体废物；I子章管辖地下储罐（UST）。

危险废物判定流程 / Hazardous Waste Determination Flow

The four-step determination process: (1) Is the material a solid waste? (2) Is it excluded from RCRA (e.g., domestic sewage, certain agricultural returns, mineral processing wastes)? (3) Is it a listed waste (F/K/P/U)? (4) Does it exhibit a characteristic (ignitability/corrosivity/reactivity/toxicity)? If yes to either (3) or (4) — it is hazardous. 四步判定流程：(1) 该物质是固体废物吗？(2) 是否被排除在RCRA之外（如生活污水、某些农业返料、矿物加工废物）？(3) 是否属于名录废物（F/K/P/U）？(4) 是否具有特征性（可燃/腐蚀/反应/毒性）？(3)或(4)任一为是——即为危险废物。

特征危险废物（D代码） / Characteristic Hazardous Wastes (D-codes)

14.6 6. 污染预防与最佳管理实践 · Pollution Prevention (P2) & Best Management Practices (BMPs)☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Pollution Prevention Act · Brauer Ch.27 (Hazardous Waste) · Yates Ch.34 (Environmental Management)

Pollution Prevention (P2) is defined by EPA as "source reduction" — any practice that reduces, eliminates, or prevents pollution at its source BEFORE recycling, treatment, or disposal. The Pollution Prevention Act of 1990 established the national policy hierarchy. 污染预防（P2）被EPA定义为"源头减量"——任何在再循环、处理或处置之前从源头减少、消除或防止污染的做法。《1990年污染预防法》确立了国家政策层级。

污染预防层级 / P2 Hierarchy

源头减量 Source Reduction > 再循环 Recycling > 处理 Treatment > 处置 Disposal
(Most preferred to least preferred). Treatment and disposal are the LAST resort, not the first. （从最优到最次）。处理和处置是最后手段，而非首选。

具体P2措施 / Specific P2 Measures

  • Equipment/technology modifications — closed-loop systems, counter-current rinsing, dry cleanup methods设备/技术改进——闭环系统、逆流冲洗、干式清理法
  • Process modifications — reducing batch to continuous, improving control instrumentation工艺改进——批次改连续、改进控制仪表
  • Reformulation/redesign of products — eliminating toxic constituents产品配方/重新设计——消除有毒成分
  • Raw material substitution — water-based vs. solvent-based coatings原料替代——水性涂料替代溶剂型涂料
  • Good operating practices — inventory control, spill prevention, waste segregation良好操作实践——库存控制、泄漏预防、废物分类

最佳管理实践（BMPs）/ Best Management Practices

BMPs are structural or non-structural controls to prevent or reduce pollution, commonly associated with stormwater management under the CWA. Examples: spill kits and absorbents, drip pans, covered storage, secondary containment berms, good housekeeping, erosion/sediment controls, preventive maintenance schedules. BMPs是为防止或减少污染而采取的结构性或非结构性控制措施，通常与CWA下的雨水管理相关。示例：泄漏应急包和吸油材料、滴油盘、遮盖储存、二次围堵护堤、良好内务管理、侵蚀/沉积物控制、预防性维护计划。

P2 vs. 污染控制 / P2 vs. Pollution Control:
Pollution prevention eliminates the waste at the source (best). Pollution control (end-of-pipe treatment, scrubbers, filters, incineration) manages waste after it is created (acceptable but less desirable). CSP questions often ask which approach to choose — prefer source reduction whenever feasible. 污染预防在源头消除废物（最佳）。污染控制（末端治理：洗涤器、过滤器、焚烧）在废物产生后加以管理（可接受但不理想）。CSP题目常问应选择哪种方法——只要可行就优先源头减量。
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: S-R-T-D = Source Reduction > Recycling > Treatment > Disposal（源头减量 > 再循环 > 处理 > 处置）——污染预防层级，源头减量最优。 / P2 hierarchy, most to least preferred.

"A company plans to replace a solvent-based parts cleaner with a water-based cleaner to eliminate VOC emissions. Under the pollution prevention hierarchy, which approach does this represent?"
最佳答案 / Best answer: Raw material substitution — a source reduction measure under P2, eliminating the pollutant at the source. 原料替代，属源头减量措施。

14.7 7. 危险物质运输与应急响应 · Hazmat Transportation & Emergency Response☆

📖 来源 / Sources: DOT 49 CFR (Hazmat) · 29 CFR 1910.120 (HAZWOPER) · Brauer Ch.14 (Transportation)

DOT危险品运输 / DOT Hazmat Transportation (49 CFR)

  • Hazard Classes / 危险类别 (9 classes): Class 1 Explosives, Class 2 Gases, Class 3 Flammable Liquids, Class 4 Flammable Solids, Class 5 Oxidizers & Organic Peroxides, Class 6 Toxic & Infectious, Class 7 Radioactive, Class 8 Corrosives, Class 9 Miscellaneous.9个危险类别：1类爆炸品、2类气体、3类易燃液体、4类易燃固体、5类氧化剂和有机过氧化物、6类有毒和感染性物质、7类放射性物质、8类腐蚀品、9类杂项危险物质。
  • Shipping papers, markings, labels, and placards required. Placard must be on the vehicle, container, or rail car — four-sided display requirement.需要运输文件、标记、标签和警示牌（placard）。警示牌必须安装在车辆、容器或铁路车皮上——四面显示要求。
  • UN identification numbers (e.g., UN1203 for gasoline) and Proper Shipping Names.UN识别编号（如汽油UN1203）和正确运输名称。
  • Hazmat Employee Training — every 3 years: general awareness, function-specific, safety, security awareness, and modal-specific training (49 CFR 172.704).危险品员工培训——每3年一次：一般意识、职能特定、安全、安保意识和特定运输方式培训（49 CFR 172.704）。

紧急响应——危险品事故 / Emergency Response — Hazmat Incidents

The Emergency Response Guidebook (ERG) is the DOT/Transport Canada/SCT quick reference for initial hazmat response. It uses the 4-digit UN number OR the placard class to locate the orange-bordered guide page. The ERG includes isolation distances and protective action guidance for initial response only (first ~30 minutes to 1 hour). 应急响应指南（ERG）是DOT/加拿大运输部/SCT的危险品初期响应快速参考。使用4位UN编号或警示牌类别定位橙色边框的指南页。ERG包含隔离距离和防护行动指导，仅用于初期响应（最初约30分钟至1小时）。

14.8 8. 二次围堵容积计算 · Secondary Containment Volume Calculations☆

📖 来源 / Sources: 40 CFR 112 (SPCC) · Brauer Ch.27 (Hazardous Waste)

SPCC (40 CFR 112.7(c)) requires that secondary containment for bulk storage hold at least the capacity of the largest single container plus adequate freeboard for precipitation. Many facilities design to 110% of the largest container OR 10% of the aggregate volume (whichever is larger). SPCC（40 CFR 112.7(c)）要求散装储存的二次围堵至少容纳最大单个容器的容量加上足够的降水超高。许多设施按最大容器的110%或总容积的10%（取较大者）设计。

基本公式 / Basic Formulas

围堤容积 = 长度 × 宽度 × 高度（有效深度）/ Containment Volume = L × W × H(effective)

所需围堤高度 = (最大罐容积 × 1.10) / 围堤底面积 + 超高 / Required Berm Height = (Largest Tank Volume × 1.10) / (Berm Floor Area) + Freeboard

罐容积（圆柱）= π × r² × h / Tank Volume (cylinder) = π × r² × h
罐容积（立式罐，加仑）= 0.785 × D² × H（英尺）× 7.48 gal/ft³ / Tank volume (gallons) = 0.785 × D² × H (ft) × 7.48 gal/ft³
考试提示 / Exam Tip:
Common conversion: 1 ft³ = 7.48 gallons. 1 barrel = 42 gallons. Remember to subtract the tank footprint volume (the space the tank occupies inside the berm) when determining how much liquid the berm can actually hold above normal levels. 常用换算：1立方英尺 = 7.48加仑。1桶 = 42加仑。确定围堤在正常液位以上实际能容纳多少液体时，记得减去储罐占地面积所占的体积。
📝 模拟情景题 / Practice Scenario
※ 模拟情景题，根据考点编写，非历年真题 / Practice scenario based on exam topics, not a past real question
"A storage berm is 40 ft long × 25 ft wide with a usable depth of 3 ft. What is the containment capacity in gallons?"
最佳答案 / Best answer: Volume = L×W×H = 40×25×3 = 3,000 ft³; ×7.48 gal/ft³ = 22,440 gallons. 容积 = 长×宽×高，再乘以7.48换算加仑。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: A facility has tanks of 10,000, 8,000, and 6,000 gal. Design to 110% of largest tank. Required containment volume? 按最大罐110%设计，所需围堵容积？
答题要点 / Key point: 110% × largest = 1.10 × 10,000 = 11,000 gal (vs 10% of aggregate = 2,400 gal — larger governs). Key conversions: 1 ft³ = 7.48 gal; 1 barrel = 42 gal. 记住1立方英尺=7.48加仑。
