18.1 1. NIOSH修订版提举方程 · NIOSH Revised Lifting Equation☆

📖 来源 / Sources: NIOSH (Applications Manual, 1994) · Brauer Ch.33 (Ergonomics) · Yates Ch.22 (Ergonomics)

The NIOSH Revised Lifting Equation (1991, updated 1994) is the most widely accepted tool for evaluating manual lifting tasks. It calculates a Recommended Weight Limit (RWL) and a Lifting Index (LI). NIOSH修订版提举方程（1991年，1994年更新）是最广泛接受的评估人工提举任务的工具。它计算推荐重量限值（RWL）和提举指数（LI）。

方程 / The Equation

RWL = LC × HM × VM × DM × AM × FM × CM

LI = Load Weight / RWL

乘数定义 / Multiplier Definitions

乘数 / Multiplier名称 / Name公式 / Formula关键说明 / Key Notes
LC 负荷常数 / Load Constant 51 lbs (23 kg) Maximum recommended weight under IDEAL lifting conditions. Fixed value — does not change.理想提举条件下的最大推荐重量。固定值——不变。
HM 水平乘数 / Horizontal Multiplier 10 / H H = horizontal distance from ankle midpoint to hands. Reaching out = higher risk. HM = 1.0 at H ≤10".H = 从踝关节中点到手的水平距离。伸手越远=风险越高。H≤10"时HM=1.0。
VM 垂直乘数 / Vertical Multiplier 1 — 0.0075 × |V — 30| V = vertical height of hands at lift origin (inches from floor). Optimal V = 30" (knuckle height — VM = 1.0).V = 提举起点的双手垂直高度（距地面英寸）。最优V=30"（指关节高度——VM=1.0）。
DM 距离乘数 / Distance Multiplier 0.82 + (1.8 / D) D = vertical travel distance = |Vdestination — Vorigin|. Greater vertical travel = more demanding. DM = 1.0 at D ≤10".D = 垂直移动距离 = |V终点—V起点|。垂直移动距离越大=要求越高。D≤10"时DM=1.0。
AM 不对称乘数 / Asymmetric Multiplier 1 — 0.0032 × A A = angle of twist (degrees). Twisting = higher risk. Max A = 135° (AM ≈ 0.57). AM = 1.0 at A ≤30°.A = 扭转角（度）。扭转=更高风险。最大A=135°（AM≈0.57）。A≤30°时AM=1.0。
FM 频率乘数 / Frequency Multiplier Table lookup / 查表 Depends on lifts/min, work duration, and vertical location. Higher frequency → lower FM.取决于提举次数/分钟、工作持续时间和垂直位置。频率越高→FM越低。
CM 耦合乘数 / Coupling Multiplier Table lookup / 查表 Good / 良好 = 1.00, Fair / 一般 = 0.95, Poor / 差 = 0.90. Based on handle design and hand-hold quality.基于手柄设计和握持质量。良好=1.00、一般=0.95、差=0.90。

提举指数解读 / Interpreting the Lifting Index (LI)

提举指数 / Lifting Index解读 / Interpretation行动 / Action
LI ≤ 1.0Nominal risk — task is safe for nearly all workersNo action required / 无需行动
1.0 < LI ≤ 2.0Moderate risk — some workers may be at increased riskJob redesign recommended / 建议作业再设计
2.0 < LI ≤ 3.0High risk — many workers at elevated riskRedesign high priority / 高优先级再设计
LI > 3.0Very high risk — nearly all workers at elevated riskRedesign essential / 必须再设计
考试笔记 / Exam Note:
The NIOSH equation is valid ONLY for two-handed, symmetric lifting of loads with good coupling, in a temperature-neutral environment, with no one-hand lifting, no seated/kneeling lifts, no unstable loads, and when the feet are on a stable surface with adequate friction (μ ≥ 0.4). It assumes lifting duration ≤ 8 hours. NIOSH方程仅适用于：双手对称提举、良好耦合、温度中性环境、无单手提举、无坐姿/跪姿提举、无不稳定负荷、双脚在稳定表面上且有足够摩擦（μ ≥ 0.4）。假设提举持续时间≤8小时。

完整RWL计算示例 / Full RWL Calculation Example

A worker lifts a 35-lb box from a table (V = 30") to a shelf (V = 48"). H = 12", no twisting (A = 0), frequency 2 lifts/min for 1 hour, good coupling. 一名工人将35磅箱子从桌子（V=30"）提到货架（V=48"）。H=12"，无扭转（A=0），频率2次/分钟持续1小时，耦合良好。

LC = 51, HM = 10/12 = 0.833, VM = 1 - 0.0075(30-30) = 1.0,
D = |48-30| = 18, DM = 0.82 + 1.8/18 = 0.92, AM = 1.0 (no twist),
FM = 0.95 (2 lifts/min, 1 hr, knuckle height), CM = 1.0 (good)

RWL = 51 × 0.833 × 1.0 × 0.92 × 1.0 × 0.95 × 1.0 = 37.1 lbs
LI = 35 / 37.1 = 0.94 — 名义风险，无需行动 / nominal risk, no action
📝 真题情景 / Exam Scenario:
"A worker lifts from V = 20" to V = 55". What is the vertical travel distance D and the Distance Multiplier (DM)?"
最佳答案 / Best answer: D = |55 − 20| = 35"; DM = 0.82 + 1.8/35 = 0.87. 垂直距离 = |终点−起点|；DM = 0.82 + 1.8/D。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: What is the Load Constant (LC) in the NIOSH Revised Lifting Equation, and what does HM = 10/H equal at H = 20 inches? LC与HM计算？
答题要点 / Key point: LC = 51 lbs (23 kg). HM = 10/20 = 0.50. RWL = LC×HM×VM×DM×AM×FM×CM; LI = Load/RWL (LI>1 = risk, LI>3 = significant). 记住LC=51磅、HM=10/H。
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: 51-30-10：LC = 51磅；最优V = 30英寸（指关节高度）；HM = 10/H。 / Load constant, optimal vertical height, and horizontal multiplier constant.

18.2 2. RULA和REBA——姿势评估工具 · RULA & REBA — Posture Assessment Tools☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.33 (Ergonomics) · Yates Ch.22 (Ergonomics)

RULA — 快速上肢评估 / Rapid Upper Limb Assessment

Developed by McAtamney & Corlett (1993). Assesses risk to the upper limbs (neck, trunk, arms, wrists). Each body part scores based on posture angle; scores combine into a Grand Score. 由McAtamney & Corlett（1993年）开发。评估上肢（颈部、躯干、手臂、手腕）的风险。每个身体部位根据姿势角度评分；分数汇总为总分。

分数范围 / Score Range行动级别 / Action Level所需行动 / Required Action
1-2AL1Acceptable posture — no action required可接受的姿势——无需行动
3-4AL2Further investigation needed; changes may be required需进一步调查；可能做出改变
5-6AL3Investigation and changes required soon需要尽快调查并做出改变
7+AL4Investigation and changes required immediately需要立即调查并做出改变

REBA — 快速全身评估 / Rapid Entire Body Assessment

Developed by Hignett & McAtamney (2000). Expanded from RULA to include lower body (legs). Useful for tasks with unpredictable postures (healthcare, construction). 由Hignett & McAtamney（2000年）开发。从RULA扩展以包含下半身（腿部）。适用于姿势不可预测的任务（医疗保健、建筑业）。

分数范围 / Score Range行动级别 / Action Level所需行动 / Required Action
10 (可忽略 / Negligible)No action necessary / 无需行动
2-31 (低 / Low)Change may be needed / 可能需要改变
4-72 (中 / Medium)Further investigation; implement change soon / 进一步调查；尽快实施改变
8-103 (高 / High)Investigate and implement change ASAP / 尽快
11-154 (非常高 / Very High)Implement change NOW / 现在
⚖️ 易混淆对比 / Common Confusions:
概念A概念B关键区别
RULA 快速上肢评估REBA 快速全身评估RULA = upper limbs (neck, trunk, arms, wrists); REBA = adds LOWER body (legs), for unpredictable postures (healthcare, construction).
RULA 分数7+REBA 分数11-15RULA 7+ = AL4 immediate changes; REBA 11-15 = level 4 (very high) — implement change NOW.
📝 真题情景 / Exam Scenario:
"Which ergonomic assessment tool is BEST suited for evaluating a healthcare task involving unpredictable whole-body postures such as lifting a patient?"
最佳答案 / Best answer: REBA — it includes the lower body and was designed for unpredictable postures common in healthcare. 医护搬运患者等不可预测全身姿势用REBA。

18.4 4. 视觉敏锐度、人体测量学与疲劳管理 · Visual Acuity, Anthropometrics & Fatigue Management☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.33 (Ergonomics) · Brauer Ch.20 (Visual Environment) · Yates Ch.22 (Ergonomics)

视觉敏锐度 / Visual Acuity

  • Snellen Chart / 斯内伦视力表: Standard test for distance visual acuity. 20/20 = normal vision.远距离视觉敏锐度的标准测试。20/20 = 正常视力。
  • Near Vision Acuity / 近视敏度: Tested with Jaeger cards. Critical for precision work, inspection tasks, lab work, reading SDS and instrumentation.用Jaeger视力卡测试。对于精密作业、检查任务、实验室工作、阅读SDS和仪器仪表至关重要。
  • Color Vision / 色觉: Ishihara test is the standard screening tool. Red-green color deficiency affects ~8% of males, ~0.5% of females.石原测试是标准筛查工具。红绿色觉缺陷影响约8%的男性、约0.5%的女性。
  • Vision and Lighting / 视力与照明: A 60-year-old typically needs 2-3x more light than a 20-year-old for the same task. IESNA publishes recommended illuminance by task (30 fc general offices, 100 fc detailed inspection).60岁的人通常需要比20岁的人多2-3倍的光线来完成同样任务。IESNA按任务发布推荐照度（一般办公室30 fc，精细检查100 fc）。

人体测量学 / Anthropometrics

  • Design Principle / 设计原则: Design for the range, not the average. The "average person" does not exist. Design for adjustability: accommodate the 5th percentile female to the 95th percentile male.为范围而非平均值而设计。"平均人"并不存在。为可调节性而设计：容纳第5百分位女性到第95百分位男性。
  • Static vs. Dynamic / 静态vs动态: Static (structural): fixed-position measurements (height, arm length). Dynamic (functional): measurements during movement (reach envelopes, clearance).静态（结构）：固定姿势测量（身高、臂长）。动态（功能）：运动中测量（可达范围、间隙）。
  • Key applications / 关键应用: Maximum reach = 5th percentile female; minimum clearance = 95th percentile male; workstation height = elbow height.最大可达距离 = 第5百分位女性；最小间隙 = 第95百分位男性；工位高度 = 肘部高度。

疲劳管理 / Fatigue Management

  • Fatigue Risk Factors / 疲劳风险因素: (1) Sleep debt (<7-9 hrs); (2) Extended work hours (>12-hr shifts, >50-hr weeks); (3) Night/shift work (circadian low point ~2-5 AM); (4) Quick returns (<10 hrs between shifts); (5) Monotonous tasks.(1) 睡眠负债（<7-9小时）；(2) 延长工作时间（>12小时班次、>50小时周）；(3) 夜班/轮班（昼夜节律最低点~凌晨2-5点）；(4) 快速返回（班次间隔<10小时）；(5) 单调任务。
  • Regulatory Context / 监管背景: DOT HOS rules limit driving (11-hr driving, 14-hr on-duty for truck drivers). NRC fatigue rules (10 CFR 26). ANSI/API RP 755 for petrochemical. General Duty Clause applies otherwise.DOT服务时间规则限制驾驶（卡车司机11小时驾驶、14小时值班）。NRC疲劳规则（10 CFR 26）。ANSI/API RP 755适用于石化。其他情况适用一般责任条款。
  • Fatigue Countermeasures / 疲劳对策: Adequate sleep (7-9 hrs), strategic napping (15-20 min; avoid >45 min), forward rotation (day→evening→night), caffeine (~30 min onset), bright light. Only sleep reverses fatigue.充足睡眠（7-9小时）、战略性小睡（15-20分钟；避免>45分钟）、正向轮班（白班→晚班→夜班）、咖啡因（约30分钟生效）、强光。只有睡眠能消除疲劳。

答题要点 / Key point: Anthropometrics. Reach = 5th percentile female; clearance = 95th percentile male; workstation height = elbow height. Never design for the "average" person. 记住人体测量学设计原则。
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: 5-95：可达距离按第5百分位女性，间隙按第95百分位男性——为范围而非平均值设计。 / Design for the range, not the average.

18.5 5. 化学封存量与物理核心概念 · Chemistry Containment & Physics Core Concepts☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.10 (Mechanics and Structures) · Brauer Ch.24 (Chemicals) · Yates Ch.15 (Mechanics) · Yates Ch.16 (Hydrostatics and Hydraulics)

化学品封存与储存量 / Chemistry Principles for Containment Volumes & Storage (CSP11 Domain 6.5)

  • Secondary Containment Volume / 二次围堵容积: Largest single container + freeboard (typically 10% or 6 inches, whichever is greater per SPCC).最大单个容器+降水超高（通常10%或6英寸，以较大者为准，依据SPCC）。
  • Mass-Balance Principle / 质量平衡原理: Mass in = Mass out + Accumulation + Consumption. Used to calculate emission rates, spill volumes, capture efficiency.输入质量 = 输出质量 + 累积 + 消耗。用于计算排放速率、泄漏量和捕获效率。
  • Ideal Gas Law for Chemical Storage / 理想气体定律: PV = nRT. Used to calculate pressure buildup from temperature changes, gas generation, relief vent sizing.PV = nRT。用于计算温度变化导致的压力积聚、气体产生和泄压口尺寸。

物理核心概念 / Physics Core Concepts (CSP11 Domain 6.6)

概念 / Concept公式/原则 / Formula/Principle安全应用 / Safety Application
力 / Force F = ma (Newton's 2nd Law). Weight: W = mg (g = 32.2 ft/s² or 9.81 m/s²). Force on guardrails (200 lb), fall arrest forces (max 1,800 lb per ANSI Z359), impact forces (F = m × Δv/Δt).
应力与应变 / Stress & Strain Stress σ = F/A (psi or Pa). Strain ε = ΔL/L₀. Hooke's Law: σ = E × ε. Structural integrity, column buckling, sling tension = Load / (n × sin θ), wire rope design factor (typically 5:1).
能量形式 / Forms of Energy KE = ½mv². PE = mgh. Q = mcΔT. P = VI. Impact energy, falling-object hazards, stored energy in pressurized systems, LOTO energy isolation.
功与功率 / Work & Power Work: W = F × d × cos(θ). Power: P = W/t. 1 hp = 550 ft-lb/s = 746 W. Motor/pump sizing for ventilation, conveyor/hoist capacity, metabolic work rate (light <200 kcal/hr, moderate 200-350, heavy 350-500, very heavy >500).
力矩与杠杆 / Torque & Levers τ = F × d (moment arm). ΣM = 0 at equilibrium. Crane load moment indicators, forklift stability triangle, scaffold overturning, spine compression moment from lifting.
流体力学 / Fluid Mechanics Bernoulli: P₁ + ½ρv₁² + ρgh₁ = constant. Continuity: A₁v₁ = A₂v₂ (Q = VA). Ventilation design, duct sizing, flow measurement (Pitot tube), air cleaner pressure drop, siphon hazards.
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MID
典型问法 / Typical question: What is the definition of stress (σ) and strain (ε)? 应力与应变的定义？
答题要点 / Key point: σ = F/A (force per unit area); ε = ΔL/L₀ (dimensionless deformation); Hooke's Law σ = E×ε. Key safety applications: sling tension, PFAS 5,000-lb anchorage, forklift stability triangle (moment balance). 记住F=ma、KE=½mv²、W=F×d×cosθ。
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: σ = F/A（应力=力/面积）；Q = mcΔT（热量）；1 hp = 550 ft-lb/s = 746 W。 / Key physics formulas for the exam.

18.6 6. 通风——一般原则 · Ventilation — General Principles☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.25 (Ventilation) · Yates Ch.7 (Ventilation) · ACGIH Industrial Ventilation Manual

稀释（全面）通风 / Dilution (General) Ventilation

Used to dilute contaminants with clean air to acceptable concentrations. Most appropriate for low-toxicity contaminants released at relatively uniform rates. 用于使用洁净空气将污染物稀释至可接受的浓度。最适用于以相对均匀的速率释放的低毒性污染物。

Q = V × A

Where / 其中:
Q = 体积流量 (cfm) / Volumetric flow rate
V = 平均风速 (fpm) / Average air velocity
A = 截面积 (ft²) / Cross-sectional area

稀释通风计算公式 / Dilution Ventilation Formula

Q = (q × K × 10⁶) / (C − C₀)

q = 污染物产生率（cfm，液体蒸发）/ generation rate of contaminant (cfm)
K = 安全系数（通常3–10）/ safety factor (typically 3-10)
C = 目标浓度 (ppm) / target concentration (PEL/TLV)
C₀ = 进风浓度 (ppm) / incoming air concentration

For liquids: q (cfm) = [0.0042 × M × (0.7)^0.5 × P_vap × Q_liq] / ... — evaporating liquid formulas are complex; know the general form. 对于液体蒸发，q的计算较复杂；掌握通式即可。
When to use dilution ventilation / 何时使用稀释通风:
Low toxicity contaminants, low emission rates, uniform generation, workers are away from the source. When NOT to use / 何时不应使用: Highly toxic substances, large quantities, non-uniform release, workers close to the source — use Local Exhaust Ventilation (LEV) / 局部排风 instead. 低毒性污染物、低排放率、均匀产生、工人远离污染源。不应使用的场合：高毒性物质、大量排放、不均匀释放、工人靠近污染源——应改用局部排风（LEV）。
📝 真题情景 / Exam Scenario:
"A ventilation duct has a cross-sectional area of 2 ft² and carries air at 2,500 fpm. What is the volumetric flow rate?"
最佳答案 / Best answer: Q = V × A = 2,500 fpm × 2 ft² = 5,000 cfm. Q = VA是通风计算的核心公式。

典型问法 / Typical question: When should dilution ventilation NOT be used, and what should be used instead? 何时不应使用稀释通风？

18.7 7. 局部排风 · Local Exhaust Ventilation (LEV)☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.25 (Ventilation) · Yates Ch.7 (Ventilation) · ACGIH Industrial Ventilation Manual

Captures contaminants at or near the source before they enter the breathing zone. Preferred method for highly toxic contaminants or large emission rates. 在污染物进入呼吸带之前在源头或其附近捕获污染物。对于高毒性污染物或大排放量是首选方法。

LEV系统组件（按气流方向排序） / LEV System Components (in order of airflow)

  1. Hood / 罩口 — Captures contaminants at the source. Types: enclosing (most effective), capturing (exterior — slot, canopy, side draft), receiving.在源头捕获污染物。类型：封闭式（最有效）、外部式（槽型罩、顶篷罩、侧吸罩）、接收式。
  2. Ducts / 管道 — Transport contaminated air at adequate transport velocity (typically 2,000-4,000 fpm for dusts).以足够的输送速度输送污染空气（大多数粉尘通常为2,000-4,000 fpm）。
  3. Air Cleaning Device / 空气净化装置 — Cyclones, electrostatic precipitators (ESP), baghouse/fabric filters, wet scrubbers, afterburners, activated carbon adsorbers.旋风除尘器、静电除尘器（ESP）、袋式/织物过滤器、湿式洗涤器、后燃器/热氧化器、活性炭吸附器。
  4. Fan / 风机 — Provides motive force. Located after the air cleaner (clean side). Types: centrifugal (high pressure), axial (high volume).提供动力。位于空气净化器之后（洁净侧）。类型：离心式（高压）、轴流式（高流量）。
  5. Stack (Exhaust) / 排气管 — Discharges cleaned air outside. Must avoid re-entrainment into building air intakes.向室外排放清洁空气。必须避免重新进入建筑进风口。

关键LEV速度参数 / Key LEV Velocity Parameters

参数 / Parameter定义 / Definition典型范围 / Typical Range
捕获速度 / Capture Velocity Air velocity at the point of contaminant generation required to draw contaminant into the hood 50-500 fpm (low for still air; high for grinding)
面风速 / Face Velocity Average air velocity at the hood opening/face Lab fume hoods: 80-120 fpm (100 fpm typical). Paint spray booths: 100 fpm.
输送速度 / Transport Velocity Velocity within ducts to keep particulates in suspension Gases/vapors: 1,000-3,000 fpm. Light dusts: 3,000-4,000 fpm. Heavy dusts: 4,000-5,000 fpm.

补风 / Makeup Air

  • For every cubic foot of air exhausted, a cubic foot of makeup air must be supplied to the building.每排出一立方英尺空气，必须向建筑供应一立方英尺补风。
  • Insufficient makeup air creates negative pressure — causes back-drafting of combustion appliances (CO hazard), difficulty opening doors, infiltration of unconditioned outdoor air, poor LEV performance.补风不足会产生负压——导致燃烧设备反抽（CO危险）、开门困难、未调节外界空气渗入、LEV性能不佳。
  • Makeup air should be tempered (heated/cooled) and filtered. Supply should be ≥90% of exhaust for general ventilation.补风应经过调温（加热/冷却）和过滤。一般通风的供气应≥排气的90%。
考试笔记 / Exam Note:
Laboratory fume hood face velocity standard is 80-120 fpm, with 100 fpm being the most commonly used standard. ASHRAE 110 is the standard test method for fume hood performance (uses SF₆ tracer gas). Hoods should be tested at least annually. 实验室通风橱面风速标准为80-120 fpm，100 fpm是最常用的标准。ASHRAE 110是通风橱性能的标准测试方法（使用SF₆示踪气体）。通风橱应至少每年测试一次。
📝 真题情景 / Exam Scenario:
"A lab fume hood has an opening of 5 ft wide × 3 ft high. If the desired face velocity is 100 fpm, what is the required exhaust airflow?"
最佳答案 / Best answer: Q = V × A = 100 fpm × 15 ft² = 1,500 cfm. 面风速 × 开口面积 = 排风量。Fume hood face velocity standard: 80-120 fpm (100 fpm typical), ASHRAE 110 (SF₆) test.
