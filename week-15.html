📝 真题情景 / Exam Scenario:
"A facility generates 1,200 kg of non-acute hazardous waste in one calendar month. What is its generator classification?"

15.2 2. 通用废物 · Universal Waste (40 CFR 273)☆

📖 来源 / Sources: 40 CFR 273 (Universal Waste) · RCRA · Yates Ch.34 (Environmental Management)

Universal Waste is a special category of hazardous waste that is commonly generated and can be managed under streamlined standards (reduced regulatory burden) to encourage recycling. 通用废物是一类特殊的危险废物，常见且可在简化标准（减轻监管负担）下管理，以鼓励回收。

通用废物类型 / Types of Universal Waste

  • Batteries / 电池 — Lead-acid, nickel-cadmium, lithium, mercury, silver-containing铅酸、镍镉、锂、含汞、含银电池
  • Pesticides / 农药 — Recalled, suspended, or canceled pesticides, unused pesticides collected under management programs召回、暂停或取消的农药，管理计划下收集的未使用农药
  • Mercury-Containing Equipment / 含汞设备 — Thermostats, switches, thermometers, manometers, barometers, relays温控器、开关、温度计、压力计、气压计、继电器
  • Lamps (Bulbs) / 灯管（灯泡） — Fluorescent tubes, compact fluorescent lamps (CFLs), HID, mercury vapor, metal halide, high-pressure sodium, neon荧光灯管、紧凑型荧光灯（CFL）、HID灯、汞蒸气灯、金属卤化物灯、高压钠灯、霓虹灯

通用废物管理标准 / Universal Waste Management Standards

  • Accumulation time limit: 1 year from the date of generation (or receipt)累积时间限制：自产生（或接收）之日起1年
  • Must be labeled with the words "Universal Waste — [type]" and the accumulation start date必须标有"通用废物——[类型]"字样及累积开始日期
  • Containers must be closed, structurally sound, and compatible with contents容器必须封闭、结构完好、与内容物相容
  • Employee training: basic information on proper handling and emergency procedures员工培训：正确操作和应急程序的基本信息
  • No manifest required for shipment (uses bills of lading instead)运输不需要转运清单（改用货运提单）
  • Handlers classified as: Small Quantity Handler (SQHUW — <5,000 kg total at any time) or Large Quantity Handler (LQHUW — ≥5,000 kg)处理者分类为：小量处理者（SQHUW——任何时候总计<5,000 kg）或大量处理者（LQHUW——≥5,000 kg）
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: B-P-M-L = Batteries, Pesticides, Mercury-containing equipment, Lamps（电池、农药、含汞设备、灯管）——四类通用废物。 / The four universal waste types.
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: Which of the following is classified as universal waste — spent fluorescent lamps, used oil, asbestos floor tiles, or triple-rinsed containers? 哪项属于通用废物？
答题要点 / Key point: Spent fluorescent lamps = universal waste (mercury). Used oil = 40 CFR 279, NOT universal waste. Asbestos = NESHAP/OSHA. Accumulation limit: 1 year. 灯管属通用废物，废油不属。

15.3 3. CERCLA / 超级基金法 · CERCLA / Superfund☆

📖 来源 / Sources: CERCLA (Superfund) · Brauer Ch.27 (Hazardous Waste) · Yates Ch.34 (Environmental Management)

The Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation, and Liability Act (CERCLA, 1980) — commonly known as Superfund — addresses abandoned or uncontrolled hazardous waste sites. 综合环境反应、赔偿和责任法（CERCLA，1980年）——通常称为超级基金法——处理废弃或失控的危险废物场所。

关键要素 / Key Elements

  • Reportable Quantity (RQ) / 报告量: The amount of a hazardous substance that, if released, must be reported to the National Response Center (NRC). If the release is equal to or exceeds the RQ, it must be reported immediately.危险物质的量，如果泄漏达到或超过该量，必须向国家响应中心（NRC）报告。必须立即报告。
  • National Priorities List (NPL) / 国家优先清理名单: EPA's list of the most serious uncontrolled or abandoned hazardous waste sites identified for possible long-term remedial action under Superfund. Sites are scored using the Hazard Ranking System (HRS).EPA列出最严重的废弃或失控危险废物场所名单，确定为可能需要长期修复行动。场所使用危害分级系统（HRS）评分。
  • Potentially Responsible Parties (PRPs) / 潜在责任方: Individuals, companies, or entities that may be liable for cleanup costs. Types of PRPs: current owners/operators, owners/operators at the time of disposal, arrangers (arranged for disposal), transporters (selected the disposal site).可能对清理费用负责的个人、公司或实体。PRP类型：现任所有者/经营者、处置时的所有者/经营者、安排者（安排处置）、运输者（选择处置地点）。

CERCLA责任 / CERCLA Liability

  • Strict liability / 严格责任: The government does not need to prove fault or negligence — only that a release or threat of release occurred.政府无需证明过错或疏忽——只需证明发生了泄漏或泄漏威胁。
  • Joint and several liability / 连带责任: Any one PRP can be held responsible for the ENTIRE cleanup cost, regardless of their individual contribution. They may then seek contribution from other PRPs.任何一个PRP都可能被要求承担全部清理费用，无论其个人贡献多少。之后可向其他PRP追偿。
  • Retroactive liability / 追溯责任: Liability extends to actions that occurred before CERCLA was enacted (pre-1980), as long as the disposal was legal at the time.责任延伸至CERCLA颁布之前（1980年前）的行为，只要当时的处置是合法的。
⚠ CSP考试笔记 / CSP Exam Note:
The three pillars of CERCLA liability — strict, joint & several, and retroactive — make it one of the most powerful environmental statutes. These represent a departure from ordinary tort law. CERCLA责任的三大支柱——严格责任、连带责任和追溯责任——使其成为最强大的环境法规之一。这些代表了与传统侵权法的背离。

现场评估与尽职调查 / Site Assessment & Due Diligence

  • Phase I ESA: Records review, site reconnaissance, interviews, and evaluation of historical use. Conducted per ASTM E1527 to identify potential environmental concerns (recognized environmental conditions, RECs).第一阶段环境现场评估：记录审查、现场踏勘、访谈和历史使用评估。按ASTM E1527执行，识别潜在环境问题（公认环境条件，RECs）。
  • All Appropriate Inquiries (AAI): The due diligence standard that must be met to qualify for the CERCLA "innocent landowner," "bona fide prospective purchaser," or "contiguous property owner" defenses.所有适当调查（AAI）：必须达到的尽职调查标准，以获得CERCLA"无辜土地所有者"、"善意潜在购买者"或"相邻财产所有者"抗辩资格。
  • Phase II ESA: Sampling and analysis to confirm the presence and extent of contamination identified in Phase I.第二阶段环境现场评估：采样和分析，以确认第一阶段识别出的污染是否存在及其范围。
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: S-J-R = Strict, Joint & several, Retroactive liability（严格、连带、追溯责任）——CERCLA责任三大支柱。 / The three pillars of CERCLA liability.
⚖️ 易混淆对比 / Common Confusions:
概念A概念B关键区别
RCRA 前瞻性 / prospectiveCERCLA 回溯性 / retrospectiveRCRA manages CURRENT waste operations (cradle-to-grave); CERCLA cleans up PAST/abandoned sites.
Phase I ESA 记录审查Phase II ESA 采样分析Phase I = records, reconnaissance, interviews (ASTM E1527, no sampling); Phase II = sampling to confirm contamination.
RQ 报告量NPL 优先名单RQ = release amount requiring NRC notification; NPL = most serious sites (HRS-scored).
📝 真题情景 / Exam Scenario:
"A release of 800 lb of a CERCLA hazardous substance (RQ = 1,000 lb) occurs. What is required?"
最佳答案 / Best answer: No NRC report required — release is below the RQ. Notification is only required when release equals or EXCEEDS the RQ. 低于RQ无需报告。

15.4 4. 清洁空气法与清洁水法 · Clean Air Act & Clean Water Act☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Clean Air Act · Clean Water Act (NPDES) · Yates Ch.34 (Environmental Management)

清洁空气法 / Clean Air Act (CAA)

  • Criteria Pollutants / 标准污染物 (6): Carbon monoxide (CO) / 一氧化碳, Lead (Pb) / 铅, Nitrogen dioxide (NO₂) / 二氧化氮, Ozone (O₃) / 臭氧, Particulate matter (PM₁₀ and PM₂.₅) / 颗粒物, Sulfur dioxide (SO₂) / 二氧化硫
  • NAAQS: National Ambient Air Quality Standards / 国家环境空气质量标准 — EPA sets primary (public health) and secondary (public welfare) standards for criteria pollutantsEPA为标准污染物设定一级（公共健康）和二级（公共福利——能见度、农作物、建筑物）标准
  • HAPs: Hazardous Air Pollutants / 有害空气污染物 — 187 originally listed (now 188), toxic pollutants known or suspected to cause cancer or other serious health effects. Regulated under NESHAPs.最初187种（现188种），已知或怀疑会导致癌症或其他严重健康影响的有毒污染物。根据NESHAPs监管。
  • Title V Operating Permits / 第五章运营许可证: Required for major sources of air pollutants. Consolidated permit covering all CAA requirements. Issued for up to 5 years.主要空气污染源需要。涵盖所有CAA要求的综合许可证。有效期最长为5年。
  • PSD / 防止显著恶化: Applies to areas in attainment with NAAQS — requires Best Available Control Technology (BACT).适用于达标地区——要求最佳可用控制技术（BACT）。
  • Non-attainment Areas / 未达标地区: Areas not meeting NAAQS — requires Lowest Achievable Emission Rate (LAER) and emission offsets.未达到NAAQS的地区——要求最低可实现排放率（LAER）和排放抵消。

清洁水法 / Clean Water Act (CWA)

  • NPDES: National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System / 国家污染物排放消除系统 — permits for point source discharges into waters of the United States. Includes industrial stormwater permits.对向美国水域排放的点源发放许可证。包括工业雨水排放许可证。
  • Point Source / 点源: Discrete conveyance (pipe, ditch, channel). Non-Point Source / 非点源: Diffuse runoff (agricultural fields, parking lots, construction sites).点源——离散输送（管道、沟渠、通道）。非点源——分散径流（农田、停车场、建筑工地）。
  • TMDL: Total Maximum Daily Load / 总最大日负荷 — the maximum amount of a pollutant that a water body can receive and still meet water quality standards. Includes a margin of safety.水体在仍满足水质标准的情况下能接收的污染物最大量。包含安全余量。
  • SWPPP: Stormwater Pollution Prevention Plan / 雨水污染防治计划 — required with NPDES stormwater permits for industrial facilities and construction sites disturbing ≥1 acre.工业和建筑工地（扰动面积≥1英亩）的NPDES雨水排放许可证所需。
  • Section 404 / 第404条: Regulates dredge and fill activities in waters of the U.S.; permits issued by the US Army Corps of Engineers.监管美国水域的疏浚和填方活动；许可证由美国陆军工程兵团发放。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MID
典型问法 / Typical question: Which standard applies to new major sources in NON-ATTAINMENT areas under the CAA? 未达标地区新污染源适用哪项标准？
答题要点 / Key point: Non-attainment = LAER + offsets (stricter); Attainment/PSD = BACT. MACT applies to HAPs. Six criteria pollutants: CO, Pb, NO₂, O₃, PM, SO₂ (NOT CO₂). 记住LAER vs BACT与六种标准污染物。
⚖️ 易混淆对比 / Common Confusions:
概念A概念B关键区别
点源 / Point source非点源 / Non-point sourcePoint = discrete conveyance (pipe) → NPDES permit; Non-point = diffuse runoff (agriculture) → BMPs/TMDLs.
LAER（未达标）BACT（达标/PSD）LAER stricter than BACT — non-attainment areas already exceed standards.

15.5 5. TSCA和EPCRA · TSCA & EPCRA☆

📖 来源 / Sources: TSCA · EPCRA (SARA Title III) · Yates Ch.34 (Environmental Management)

TSCA — 有毒物质控制法 / Toxic Substances Control Act (1976)

  • EPA maintains the TSCA Chemical Substance Inventory / TSCA化学物质清单 — all chemicals manufactured, processed, or imported into the US (now ~86,000+ chemicals)所有在美国制造、加工或进口的化学品（现已超过86,000种）
  • New chemicals / 新化学品: Manufacturers must submit a Premanufacture Notice (PMN) at least 90 days before producing a new chemical. EPA reviews and can restrict or ban.制造商必须在生产新化学品前至少90天提交制造前通知（PMN）。EPA审查并可以限制或禁止。
  • 2016 Lautenberg Amendments / 2016劳滕伯格修正案: Updated TSCA to mandate EPA risk evaluations of existing chemicals, no longer requiring cost-benefit analysis (health-only standard).更新TSCA，要求EPA对现有化学品进行风险评估，不再要求成本效益分析（仅健康标准）。
  • Asbestos, PCBs, and lead-based paint / 石棉、多氯联苯和含铅涂料 are regulated under TSCA Sections 6 and 8.石棉、PCBs和含铅涂料根据TSCA第6条和第8条监管。

EPCRA — 应急规划和社区知情权法 / Emergency Planning & Community Right-to-Know Act (1986)

  • LEPC / 地方应急规划委员会: Local Emergency Planning Committee — develops local emergency response plans, receives chemical inventory information.制定地方应急响应计划，接收化学品库存信息。
  • Tier II Reports / 二级报告 (Sections 311/312): Facilities storing hazardous chemicals above threshold planning quantities (TPQs) must submit annual inventory reports to SERC, LEPC, and local fire department.储存超过阈值规划量（TPQs）危险化学品的设施必须向SERC、LEPC和地方消防部门提交年度库存报告。
  • TRI — Toxics Release Inventory / 有毒物质释放清单 (Section 313): Certain facilities must report annually on releases of listed toxic chemicals to air, water, and land. Data is publicly available.某些设施必须每年报告列名有毒化学品向空气、水和土地的释放情况。数据公开可用。
  • Section 304: Emergency release notification — immediate notification to SERC and LEPC of releases of CERCLA hazardous substances or EPCRA extremely hazardous substances (EHS) above RQs.应急释放通知——超过RQ的CERCLA危险物质或EPCRA极度危险物质（EHS）释放需立即通知SERC和LEPC。
  • TRI thresholds / TRI阈值: Manufacturing/processing = 25,000 lb/year; otherwise use = 10,000 lb/year. Applies to facilities with ≥10 full-time employees in covered SIC/NAICS codes.制造/加工 = 25,000磅/年；其他用途 = 10,000磅/年。适用于覆盖SIC/NAICS代码中≥10名全职员工的设施。
考试笔记 / Exam Note:
Remember the distinction: Tier II = what you STORE (inventory). TRI (Form R) = what you RELEASE (emissions). Both are EPCRA, but different sections and purposes. 记住区别：二级报告 = 你储存了什么（库存）。TRI（R表格）= 你释放了什么（排放）。两者都属于EPCRA，但章节和目的不同。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MID
典型问法 / Typical question: Which EPCRA report covers the annual inventory of hazardous chemicals STORED at a facility? EPCRA哪个报告覆盖现场储存库存？
答题要点 / Key point: Tier II (Sections 311/312) = what you STORE; TRI Form R (Section 313) = what you RELEASE. TSCA: PMN required 90 days before producing a new chemical. 二级报告=储存，TRI=排放。
⚖️ 易混淆对比 / Common Confusions:
概念A概念B关键区别
Tier II（311/312）TRI Form R（313）Tier II = stored inventory; TRI = releases to environment.
LEPC 地方委员会SERC 州委员会LEPC = local planning + Tier II recipient; SERC = state-level body.

15.6 6. 可持续发展、ESG与石棉 · Sustainability, ESG & Asbestos☆

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1910.1001 (Asbestos) · Brauer Ch.27 (Hazardous Waste) · Brauer Ch.24 (Chemicals)

气候变化与环境影响 / Climate Change & Environmental Impacts

  • Climate Change / 气候变化: Global alteration of weather patterns due to anthropogenic GHG emissions. Key workplace implications: increased frequency/severity of extreme weather events (business continuity risk), heat stress for outdoor workers, changing disease vector patterns (e.g., expanding Lyme disease range), supply chain disruptions, and regulatory transition risks (carbon pricing, emissions reporting).因人为温室气体排放导致的全球天气模式改变。对工作场所的主要影响：极端天气事件的频率/严重性增加（业务连续性风险）、户外工作者的热应激、疾病媒介模式变化（如莱姆病范围扩大）、供应链中断、以及监管转型风险（碳定价、排放报告）。
  • Air Pollution / 空气污染: Beyond the six criteria pollutants (CAA): Ground-level ozone, particulate matter (PM2.5 = respirable, penetrates deep into lungs), diesel particulate matter (DPM — IARC Group 1 carcinogen). Indoor air quality (IAQ) issues: CO₂ buildup, VOCs from building materials, radon (2nd leading cause of lung cancer after smoking).超出六种标准污染物（CAA）：地面臭氧、颗粒物（PM2.5=可吸入，深入肺部）、柴油颗粒物（DPM——IARC第1类致癌物）。室内空气质量（IAQ）问题：CO₂积聚、建筑材料挥发的VOCs、氡（仅次于吸烟的第二大肺癌原因）。

ESG——环境、社会与治理 / ESG — Environmental, Social & Governance

  • ESG Framework / ESG框架: A set of criteria used by investors, regulators, and stakeholders to evaluate an organization's sustainability and ethical impact. CSP11 Domain 5.5 explicitly includes ESG as a knowledge area.一套由投资者、监管机构和利益相关者用来评估组织可持续性和道德影响的准则。CSP11领域5.5明确将ESG列为知识领域。
  • Environmental (E) / 环境: Carbon emissions, energy efficiency, waste management, water stewardship, biodiversity impact, pollution prevention.碳排放、能源效率、废物管理、水资源管理、生物多样性影响、污染预防。
  • Social (S) / 社会: Worker health and safety (TRIR, DART, LTIFR), labor practices, human rights in supply chain, diversity and inclusion, community relations.工人健康与安全（TRIR、DART、LTIFR）、劳动实践、供应链中的人权、多元化与包容性、社区关系。
  • Governance (G) / 治理: Board oversight of safety, executive compensation linked to safety performance, whistleblower protections, ethics and compliance programs, transparency in reporting.董事会对安全的监督、与安全绩效挂钩的高管薪酬、举报人保护、道德与合规计划、报告透明度。
  • Key ESG Reporting Frameworks / 主要ESG报告框架: GRI (Global Reporting Initiative — most widely used), SASB (Sustainability Accounting Standards Board — industry-specific), ISSB (International Sustainability Standards Board), TCFD (Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures), CDP (formerly Carbon Disclosure Project).GRI（全球报告倡议——最广泛使用）、SASB（可持续发展会计准则委员会——行业特定）、ISSB（国际可持续发展准则理事会）、TCFD（气候相关财务披露工作组）、CDP（原碳披露项目）。

可持续发展层级与概念 / Sustainability Hierarchy & Concepts

  • Reduce, Reuse, Recycle / 减量、再利用、再循环: Waste management hierarchy — source reduction (most preferred), reuse, recycling/composting, energy recovery, treatment & disposal (least preferred).废物管理层级——源头减量（最优）、再利用、再循环/堆肥、能量回收、处理与处置（最次）。
  • Lifecycle Assessment (LCA) / 生命周期评估: Systematic analysis of environmental impacts of a product across all stages — raw material extraction → manufacturing → distribution → use → end-of-life.系统分析产品在所有阶段的环境影响——原料提取→制造→分销→使用→寿命终止（处置/再循环）。
  • Carbon Footprint / 碳足迹: Total greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions caused by an individual, organization, event, or product — expressed as CO₂ equivalent (CO₂e). Scope 1 (direct), Scope 2 (indirect — purchased energy), Scope 3 (other indirect — supply chain).个人、组织、事件或产品造成的温室气体（GHG）排放总量——以CO₂当量表示。范围1（直接）、范围2（间接——购买能源）、范围3（其他间接——供应链等）。
  • Circular Economy / 循环经济: An economic system aimed at eliminating waste and continual use of resources — designing out waste, keeping products and materials in use, regenerating natural systems.旨在消除废物和持续利用资源的经济体系——杜绝废物设计、保持产品和材料在使用中、再生自然系统。对比传统的线性经济（开采→制造→处置）。

绿色建筑——LEED / Green Building — LEED

  • LEED: Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design / 能源与环境设计先锋 — green building certification program by USGBC.USGBC的绿色建筑认证项目。
  • Rating levels / 评级: Certified / 认证级 (40-49 pts), Silver / 银级 (50-59), Gold / 金级 (60-79), Platinum / 白金级 (80+).

石棉 / Asbestos

  • ACM / 含石棉材料: Asbestos-Containing Material — any material containing >1% asbestos.任何含有>1%石棉的材料。
  • PACM / 假定含石棉材料: Presumed Asbestos-Containing Material — thermal system insulation (TSI) and surfacing material found in buildings constructed no later than 1980, treated as ACM unless proven otherwise.在不晚于1980年建造的建筑物中发现的隔热系统保温材料（TSI）和表面材料，除非经证实，否则按ACM对待。
  • OSHA 1910.1001 (General Industry / 一般工业) and 1926.1101 (Construction / 建筑): PEL = 0.1 f/cc (fibers per cubic centimeter) as an 8-hour TWA. Excursion limit = 1.0 f/cc over 30 minutes.PEL = 0.1 f/cc（纤维/立方厘米）作为8小时TWA。短时暴露限值 = 30分钟内1.0 f/cc。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MID
典型问法 / Typical question: A demolition contractor removes thermal system insulation from a building built in 1975, untested for asbestos. How must it be treated? 1975年建筑的TSI未检测，如何处理？
答题要点 / Key point: PACM — Presumed Asbestos-Containing Material (TSI/surfacing in buildings built no later than 1980). Asbestos PEL = 0.1 f/cc TWA; excursion limit 1.0 f/cc/30 min. 未检测的TSI按PACM处理。

15.7 7. 环境管理体系与环境影响评估 · Environmental Management Systems (EMS) & Environmental Impact Assessment☆

📖 来源 / Sources: ISO 14001 (EMS) · NEPA (Environmental Impact Assessment) · Yates Ch.34 (Environmental Management)

An Environmental Management System (EMS) is a structured framework for managing an organization's environmental impacts, using the Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA) cycle. The international standard is ISO 14001. 环境管理体系（EMS）是使用计划-执行-检查-处置（PDCA）循环管理组织环境影响的框架。国际标准为ISO 14001。

ISO 14001核心要素 / ISO 14001 Core Elements

  • Environmental Policy / 环境方针 — commitment to continual improvement, compliance, and pollution prevention持续改进、合规和污染预防的承诺
  • Aspects & Impacts / 环境因素与影响 — identify activities that interact with the environment (aspects) and their effects (impacts)识别与环境相互作用的活动（因素）及其影响
  • Legal Compliance / 法规合规 — identify applicable legal requirements and maintain compliance识别适用的法律要求并保持合规
  • Objectives, Targets, Programs / 目标、指标、方案 — measurable environmental goals with action plans可衡量的环境目标及行动计划
  • Operational Control / 运行控制 — procedures to control significant aspects控制重要环境因素的程序
  • Internal Audit & Management Review / 内部审核与管理评审 — periodic verification and top-management review定期验证和最高管理层评审

PDCA在所有管理体系中的核心作用 / PDCA is central to all management systems:
ISO 14001 (environment), ISO 45001 (safety), and ISO 9001 (quality) all use the Plan-Do-Check-Act cycle. A CSP should recognize this common structure when helping organizations integrate management systems. ISO 14001（环境）、ISO 45001（安全）和ISO 9001（质量）都使用计划-执行-检查-处置循环。CSP应认识到这一共同结构，帮助组织整合管理体系。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MID
典型问法 / Typical question: Which management system standard uses the PDCA cycle — ISO 14001, ISO 45001, or ANSI Z10? 哪个标准基于PDCA循环？
答题要点 / Key point: ALL THREE — ISO 14001 (environment), ISO 45001 (OHS), ISO 9001 (quality) share the PDCA structure. ISO 14001 = EMS; ANSI/ASSP Z10 = US OHS management system. 三者共享PDCA结构。

15.8 8. 地下储罐与废油管理 · Underground Storage Tanks (USTs) & Used Oil☆

📖 来源 / Sources: 40 CFR 280 (USTs) · 40 CFR 279 (Used Oil) · Brauer Ch.27 (Hazardous Waste) · Yates Ch.34

地下储罐 / Underground Storage Tanks (RCRA Subtitle I, 40 CFR 280/281)

  • Defined as a tank and connected piping with at least 10% of its volume underground.定义为至少有10%体积在地下的储罐及相连管道。
  • Regulated substances: petroleum products and CERCLA hazardous substances. Registration required; leak detection, corrosion protection (cathodic), release reporting, and closure requirements.受管物质：石油产品和CERCLA危险物质。需要登记；泄漏检测、腐蚀防护（阴极保护）、释放报告和关闭要求。
  • Major releases must be reported to the state (typically within 24 hours); suspected releases must be investigated.重大泄漏必须向州报告（通常24小时内）；可疑泄漏必须调查。
  • Exemptions: farm/residential tanks ≤1,100 gal storing motor fuel for non-commercial use, and heating-oil tanks for on-premises use.豁免：农场/住宅≤1,100加仑的非商业用机动燃料储罐，以及用于现场供暖的燃油罐。

废油管理 / Used Oil Management (40 CFR 279)

  • Used oil / 废油 is any oil that has been refined from crude oil (or synthetic) and is contaminated by physical or chemical impurities through use.废油指从原油（或合成）精炼而来，因使用而受到物理或化学杂质污染的油。
  • Not automatically a hazardous waste — managed under the used oil rule (40 CFR 279) with distinct standards for generators, transporters, burners, and processors/re-refiners.不自动属于危险废物——按废油规则（40 CFR 279）管理，对产生者、运输者、燃烧者和加工者/再精炼者有不同的标准。
  • Disposal, mixing with hazardous waste, or mixing with any hazardous constituent can cause the mixture to be regulated as hazardous waste ("petroleum refinery waste" rules differ).处置、与危险废物混合或与任何危险成分混合，可能导致混合物被作为危险废物监管（"炼油厂废物"规则不同）。
  • On-site storage tanks must be labeled "Used Oil"; spill cleanup and proper containment required; ≤100% capacity margin with weekly visual inspection recommended.现场储罐必须标注"废油"；需要泄漏清理和适当围堵；建议每周目视检查。
典型CSP辨析 / Typical CSP Distinction:
Spent fluorescent lamps = universal waste. Used oil = managed under 40 CFR 279, NOT RCRA hazardous waste (unless mixed with hazardous waste). Lead-acid batteries = universal waste. Asbestos-containing floor tiles = regulated under NESHAP/OSHA, not universal waste. 废弃荧光灯 = 通用废物。废油 = 按40 CFR 279管理，不属于RCRA危险废物（除非与危险废物混合）。铅酸电池 = 通用废物。含石棉地板砖 = 按NESHAP/OSHA监管，不属于通用废物。

📝 真题情景 / Exam Scenario:
"A facility stores used engine oil. Is it regulated as a RCRA hazardous waste?"
最佳答案 / Best answer: No — used oil is managed under its own rule (40 CFR 279). It becomes hazardous waste only if it exhibits a characteristic or is mixed with hazardous waste. 废油按40 CFR 279管理，非自动危险废物。
