16.1 1. AREC框架——工业卫生流程 · The AREC Framework — Industrial Hygiene Process☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.24 (Chemicals) · Yates Ch.6 (Industrial Hygiene Air Sampling) · 29 CFR 1910.1020 (Access to Exposure Records)

The AREC framework (also called the IH decision-making framework or "anticipate, recognize, evaluate, control") is the systematic approach used by industrial hygienists: AREC框架（也称IH决策框架或"预判、识别、评估、控制"）是工业卫生学家使用的系统性方法：

阶段 / Phase描述 / Description活动 / Activities
A — Anticipate / 预判 Predict potential hazards BEFORE they exist在危害存在之前预判潜在危害 Design review of new processes, Pre-Startup Safety Review (PSSR), Prevention through Design (PtD), review of SDS before purchasing new chemicals, reviewing planned changes (MOC)新工艺的设计审查、启动前安全审查（PSSR）、预防性设计（PtD）、购买新化学品前审查SDS、审查计划变更（MOC）
R — Recognize / 识别 Identify existing hazards in the workplace识别工作场所中存在的危害 Walkthrough surveys (qualitative assessment), SDS review, employee interviews, review of injury/illness logs, process flow diagram review, job hazard analysis (JHA)走查调查（定性评估）、SDS审查、员工访谈、伤害/疾病记录审查、工艺流程图审查、作业危害分析（JHA）
E — Evaluate / 评估 Measure the magnitude of exposure测量暴露的程度 Personal sampling, area sampling, direct-reading instruments, exposure modeling, statistical analysis of data, comparison to OELs (PELs, TLVs, RELs)个人采样、区域采样、直读仪器、暴露模型、数据统计分析、与OELs（PELs、TLVs、RELs）对比
C — Control / 控制 Implement measures to reduce or eliminate exposure实施措施以减少或消除暴露 Hierarchy of controls: Elimination → Substitution → Engineering controls → Administrative controls → PPE控制层级：消除→替代→工程控制→行政控制→PPE
⚠ CSP考试笔记 / CSP Exam Note:
The AREC sequence is tested frequently. Remember: Anticipate comes FIRST (before the hazard exists), Evaluate is where quantitative sampling happens, and Control uses the hierarchy of controls. AREC顺序经常被考。记住：预判最先（在危害存在之前），评估阶段进行定量采样，控制使用控制层级。
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: AREC = Anticipate → Recognize → Evaluate → Control（预判 → 识别 → 评估 → 控制）——工业卫生四步流程，预判最先、评估定量采样、控制用层级。 / The IH decision-making sequence.
📝 真题情景 / Exam Scenario:
"An IH conducts a design review of a new process, a Pre-Startup Safety Review, and reviews the SDS before purchasing a new chemical. Which AREC phase is this?"
最佳答案 / Best answer: Anticipate — predicting potential hazards BEFORE they exist (PSSR, PtD, MOC, SDS review). 预判阶段——在危害存在之前预测。

16.2 2. 工业卫生危害因素 · Industrial Hygiene Stressors☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.24 (Chemicals) · Yates Ch.4 (Particulates and Gases) · Brauer Ch.23 (Noise and Vibration)

化学危害因素 / Chemical Stressors

形态 / Form定义 / Definition示例 / Example
气体 / GasesFormless fluids that occupy space; expand to fill container at NTP占据空间的流质体；在常温常压下膨胀充满容器CO, H₂S, Cl₂, NH₃, O₃
蒸气 / VaporsGaseous form of a substance normally solid or liquid at NTP常温常压下通常为固体或液体的物质的气态形式Gasoline vapors, solvent vapors (toluene, benzene)
粉尘 / DustsSolid particles generated by mechanical action (crushing, grinding, sanding) — 0.1-30 μm机械作用（破碎、研磨、打磨）产生的固体颗粒——0.1-30μmSilica dust, wood dust, coal dust, grain dust
烟尘 / FumesSolid particles formed by condensation of volatilized solids (usually from thermal processes) — <1 μm挥发性固体冷凝形成的固体颗粒（通常来自热处理过程）——<1μmWelding fumes, lead fumes, zinc oxide fumes
雾 / MistsLiquid droplets suspended in air, formed by atomization or condensation悬浮在空气中的液滴，由雾化或冷凝形成Acid mists, oil mists, paint overspray
纤维 / FibersElongated particles with aspect ratio ≥3:1长径比≥3:1的细长颗粒Asbestos fibers, fiberglass, ceramic fibers

物理危害因素 / Physical Stressors

  • Noise / 噪声 — Unwanted sound, measured in dBA (A-weighted decibels)不需要的声音，以dBA（A加权分贝）测量
  • Radiation / 辐射 — Ionizing (alpha, beta, gamma, X-ray, neutron) and Non-ionizing (UV, visible, IR, microwave, RF, laser)电离辐射（α、β、γ、X射线、中子）和非电离辐射（紫外线、可见光、红外、微波、射频、激光）
  • Temperature extremes / 极端温度 — Heat stress (WBGT, heat index) and Cold stress (wind chill, hypothermia risk)热应激（WBGT、热度指数）和冷应激（风寒指数、低体温风险）
  • Vibration / 振动 — Hand-arm vibration (HAVS) and Whole-body vibration手臂振动（HAVS）和全身振动
  • Pressure extremes / 极端压力 — Hyperbaric and hypobaric environments高压和低压环境

生物与人因危害因素 / Biological & Ergonomic Stressors

  • Bacteria (Legionella, TB), Viruses (Hepatitis B/C, HIV, influenza, COVID-19), Fungi/mold, Bloodborne pathogens (29 CFR 1910.1030), allergens, endotoxins细菌（军团菌、结核杆菌）、病毒（乙肝/丙肝、HIV、流感、COVID-19）、真菌/霉菌、血源性病原体（29 CFR 1910.1030）、过敏原、内毒素
  • Ergonomic: repetition, force, awkward posture, static posture, contact stress, vibration, cold, work organization factors人因：重复、力量、不良姿势、静止姿势、接触应力、振动、低温、工作组织因素

特殊危害 / Special Hazards (CSP11 Domain 6.1)

危害 / Hazard关键要点 / Key Points暴露限值/标准 / Limits/Standards
纳米颗粒 / Nanoparticles Engineered particles with at least one dimension <100 nm. Unique toxicity due to high surface-area-to-mass ratio; can cross cell membranes and blood-brain barrier. NIOSH REL for carbon nanotubes: 1.0 μg/m³ (respirable elemental carbon). No OSHA PEL.
可燃粉尘 / Combustible Dust Fine particulates that can ignite and explode when suspended in air. Dust pentagon: fuel + oxygen + ignition source + dispersion + confinement. Kst (deflagration index); St1 (0-200), St2 (200-300), St3 (>300 bar-m/s). NFPA 652 (Fundamentals of Combustible Dust). NFPA 654 (manufacturing), NFPA 61 (agricultural), NFPA 484 (metals).
二氧化硅 / Silica (Respirable Crystalline) Quartz, cristobalite, tridymite. Causes silicosis (progressive, irreversible), lung cancer (IARC Group 1), COPD, kidney disease. OSHA 1910.1053 PEL: 50 μg/m³ (8-hr TWA). Action Level: 25 μg/m³. Medical surveillance at AL for >30 days/yr.
粉末与喷涂 / Powder & Spray Powder coating, thermal spraying, paint spraying. Hazards: overspray mists, flammable atmospheres, combustible powder clouds, heavy metals, isocyanates. NFPA 33 (spray application). Spray booths: 100 fpm face velocity. Air-supplied respirators for isocyanate paints.
喷砂作业 / Abrasive Blasting High-pressure propulsion of abrasive media. Hazards: respirable silica (if sand used), heavy metal dust, high noise (>100 dBA), ergonomic stress. OSHA 1910.94(a). Type CE continuous-flow supplied-air respirator. NIOSH recommends substituting silica sand.
熔融金属 / Molten Metals Foundries, smelters. Hazards: radiant IR heat, metal fume fever (zinc/copper oxides), burns, CO, noise. ACGIH WBGT heat stress TLV. Metal fume fever: self-limiting (24-48 hrs). Aluminized suits, IR/UV face shields.
动火作业 / Hot Work Welding, cutting, brazing. Hazards: fire/explosion, welding fumes (hex chrome from stainless, manganese, zinc), UV (arc eye), electric shock. OSHA 1910.252, NFPA 51B. Hot Work Permit: 35-ft combustible clearance. Fire watch: 30 min post-work. Hex chrome PEL: 5 μg/m³.
高压 / High Pressure Hydraulic/pneumatic systems, compressed gas, autoclaves, water jetting. Hazards: injection injuries (surgical emergency), hose whip, vessel rupture. ASME B31.3. Hydrostatic preferred over pneumatic (water incompressible — less stored energy). Never exceed MAWP.
激光 / Lasers Class 1 (safe) to Class 4 (>500 mW — eye/skin/fire). Primary hazard: retinal burns (eye lens focuses light, amplifying irradiance ~100,000x). ANSI Z136.1. Laser Safety Officer (LSO) for Class 3B/4. Nominal Hazard Zone (NHZ). OD-matched eyewear.
冷/热应激 / Cold & Heat Stress Cold: hypothermia (core <95F/35C), frostbite, trench foot. Heat: heat stroke (core >104F/40C — MEDICAL EMERGENCY), heat exhaustion, heat cramps. ACGIH WBGT heat stress TLV. ACGIH cold stress (WCT). NIOSH heat recommendations. OSHA heat NEP.
⚖️ 易混淆对比 / Common Confusions:
概念A概念B关键区别
气体 / Gas蒸气 / VaporGas is gaseous at room temperature (CO, Cl₂); vapor is the gaseous form of a normally liquid/solid substance (solvent).
粉尘 / Dust烟尘 / FumeDust = mechanical generation (0.1-30 μm); fume = condensation of volatilized solids (<1 μm, e.g., welding fume).
可吸入 / Respirable吸入性 / InhalableRespirable <10 μm reaches alveoli; inhalable <100 μm. 可吸入到达肺泡，健康风险关键。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MID
典型问法 / Typical question: What is the OSHA PEL for respirable crystalline silica? 可吸入结晶二氧化硅的OSHA PEL？
答题要点 / Key point: 50 μg/m³ (8-hr TWA); Action Level 25 μg/m³. 记住二氧化硅PEL 50 μg/m³、行动水平25 μg/m³。Combustible dust pentagon: fuel + oxygen + ignition + dispersion + confinement.

16.3 3. 采样类型与时长 · Sampling Types & Duration☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Yates Ch.6 (Industrial Hygiene Air Sampling) · Brauer Ch.24 (Chemicals) · Brauer Ch.25 (Ventilation)

个人采样 vs. 区域采样 / Personal vs. Area Sampling

特征 / Feature个人采样 / Personal Sampling区域采样 / Area Sampling
采样位置 / Sampling location Breathing zone — within 12 inches radius of the nose and mouth呼吸带——距离口鼻12英寸半径内 Fixed location in the work area工作区域的固定位置
目的 / Purpose Measures individual worker exposure — representative of what the worker actually inhales测量个体工人暴露——代表工人实际吸入的物质 Characterizes background concentrations, identifies emission sources, evaluates control effectiveness表征背景浓度、识别排放源、评估控制效果
用途 / Used for Determining compliance with PELs/TLVs, exposure assessment, epidemiological studies确定PELs/TLVs合规性、暴露评估、流行病学研究 Evaluating ventilation system performance, mapping contaminant distribution, leak detection评估通风系统性能、绘制污染物分布图、泄漏检测
设备放置 / Device placement Sampling pump on worker's belt; collection media clipped to collar/lapel in breathing zone采样泵挂在工人腰带上；收集介质夹在衣领/翻领的呼吸带位置 Stationary tripod or fixed mounting bracket固定三脚架或安装支架

采样时长类型 / Sampling Duration Types

类型 / Type时长 / Duration目的 / Purpose
TWA — 时间加权平均 / Time-Weighted Average Full-shift (typically 8 hours), may be longer for extended shifts整班次（通常8小时），延长班次可能更长 Integrated average exposure over entire work shift — compared to OSHA PEL-TWA and ACGIH TLV-TWA整个工作班次的综合平均暴露——与OSHA PEL-TWA和ACGIH TLV-TWA比较
STEL — 短时间暴露限值 / Short-Term Exposure Limit 15 minutes / 15分钟 Maximum average concentration for any 15-minute period during the workday. No more than 4 excursions per day, with at least 60 minutes between exposures工作日任一个15分钟期间的最大平均浓度。每天不超过4次，两次暴露之间至少间隔60分钟
Ceiling (C) — 上限值 Instantaneous / 瞬时 Concentration that must never be exceeded, even momentarily. Real-time monitoring required必须永远不被超过的浓度，即使是瞬间也不行。需要实时监测
⚠ CSP考试笔记 / CSP Exam Note:
For extended work shifts (e.g., 10-hr, 12-hr), TWA limits must be adjusted. The Brief & Scala model is the most commonly cited: Adjusted OEL = OEL × (8/h) × (24-h)/16, where h = hours worked. 对于延长工作班次（如10小时、12小时），TWA限值必须调整。Brief & Scala模型最常被引用：调整后OEL = OEL × (8/h) × (24-h)/16，其中h为工作小时数。

概念A概念B关键区别
个人采样 / Personal sampling区域采样 / Area samplingPersonal = breathing zone (12-inch radius), measures worker exposure for compliance; Area = fixed location, characterizes background.
STEL 15分钟Ceiling 瞬时STEL = 15-min TWA, max 4 excursions/day, 60 min apart; Ceiling = never exceeded, even momentarily.
TWA 整班Grab sample 抓取TWA = full-shift integrated average; grab = instantaneous for peak/STEL evaluation.
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: For a 12-hour extended work shift, how should the OEL be adjusted? 12小时延长班次如何调整OEL？
答题要点 / Key point: Brief & Scala model: Adjusted OEL = OEL × (8/h) × (24−h)/16. For h=12: factor = 0.5 (limit halved). 记住Brief & Scala模型，12小时班次限值减半。

16.4 4. 采样设备与方法 · Sampling Equipment & Methods☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Yates Ch.6 (Industrial Hygiene Air Sampling) · Brauer Ch.24 (Chemicals)

空气采样泵 / Air Sampling Pumps

泵类型 / Pump Type流速 / Flow Rate目标污染物 / Target Contaminants收集介质 / Collection Media
低流量泵 / Low-flow pumps 0.01–0.5 L/min Gases and vapors / 气体和蒸气 Sorbent tubes (charcoal, silica gel, Tenax), impingers, Tedlar bags吸附管（活性炭、硅胶、Tenax）、吸收瓶、Tedlar采样袋
高流量泵 / High-flow pumps 1–4 L/min Particulates (dusts, fumes, mists, fibers) / 颗粒物（粉尘、烟尘、雾、纤维） Filter cassettes (PVC, MCE, PTFE, glass fiber), cyclone pre-selectors滤膜夹（PVC、MCE、PTFE、玻璃纤维）、旋风预分离器

样品收集方法 / Sample Collection Methods

  • Passive / Diffusion Badges / 被动式/扩散式徽章: No pump required — contaminants diffuse onto collection media at a known rate (based on Fick's Law). Advantages: unobtrusive, no pump maintenance. Disadvantages: affected by air movement (face velocity), not suitable for all chemicals.无需泵——污染物以已知速率（基于菲克定律）扩散到收集介质上。优点：不显眼、无泵维护。缺点：受空气流动影响（面风速）、不适用于所有化学品。
  • Direct-Reading Instruments / 直读式仪器: Provide real-time or near-real-time results. Examples: electrochemical sensors, colorimetric tubes (detector tubes), photoionization detectors (PID — 10.6 eV lamp for VOCs), flame ionization detectors (FID), infrared spectrophotometers (FTIR), aerosol monitors.提供实时或近实时结果。示例：电化学传感器、检气管（比色管）、光离子化检测器（PID——10.6eV灯用于VOCs）、火焰离子化检测器（FID）、红外光谱仪（FTIR）、气溶胶监测仪。
  • Detector Tubes (Colorimetric) / 检气管（比色管）: Glass tube with reagent; air drawn through by hand pump (e.g., Draeger or Gastec). Color change length indicates concentration. Accuracy typically ±25%. Quick screening tool, not for compliance.装有试剂的玻璃管；通过手泵（如Draeger或Gastec）抽气。颜色变化长度表示浓度。精度通常±25%。快速筛查工具，不用于合规判定。

标定 / Calibration

  • Pumps must be calibrated before and after each sampling session using a primary standard (e.g., electronic soap-bubble flowmeter, BIOS DryCal)泵必须在每次采样前后使用一级标准（如电子皂膜流量计、BIOS DryCal）进行标定
  • Flow rate must be within ±5% of the target flow rate流速必须在目标流速的±5%以内
  • Sample volume (L) = Flow rate (L/min) × Sampling time (min)采样体积（L）= 流速（L/min）× 采样时间（min）
样品体积计算 / Sample Volume Calculation:
V (L) = Flow Rate (L/min) × Time (min)

浓度计算 / Concentration Calculation:
C (mg/m³) = [Mass on Filter (mg) × 1000] / Sample Volume (L)
C (ppm) = [C (mg/m³) × 24.45] / Molecular Weight
📝 真题情景 / Exam Scenario:
"A sampling pump is calibrated at 2.0 L/min and runs for 480 minutes collecting a particulate sample. What is the total sample volume?"
最佳答案 / Best answer: V = Flow × Time = 2.0 L/min × 480 min = 960 L. Sample volume = flow rate × sampling time. 采样体积 = 流速 × 时间。
⚖️ 易混淆对比 / Common Confusions:
概念A概念B关键区别
低流量泵 0.01-0.5 L/min高流量泵 1-4 L/minLow-flow = sorbent tubes (gases/vapors); High-flow = filter cassettes (particulates).
活性炭管 / Charcoal tube硅胶管 / Silica gel tubeCharcoal = organic vapors (benzene, toluene); Silica gel = polar compounds (amines, alcohols).
PID 光离子化FID 火焰离子化PID = UV lamp, selective, no fuel gas; FID = hydrogen flame, responds to nearly all hydrocarbons.

16.5 5. TWA与PEL混合计算 · TWA & PEL Mixture Calculations☆

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1910.1000 (Air Contaminants) · Yates Ch.6 (Industrial Hygiene Air Sampling) · Brauer Ch.24 (Chemicals)

TWA计算公式 / TWA Calculation Formula

TWA = Σ(Ci × Ti) / ΣTi

Where / 其中:
Ci = 采样时段i的浓度 / Concentration measured during sampling period i
Ti = 采样时段i的时长（小时）/ Duration of sampling period i (in hours)
ΣTi = 总采样时长（TWA通常为8小时）/ Total sampling duration (typically 8 hours for TWA)

TWA计算示例 / Example TWA Calculation

A worker is monitored for 8 hours: 一名工人被监测8小时：

  • Period 1: 4 hours at 75 ppm阶段1：4小时，浓度75 ppm
  • Period 2: 2 hours at 120 ppm阶段2：2小时，浓度120 ppm
  • Period 3: 2 hours at 0 ppm (lunch/break)阶段3：2小时，浓度0 ppm（午餐/休息）
TWA = (75 × 4 + 120 × 2 + 0 × 2) / 8
TWA = (300 + 240 + 0) / 8
TWA = 540 / 8 = 67.5 ppm

If the PEL for this substance is 100 ppm, the 8-hour TWA of 67.5 ppm is below the limit — no overexposure. 如果该物质的PEL为100 ppm，则8小时TWA 67.5 ppm低于限值——无过度暴露。

混合物计算（加和效应）/ Mixture Calculation (Additive Effects)

When workers are exposed to multiple substances with similar toxicological effects (e.g., same target organ), the combined effect is considered additive: 当工人暴露于具有相似毒理学效应（如同一靶器官）的多种物质时，综合效应视为加和：

Em = C1/L1 + C2/L2 + ... + Cn/Ln

Where / 其中:
Em = 混合物的等效暴露 / Equivalent exposure for the mixture
Ci = 物质i的浓度 / Concentration of substance i
Li = 物质i的OEL (PEL或TLV) / OEL of substance i

If Em > 1, 混合物超过允许暴露限值 / the mixture exceeds the permissible exposure limit
考试笔记 / Exam Note:
The mixture formula applies only when the substances act on the SAME target organ system and the effects are additive. If Em > 1, the combined exposure is considered to exceed the limit even if each individual substance is below its own PEL. 混合物公式仅适用于物质作用于相同靶器官系统且效应为加和的情况。如果Em > 1，即使每种单独物质都低于各自的PEL，综合暴露也被视为超标。
📝 真题情景 / Exam Scenario:
"A worker is exposed to Solvent A at 60 ppm (PEL 100) and Solvent B at 25 ppm (PEL 50), both affecting the same target organ. Using the mixture formula, does the combined exposure exceed the PEL?"
最佳答案 / Best answer: Em = 60/100 + 25/50 = 0.60 + 0.50 = 1.10 > 1.0 → exceeds the limit, even though each substance is below its own PEL. 混合物公式，Em>1即超标。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: A worker is monitored: 100 ppm for 3 h, 160 ppm for 2 h, 0 ppm for 3 h. What is the 8-hour TWA? 8小时TWA计算？
答题要点 / Key point: TWA = (100×3 + 160×2 + 0×3)/8 = 620/8 = 77.5 ppm. TWA = Σ(C×T)/ΣT. 记住TWA加权平均公式。

PEL = Policy (law). TLV = Target (guideline). REL = Research (recommendation). PELs are mandatory but outdated; TLVs are more protective and updated annually; RELs are often the lowest (most protective). PEL = 政策（法律）。TLV = 目标（指南）。REL = 研究（建议）。PEL是强制性的但已过时；TLV更具保护性且每年更新；REL通常是最低的（最具保护性）。

IDLH — 立即危及生命或健康浓度 / Immediately Dangerous to Life or Health

  • Defined by NIOSH: atmospheric concentration that poses an immediate threat to life, would cause irreversible adverse health effects, or would impair an individual's ability to escape由NIOSH定义：对生命构成即时威胁、会导致不可逆的不良健康影响、或损害个人逃生能力的大气浓度
  • Used to select respiratory protection: above IDLH requires SCBA or SAR with escape bottle用于选择呼吸防护：超过IDLH需要使用自给式呼吸器（SCBA）或带逃生瓶的供气式呼吸器（SAR）
  • OSHA requires respirator use at IDLH atmosphere regardless of assigned protection factorOSHA要求在IDLH环境中使用呼吸器，无论指定的防护因数如何

16.7 7. 暴露评估策略与统计分析 · Exposure Assessment Strategies & Statistics☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Yates Ch.13 (Statistics for the Safety Professional) · Yates Ch.6 (Industrial Hygiene Air Sampling) · AIHA Exposure Assessment Strategies

采样策略 / Sampling Strategy

  • Similar Exposure Groups (SEGs) / 相似暴露组: Group workers with similar tasks, processes, and exposure profiles to reduce sampling burden while representing the exposed population.将具有相似任务、工艺和暴露特征的人员分组，减少采样负担同时代表暴露人群。
  • Full-shift vs. grab sampling / 整班次采样 vs. 抓取采样: Full-shift (integrated) sampling gives TWA; grab samples give instantaneous concentrations for peak evaluation or STEL/ceiling comparisons.整班次（综合）采样给出TWA；抓取采样给出瞬时浓度，用于峰值评估或STEL/ceiling对比。
  • Random vs. worst-case selection / 随机 vs. 最坏情况选择: Worst-case sampling (highest exposure periods) is conservative and useful for compliance screening; random sampling supports statistical inference.最坏情况采样（最高暴露时段）较为保守，适合合规筛查；随机采样支持统计推断。

结果解释 / Interpretation of Results

结果模式 / Result Pattern结论 / Conclusion行动 / Action
全部 < 10% OEL / All below 10% OEL暴露可忽略 / Negligible exposure无需进一步行动 / No further action
部分在 10–50% OEL / Some in 10-50% OEL低暴露 / Low exposure定期复查 / Periodic recheck
部分在 50–100% OEL / Some in 50-100% OEL接近限值 / Approaching limit考虑控制措施 / Consider controls; more sampling
超过 100% OEL / Above 100% OEL过度暴露 / Overexposure立即控制 + 重复采样 / Immediate controls + resampling
抽样统计要点 / Sampling Statistics:
Use the 95th percentile (or upper confidence limit, UCL) of a small data set to assess compliance rather than a single sample. OSHA assumes a lognormal distribution of exposures. A single sample above the PEL does NOT automatically mean non-compliance if more data is collected; conversely, one clean sample does not prove compliance. 使用小数据集中的95百分位（或置信上限UCL）评估合规性，而非单个样本。OSHA假定暴露呈对数正态分布。单个样本超过PEL不自动意味着不合规（如果收集更多数据）；反之，一个清洁样本也不能证明合规。

典型问法 / Typical question: A safety professional selects the "worst-case" day (highest production) for sampling. Is this valid? 选择最坏情况日采样是否有效？
答题要点 / Key point: Yes — conservative for compliance screening: if the highest scenario is below the OEL, all other days are too. Use 95th percentile/UCL for compliance decisions, not a single sample. 最坏情况采样是保守的合规筛查策略。
