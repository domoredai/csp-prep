4.1 密闭空间 — 29 CFR 1910.146 · Confined Spaces — 29 CFR 1910.146☆

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1910.146 · Yates Ch.2

密闭空间Confined spaces are a leading cause of multiple-fatality incidents in the workplace (especially in agriculture, construction, and maritime). Understanding the classification system and entry requirements is critical for the CSP exam. 密闭空间是工作场所多人死亡事故的主要原因（尤其是在农业、建筑和海运行业）。理解分类系统和进入要求对CSP考试至关重要。

密闭空间定义 / Definition of a Confined Space

A space must meet ALL THREE criteria to be a confined space: 空间必须满足全部三项标准才算是密闭空间：

  1. Large enough and so configured that an employee can bodily enter and perform assigned work 足够大且配置允许员工身体进入并执行指定工作
  2. Has limited or restricted means of entry or exit (e.g., manholes, hatches, small openings) 进出方式有限或受限（如人孔、检修口、小开口）
  3. Is not designed for continuous employee occupancy 非为员工连续占用而设计

许可密闭空间 (PRCS) 与非许可密闭空间 / Permit-Required Confined Space (PRCS) vs. Non-Permit

A permit-required confined space has one or more of the following characteristics: 许可密闭空间具有以下一种或多种特征：

  1. Contains or has the potential to contain a hazardous atmosphere 含有或可能含有危险气氛
  2. Contains material that has the potential for engulfing an entrant 含有可能吞没进入者的物质
  3. Has an internal configuration that could trap or asphyxiate an entrant by inwardly converging walls or a floor that slopes downward and tapers to a smaller cross-section 内部结构可能通过向内收缩的墙壁或向下倾斜并逐渐变窄的地面困住或窒息进入者
  4. Contains any other recognized serious safety or health hazard (e.g., unguarded machinery, exposed live electrical parts, heat stress) 含有任何其他公认的严重安全或健康危害（如无防护机械、裸露带电部件、热应激）

If a confined space does NOT have any of these four hazards, it is a non-permit confined space — still a confined space, just without the additional hazards that trigger permit requirements. 如果密闭空间没有任何以上四种危害，则属于非许可密闭空间——仍然是密闭空间，只是没有触发许可要求的额外危害。

⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
If ALL hazards in a PRCS can be eliminated (not just controlled) before entry, the employer may reclassify it as a non-permit space for as long as the hazards remain eliminated. Note: atmospheric hazards achieved by forced-air ventilation alone do NOT constitute elimination — they are controlled, not eliminated. 如果PRCS中的所有危害在进入前可以被消除（而不仅仅是控制），雇主可以将其重新分类为非许可空间，只要危害保持消除状态。注意：仅通过强制通风实现的大气危害不构成消除——它们是被控制，而非被消除。
📋 实际案例 / Case Study:
Three workers entered a 12-ft-deep utility manhole to fix a valve. No atmospheric testing was done. The first worker collapsed (oxygen-deficient atmosphere), and the two others descended to rescue him — all three died from oxygen deficiency. The attendant had also entered, leaving no one outside to call for help. Root causes: no atmospheric testing, no permit, untrained entrants, and the "good Samaritan" rescuer effect. Non-entry rescue (tripod + winch + retrieval line) would have prevented the multiple fatality. 三名工人进入一个12英尺深的市政检查井修理阀门，未做大气检测。第一名工人倒下（缺氧气氛），另外两人下井施救——三人均因缺氧死亡。监护员也下了井，外面无人呼救。根本原因：未做大气检测、无许可证、进入者未经培训、"好撒玛利亚人"救援效应。非进入救援（三脚架+绞车+救援绳）本可避免这次多人死亡。

密闭空间角色 / Confined Space Roles

角色 / Role职责 / Responsibilities
进入者Entrant☆ Person who enters the confined space. Must know the hazards, use PPE properly, maintain communication with the attendant, and exit immediately when ordered or when an alarm sounds or when they recognize a hazard. 进入密闭空间的人员。必须了解危害、正确使用PPE、与监护员保持沟通，并在接到命令、警报响起或识别到危害时立即撤离。
监护员Attendant☆ Person stationed OUTSIDE the confined space who monitors entrants. Must know the hazards, maintain an accurate count of entrants, remain outside the space during entry (unless relieved), communicate with entrants, summon rescue (must NOT enter to attempt rescue unless trained, equipped, and relieved), and order evacuation when conditions warrant. The attendant performs no other duties that could interfere with monitoring. 驻扎在密闭空间外部监控进入者的人员。必须了解危害、准确统计进入者人数、在进入期间留在空间外（除非被接替）、与进入者沟通、呼叫救援（除非经过培训、配备装备并被接替，否则不得进入尝试救援），并在条件需要时下令撤离。监护员不得执行任何可能干扰监控的其他职责。
进入主管Entry Supervisor☆ Person who determines if entry conditions are acceptable, authorizes entry, oversees entry operations, and terminates entry. Must know the hazards, verify that the permit is complete and tests have been conducted, verify rescue services are available, and cancel the permit when the operation is complete or when an unacceptable condition arises. 确定进入条件是否可接受、授权进入、监督进入操作并终止进入的人员。必须了解危害、核实许可证完整且测试已完成、核实救援服务可用，并在操作完成或出现不可接受的情况时取消许可证。

4.2 大气检测与监测 / Atmospheric Testing & Monitoring

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1910.146 · Yates Ch.2

大气检测顺序 / Atmospheric Testing Sequence

Testing must be performed in the following order. This is a frequently tested sequence: 检测必须按以下顺序进行。这是经常考查的顺序：

  1. Oxygen (O₂) / 氧气 — Acceptable range: 19.5% to 23.5% 可接受范围：19.5%至23.5%
  2. Flammable/Combustible Gases and Vapors / 可燃气体和蒸气 — LEL must be less than 10% LEL必须低于10%
  3. Toxic Air Contaminants / 有毒空气污染物 — Must be below the PEL/TLV (typically H₂S, CO are primary concerns) 必须低于PEL/TLV（通常H₂S、CO是主要关注点）
为什么是这个顺序？/ Why this order?
You must test oxygen FIRST because: (a) oxygen deficiency or enrichment affects the accuracy of combustible gas sensors, and (b) oxygen deficiency is itself life-threatening. Test flammable atmospheres second because flammability is an immediate explosion risk. Toxics are tested last. 必须首先检测氧气，因为：(a) 氧气不足或富集影响可燃气体传感器的准确性，(b) 氧气不足本身危及生命。第二步检测可燃气体，因为可燃性是即时爆炸风险。最后检测有毒物质。

关键监测值 / Key Monitoring Values

O₂: 19.5%–23.5% (below 19.5% = oxygen-deficient; above 23.5% = oxygen-enriched, fire hazard)（低于19.5%=缺氧；高于23.5%=富氧，火灾危险） LEL: <10% (10% LEL is the maximum for entry)（10% LEL是进入的最大值） H₂S: PEL 20 ppm ceiling; IDLH 100 ppm（上限值20 ppm；IDLH 100 ppm） CO: PEL 50 ppm (8-hr TWA); IDLH 1,200 ppm（8小时TWA 50 ppm；IDLH 1,200 ppm） LEL of a gas: 100% LEL = its Lower Explosive Limit; at or above this concentration the gas can ignite（100% LEL = 该气体的爆炸下限；达到或超过此浓度即可点燃）

LEL% 与可燃气体浓度 / LEL% vs. Gas Concentration

读数读作"占LEL的百分比"：10% LEL意味着浓度仅为爆炸下限的十分之一。
A meter reading "10% LEL" means the gas concentration is one-tenth of its lower explosive limit.

示例 / Example: 如果可燃气体A的LEL为5%体积浓度，则10% LEL读数 = 5% × 0.10 = 0.5%体积浓度。
If gas A has an LEL of 5% by volume, a 10% LEL reading = 5% × 0.10 = 0.5% by volume.

连续监测与初始检测 / Continuous vs. Initial Testing

  • Initial (pre-entry) testing: Performed before entry, using testing instruments calibrated and tested, and covering all areas of the space (top, middle, bottom — stratified atmospheres may differ by depth). 初始（进入前）检测：进入前进行，使用经校准和测试的仪器，覆盖空间所有区域（顶部、中部、底部——分层气氛可能随深度不同）。
  • Continuous monitoring: Required when a hazardous atmosphere is present or reasonably anticipated. If the atmosphere is continuously monitored, workers may be alerted automatically. For confined spaces where the atmosphere may change, continuous monitoring of the entrant\'s breathing zone is required. 连续监测：当存在或合理预期存在危险气氛时需要。若气氛被连续监测，可自动提醒工人。对于气氛可能变化的密闭空间，要求连续监测进入者的呼吸区。
  • When to re-test: The atmosphere must be re-tested whenever conditions change, after ventilation is adjusted, or when a hazard is suspected. If entry conditions change, entrants must exit and the space must be re-evaluated. 何时复测：每当条件变化、通风调整后或怀疑有危害时，必须重新检测气氛。若进入条件变化，进入者必须撤离并重新评估空间。
⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
The three test values (O₂ 19.5–23.5%, flammable <10% LEL, toxics below PEL/TLV) are memorization targets. Also note: 10%–25% LEL allows continued monitoring but NO entry; ≥25% LEL is IDLH. And O₂ enrichment above 23.5% increases the fire/explosion hazard — it is not "safer." 三个测试值（O₂ 19.5–23.5%、可燃物<10% LEL、毒物低于PEL/TLV）是记忆目标。还要注意：10%–25% LEL允许继续监测但禁止进入；≥25% LEL为IDLH。O₂富集超过23.5%会增加火灾/爆炸危害——并不"更安全"。

典型问法 / Typical question: "What are the acceptable atmospheric test values before entering a confined space?"
答题要点 / Key point: 三值必背：O₂ 19.5%–23.5%；可燃<10% LEL（<25%可监测但禁止进入；≥25%为IDLH）；毒物低于PEL/TLV。检测顺序：氧气→可燃→毒物（按危害优先）。气氛变化须复测；存在危险气氛须连续监测。
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: "19.5-23.5-10-25" — O₂下限19.5%、上限23.5%；可燃<10% LEL可进、<25%监测禁进、≥25% IDLH。中文"氧气一九点五起，二十三半点五止，可燃一成二成五"。检测顺序"氧→燃→毒"。

4.3 进入程序与救援 / Entry Procedures & Rescue

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1910.146 · Yates Ch.2

进入许可 / Entry Permit

A written entry permit must be completed before any PRCS entry. The permit documents: 任何PRCS进入前必须完成书面进入许可。许可记录：

  • The space to be entered and the purpose of the entry 进入的空间和进入目的
  • Date and authorized duration of entry 进入的日期和授权持续时间
  • Names of authorized entrants and attendant(s) 授权进入者和监护员的姓名
  • The hazards of the space 空间的危害
  • Measures used to isolate the space and eliminate/control hazards (LOTO, purging, ventilation) 用于隔离空间和消除/控制危害的措施（LOTO、吹扫、通风）
  • Acceptable entry conditions and results of initial testing 可接受的进入条件和初始检测结果
  • Rescue services, their contact info, and means of summoning them 救援服务、联系方式及召唤方式
  • Equipment to be used (communication, retrieval, PPE, testing) 要使用的设备（通信、救援、PPE、检测）

The permit must be signed by the entry supervisor who authorizes entry, and must be available to all entrants or posted at the entry. The permit expires when the operation is completed or a new condition exists. 许可必须由授权进入的进入主管签署，并可供所有进入者查阅或张贴在入口处。当操作完成或出现新条件时许可失效。

进入前隔离 / Pre-Entry Isolation

  • LOTO: Disconnect and lockout all energy sources (mechanical, electrical, hydraulic, pneumatic, chemical, thermal). LOTO：断开并锁定所有能量源（机械、电气、液压、气动、化学、热力）。
  • Line breaking / blanking: Physically blank or cap lines that could introduce hazardous materials (e.g., using blind flanges or line blanks), not just close a valve. 管道断开/盲板：物理盲断或封堵可能引入危险物料的管线（如使用盲板法兰或管线盲板），而不只是关闭阀门。
  • Purge and clean: Remove hazardous materials; purge with inert gas where appropriate; ventilate before entry. 吹扫和清洁：清除危险物料；必要时用惰性气体吹扫；进入前通风。

救援要求 / Rescue Requirements

  • Employers must ensure that rescue services are available and can respond in a timely manner. 雇主必须确保救援服务可用并能及时响应。
  • Rescue team members must be provided with and trained in the proper use of PPE and rescue equipment. 救援团队成员必须配备PPE和救援设备，并接受正确使用培训。
  • Rescue teams must practice permit space rescues at least once every 12 months. 救援团队必须至少每12个月进行一次许可空间救援演练。
  • Non-entry rescue (using retrieval systems: harness, tripod, winch) is preferred whenever possible — the attendant must never enter the space to attempt rescue unless properly trained, equipped, and relieved by another attendant. 非进入救援（使用救援系统：安全带、三脚架、绞车）在可能时应优先使用——除非经过适当培训、配备装备并被另一名监护员接替，监护员绝不得进入空间尝试救援。
  • A retrieval line must be attached to the entrant\'s full-body harness and connected to a winch/tripod when the entry is through a top opening, unless it would increase the entrant\'s risk. 当从顶部开口进入时，必须将救援绳连接到进入者的全身式安全带，并连接到绞车/三脚架，除非这会增加进入者的风险。
📋 实际案例 / Case Study (successful non-entry rescue):
An entrant working in a sewer manhole reported dizziness over the radio. The attendant, who had maintained continuous communication and a retrieval line attached to the entrant\'s harness, activated the winch and brought the entrant out in under 30 seconds. The entrant was evaluated for oxygen deficiency by EMS and recovered. The pre-planned retrieval system and attendant discipline prevented what could have been a fatal rescue-chain incident. 一名进入者在污水检查井中作业，通过对讲机报告头晕。监护员保持持续通信并将救援绳连接到进入者的安全带，启动绞车在30秒内将进入者拉出。EMS评估缺氧情况后进入者康复。预先规划的救援系统和监护员纪律，避免了一场可能致命的多米诺救援事故。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "What rescue must be available for permit-required confined spaces?"
答题要点 / Key point: 优先非进入救援（救援绳+三脚架+绞车）；救援队每12个月至少演练一次；救援队须配PPE并培训。进入前隔离：LOTO全部能量源、管线盲板断开（不止关阀）、吹扫清洁+通风。监护员绝不进入空间救援。

4.4 上锁挂牌 (LOTO) — 29 CFR 1910.147 · Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) — 29 CFR 1910.147☆

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1910.147 · Yates Ch.2 · Yates Ch.23

危险能量控制 (LOTO)The Control of Hazardous Energy (LOTO) standard applies to the servicing and maintenance of machines and equipment where unexpected energization, startup, or release of stored energy could cause injury. 危险能量控制（LOTO）标准适用于机器的维修和保养，其中意外通电、启动或储存能量的释放可能导致伤害。

危险能量的类型 / Types of Hazardous Energy

  • Electrical / 电能: Mains supply, capacitors, batteries. 主电源、电容器、电池。
  • Mechanical / 机械能: Rotating flywheels, elevated machine members, springs (compressed/tensioned). 旋转飞轮、升高的机器部件、弹簧（压缩/拉伸）。
  • Hydraulic & pneumatic / 液压与气动: Pressurized fluid or gas in accumulators, cylinders, and lines. 蓄能器、气缸和管路中的带压流体或气体。
  • Chemical / 化学能: Reactive chemicals, pressurized reactants. 活性化学品、带压反应物。
  • Thermal / 热能: Steam, hot surfaces, cryogenic fluids. 蒸汽、高温表面、低温流体。

LOTO覆盖范围与例外 / LOTO Coverage and Exceptions

Covered: Normal production operations when a guard is bypassed or removed; servicing and maintenance during which unexpected energization could occur. 适用：正常生产操作中防护装置被绕过或移除时；维修保养期间可能发生意外通电时。

Exceptions (LOTO does NOT apply): 例外（LOTO不适用）：

  • Work on cord-and-plug connected electrical equipment where the plug is under the exclusive control of the employee performing the work 对插头连接的电气设备进行作业，且插头在执行作业的员工专属控制之下
  • Hot tap operations on pressurized pipelines (gas, steam, water, petroleum) where continuity of service is essential and shutdown is impractical — requires a specific documented procedure 对加压管道（燃气、蒸汽、水、石油）进行带压开孔作业，服务连续性至关重要且停运不可行——需要具体的书面程序
  • Minor tool changes and adjustments that are routine, repetitive, and integral to production — provided they are performed with alternative effective protection 常规、重复且为生产必需的微小工具更换和调整——前提是采用替代的有效防护

六步LOTO程序 / The 6-Step LOTO Procedure

  1. Prepare for shutdown / 准备停机 — Identify all energy sources, hazards, and the proper shutdown sequence. 识别所有能量源、危害和正确的停机顺序。
  2. Notify affected employees / 通知受影响员工 — Inform all employees who work in the area or operate the equipment that LOTO is about to occur. 通知所有在该区域工作或操作设备的员工即将进行LOTO。
  3. Shut down the equipment / 关闭设备 — Follow the established, orderly shutdown procedure. 按照既定的有序停机程序执行。
  4. Isolate energy sources / 隔离能量源 — Operate all energy-isolating devices (breakers, valves, disconnects) so the equipment is isolated from its energy sources. 操作所有能量隔离装置（断路器、阀门、隔离开关），使设备与其能量源隔离。
  5. Apply lockout/tagout devices / 施加LOTO装置 — Attach lock(s) and tag(s) to each energy-isolating device. Each authorized employee applies their own lock and tag. 将锁具和标签附加到每个能量隔离装置上。每名授权员工施加自己的锁具和标签。
  6. Release, block, or dissipate stored energy / 释放、阻断或消散储存能量 — Check for and safely release all stored or residual energy (capacitors, springs, elevated machine members, rotating flywheels, hydraulic/pneumatic pressure, steam). Then verify isolation by attempting to start the equipment (ensuring the start button/switch is returned to OFF afterward).
    7. 检查并安全释放所有储存或残余能量（电容器、弹簧、升高的机器部件、旋转飞轮、液压/气动压力、蒸汽）。然后通过尝试启动设备来验证隔离（确保之后将启动按钮/开关恢复到OFF）。
⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
The "verify isolation" step — attempting to start the equipment after energy is released — is a frequently tested concept. The start button must be returned to the OFF position after the verification attempt. Also remember: step 4 (isolate) comes BEFORE step 5 (apply devices), and step 6 includes both releasing stored energy AND verification. "验证隔离"步骤——在能量释放后尝试启动设备——是高频考点。验证尝试后必须将启动按钮恢复到OFF位置。还要记住：第4步（隔离）在第5步（施加装置）之前，第6步既包括释放储存能量也包括验证。

典型问法 / Typical question: "What is the correct sequence for applying LOTO?" / "Which step verifies isolation?"
答题要点 / Key point: 六步：通知 → 关停 → 隔离能量源 → 施加锁具/标签（每名授权员工用自己的锁和标签）→ 释放储存能量+验证隔离（尝试启动再恢复到OFF）。高频陷阱：验证=尝试启动；启动按钮验证后须回到OFF；隔离(step4)在施加装置(step5)之前。

4.5 LOTO人员职责与程序管理 / LOTO Roles, Devices & Program Administration

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1910.147 · Yates Ch.2

授权员工与受影响员工 / Authorized vs. Affected Employees

授权员工 / Authorized Employee受影响员工 / Affected Employee
One who locks or tags out equipment to perform servicing/maintenance. Must be trained to recognize hazardous energy sources, the type and magnitude of energy, and the means of energy isolation and control. 对设备执行上锁/挂牌以进行维修/保养的人员。必须经过培训，能够识别危险能量源、能量的类型和大小以及能量隔离和控制的方法。 One whose job requires operating or working in an area where LOTO is being performed. Must be trained on the purpose and use of LOTO procedures, and must NEVER attempt to restart or re-energize locked/tagged-out equipment. 工作需要操作或在执行LOTO的区域工作的人员。必须经过培训，了解LOTO程序的目的和使用，并且绝不得尝试重新启动或重新通电已上锁/挂牌的设备。

群体LOTO / Group LOTO

When servicing is performed by a crew, a single authorized employee is designated as the primary authorized employee with overall responsibility. Each authorized employee affixes their own personal lock to a group lockout mechanism (lockbox or hasp). The primary authorized employee applies the group lockout/tagout and verifies isolation; individual crew members attach/detach their personal locks before and after their portion of the work. 当由一组人员执行维修时，指定一名授权员工作为主要负责人，承担总体责任。每名授权员工将其个人锁具附加到群体上锁装置（锁箱或搭扣）上。主要负责人执行群体上锁挂牌并验证隔离；各组成员在其工作部分开始前附上、结束后摘除个人锁具。

定期检查 / Periodic Inspections

LOTO procedures must be inspected by an authorized employee (other than those using the procedure) at least annually. The inspection must: LOTO程序必须由一名授权员工（非使用该程序的人员）至少每年检查一次。检查必须：

  • Review each authorized employee's responsibilities under the procedure 审查程序下每名授权员工的职责
  • Be documented (certified) — identifying the equipment, inspection date, employees included, and the inspector 记录（认证）——注明设备、检查日期、参与员工和检查员

LOTO装置要求 / LOTO Device Requirements

  • Durable / 耐用: Must withstand the environment for the duration of exposure 必须在暴露期间承受环境条件
  • Standardized / 标准化: Same color, shape, or size within a facility (locks must be distinct from other locks in the workplace) 设施内统一的颜色、形状或尺寸（锁具必须与工作场所其他锁具区分开）
  • Substantial / 牢固: Tags must be non-reusable, self-locking, non-releasable with ≥ 50 lbs locking strength; must say "DO NOT START," "DO NOT OPEN," or similar 标签必须不可重复使用、自锁、不可脱开，锁定强度≥50磅；必须标注"DO NOT START""DO NOT OPEN"或类似措辞
  • Identifiable / 可识别: Must identify the employee who applied the device 必须标明施加装置的员工身份
  • Singular purpose / 专用: LOTO devices shall not be used for purposes other than controlling hazardous energy LOTO装置不得用于控制危险能量以外的目的

恢复作业程序 / Restoring Equipment to Service

  1. Inspect the work area to ensure all tools and nonessential items are removed and guards are back in place. 检查工作区域，确保所有工具和非必需物品已移除，防护装置已复位。
  2. Ensure all employees are clear of the equipment. 确保所有员工远离设备。
  3. Each authorized employee removes their own lock and tag (only the person who applied a lock may remove it; in rare cases a supervisor may remove it under strict documented procedures). 每名授权员工摘除自己的锁具和标签（只有施加锁具的人才能摘除；极少数情况下主管可在严格的书面程序下摘除）。
  4. Notify affected employees that servicing is complete and the equipment may be restarted. 通知受影响员工维修已完成，设备可重新启动。
📋 实际案例 / Case Study:
A mixer in a food plant was being serviced by a maintenance crew using group LOTO. The primary authorized employee removed the group hasp at the end of his shift before the repair was complete, believing the work was done. Another operator started the mixer, seriously injuring a mechanic still inside. Root causes: premature removal of group LOTO, no relief/shift-turnover process for the primary authorized employee, and no verification that all crew members had removed their personal locks. 一家食品厂的搅拌机由维修班组使用群体LOTO进行维修。主要负责人在下班时提前摘除了群体搭扣，以为工作已完成。另一名操作员启动了搅拌机，重伤了仍在机器内的机械工。根本原因：过早摘除群体LOTO、主要负责人无交班/接替程序、未核实所有成员都已摘除个人锁具。

答题要点 / Key point: LOTO程序每年至少检查一次，由授权员工（非使用该程序者）进行并记录认证。锁具要求：耐用/标准化/牢固/可识别/专用；标签锁定强度≥50磅。只能由施加锁具者摘除自己的锁；提前摘除群体LOTO是最典型事故根因。

4.6 挖掘与沟槽 — 29 CFR 1926 Subpart P · Excavation and Trenching — 29 CFR 1926 Subpart P☆

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1926 Subpart P · Yates Ch.23

挖掘安全Excavation safety is one of the most frequently cited construction standards. Trench collapses cause dozens of fatalities annually, and virtually all of them are preventable. Trench cave-ins are the most common caught-in/between fatality in construction. 挖掘安全是最常被引用的建筑标准之一。沟槽坍塌每年造成数十人死亡，几乎所有这些事故都是可以预防的。沟槽坍塌是建筑施工中最常见的卷入/挤压致死原因。

关键定义 / Key Definitions

  • Excavation / 挖掘: Any man-made cut, cavity, trench, or depression formed by earth removal. 由移除土方形成的任何人造切口、空腔、沟槽或凹陷。
  • Trench / 沟槽: A narrow excavation (in relation to its length) where the depth is greater than the width, and the width at the bottom is ≤ 15 feet. 窄型挖掘（相对于其长度），深度大于宽度，底部宽度≤15英尺。
  • Competent Person / 合格人员: Capable of identifying existing and predictable hazards in the surroundings, or working conditions that are hazardous or dangerous to employees, and who has authorization to take prompt corrective measures. Must inspect excavations daily and as conditions change. 能够识别环境中存在的和可预测的危害，或对员工危险的工作条件，并有权采取及时纠正措施的人员。必须每天以及在条件变化时检查挖掘。

土壤分类 / Soil Classification

A型 — 最稳定（最高抗压强度）/ Type A — Most Stable (highest compressive strength)
• 无侧限抗压强度≥1.5吨/平方英尺(tsf)的粘性土壤 / Cohesive soils with unconfined compressive strength ≥ 1.5 tsf
• 示例：粘土、粉质粘土、砂质粘土、粘壤土 / Examples: clay, silty clay, sandy clay, clay loam
• 如果土壤有裂缝、受振动影响或曾被扰动，则不属于A型 / NO soil is Type A if it is fissured, subject to vibration, or has been previously disturbed

B型 — 中等稳定 / Type B — Moderately Stable
• 无侧限抗压强度>0.5 tsf但<1.5 tsf的粘性土壤 / Cohesive soils with unconfined compressive strength > 0.5 tsf but < 1.5 tsf
• 示例：角砾石、粉土、粉壤土、曾被扰动的土壤 / Examples: angular gravel, silt, silt loam, previously disturbed soils

C型 — 最不稳定（最低抗压强度）/ Type C — Least Stable (lowest compressive strength)
• 无侧限抗压强度≤0.5 tsf的粘性土壤 / Cohesive soils with unconfined compressive strength ≤ 0.5 tsf
• 示例：砾石、砂、壤砂土、水下土壤、自由渗水的土壤 / Examples: gravel, sand, loamy sand, submerged soil, soil from which water is freely seeping

稳定岩层 / Stable Rock: 可垂直侧壁开挖并保持完好的天然固体矿物 / Natural solid mineral matter that can be excavated with vertical sides and remains intact while exposed.
⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
A soil that is fissured, exposed to vibration (e.g., heavy equipment nearby, blasting), or previously disturbed CANNOT be Type A — it must be downgraded. Also, all soil in the bottom of the trench or under water is assumed Type C unless proven otherwise. These downgrades are frequently tested. 有裂缝、受振动影响（如附近重型设备、爆破）或曾被扰动的土壤不能归为A型——必须降级。另外，沟槽底部或被水淹没的所有土壤，除非另有证明，均假定为C型。这些降级规则是高频考点。

防护系统 / Protective Systems

系统 / System描述 / Description关键要求 / Key Requirements
放坡/台阶式开挖Sloping / Benching☆ Cutting back the trench wall at an angle inclined away from the excavation. Maximum allowable slope depends on soil type. 以远离挖掘的角度削切沟槽壁。最大允许坡度取决于土壤类型。 Type A: ¾:1 (53°). Type B: 1:1 (45°). Type C: 1½:1 (34°). Benching is NOT permitted in Type C soils. A型：3/4:1(53°)。B型：1:1(45°)。C型：1.5:1(34°)。C型土壤不允许台阶式开挖。
防护（沟槽箱）Shielding (Trench Box)☆ A structure (typically a trench box) capable of withstanding the forces of a cave-in to protect workers inside. 能够承受塌方力以保护内部工人的结构（通常为沟槽箱）。 Must extend at least 18 inches above the trench wall top if vertical travel is involved, and no more than 2 feet below the trench bottom (unless designed otherwise by a registered PE). Workers must stay inside the shield at all times. 如果有垂直移动，必须延伸到沟槽壁顶部上方至少18英寸，底部不超过沟槽底部下方2英尺（除非注册专业工程师另有设计）。工人必须始终留在防护装置内。
支撑Shoring☆ A support system (hydraulic, pneumatic, timber) installed to prevent cave-in by applying positive pressure against the trench walls. 通过向沟槽壁施加正压力来防止塌方的支撑系统（液压、气动、木质）。 Must be designed by a qualified person or per manufacturer tabulated data. Installed from the top down, removed from the bottom up. 必须由合格人员设计或依据制造商表格数据。自上而下安装，自下而上拆除。

答题要点 / Key point: 沟槽定义：深度>宽度、底部宽度≤15英尺。深度≥5英尺必须有防护系统（坡度/沟槽箱/支撑），除非开挖在实岩中且无坍塌可能。坡度：A型3/4:1、B型1:1、C型1.5:1（C型禁台阶式）。沟槽箱：顶部超出≥18英寸、底部不超2英尺、工人须始终在箱内。支撑：自上而下装、自下而上拆。

⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
The "5-foot rule" (protective system required at 5 ft+) is heavily tested, but also: trenches under 5 feet DO require a protective system if a competent person determines a cave-in hazard exists. Always read the question carefully — some scenarios imply unstable soil conditions even at shallow depths. Also remember the 4-ft atmospheric testing trigger and the 25-ft egress rule. "5英尺规则"（5英尺以上需要防护系统）是高频考点，但也要注意：如果合格人员确定存在塌方危险，低于5英尺的沟槽也需要防护系统。务必仔细阅读题目——有些场景暗示即使浅层也存在不稳定土壤条件。还要记住4英尺大气检测触发和25英尺进出规则。

挖掘安全计算 / Excavation Math

放坡宽度计算 / Sloping Width Calculation

放坡水平距离 = 沟槽深度 × 坡度比
Slope horizontal distance = trench depth × slope ratio

示例 / Example: 在C型土壤（坡度1.5:1）中开挖8英尺深的沟槽。
An 8-ft-deep trench in Type C soil (1.5:1 slope).
  每侧放坡水平距离 = 8 英尺 × 1.5 = 12 英尺 / Slope horizontal distance per side = 8 × 1.5 = 12 ft
  总开挖宽度（若底部宽3英尺）= 3 + 2×12 = 27 英尺 / Total width (if bottom 3 ft) = 3 + 2×12 = 27 ft
📋 实际案例 / Case Study:
Two workers were laying pipe in a 7-foot-deep trench in sandy soil (Type C). The spoil pile was placed 1 foot from the edge, no protective system was installed, and the competent person had not inspected after heavy rain the previous night. The wall collapsed, burying both workers. One survived with injuries; one died. Root causes: Type C soil treated as stable, no shoring/sloping/shielding at 7 ft (well over the 5-ft threshold), spoil pile too close, and no post-rain inspection. 两名工人在砂性土壤（C型）的7英尺深沟槽中铺设管道。弃土堆放在距边缘1英尺处，未安装防护系统，且合格人员在前夜大雨后未检查。侧壁坍塌掩埋了两名工人。一人受伤幸存，一人死亡。根本原因：将C型土壤当作稳定土壤、7英尺（远超5英尺阈值）未做支撑/放坡/防护、弃土过近、雨后未检查。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "A trench is 6 ft deep in Type C soil. What protective system is required?"
答题要点 / Key point: ≥5英尺须防护（实岩除外）；若合格人员认定有坍塌风险，<5英尺也须防护。弃土距沟边≥2英尺；梯子伸出沟顶≥3英尺；地表设施距边≥2英尺；深度>4英尺须做大气检测；>25英尺须配备出逃通道（25英尺内设梯子/坡道）。放坡计算：水平距离=深度×坡度比（C型1.5:1 → 8英尺深=每侧12英尺）。
📝 模拟情景题 / Practice Scenario
※ 模拟情景题，根据考点编写，非历年真题 / Practice scenario based on exam topics, not a past real question
"After overnight heavy rain, a competent person must do what before workers enter an 8-ft-deep trench in Type C soil?"
最佳答案 / Best answer: Inspect the trench daily and after each rain/change that could affect stability, and ensure a protective system (sloping 1.5:1, shoring, or trench box) is in place. 解析：大雨改变了土壤条件，班前必须由合格人员重新检查；弃土须≥2英尺外；梯子须伸出≥3英尺。未检查即进入是C型土壤塌方的主要根因。

4.8 物料搬运与储存 · Materials Handling and Storage☆

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1910.178 · 29 CFR 1926 Subpart CC · Brauer Ch.15 · Yates Ch.28 · NIOSH Lifting Equation

动力工业车辆（叉车）— 29 CFR 1910.178 / Powered Industrial Trucks (Forklifts) — 29 CFR 1910.178

  • Training / 培训: Required before operation. Must include formal instruction (lecture/video), practical training (hands-on), and an evaluation. Retraining required every 3 years (at minimum) or after an accident, near-miss, or observation of unsafe operation. 操作前必须进行培训。必须包括正式教学（讲座/视频）、实际操作培训（动手）和评估。至少每3年或在事故、未遂事件或观察到不安全操作后进行复训。
  • Operator evaluation / 操作员评估: Performance evaluation must be conducted at least every 3 years. 至少每3年进行一次操作表现评估。
  • Modifications / 改装: No modifications or additions affecting capacity or safe operation without manufacturer's prior written approval. 未经制造商事先书面批准，不得进行影响载重能力或安全操作的改装或加装。
  • Capacity / 载重: Never exceed the rated load capacity. Load capacity changes with lift height and tilt angle — consult the load chart. 绝不得超过额定载重。载重能力随举升高度和倾斜角度变化——查阅载荷表。
  • Travel / 行驶: Keep load tilted back and carried as low as practical (4-6 inches from ground). Drive in reverse when the load obstructs forward view. No riders unless a seat is provided. Maintain safe following distance (3 truck lengths). 保持负载向后倾斜，并尽可能低地运载（距地面4-6英寸）。负载阻碍前视时倒车行驶。除非提供座位，否则不得载人。保持安全跟车距离（3个车身长度）。
  • Parked forklifts / 驻车: Lower forks fully, neutralize controls, shut off power, set brakes, block wheels if on incline. 完全降下叉齿、控制装置归中位、关闭电源、设置刹车、如在坡道上则挡住车轮。
  • Fuel / 燃料: Refuel/recharge only in designated areas. LP-gas forklifts: exchange tanks only in well-ventilated areas away from ignition sources. 仅在指定区域加油/充电。液化气叉车：仅在远离点火源的通风良好区域更换气瓶。

起重机安全 — 29 CFR 1926 Subpart CC / Crane Safety — 29 CFR 1926 Subpart CC

  • Operator certification / 操作员认证: Crane operators must be certified by an accredited testing organization OR through an employer-audited program. 起重机操作员必须通过认可的测试机构或雇主审核计划获得认证。
  • Signal person / 信号员: Required when the operator cannot see the load throughout the lift. Must be qualified using either: third-party qualified evaluator, or employer's qualified evaluator. 当操作员无法在整个吊运过程中看到负载时需要。必须通过以下方式获得资格：第三方合格评估员或雇主的合格评估员。
  • Assembly/disassembly / 组装/拆卸: Must be supervised by a person meeting both "competent person" and "qualified person" definitions. 必须由同时满足"合格人员"和"资质人员"定义的人员监督。
  • Power line clearance / 电力线安全距离: 10 feet for lines ≤ 50 kV; 10 feet + 0.4 inches for each kV over 50 kV. ≤50 kV电力线10英尺；每超过50 kV 1 kV增加0.4英寸。
  • Annual inspection / 年度检查: All cranes must be inspected by a competent person at least annually (monthly for frequent-use components). 所有起重机必须由合格人员至少每年检查一次（频繁使用部件每月检查）。
  • Rigging / 索具: All rigging hardware (slings, shackles, hooks) must be inspected before each shift. Remove from service any with: cracks, >10% wear, deformation, or missing/damaged safety latches. 所有索具硬件（吊索、卸扣、吊钩）必须在每次换班前检查。出现以下情况立即停用：裂纹、磨损>10%、变形、安全锁扣缺失/损坏。

人工搬运与人因工程 / Manual Lifting and Ergonomics

NIOSH提举方程 / NIOSH Lifting Equation

RWL (推荐重量限值 / Recommended Weight Limit) = LC × HM × VM × DM × AM × FM × CM
LC (载荷常数 / Load Constant) = 51 磅 (23 kg)
LI (提举指数 / Lifting Index) = 载荷重量 / RWL  →  Load Weight / RWL

判定 / Interpretation: LI > 1.0 = 风险增加 / increased risk; LI > 3.0 = 重大风险 / significant risk
  • Key risk factors / 关键风险因素: Heavy loads (weight), horizontal distance from spine (moment arm), vertical location at origin/destination, asymmetric (twisting) lifting, high frequency, long duration, poor coupling. 重负荷（重量）、距脊柱的水平距离（力臂）、起止点的垂直位置、不对称（扭转）提举、高频率、长时间、不良耦合。

无人机在物料搬运与检查中的应用 / Drones in Materials Handling & Inspection

无人机Drones (Unmanned Aircraft Systems / UAS)☆ are increasingly used in safety applications. The CSP11 blueprint (Domain 1, item 6) explicitly mentions drones under materials handling. 无人机(UAS)越来越多地用于安全领域。CSP11蓝图（领域1，第6项）在物料搬运下明确提及无人机。

  • Applications / 应用: Inspections of elevated structures (bridges, towers, roofs), confined space pre-entry assessment, industrial hygiene monitoring, emergency response situational awareness, aerial surveying of excavation/spoil pile stability, inventory management in large warehouses and stockpiles. 高空结构检查（桥梁、塔架、屋顶）、密闭空间进入前评估、工业卫生监测、应急响应态势感知、挖掘/弃土稳定性航空测量、大型仓库和堆场的库存管理。
  • Regulatory / 法规: In the US, FAA Part 107 governs commercial drone operations. Key requirements: Remote Pilot Certificate required, drone weight ≤ 55 lbs, visual line-of-sight (VLOS) maintained, operations below 400 ft AGL, daylight operations (or with anti-collision lighting). 在美国，FAA第107部管辖商用无人机操作。关键要求：需要远程飞行员证书，无人机重量≤55磅，保持目视视线(VLOS)，在离地400英尺(AGL)以下操作，白天操作（或配备防撞灯）。
  • Safety benefits / 安全效益: Eliminates or reduces working at heights, reduces confined space entries, enables rapid post-incident scene documentation, improves efficiency of large-area inspections. 消除或减少高处作业、减少密闭空间进入、实现快速的事故后现场记录、提高大面积检查效率。
  • Key hazards / 主要危害: Rotating propellers (laceration), battery fires (lithium-polymer), loss of control / fly-away, collision with structures or aircraft, privacy concerns. 旋转螺旋桨（割伤）、电池火灾（锂聚合物）、失控/飞离、与结构物或飞行器碰撞、隐私问题。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "A forklift load obstructs the driver's forward view. What should the driver do?"
答题要点 / Key point: 叉车：负载距地4–6英寸、阻碍前视时倒车行驶、跟车3个车身、驻车降叉齿+关电+制动。起重机：<50kV距电力线10英尺；索具每次班前检查，磨损>10%/裂纹即停用；组装拆卸须"胜任人员+资质人员"双条件监督。NIOSH方程：LC=51磅；LI>1风险增加、>3重大风险。无人机：FAA Part 107，≤55磅、VLOS、<400英尺。
