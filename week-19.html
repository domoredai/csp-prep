19.1 培训需求评估 / Training Needs Assessment

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.32 (Procedures, Rules, and Training) · Yates Ch.17 (Training) · ANSI/ASSP Z490.1

Before designing any training program, a thorough 培训需求评估Training Needs Assessment (TNA)☆ must be conducted at three levels to ensure training addresses real gaps rather than being a knee-jerk reaction to incidents. 在设计任何培训项目之前，必须进行全面的培训需求评估（TNA），从三个层面确保培训解决的是真正的差距，而非对事故的应激反应。

层面 / Level 关注点 / Focus 关键问题 / Key Questions
组织分析 / Organizational Analysis The big picture — where training fits into the organization's strategic direction 宏观层面——培训如何契合组织的战略方向 Where is training needed across the organization? What are the strategic goals? What resources are available? What is the organizational climate for training? 整个组织哪里需要培训？战略目标是什么？有哪些可用资源？组织的培训氛围如何？
任务/工作分析 / Task / Job Analysis The work itself — identifying the specific competencies required for safe and effective performance 工作本身——确定安全有效执行所需的具体能力 What tasks require training? What are the competency requirements? What are the performance standards? What hazards are associated with each task? 哪些任务需要培训？能力要求是什么？绩效标准是什么？每项任务关联哪些危害？
人员/个人分析 / Person / Individual Analysis The learner — identifying who specifically needs training and what gaps exist 学习者——确定具体谁需要培训以及存在哪些差距 Who needs training? What are the current competency gaps? How do individual learning styles and backgrounds affect training needs? What is each person's current performance level? 谁需要培训？当前的能力差距是什么？个人学习风格和背景如何影响培训需求？每个人目前的绩效水平如何？
⚠ 关键要点 / Key Point:
Training is not always the answer. A performance gap may stem from unclear procedures, inadequate tools, poor supervision, or lack of motivation — not just lack of knowledge. Use the TNA to determine whether training is the appropriate intervention. 培训不总是答案。绩效差距可能源于程序不清晰、工具不足、监管不力或缺乏动力——而不仅仅是知识匮乏。使用TNA来确定培训是否是适当的干预措施。
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: O-T-P = Organizational, Task, Person（组织、任务、人员）——TNA三层面。 / The three levels of Training Needs Assessment.
📝 模拟情景题 / Practice Scenario
※ 模拟情景题，根据考点编写，非历年真题 / Practice scenario based on exam topics, not a past real question
"An analysis reveals that workers perform a task incorrectly because the written procedure is ambiguous, not because they lack knowledge. What is the appropriate action?"
A TNA distinguishes a true knowledge/skill gap (which training can fix) from other root causes — unclear procedures, missing or inadequate tools, poor supervision, lack of motivation, or environmental barriers. When the procedure itself is ambiguous, training will not solve the performance problem; the procedure must be rewritten. Defaulting to training for every performance problem is a classic exam trap.

TNA要区分真正的知识/技能缺口（培训可解决）与其他根本原因——程序不清、工具缺失或不足、监督不力、缺乏动力或环境障碍。当程序本身含糊时，培训无法解决绩效问题；必须重写程序。把一切绩效问题都归结为培训是典型考试陷阱。

19.2 ANSI/ASSP Z490.1 标准

📖 来源 / Sources: ANSI/ASSP Z490.1 · Brauer Ch.32 (Procedures, Rules, and Training)

ANSI/ASSP Z490.1ANSI/ASSP Z490.1☆ establishes Criteria for Accepted Practices in Safety, Health, and Environmental Training. It is the national consensus standard for EHS training program quality and serves as a benchmark for acceptable training practices. ANSI/ASSP Z490.1确立了安全、健康和环境培训公认实践准则。这是EHS培训项目质量的国家共识标准，作为可接受培训实践的基准。

培训开发 / Training DevelopmentNeeds assessment, learning objectives, content, delivery methods, evaluation需求评估、学习目标、内容、交付方法、评估
培训交付 / Training DeliveryTrainer qualifications, facilities, equipment, class size培训师资质、设施、设备、班级规模
培训评估 / Training EvaluationFormative and summative evaluation, continuous improvement形成性评估和总结性评估、持续改进
文件记录 / DocumentationAttendance, test results, instructor qualifications, course materials出勤记录、考试成绩、讲师资质、课程材料

答题要点 / Key point: ANSI/ASSP Z490.1. Z10 = OHS management systems; Z359.1 = fall protection; Z244.1 = lockout/tagout. 记住Z490.1与Z10/Z359/Z244的区分。

19.3 ADDIE 模型 / The ADDIE Model

📖 来源 / Sources: ANSI/ASSP Z490.1 · Brauer Ch.32 (Procedures, Rules, and Training) · Yates Ch.17 (Training)

The ADDIE模型ADDIE model☆ is the foundational instructional design framework used in EHS training development. Each phase builds on the previous one in a systematic, iterative process. ADDIE模型是EHS培训开发中使用的基础教学设计框架。每个阶段在前一阶段的基础上系统化、迭代地进行。

阶段 / Phase 关键活动 / Key Activities
A — 分析 / Analysis Needs assessment (organizational, task, person); learner analysis (prior knowledge, demographics, motivation, learning preferences); context analysis (where will learning be applied? what constraints exist?) 需求评估（组织、任务、个人）；学习者分析（先验知识、人口特征、动机、学习偏好）；情境分析（所学内容在何处应用？存在哪些限制？）
D — 设计 / Design Write learning objectives using 布鲁姆分类学Bloom's Taxonomy☆; select instructional strategies; plan assessment methods; storyboard content; determine sequence and pacing 使用布鲁姆分类学编写学习目标；选择教学策略；规划评估方法；故事板内容；确定顺序和节奏
D — 开发 / Development Create course materials (slides, handouts, exercises, assessments); pilot test with a sample group; revise based on pilot feedback; finalize facilitator and participant guides 创建课程材料（幻灯片、讲义、练习、评估）；对样本组进行试讲；根据试讲反馈修订；定稿讲师指南和学员指南
I — 实施 / Implementation Schedule sessions; arrange logistics (venue, equipment, materials); deliver training; manage attendance and records; provide learner support 安排课程时间；协调后勤（场地、设备、材料）；交付培训；管理出勤和记录；提供学员支持
E — 评估 / Evaluation Assess effectiveness using 柯氏四级评估Kirkpatrick's Four Levels☆; collect feedback for continuous improvement; measure against original objectives; determine ROI where applicable 使用柯氏四级评估模型评估效果；收集反馈以持续改进；对照原始目标进行衡量；适当时确定ROI
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: ADDIE = Analysis, Design, Development, Implementation, Evaluation（分析、设计、开发、实施、评估）——教学设计五阶段。学习目标在设计阶段用布鲁姆分类学编写。 / ADDIE phases; objectives are written in the Design phase.
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: During which ADDIE phase are learning objectives written using Bloom's Taxonomy? 学习目标在哪个ADDIE阶段编写？
答题要点 / Key point: Design phase. Pilot testing and revision belong to Development. The Design phase produces the instructional blueprint (objectives + assessment + sequencing). 记住目标在设计阶段编写。

19.4 持续改进模型 — 培训实施 / Continuous Improvement Model — Training Implementation

📖 来源 / Sources: ANSI/ASSP Z490.1 · Brauer Ch.32 (Procedures, Rules, and Training) · Yates Ch.17 (Training)

CSP11 Domain 7.3CSP11 Domain 7.3☆ explicitly requires implementing training using a Continuous Improvement model. While ADDIE is the design framework, the Continuous Improvement approach ensures training programs are NOT static — they evolve based on data. CSP11领域7.3明确要求使用持续改进模型实施培训。ADDIE是设计框架，但持续改进方法确保培训项目不是静止的——它们基于数据不断演进。

持续改进循环 / The Continuous Improvement Cycle (per Yates, adapted from Deming's PDCA)

阶段 / Phase培训应用 / Training Application
1. 绩效分析 / Performance AnalysisIdentify performance gaps through data: incident rates, audit findings, observation data, employee feedback, regulatory changes, new equipment/processes. Is training the solution? If not, address the root cause (tools, procedures, supervision, motivation).通过数据识别绩效差距：事故率、审核发现、观察数据、员工反馈、法规变更、新设备/工艺。培训是解决方案吗？如果否，则解决根本原因（工具、程序、监督、动机）。
2. 教学设计 / Instructional DesignDesign training to address identified gaps. Apply ADDIE framework for the specific intervention. Set measurable objectives aligned with the performance gap.设计培训以解决已识别的差距。对特定干预应用ADDIE框架。设定与绩效差距对齐的可衡量目标。
3. 材料获取/开发 / Materials AcquisitionDevelop or procure training materials. Vendor selection for off-the-shelf programs. Custom content development. Technology platform selection (LMS, VR simulators, e-learning authoring tools).开发或采购培训材料。现成课程的供应商选择。定制内容开发。技术平台选择（LMS、VR模拟器、在线学习创作工具）。
4. 交付 / DeliveryExecute the training. Ensure instructor qualification, facility adequacy, equipment functionality. Monitor delivery quality. Collect Level 1 (Reaction) data.执行培训。确保讲师资质、设施充分、设备功能正常。监控交付质量。收集第1级（反应）数据。
5. 评估与反馈 / Evaluation & FeedbackMeasure against objectives using Kirkpatrick Levels 1-4. Analyze results: Did behavior change (Level 3)? Did outcomes improve (Level 4)? Feed findings back to Performance Analysis stage — close the loop. This is what makes it "continuous" — evaluation results drive the NEXT cycle of improvement.使用柯氏1-4级对目标进行衡量。分析结果：行为是否改变（第3级）？结果是否改善（第4级）？将发现反馈到绩效分析阶段——闭环。正是这一点使其成为"持续"——评估结果驱动下一个改进循环。
⚠ CSP考试关键区别 / CSP Exam Key Distinction:
ADDIE describes HOW to design individual training programs. The Continuous Improvement model (per Yates/CSP11) describes HOW to manage the overall training function — continuously scanning for gaps, implementing interventions, measuring results, and feeding data back into the cycle. The CSP exam may ask about the sequence: Performance Analysis → Instructional Design → Materials Acquisition → Delivery → Evaluation (the Yates 5-step process). ADDIE描述了如何设计单个培训项目。持续改进模型（依据Yates/CSP11）描述了如何管理整体培训职能——持续扫描差距、实施干预、衡量结果并将数据反馈到循环中。CSP考试可能会问到以下顺序：绩效分析→教学设计→材料获取→交付→评估（Yates五步流程）。
📝 模拟情景题 / Practice Scenario
※ 模拟情景题，根据考点编写，非历年真题 / Practice scenario based on exam topics, not a past real question
"What is the correct order of the Yates 5-step continuous improvement process for training?"
The continuous improvement model runs: Performance Analysis → Instructional Design → Materials Acquisition → Delivery → Evaluation & Feedback, and the evaluation results loop back to Performance Analysis, closing the cycle. It is the management-side counterpart to ADDIE (which designs a single program); the CI model governs the entire training function on an ongoing basis.

持续改进模型流程为：绩效分析 → 教学设计 → 材料获取 → 交付 → 评估与反馈，评估结果再回到绩效分析，形成闭环。它是ADDIE（设计单个培训项目）在管理侧的对应物；CI模型持续管理整个培训职能。
⚖️ 易混淆对比 / Common Confusions:
概念A概念B关键区别
ADDIE（设计单个培训项目）持续改进模型（管理整体培训职能）ADDIE = how to design individual programs; CI model (Yates/CSP11) = how to manage the whole training function continuously.

19.5 布鲁姆分类学 — 认知领域 / Bloom's Taxonomy — Cognitive Domain

📖 来源 / Sources: Bloom's Taxonomy (Anderson & Krathwohl, 2001) · Yates Ch.17 (Training) · Brauer Ch.32

Learning objectives should be written as observable, measurable behaviors using action verbs aligned to the appropriate cognitive level. 学习目标应写为可观察、可衡量的行为，使用与相应认知层次匹配的动作动词。

层次 / Level 描述 / Description 示例动词 / Sample Verbs
1. 记忆 / RememberRecall facts, terms, basic concepts回忆事实、术语、基本概念List/列出, define/定义, state/陈述, identify/识别, recall/回忆, label/标注
2. 理解 / UnderstandExplain ideas, interpret, summarize解释观点、解读、总结Explain/解释, describe/描述, summarize/总结, paraphrase/转述, interpret/解读
3. 应用 / ApplyUse information in new situations在新情境中使用信息Demonstrate/演示, execute/执行, implement/实施, use/使用, compute/计算, solve/解决
4. 分析 / AnalyzeBreak down information, find relationships分解信息、找出关系Differentiate/区分, compare/比较, contrast/对比, examine/检查, categorize/分类
5. 评价 / EvaluateMake judgments based on criteria依据标准做出判断Assess/评估, critique/评判, justify/论证, prioritize/排序, recommend/推荐, defend/辩护
6. 创造 / CreateProduce new work, design solutions产生新成果、设计解决方案Design/设计, develop/开发, construct/构建, formulate/制定, plan/规划, produce/产出
编写目标 / Writing Objectives:
Each objective should specify the audience (who), the behavior (observable action using a Bloom's verb), the condition (under what circumstances), and the degree (how well / the standard). Example: "Given a hazard scenario (condition), the employee (audience) will identify all applicable controls (behavior) with 100% accuracy (degree)." 每个目标应指定受众（谁）、行为（使用布鲁姆动词的可观察动作）、条件（在什么情况下）和程度（多好/标准）。例如："给定一个危害场景（条件），员工（受众）将以100%准确率（程度）识别所有适用的控制措施（行为）。"
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: RUAAE-C（修订版） = Remember, Understand, Apply, Analyze, Evaluate, Create（记忆、理解、应用、分析、评价、创造）——布鲁姆认知六层次。 / Bloom's revised cognitive domain.
📝 模拟情景题 / Practice Scenario
※ 模拟情景题，根据考点编写，非历年真题 / Practice scenario based on exam topics, not a past real question
"A trainer wants learners to compare and contrast fall protection systems and recommend the most appropriate one. At what Bloom's level is this objective?"
Bloom's cognitive domain runs Remember → Understand → Apply → Analyze → Evaluate → Create. Comparing and contrasting options and then recommending one against criteria is Evaluate (Level 5) — making judgments based on criteria. Create (Level 6, the highest) would require producing something new, such as designing a fall protection system.

布鲁姆认知领域为记忆 → 理解 → 应用 → 分析 → 评价 → 创造。对多个方案进行比较对照并依据标准推荐其中之一属于评价（Level 5）——依据标准作出判断。创造（Level 6，最高层）要求产出新事物，如设计一套防坠系统。

19.6 SMART 培训目标 / SMART Training Objectives

📖 来源 / Sources: Yates Ch.17 (Training) · Brauer Ch.32 (Procedures, Rules, and Training)

All training objectives should be SMARTSMART☆: 所有培训目标都应满足SMART原则：

要素 / Element 含义 / Meaning 示例 / Example
S具体的 / SpecificClearly defined — "Identify all 9 GHS pictograms"明确定义——"识别全部9个GHS象形图"
M可衡量的 / MeasurableQuantifiable — "...with at least 90% accuracy"可量化——"……至少90%的准确率"
A可实现的 / AchievableRealistic given time, resources, learner baseline在给定时间、资源、学习者基础下切实可行
R相关的 / RelevantAligned with job requirements and organizational goals与岗位要求和组织目标一致
T有时限的 / Time-boundSpecific timeframe — "...by end of Q2 onboarding"明确的时间框架——"……在Q2入职培训结束前"
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: SMART = Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound（具体、可衡量、可实现、相关、有时限）。 / SMART objectives.
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: Which objective is SMART — "Understand the importance of PPE" or "The employee will demonstrate correct respirator donning within 2 minutes with no errors"? 哪个目标符合SMART？
答题要点 / Key point: The second — it has an observable, measurable behavior with condition and degree. Vague verbs like "understand," "know," "be aware" are not measurable. 目标必须用可观察动词。

19.7 培训计划组成要素 / Training Plan Components

📖 来源 / Sources: ANSI/ASSP Z490.1 · Brauer Ch.32 (Procedures, Rules, and Training) · Yates Ch.17 (Training)

A comprehensive training plan must address each of these elements: 全面的培训计划必须涵盖以下每个要素：

要素 / Component 描述 / Description
范围 / ScopeWhat topics are covered? What is the breadth and depth? What regulatory requirements must be met?涵盖哪些主题？广度和深度如何？必须满足哪些法规要求？
受众 / AudienceWho is the target? (new hires, experienced workers, supervisors, contractors). What is the class size?目标对象是谁？（新员工、经验丰富的工人、主管、承包商）。班级规模多大？
目标 / ObjectivesSMART learning objectives using Bloom's Taxonomy verbs — what will learners be able to do?使用布鲁姆分类学动词的SMART学习目标——学习者将能做到什么？
内容大纲 / Content OutlineTopics, sequence, time allocation per module, key points, exercises, assessments主题、顺序、每个模块的时间分配、要点、练习、评估
交付方式 / Delivery MethodClassroom, e-learning, OJT, blended, simulation — selected based on content, audience, and resources课堂、在线学习、在岗培训、混合式、模拟——根据内容、受众和资源选择
时间安排 / ScheduleDate(s), time(s), duration per session, frequency (one-time vs. recurring), deadlines日期、时间、每节课时长、频率（一次性vs.周期性的）、截止日期
资源 / ResourcesInstructor(s), facility, equipment, materials, budget, technology needs讲师、设施、设备、材料、预算、技术需求
评估方法 / Evaluation MethodHow will learning be measured? (written test, skills demo, observation). Kirkpatrick level targeted.如何衡量学习效果？（笔试、技能演示、观察）。目标柯氏评估层次。
📝 模拟情景题 / Practice Scenario
※ 模拟情景题，根据考点编写，非历年真题 / Practice scenario based on exam topics, not a past real question
"A training plan includes scope, audience, SMART objectives, delivery method, 3-day schedule, equipment, instructor, and a written test plus skills demo. Which component is MISSING?"
The training plan has eight components: scope, audience, objectives, content outline, delivery method, schedule, resources (including budget), and evaluation method. The scenario lists scope, audience, SMART objectives, delivery method, schedule, equipment, instructor, and evaluation (test plus skills demo), so the missing component is the budget / financial resources — the most frequently overlooked element.

培训计划包含八大要素：范围、受众、目标、内容大纲、交付方式、时间表、资源（含预算）和评估方法。情景中已列出范围、受众、SMART目标、交付方式、时间表、设备、讲师和评估（笔试+技能demo），因此缺失的是预算/财务资源——最常被遗漏的要素。

19.8 形成性评估与总结性评估 / Formative vs. Summative Evaluation

📖 来源 / Sources: ANSI/ASSP Z490.1 · Brauer Ch.32 (Procedures, Rules, and Training) · Yates Ch.17 (Training)

Understanding the two types of evaluation is essential for designing effective assessment within the training program. 理解两种评估类型对在设计培训项目中进行有效评估至关重要。

特征 / Feature 形成性评估 / Formative 总结性评估 / Summative
时机 / TimingDuring development and delivery在开发和交付期间At the end of the program在项目结束时
目的 / PurposeImprove the training as it is developed — pilot tests, learner feedback, mid-course checks改进正在开发的培训——试讲、学员反馈、课程中检查Judge whether learning goals were achieved — final exams, certification tests, skills demos判断学习目标是否达成——期末考试、认证测试、技能演示
示例 / ExamplesPilot test, dry-run, learner reaction surveys, instructor self-assessment试讲、预演、学员反应调查、讲师自评Post-test, certification exam, practical skills evaluation后测、认证考试、实践技能评估
关键区分 / Key Distinction:
Formative evaluation improves the training while it is being built. Summative evaluation judges the final result. Both are required by ANSI/ASSP Z490.1. 形成性评估在培训构建过程中改进培训。总结性评估判断最终结果。ANSI/ASSP Z490.1要求两者都进行。
⚖️ 易混淆对比 / Common Confusions:
概念A概念B关键区别
形成性评估 / Formative总结性评估 / SummativeFormative = DURING development to improve (pilot test, dry-run, learner feedback); Summative = AT END to judge (final exam, certification).
试讲 / Pilot test期末考试 / Final examPilot test = formative; final exam = summative. Z490.1 requires BOTH.
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: Which is a CORRECT example of a formative evaluation activity? 哪项属于形成性评估？
答题要点 / Key point: Pilot testing new materials with a small group before full rollout — improves training during development. 试讲/预演是形成性评估，期末考试是总结性评估。
