考试说明 / Exam Instructions

This final mock exam is designed to be the most challenging of the monthly exam series. Questions are at or above the difficulty level of the real CSP exam, with a heavy emphasis on scenario-based reasoning, cross-domain synthesis, and subtle distinctions that separate high scorers from the rest. 本次最终模拟考试设计为月度考试系列中最具挑战性的。题目难度达到或超过真实CSP考试水平，重点强调基于场景的推理、跨领域综合以及区分高分者的细微区别。

Key features of this exam: 本次考试的关键特点：

  • Scenario-heavy: Most questions present a realistic workplace scenario and ask for the BEST course of action. 场景为主：大多数题目呈现真实工作场所场景，要求选择最佳行动方案。
  • Multi-domain synthesis: Several questions require applying knowledge from multiple CSP domains to arrive at the correct answer. 多领域综合：若干题目需要应用多个CSP领域的知识才能得出正确答案。
  • Calculation variety: At least 4 calculation questions across RPN, noise TWA, TRIR, ventilation, and NIOSH Lifting Equation types. 多种计算题：至少4道计算题，涵盖RPN、噪声TWA、TRIR、通风和NIOSH提举方程等类型。
  • Subtle distinctions: Questions that test nuanced differences — PEL vs. TLV application, NFPA standard numbers, RCRA generator categories, ICS positions vs. functions. 细微区别：测试细微差别的题目——PEL vs. TLV应用、NFPA标准编号、RCRA产生者类别、ICS岗位vs.职能。
  • PPE as distractor: At least 2 questions where PPE appears as an option but is deliberately NOT the correct answer because higher-level controls should be applied first. PPE作为干扰项：至少2道题中PPE作为选项出现但特意不是正确答案，因为应首先应用更高层级的控制措施。
  • Approximate CSP11 blueprint distribution. 近似CSP11蓝图分布。

Take your time — this exam rewards careful analysis, not speed. Select the best answer for each question. 请花时间仔细分析——本次考试看重的是仔细分析而非速度。为每个问题选择最佳答案。
