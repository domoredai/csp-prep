9.1 危害 vs. 风险——关键区别 · Hazard vs. Risk — The Critical Distinction☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.9 (General Principles of Hazard Control) · Brauer Ch.34 (Risk, Risk Assessment) · Yates Ch.24 (Risk Assessment and Management)

A 危害Hazard☆ is any condition, substance, or activity with the potential to cause harm. A hazard exists intrinsically, regardless of whether anyone is exposed. 风险Risk☆ is the combination of the probability that harm will occur and the severity of that harm. Risk requires BOTH a hazard AND exposure. 危害是任何具有造成伤害潜力的状态、物质或活动。危害固有存在，无论是否有人暴露。风险是伤害发生的概率与该伤害严重性的组合。风险需要危害和暴露同时存在。

Concept / 概念Definition / 定义Example / 示例
Hazard / 危害 A source or situation with the potential to cause harm. Intrinsic property. 具有造成伤害潜力的来源或情况。固有属性。 A puddle of oil on the floor; an unguarded machine; benzene in a lab; energized wiring. 地板上的油渍；无防护的机器；实验室中的苯；带电线路。
Risk / 风险 The probability of harm combined with its severity. Situational — depends on exposure. 伤害的概率与其严重性的组合。情境性——取决于暴露。 The likelihood a worker slips on the oil puddle × severity of the fall. Probability of benzene exposure exceeding the PEL × health consequence. 工人踩到油渍滑倒的可能性 × 跌倒的严重性。苯暴露超过PEL的概率 × 健康后果。
Risk (风险) = Probability / Likelihood (可能性) × Severity / Consequence (严重性)

Key principle: You can have a hazard with zero risk if no one is exposed (e.g., a locked vault of toxic gas with no workers present). You can NEVER have risk without a hazard. Eliminating the hazard eliminates the risk; reducing exposure or adding controls reduces the risk but the hazard remains. 关键原则：如果没有人暴露，可以有危害而零风险（如：存放有毒气体的上锁保险库，无工人进入）。没有危害就永远不会有风险。消除危害即消除风险；减少暴露或增加控制会降低风险，但危害仍然存在。

⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
A classic CSP question: "Which reduces risk without eliminating the hazard?" Answer: PPE, administrative controls, and most engineering controls. Only elimination removes the hazard itself. 经典CSP考题："哪项措施降低风险但不消除危害？"答案：PPE、行政控制以及大多数工程控制。只有消除才能真正移除危害本身。
⚖️ 易混淆对比 / Common Confusions:
Hazard 危害Risk 风险关键区别 / Key Difference
潜在伤害来源（能量、物质、条件）——静态存在可能性 × 严重性——与暴露相关、可量化有危害但无人暴露→零风险；无危害则无风险。消除危害=消除风险；降风险≠除危害
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "Can risk exist without a hazard?" / "Which control reduces risk but does NOT eliminate the hazard?"
答题要点 / Key point: 风险=可能性×严重性。无危害则无风险；有危害无暴露→零风险。只有消除(Elimination)移除危害本身；PPE/行政/多数工程控制只降风险不除危害。这是Domain 3概念题的基石。
📝 模拟情景题 / Practice Scenario
※ 模拟情景题，根据考点编写，非历年真题 / Practice scenario based on exam topics, not a past real question
"A maintenance worker is exposed to a rotating shaft that has no guard. Considering the shaft alone, with no reference to exposure or likelihood, it is BEST described as:"
A hazard is a source or situation with the potential to cause harm — the unguarded rotating shaft. Risk is the combination of the likelihood of harm and its severity given exposure, so risk requires the exposure context. The same hazard creates very different levels of risk depending on who is exposed, how often, and how close they get. CSP questions frequently test whether an item is being described as a hazard (inherent) or a risk (contextual).

危害是有潜在致害能力的事物或情形——无防护的旋转轴。风险是在暴露条件下伤害发生的可能性与严重性的组合，因此风险需要暴露情境。同一危害因暴露人员、频率和距离不同而产生截然不同的风险水平。CSP题目常考查某描述属于危害（固有）还是风险（情境相关）。

9.2 风险评估五步流程 · The Risk Assessment 5-Step Process☆

📖 来源 / Sources: ISO 31000 (Risk Management Framework) · Brauer Ch.34 (Risk, Risk Assessment) · Yates Ch.24 (Risk Assessment and Management)

Risk assessment is the cornerstone of the safety profession. The standard process follows five sequential steps, each feeding into the next and all supported by ongoing communication and consultation. This maps closely to the ISO 31000 risk management framework cycle: Identification → Analysis → Evaluation → Treatment → Monitoring & Review. 风险评估是安全专业的基石。标准流程遵循五个连续的步骤，每一步都为下一步提供支持，同时整个流程由持续的沟通和咨询支撑。这与ISO 31000风险管理框架周期高度对应：识别 → 分析 → 评估 → 处理 → 监控与审查。

  1. Identify Hazards / 识别危害 — Recognize anything with the potential to cause harm. Use techniques such as workplace inspections, incident investigations, safety data sheets (SDS), employee interviews, JHA, and review of injury/illness logs (OSHA 300). The key question: "What could go wrong?" 识别任何可能造成危害的事物。使用工作场所检查、事故调查、安全数据表（SDS）、员工访谈、JHA以及伤害/疾病记录（OSHA 300）审查等方法。关键问题："什么可能出问题？"
  2. Analyze Risks / 分析风险 — For each identified hazard, estimate likelihood (probability of occurrence) and severity (consequence if it occurs). Risk = Likelihood x Severity. This can be qualitative (low/medium/high), semi-quantitative (scored 1-5), or quantitative (numerical probability x monetary loss). 对每个已识别的危害，估计可能性（发生概率）和严重性（发生后的后果）。风险 = 可能性 x 严重性。这可以是定性（低/中/高）、半定量（1-5分制）或定量（数值概率 x 货币损失）。
  3. Evaluate / 评估 — Compare the analyzed risk level against pre-established risk acceptance criteria. Determine whether the risk is tolerable, requires additional controls, or is unacceptable. This is where ALARP (As Low As Reasonably Practicable) principles apply. 将分析得出的风险水平与预先设定的风险接受标准进行比较。确定风险是可容忍的、需要额外控制措施，还是不可接受的。在此应用ALARP（合理可行尽量低）原则。
  4. Monitor & Review / 监控与审查 — Risk assessments are living documents. Controls degrade, new hazards emerge, and circumstances change. Schedule periodic reviews and trigger-based reviews (after incidents, changes in process, new equipment, new personnel, etc.). 风险评估是动态文件。控制措施会退化，新危害会出现，情况会变化。安排定期审查和触发式审查（事故后、工艺变更、新设备、新人员等）。
  5. Communicate & Consult / 沟通与咨询 — This step wraps around the entire process. Engage workers, management, and specialists. Effective risk communication ensures everyone understands the hazards, controls, and their responsibilities. 此步骤贯穿整个流程。让工人、管理层和专家都参与进来。有效的风险沟通确保每个人都了解危害、控制措施及其职责。
⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
Remember the order precisely. A frequent question is "after identifying hazards, what comes next?" The answer is Analyze (estimate likelihood × severity), not Evaluate. Evaluation is step 3 (comparing against criteria). 精确记住顺序。常见考题是"识别危害之后下一步是什么？"答案是分析（估计可能性 × 严重性），而不是评估。评估是第3步（与标准比较）。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "After identifying hazards, what is the NEXT step in the risk assessment process?"
答题要点 / Key point: ISO 31000五步：识别→分析(L×S)→评估(对照标准)→控制→监控。高频陷阱：识别后是"分析"（估算可能性×严重性），不是"评估"（评估=对照可接受标准的第3步）。沟通与咨询贯穿全程。
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: "识-析-评-控-监" — 识别(Identify)→分析(Analyze L×S)→评估(Evaluate vs criteria)→控制(Control)→监控(Monitor)。英文"IAECM"。沟通咨询贯穿。识别后问"下一步"=分析。

9.3 风险 = 可能性 × 严重性——定量基础 · Risk = Likelihood × Severity — The Quantitative Basis☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.34 (Risk, Risk Assessment) · Yates Ch.24 (Risk Assessment and Management)

The most common scoring system uses a 1-5 likelihood scale and a 1-5 severity scale, producing risk scores from 1 to 25. The score is used to prioritize actions, not as an absolute measure of danger. 最常见的评分系统使用1-5可能性量表和1-5严重性量表，产生1到25的风险得分。该得分用于确定行动优先级，而非作为危险性的绝对度量。

Risk Score (风险评分) = Likelihood L (1-5) × Severity S (1-5) → Range 1 to 25

Likelihood Scale (1-5) / 可能性量表（1-5）

  • 1 — Rare / 罕见: Less than once in 10 years / 10年内少于一次
  • 2 — Unlikely / 不太可能: Once in 5-10 years / 5-10年一次
  • 3 — Possible / 可能: Once in 1-5 years / 1-5年一次
  • 4 — Likely / 很可能: Once per year / 每年一次
  • 5 — Almost Certain / 几乎确定: Multiple times per year / 每年多次

Severity Scale (1-5) / 严重性量表（1-5）

  • 1 — Negligible / 可忽略: First aid only / 仅需急救
  • 2 — Minor / 轻微: Medical treatment / restricted work / 医疗处理/限制工作
  • 3 — Moderate / 中等: Lost-time injury / 损失工时伤害
  • 4 — Major / 重大: Permanent disability / serious injury / 永久性残疾/严重伤害
  • 5 — Catastrophic / 灾难性: Fatality / multiple fatalities / 死亡/多人死亡

Worked Calculation / 计算示例

A worker routinely climbs an unguarded ladder to a 4-meter platform. Likelihood of a fall per year: rated 4 (Likely). Severity of a fall from 4 m: rated 4 (Major — serious injury). Risk score = 4 × 4 = 16. Under a typical risk matrix, a score of 15+ is in the red / high-priority zone and requires immediate engineered controls (e.g., guardrail, platform handrails, fall arrest). 一名工人经常攀爬无防护的梯子上到4米高的平台。每年跌落的可能性评为4（很可能）。从4米跌落的严重性评为4（重大——重伤）。风险得分 = 4 × 4 = 16。在典型风险矩阵下，15分以上属于红色/高优先级区域，需要立即采取工程控制（如护栏、平台扶手、防坠落系统）。

Annualized Risk (年度风险) = Frequency (频率/年) × Loss per Event (每次事件损失)

For monetary quantification: if a process fails twice per year at an average cost of $50,000 per failure, the expected annual loss = 2 × $50,000 = $100,000/year. This annualized expected loss is the basis for justifying control expenditures (cost-benefit analysis). 货币化量化：如果某工艺每年失效两次，每次平均损失50,000美元，则预期年度损失 = 2 × $50,000 = $100,000/年。这一年度化预期损失是论证控制支出（成本效益分析）的基础。

⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
A scoring matrix must ALWAYS be defined before use — the same score (e.g., 10) from different L×S combinations may carry different priorities. Many organizations weight severity more heavily because a single catastrophic event is considered worse than many minor ones. On the exam, when two risks have equal scores, prefer the one with higher severity. 使用评分矩阵前必须始终先定义标准——来自不同L×S组合的相同得分（如10）可能具有不同优先级。许多组织对严重性赋予更大权重，因为单一的灾难性事件被认为比多次轻微事件更严重。考试中，当两个风险得分相同时，优先考虑严重性更高的那个。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "A hazard has likelihood 4 and severity 3. What is the risk score?"
答题要点 / Key point: 风险分=可能性(1-5)×严重性(1-5)，范围1-25。年化风险=频率×每次损失（2次/年×$50k=$100k/年）。陷阱：同分不同L×S组合优先级不同；两风险同分时选严重性高者。示例：4×4=16→高优先级，需工程控制。

9.4 风险矩阵 · Risk Matrices☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.34 (Risk, Risk Assessment) · Yates Ch.24 (Risk Assessment and Management)

A risk matrix is a visual tool that plots likelihood against severity to assign a risk rating. Common sizes include 3x3, 4x4, and 5x5 grids. Each cell represents a risk level (typically color-coded green/yellow/red). The matrix is a semi-quantitative technique — it uses numbers but the scales are defined subjectively by the organization. 风险矩阵是一种可视化工具，将可能性与严重性对照绘图以分配风险等级。常见尺寸包括3x3、4x4和5x5网格。每个单元格代表一个风险水平（通常用绿色/黄色/红色标记）。矩阵是一种半定量技术——使用数字，但等级标准由组织主观定义。

⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
The matrix must be calibrated to your organization's risk appetite. A 5 likelihood x 1 severity scores 5 (same as 1 x 5), but organizations may weight severity more heavily. The matrix does NOT evaluate the adequacy of existing controls by itself — that is the role of the risk register's residual risk analysis. 矩阵必须根据组织的风险偏好进行校准。可能性5 × 严重性1得分为5（与1 × 5相同），但组织可能对严重性赋予更大权重。矩阵本身不评估现有控制措施的充分性——那是风险登记册中残余风险分析的职责。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "A 5×5 risk matrix cell at Likelihood 4 × Severity 5 scores what?"
答题要点 / Key point: 风险矩阵=半定量工具（数字但等级主观定义）。5×5最常用。L5×S1与L1×S5同分但优先级可不同——组织常更重严重性。矩阵本身不评估现有控制充分性（那是残余风险/风险登记册的事）。红色(E)=最高优先。
📝 模拟情景题 / Practice Scenario
※ 模拟情景题，根据考点编写，非历年真题 / Practice scenario based on exam topics, not a past real question
"Two hazards both score 10 on a 5×5 risk matrix. Risk A is Likelihood = 5, Severity = 2. Risk B is Likelihood = 2, Severity = 5. Which should typically be prioritized?"
When two risks share the same matrix score, examine severity first: a high-severity event with potential for fatality or major injury generally deserves priority over a frequent but minor one, because the potential consequence is far greater. The cumulative-cost argument for Risk A is a secondary financial consideration. Many organizations break ties by ranking severity highest, consistent with the principle that preventing serious harm takes precedence. The matrix score is a screening tool, not a substitute for professional judgment.

两个风险得分相同时应先看严重性：潜在致死或重伤的高严重性事件通常优先于高频但轻微的事件，因为潜在后果大得多。风险A的"累积成本"论只是次要的财务考虑。许多组织以严重性为最高优先级来打破平局，符合"预防严重伤害优先"的原则。矩阵得分是筛查工具，不能替代专业判断。

9.5 控制层级 · Hierarchy of Controls☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.9 (General Principles of Hazard Control) · Brauer Ch.28 (Personal Protective Equipment) · 29 CFR 1910 (OSHA)

The Hierarchy of Controls is the fundamental framework for selecting risk mitigation measures, ranked from most effective to least effective. CSP11 Domain 3 explicitly requires knowledge of this hierarchy for identifying and mitigating EHS hazards. 控制层级是选择风险缓解措施的基本框架，从最有效到最不有效排列。CSP11领域3明确要求掌握此层级，以识别和减轻EHS危害。

Rank / 排名Control / 控制措施Description / 描述Example / 示例
1 (Most effective / 最有效) 消除Elimination☆ Physically remove the hazard. The hazard no longer exists.物理上移除危害。危害不复存在。 Remove a hazardous chemical from the process; automate a manual task从工艺中移除危险化学品；自动化手工作业
2 替代Substitution☆ Replace the hazard with a less hazardous alternative.用危险性较小的替代品替换该危害。 Replace solvent-based paint with water-based paint; use less toxic chemical用水性漆替代溶剂型漆；使用毒性较小的化学品
3 工程控制Engineering Controls☆ Isolate people from the hazard through physical means. Does not rely on worker behavior.通过物理方式将人员与危害隔离。不依赖于工人行为。 Machine guarding, LEV, isolation rooms, noise enclosures, interlocks机器防护装置、局部排风、隔离室、噪声隔音罩、安全联锁
4 行政控制Administrative Controls☆ Change how people work through policies, procedures, training, scheduling. Relies on human compliance.通过政策、程序、培训、排班改变人们的工作方式。依赖于人的遵守。 Job rotation, safety training, warning signs, written procedures, work-rest schedules岗位轮换、安全培训、警示标志、书面程序、工作-休息制度
5 (Least effective / 最不有效) 个人防护装备PPE — Personal Protective Equipment☆ Protect the worker directly. Does NOT remove or reduce the hazard. The hazard still exists; only the worker's exposure is reduced.直接保护工人。不移除或降低危害。危害仍然存在；只有工人的暴露被降低。 Respirators, gloves, hard hats, safety glasses, hearing protection, safety shoes呼吸器、手套、安全帽、安全眼镜、听力防护、安全鞋
⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
CSP questions frequently test: (1) Elimination and substitution are ALWAYS preferred over engineering, administrative, or PPE controls. (2) PPE is the LAST resort — it does not eliminate the hazard. (3) Engineering controls are preferred over administrative controls because they do not rely on human behavior. (4) When a scenario asks for the "best" control, work DOWN the hierarchy from most to least effective. CSP考题频繁测试：(1) 消除和替代总是优先于工程、行政或PPE控制。(2) PPE是最后手段——它不能消除危害。(3) 工程控制优先于行政控制，因为它们不依赖于人的行为。(4) 当场景要求"最佳"控制措施时，从最有效到最不有效向下逐层选择。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "Which is the MOST effective hazard control?"
答题要点 / Key point: 顺序：消除→替代→工程→行政→PPE。消除和替代优先；PPE最后手段（不除危害）；工程优于行政（不依赖行为）。"最佳"控制=自上而下选最高可行层级。PPE只减暴露不除危害。
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: "ESEA-P" — Elimination, Substitution, Engineering, Administrative, PPE。中文"消替工行护"。原则：能消则消、能替则替、工程优先、行政次之、PPE最后。

9.6 风险接受标准与ALARP · Risk Acceptance Criteria & ALARP☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.34 (Risk, Risk Assessment) · Yates Ch.24 (Risk Assessment and Management)

ALARP（合理可行尽量低）ALARP (As Low As Reasonably Practicable)☆ is a principle originating from UK health and safety law (and widely used internationally) stating that risks must be reduced to a level that is as low as reasonably practicable. "Reasonably practicable" involves a cost-benefit balancing: the cost (time, money, effort) of further risk reduction is weighed against the risk reduction achieved. If the cost is grossly disproportionate to the benefit, further reduction is not required. ALARP原则源自英国健康安全法律（并在国际上广泛使用），规定风险必须降低到合理可行的尽量低水平。"合理可行"涉及成本效益平衡：将进一步降低风险的成本（时间、金钱、努力）与所取得的降低效果相比较。如果成本与收益相比明显不成比例，则不需要进一步降低。

The ALARP / Tolerability of Risk Triangle / ALARP / 风险可容忍性三角形

  • Intolerable Region (Red, top) / 不可容忍区域（红色，顶部）: Risk cannot be justified. Activity must stop or mandatory controls must be implemented regardless of cost. 风险不可被合理化。活动必须停止，或无论成本如何都必须实施强制控制措施。
  • Tolerable / ALARP Region (Yellow, middle) / ALARP/可容忍区域（黄色，中部）: Risk is tolerable only if further risk reduction is impracticable or its cost is grossly disproportionate to the improvement gained. Active risk management required. 只有当进一步降低风险不可行或其成本与获得的改进明显不成比例时，风险才是可容忍的。需要积极的风险管理。
  • Broadly Acceptable Region (Green, bottom) / 可广泛接受区域（绿色，底部）: Risk is negligible and no further action is needed beyond maintaining existing controls. 风险可忽略不计，除维持现有控制措施外无需采取进一步行动。

Risk Acceptance Criteria: Pre-defined thresholds that determine what action is required. The boundaries between the three regions must be defined by the organization. Examples of criteria: individual fatality risk of 1×10⁻⁶/yr (broadly acceptable) to 1×10⁻⁴/yr (intolerable) for members of the public. 风险接受标准：预先定义的阈值，决定需要采取什么行动。三个区域之间的界限必须由组织自行定义。标准示例：对公众的个人致命风险1×10⁻⁶/年（可广泛接受）到1×10⁻⁴/年（不可容忍）。

⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
"Reasonably practicable" (英国的"合理可行"原则) is NOT the same as "as low as possible." It explicitly allows a cost-benefit judgment. The test is gross disproportionality — if the sacrifice (cost) is grossly disproportionate to the safety gain, the measure is not required. "合理可行"（reasonably practicable）不等同于"尽可能低"。它明确允许成本效益判断。检验标准是明显不成比例——如果牺牲（成本）与安全收益明显不成比例，则该措施并非必需。

9.7 作业危害分析 / 作业安全分析 · JHA / JSA — Job Hazard Analysis / Job Safety Analysis☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.9 (General Principles of Hazard Control) · Brauer Ch.32 (Procedures, Rules, and Training) · Yates Ch.12 (Personal Protective Equipment)

JHA (Job Hazard Analysis) and JSA (Job Safety Analysis) are interchangeable terms for the same technique: a systematic method to identify hazards associated with a specific job or task and develop controls for each hazard. The process has four classic steps: JHA（作业危害分析）和JSA（作业安全分析）是同一方法的可互换术语：一种系统性方法，用于识别与特定作业或任务相关的危害，并为每个危害制定控制措施。该过程包含四个经典步骤：

  1. Select the Job / 选择作业 — Prioritize jobs with the highest injury/illness rates, potential for severe injury, new or modified jobs, infrequently performed jobs, or jobs with a history of near misses. Do not try to analyze every job at once; use a risk-based prioritization approach. 优先选择伤害/疾病率最高、有严重伤害潜力的、新的或已修改的、不经常执行的、或有过未遂事件的作业。不要试图一次性分析所有作业；采用基于风险的优先级方法。
  2. Break the Job into Steps / 分解作业步骤 — Observe the job being performed and break it into a logical sequence of discrete steps. Each step should describe a single action (e.g., "lift the box from the conveyor," "carry the box to the pallet"). Usually 10-15 steps per job is manageable. 观察作业执行过程，将其分解为离散步骤的逻辑序列。每个步骤应描述单一动作（如"从传送带抬起箱子"，"将箱子搬到托盘"）。通常每个作业10-15个步骤是可控的。
  3. Identify Hazards in Each Step / 识别每步危害 — For each step, ask: What can go wrong? What are the potential hazards? Consider mechanical, electrical, chemical, ergonomic, environmental, and behavioral hazards. Use hazard categories as prompts. 对每个步骤提问：什么可能出问题？潜在危害是什么？考虑机械、电气、化学、人因工程、环境和行为危害。使用危害类别作为提示。
  4. Develop Controls for Each Hazard / 为每个危害制定控制措施 — Apply the hierarchy of controls (elimination, substitution, engineering controls, administrative controls, PPE) to each identified hazard. Document the specific control measure, responsible person, and implementation date. 对每个已识别的危害应用控制层级（消除、替代、工程控制、行政控制、PPE）。记录具体的控制措施、负责人和实施日期。

Example JHA Worksheet / JHA工作表示例

Step / 步骤Hazard / 危害Control / 控制措施
Lift box from conveyor / 从传送带抬箱Back strain; dropped box / 腰背劳损；箱子掉落Team lift; mechanical assist / 团队搬运；机械辅助
Carry box to pallet / 搬箱到托盘Trip hazard; pinch point / 绊倒危害；挤压点Clear path; PPE gloves / 清理通道；PPE手套

Key principle: The JHA/JSA must involve the workers who actually perform the job. They have the most intimate knowledge of the tasks and often know about hazards that supervisors or safety professionals might miss. 关键原则：JHA/JSA必须让实际执行作业的工人参与进来。他们对任务有最深入的了解，通常知道主管或安全专业人员可能忽略的危害。

9.8 风险登记册 · The Risk Register☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.34 (Risk, Risk Assessment) · Yates Ch.24 (Risk Assessment and Management)

A risk register (also called a risk log) is the central document for recording and tracking risks. It is the output of the risk assessment process and the primary tool for managing risk over time. 风险登记册（也称风险日志）是记录和跟踪风险的中心文档。它是风险评估过程的输出，也是随时间管理风险的主要工具。

Essential Components / 必要组成部分

  1. Hazard Description / 危害描述: Clear, specific description — what, where, who, and under what circumstances.对危害的清晰、具体描述——什么、哪里、谁以及在什么情况下。
  2. Inherent (Gross) Risk Rating / 固有（毛）风险评级: The L × S score before controls.应用控制措施之前的L × S得分。
  3. Existing Controls / 现有控制措施: Controls already in place (engineering, administrative, PPE).已经到位的控制措施（工程、行政、PPE）。
  4. Residual (Net) Risk Rating / 残余（净）风险评级: The L × S score after existing controls. This is what the organization actually faces day-to-day.考虑现有控制措施之后的L × S得分。这是组织日常实际面临的风险。
  5. Additional Controls / 额外控制措施: Planned further controls to reduce residual risk to an acceptable level.计划中的进一步控制措施，以将残余风险降低到可接受水平。
  6. Responsible Person / 负责人: Named individual accountable for implementing each control.对实施每项控制措施负责的指定人员。
  7. Target Date / 目标日期: When the control should be implemented.控制措施应实施的日期。
  8. Review Date / 审查日期: When the risk assessment should be revisited.应重新进行风险评估的日期。
  9. Status / 状态: Open, in progress, closed, or on hold.未处理、进行中、已关闭或暂停。
⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
Know the difference between inherent risk (before controls — the "gross" risk) and residual risk (after controls — the "net" risk the organization actually faces). A common exam question asks which value should drive prioritization — the answer is the residual risk, evaluated against acceptance criteria. 区分固有风险（控制前——"毛"风险）和残余风险（控制后——组织实际面临的"净"风险）。常见考题询问哪个值应驱动优先级排序——答案是残余风险，并与接受标准比较。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MEDIUM
典型问法 / Typical question: "Which risk rating should drive prioritization — inherent or residual?"
答题要点 / Key point: 风险登记册核心：固有风险（控制前L×S）vs 残余风险（控制后L×S，组织实际面临的）。驱动优先级的是残余风险，对照接受标准评估。登记册还含现有/额外控制、负责人、目标日期、状态。
⚖️ 易混淆对比 / Common Confusions:
Inherent Risk 固有风险Residual Risk 残余风险关键区别 / Key Difference
控制实施前的L×S（毛风险）现有控制实施后的L×S（净风险）驱动优先级排序的是残余风险；残余风险须与接受标准比较，仍不可接受则需额外控制

9.9 风险评估方法、监控与沟通 · Assessment Methods, Monitoring & Communication☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.34 (Risk, Risk Assessment) · Yates Ch.24 (Risk Assessment and Management) · ISO 31000

Qualitative vs. Semi-Quantitative vs. Quantitative / 定性 vs. 半定量 vs. 定量

Method / 方法Output / 输出Example / 示例
Qualitative / 定性Descriptive categories (Low/Medium/High) / 描述性类别（低/中/高）Simple risk ranking, expert judgment / 简单风险排序、专家判断
Semi-quantitative / 半定量Numerical scores from defined scales / 来自已定义量表的数值得分5×5 risk matrix, RPN-type scoring / 5×5风险矩阵、RPN式评分

When to Review a Risk Assessment / 何时审查风险评估

  • Scheduled periodic review / 定期审查: Typically annually, or per organization policy.通常每年一次，或按组织政策。
  • After an incident or near miss / 事故或未遂事件后: The event reveals actual failures.事件揭示了实际失效。
  • Process or equipment change / 工艺或设备变更: New hazards may be introduced.可能引入新的危害。
  • New personnel or new tasks / 新人员或新任务: Familiarity levels change.熟悉程度发生变化。
  • Control failure or degradation / 控制措施失效或退化: Discovered during inspection or audit.在检查或审计中发现。

Risk Communication / 风险沟通

Effective risk communication is a two-way process — not just issuing warnings. It includes: worker consultation during hazard identification, clear communication of control requirements, timely notification of new hazards, and transparent reporting of risk assessment results to management. Poor risk communication is a recognized root cause in many incidents. 有效的风险沟通是双向过程——不仅仅是发布警告。包括：在危害识别期间咨询工人、清晰传达控制要求、及时通知新危害、以及向管理层透明报告风险评估结果。风险沟通不畅是许多事故中公认的根本原因。

⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
Communication & consultation is the fifth step but it "wraps around" the entire process — it is NOT a once-at-the-end activity. On the exam, pick the option that describes communication as continuous and two-way. 沟通与咨询是第五步，但它"贯穿"整个过程——不是结束时一次性活动。考试时，选择将沟通描述为持续、双向的选项。

答题要点 / Key point: 定性(低/中/高)vs半定量(5×5矩阵/RPN)vs定量(故障树概率/年化损失)。风险评估复审时机：定期(年)、事故/未遂后、工艺/设备变更、新人员/任务、控制失效。沟通与咨询贯穿全程且双向——不是最后一次性活动。
📝 模拟情景题 / Practice Scenario
※ 模拟情景题，根据考点编写，非历年真题 / Practice scenario based on exam topics, not a past real question
"A risk assessment was completed 18 months ago. Which of the following is MOST likely to trigger a review of that assessment?"
A risk assessment is a living document: it must be reviewed whenever there is a change in process, equipment, materials, personnel competence, or applicable regulations, and after incidents or near misses. Change is the classic trigger. Scheduled reviews and new hires are routine and do not by themselves alter the risk picture, and insurance renewal does not change workplace hazards. Monitoring and communication wrap around the whole process so that changes are captured and acted on.

风险评估是动态文件：当工艺、设备、材料、人员能力或适用法规发生变化，以及发生事故或未遂事件时，都必须复审。变更是最典型的触发因素。定期复审和新员工入职属常规，本身不改变风险状况；保险续保不改变工作场所危害。监控与沟通贯穿整个流程，以确保变更被捕捉并处理。
