12.1 应急响应——覆盖的紧急情况类型 · Emergency Types Covered by ERP (CSP11 Domain 4.1)☆

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1910.38 (Emergency Action Plans) · Brauer Ch.29 (Emergencies and Security)

CSP11 Domain 4.1 explicitly requires knowledge of Emergency Response Plans covering these eight emergency types. The ERP must address ALL applicable hazards, not just fire (the all-hazards approach). CSP11领域4.1明确要求掌握涵盖以下八种紧急情况类型的应急响应计划。ERP必须处理所有适用的危害，而不仅仅是火灾（全灾害方法）。

Emergency Type / 紧急情况类型Key Planning Considerations / 关键规划考虑因素
🔥 火灾Fire Most common workplace emergency. EAP + FPP required. Detection, alarm, evacuation, fire suppression, fire department coordination.最常见的工作场所紧急情况。需要EAP+FPP。探测、报警、疏散、灭火、消防部门协调。
🌪️ 恶劣天气Severe Weather Tornadoes, hurricanes, floods, blizzards, extreme heat/cold. Shelter-in-place locations, weather monitoring, early warning systems, post-storm damage assessment.龙卷风、飓风、洪水、暴风雪、极端高温/低温。就地避难地点、天气监测、预警系统、灾后损害评估。
☢️ 核事件Nuclear Incidents Radiological release from nuclear facilities, transportation accidents involving radioactive materials, dirty bombs (RDD). Evacuation zones, KI (potassium iodide) distribution, radiation monitoring, decontamination.核设施放射性泄漏、涉及放射性物质的运输事故、脏弹（RDD）。疏散区域、碘化钾（KI）分发、辐射监测、去污。
🌊 自然灾害Natural Disasters Earthquakes, tsunamis, volcanic eruptions, landslides. Structural integrity assessment, utility shutdown, search and rescue, humanitarian assistance.地震、海啸、火山爆发、山体滑坡。结构完整性评估、公用设施关闭、搜救、人道援助。
💣 恐怖袭击Terrorist Attacks Active shooter, bomb threats, vehicle-borne IEDs, chemical/biological/radiological attacks. Lockdown vs. evacuation decision, law enforcement coordination, suspicious package protocols.活跃枪手、炸弹威胁、车载IED、化学/生物/辐射攻击。封锁vs疏散决策、执法部门协调、可疑包裹处理程序。
🧪 化学品泄漏Chemical Spills Toxic, flammable, or corrosive substance releases. HAZWOPER response levels, containment vs. evacuation, air monitoring, decontamination, regulatory reporting (NRC, SERC, LEPC).有毒、易燃或腐蚀性物质泄漏。HAZWOPER响应级别、围堵vs疏散、空气监测、去污、监管报告（NRC、SERC、LEPC）。
⚡ 公用设施中断Utilities Systems Failure Power outage, natural gas leak, water supply loss, telecommunications failure, HVAC failure. Backup power (generators, UPS), critical system redundancy, manual override procedures.停电、天然气泄漏、供水中断、电信故障、暖通空调故障。备用电源（发电机、UPS）、关键系统冗余、手动越控程序。
💻 网络安全Cyber Security Ransomware attacks, SCADA/ICS compromise, data breach, unauthorized access to safety-critical systems. IT disaster recovery, offline backups, manual control capability, communication alternatives.勒索软件攻击、SCADA/ICS入侵、数据泄露、未经授权访问安全关键系统。IT灾难恢复、离线备份、手动控制能力、替代通信方案。
⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
The CSP exam tests that ERP scope goes beyond fire — the modern ERP uses an all-hazards approach. Know which emergencies require shelter-in-place vs. evacuation (e.g., chemical release may require shelter-in-place with HVAC shutdown; fire always requires evacuation; tornadoes require shelter, hurricanes may require evacuation). CSP考试测试ERP范围超出火灾——现代ERP采用全灾害方法。要了解哪些紧急情况需要就地避难vs疏散（如化学品泄漏可能需要就地避难并关闭暖通空调；火灾始终需要疏散；龙卷风需要避难，飓风可能需要疏散）。

Reportable Releases — Who Must Be Notified / 可报告泄漏——必须通知谁

When a hazardous substance release meets or exceeds its reportable quantity (RQ), the employer must notify the National Response Center (NRC), the State Emergency Response Commission (SERC), and the Local Emergency Planning Committee (LEPC). These community right-to-know reporting duties (under CERCLA/EPCRA) are part of emergency planning — the LEPC is the local body that receives and coordinates chemical-hazard information for the community. 当危险物质泄漏达到或超过其可报告数量（RQ）时，雇主必须通知国家响应中心（NRC）、州应急响应委员会（SERC）和地方应急规划委员会（LEPC）。这些社区知情权报告义务（依据CERCLA/EPCRA）是应急规划的组成部分——LEPC是接收并协调社区化学危害信息的当地机构。

🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "Which emergency typically requires shelter-in-place rather than evacuation?"
答题要点 / Key point: 现代ERP=全灾害(all-hazards)方法，范围超过火灾。避难vs疏散：化学泄漏→就地避难+关HVAC；火灾→始终疏散；龙卷风→避难；飓风→可能疏散。网络/恐怖袭击/供应链中断也纳入ERP。
⚖️ 易混淆对比 / Common Confusions:
Shelter-in-place 就地避难Evacuation 疏散关键区别 / Key Difference

📝 模拟情景题 / Practice Scenario
※ 模拟情景题，根据考点编写，非历年真题 / Practice scenario based on exam topics, not a past real question
"At an expanding incident, one supervisor now has 9 direct reports. According to ICS principles, what should occur?"
ICS uses a modular organization that expands or contracts with incident complexity, and the recommended span of control is 3–7 subordinates per supervisor, with 5 considered ideal. When a supervisor exceeds that range, the structure should be expanded — adding a branch, division, or intermediate level — to preserve effective command and control. Keeping 9 reports, working longer hours, or transferring command do not address the underlying span-of-control problem.

ICS采用随事件复杂度扩展或收缩的模块化组织，推荐的控制幅度为每名主管3–7名下属，理想为5。当超出该范围时应扩展组织结构——增设分支、分区或中间层级——以保持有效的指挥与控制。维持9名下属、延长工时或转移指挥都未解决控制幅度这一根本问题。

12.2 应急行动计划 · Emergency Action Plans — 29 CFR 1910.38☆

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1910.38 (Emergency Action Plans) · Brauer Ch.29 (Emergencies and Security)

OSHA's Emergency Action Plan (EAP) standard applies when another OSHA standard requires an EAP, or when an employer provides portable fire extinguishers for employee use (exempted if the employer has a total evacuation policy with no employee fire extinguisher use). OSHA的应急行动计划（EAP）标准适用于：当另一个OSHA标准要求制定EAP时，或当雇主提供便携式灭火器供员工使用时（如果雇主有完全疏散政策且员工不使用灭火器，则豁免）。

Required EAP Elements (29 CFR 1910.38(c)) / 必需的EAP要素

  1. Alarm Systems / 报警系统: Procedures for reporting fires and other emergencies. Alarm must be distinctive and recognizable. Means of reporting (phone numbers, pull stations, intercom). 报告火灾和其他紧急情况的程序。报警信号必须独特且可识别。报告方式（电话号码、手动报警器、对讲系统）。
  2. Evacuation Procedures / 疏散程序: Types of evacuation (full vs. partial). Designated escape routes and floor plans posted. Maps showing primary and secondary exits. 疏散类型（全面与局部）。指定的逃生路线和张贴的平面图。显示主要和次要出口的地图。
  3. Critical Operations / 关键操作: Procedures for employees who must remain to operate critical plant operations before evacuating (e.g., shutting down hazardous processes). 必须在疏散前留下来操作关键工厂运行的员工程序（如：关闭危险工艺）。
  4. Accounting for Employees / 清点员工: Procedures to account for all employees after evacuation. Designated assembly areas/muster points. Head-count methods. 疏散后清点所有员工的程序。指定的集合区域/集合点。清点人数的方法。
  5. Rescue & Medical Duties / 救援和医疗职责: Identification of employees designated to perform rescue and medical duties, and their training requirements. 指定执行救援和医疗职责的员工，及其培训要求。
  6. Emergency Contacts / 紧急联系人: Names and job titles of persons who can be contacted for further information or explanation of duties. 可联系以获取更多信息或解释职责的人员姓名和职务。

Review & communication / 审查与传达: The EAP must be reviewed whenever the plan or employee responsibilities change, and must be communicated to all employees (orally for employers with 10 or fewer employees). The EAP does NOT need to be written if 10 or fewer employees — but the standard still requires the required elements be communicated. 每当计划或员工职责变更时，必须审查EAP，并且必须传达给所有员工（对于拥有10名或以下员工的雇主，可以口头传达）。如果员工人数不超过10人，EAP可以不书面化——但标准仍要求传达必需要素。

⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
1910.38(c) elements are commonly tested: alarm, evacuation, critical operations, accounting, rescue/medical, and contacts. Distinguish the EAP (response — what to do when an emergency happens) from the FPP (prevention — stop fires from starting). 1910.38(c)要素常被测试：报警、疏散、关键操作、清点、救援/医疗和联系人。区分EAP（响应——紧急情况发生时做什么）与FPP（预防——阻止火灾发生）。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "Which element is required in an Emergency Action Plan under 1910.38(c)?"
答题要点 / Key point: EAP六要素：报警→疏散→关键操作→清点→救援/医疗→联系人。≤10名员工可口头传达、可不书面。计划/职责变更时须审查。EAP=响应（发生时怎么办）；FPP=预防（防止火灾）。
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: "报-疏-关-清-救-联" — Alarm, Evacuation, Critical operations, Accounting, Rescue/medical, Contacts。≤10人可口头。EAP响应、FPP预防。

12.3 防火计划 · Fire Prevention Plans — 29 CFR 1910.39☆

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1910.39 (Fire Prevention Plans) · Yates Ch.10 (Fire Protection and Prevention) · Brauer Ch.16 (Fire Protection and Prevention)

Required when another OSHA standard requires it. The Fire Prevention Plan (FPP) focuses on PREVENTION — keeping fires from starting. It must include: 当另一个OSHA标准要求时需要。防火计划（FPP）侧重于预防——防止火灾发生。它必须包括：

  1. Major Fire Hazards / 主要火灾危害: List of all major workplace fire hazards.所有主要工作场所火灾危害的列表。
  2. Handling & Storage / 处理和储存: Procedures for handling and storing hazardous materials (flammables, combustibles).处理和储存危险材料的程序（易燃物、可燃物）。
  3. Ignition Source Control / 点火源控制: Procedures to control sources of ignition (open flames, sparks, static electricity, hot work).控制点火源的程序（明火、火花、静电、动火作业）。
  4. Fire Protection Equipment / 消防设备: Type of equipment and maintenance procedures (extinguishers, sprinklers, alarms, standpipes).设备类型和维护程序（灭火器、喷淋系统、报警器、消防立管）。
  5. Housekeeping / 内务管理: Procedures to control accumulation of flammable and combustible waste.控制易燃和可燃废物积聚的程序。
  6. Responsible Persons / 负责人: Names/job titles of employees responsible for maintaining equipment, controlling fuel source hazards, and maintaining the plan.负责维护设备、控制燃料源危害和维护计划的员工姓名/职务。
⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
EAP (1910.38) = RESPONSE (evacuation, alarm, accounting). FPP (1910.39) = PREVENTION (hazards list, ignition sources, housekeeping). Both are required only when another standard requires them — they are complementary, not identical. EAP（1910.38）= 响应（疏散、报警、清点）。FPP（1910.39）= 预防（危害清单、点火源、内务管理）。两者仅在另一标准要求时才需要——它们是互补的，而非相同。
⚖️ 易混淆对比 / Common Confusions:
EAP 应急行动计划 (1910.38)FPP 防火计划 (1910.39)关键区别 / Key Difference
响应：报警、疏散路线、清点、救援医疗预防：火灾危害清单、点火源控制、内务管理、消防设备EAP=火灾/紧急发生时怎么办；FPP=如何防止火灾。两者互补、由其他标准触发才需要
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "Which is required in a Fire Prevention Plan (1910.39)?"
答题要点 / Key point: FPP六项：火灾危害清单→处理储存→点火源控制→消防设备→内务管理→负责人。EAP=响应、FPP=预防。灭火器与喷淋属于FPP的设备维护内容。两者由其他标准触发（如1910.38/39条款）才要求。

12.4 事故指挥系统 / 国家事故管理系统 · Incident Command System (ICS) / NIMS☆

📖 来源 / Sources: NIMS / ICS (FEMA) · Brauer Ch.29 (Emergencies and Security) · 29 CFR 1910.120 (HAZWOPER — Incident Command)

The 国家事故管理系统National Incident Management System (NIMS)☆ provides a standardized framework for incident management across all levels of government and the private sector. The Incident Command System (ICS) is the on-scene management component of NIMS. 国家事故管理系统（NIMS）为各级政府及私营部门提供了标准化的事故管理框架。事故指挥系统（ICS）是NIMS的现场管理组成部分。

Five Functional Areas of ICS / ICS的五个功能区域

Area / 区域Role / 角色Typical Responsibilities / 典型职责
指挥Command☆ Overall incident management / 全面事故管理 Sets objectives, establishes strategy, approves resource orders. Led by Incident Commander (IC).设定目标、制定战略、批准资源调配。由事故指挥官（IC）领导。
行动Operations☆ Tactical operations to achieve objectives / 实现目标的战术行动 Directs all tactical resources. Implements the Incident Action Plan (IAP).指挥所有战术资源。执行事故行动计划（IAP）。
规划Planning☆ Situation monitoring and planning / 态势监控与规划 Collects and evaluates information, develops IAP, tracks resources, maintains documentation.收集和评估信息，制定IAP，跟踪资源，维护文档。
后勤Logistics☆ Support and resources / 支持和资源 Provides facilities, transportation, supplies, equipment, food, medical, communications.提供设施、运输、物资、设备、食品、医疗、通信。
财务/行政Finance / Administration☆ Cost tracking and financial management / 成本跟踪与财务管理 Tracks incident costs, timekeeping, procurement, compensation/claims, contracts.跟踪事故成本、考勤、采购、补偿/索赔、合同。

Key ICS Concepts / ICS关键概念

  • Span of Control / 控制幅度: 3-7 direct reports per supervisor, with 5 being the ideal. Exceeding 7 should trigger organizational expansion. 每位主管3-7名直接下属，5人为理想值。超过7人应触发组织扩展。
  • Unified Command / 联合指挥: When multiple agencies/jurisdictions share incident management responsibility. Representatives jointly make decisions through a single Incident Action Plan. 当多个机构/管辖区共同承担事故管理责任时。各机构代表通过单一的事故行动计划共同决策。
  • Incident Commander / 事故指挥官: The single person with overall responsibility. There is always exactly ONE IC (unless Unified Command is in effect). Transfer of command must include a face-to-face briefing. 承担全面责任的唯一人员。始终只有一位IC（除非联合指挥生效）。指挥权转移必须包括面对面交接简报。
  • Modular Organization / 模块化组织: ICS structure expands and contracts based on incident complexity. Only positions needed are filled. ICS结构根据事故复杂性扩展和收缩。只设置需要的岗位。
  • Common Terminology / 通用术语: All organizations use the same terms for facilities, resources, and positions. 所有组织在设施、资源和岗位方面使用相同的术语。
⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
Memorize the five sections: Command, Operations, Planning, Logistics, Finance/Admin. Mnemonic: "COPLF." Planning develops the IAP; Operations executes it; Logistics supports; Finance tracks costs. Span of control 3-7, ideal 5. 记住五个部分：指挥、行动、规划、后勤、财务/行政。助记："COPLF"。规划制定IAP；行动执行它；后勤支持；财务跟踪成本。控制幅度3-7，理想5。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "Which ICS section develops the Incident Action Plan (IAP)?"
答题要点 / Key point: ICS五区：指挥(Command)→行动(Operations)→规划(Planning)→后勤(Logistics)→财务(Finance)。规划制定IAP、行动执行、后勤支持、财务跟踪成本。控制幅度3-7、理想5；IC始终唯一（联合指挥除外）；指挥权转移须面对面简报；模块化组织按需扩展收缩；通用术语。
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: "COPLF" — Command, Operations, Planning, Logistics, Finance。中文"指-行-规-后-财"。控制幅度3-7、理想5。规划造IAP、行动执行、后勤供资源、财务记成本。

12.5 业务连续性 vs. 灾难恢复 · Business Continuity vs. Disaster Recovery☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.29 (Emergencies and Security) · ISO 22301 (Business Continuity)
Aspect / 方面Business Continuity (BCP) / 业务连续性Disaster Recovery (DRP) / 灾难恢复
Focus / 重点 Keeping the BUSINESS running during and after a disruption在中断期间和之后保持业务运行 Restoring IT / technical INFRASTRUCTURE after a disruption在中断后恢复IT/技术基础设施
Scope / 范围 Broader — includes people, processes, facilities, communications, supply chain Narrower — primarily data, systems, networks, and applications
Key Metrics / 关键指标 RTO（恢复时间目标）RTO, RPO（恢复点目标）RPO, MAO (Maximum Acceptable Outage) RTO and RPO for IT systems specifically
Example / 示例 Plan for operating from an alternate site; remote work policies; alternate supply chain Plan to restore servers from backup; failover to secondary data center

RTO and RPO / 恢复时间目标与恢复点目标

RTO (Recovery Time Objective) / 恢复时间目标: The maximum acceptable time to restore operations after a disruption. RPO (Recovery Point Objective) / 恢复点目标: The maximum acceptable amount of data loss, measured in time (e.g., a 4-hour RPO means losing up to 4 hours of data is acceptable). Lower RTO/RPO = higher cost and more resilience. RTO：中断后恢复运营的最大可接受时间。RPO：以时间衡量的最大可接受数据丢失量（如4小时RPO意味着丢失最多4小时的数据是可接受的）。RTO/RPO越低 = 成本越高、韧性越强。

⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
RTO = how quickly must we be BACK UP (time to restore). RPO = how much data can we afford to LOSE (time going back to the last backup). A lower RPO means more frequent backups; a lower RTO means faster recovery capability. RTO = 我们必须多快恢复（恢复所需时间）。RPO = 我们能承受丢失多少数据（回到最后一次备份的时间）。更低的RPO意味着更频繁的备份；更低的RTO意味着更快的恢复能力。
⚖️ 易混淆对比 / Common Confusions:
BCP 业务连续性计划DRP 灾难恢复计划关键区别 / Key Difference
更广：人员、流程、设施、通信、供应链更窄：数据、系统、网络、应用BCP=业务整体如何继续；DRP=IT系统如何恢复。共用RTO/RPO/MAO指标
RTO 恢复时间目标RPO 恢复点目标关键区别 / Key Difference
中断后恢复运营的最大可接受时间最大可接受数据丢失量（时间）RTO=多快恢复；RPO=能丢多少数据。越低成本越高、韧性越强
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MEDIUM
典型问法 / Typical question: "A 4-hour RPO means what?"
答题要点 / Key point: RTO=恢复时间目标（多快恢复）；RPO=恢复点目标（能丢多少数据，回退到最近备份）。RPO低→更频繁备份；RTO低→更快恢复。BCP=业务全局、DRP=IT数据恢复。MAO=最大可接受停机。
📝 模拟情景题 / Practice Scenario
※ 模拟情景题，根据考点编写，非历年真题 / Practice scenario based on exam topics, not a past real question
"A responder approaches a leaking valve and attempts to close it. What is the MINIMUM HAZWOPER training level required?"
HAZWOPER (29 CFR 1910.120) training levels: Awareness (recognize and notify, take no action); Operations (8 hours, defensive actions to contain a release from a safe distance without stopping it); Technician (24 hours, offensive actions — approaching the source to stop the release, such as closing a valve); and On-Scene Incident Commander (24 hours, command responsibilities). Closing a leaking valve is an offensive action at the source, which requires the Technician level. All trained responders require an annual refresher.

HAZWOPER（29 CFR 1910.120）培训级别：认知级（识别并上报，不采取行动）；操作级（8小时，在安全距离外采取防御性措施，不制止泄漏）；技术员级（24小时，进攻性行动——接近源头制止泄漏，如关闭阀门）；现场事故指挥官（24小时，指挥职责）。关闭泄漏阀门是源头的进攻性行动，需要技术员级。所有受训响应人员每年必须复训。

12.6 应急演练 · Emergency Drills & Exercises☆

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1910.38 (Emergency Action Plans) · Brauer Ch.29 (Emergencies and Security)

Exercises test emergency plans, identify gaps, and build team competence. The Homeland Security Exercise and Evaluation Program (HSEEP) provides a framework with discussion-based and operations-based categories. 演练测试应急计划，找出差距，并建立团队能力。国土安全演练与评估计划（HSEEP）提供了框架，包含基于讨论和基于操作的类别。

Exercise Types (Increasing Complexity) / 演练类型（复杂度递增）

Type / 类型Description / 描述Resources / 资源
桌面演练Tabletop Exercise☆ Discussion-based. Participants gather in a room and walk through a scenario verbally. Tests plans, policies, and procedures without deploying resources.基于讨论。参与者聚集在一个房间内，口头演练情景。测试计划、政策和程序，无需部署资源。 Low / 低
功能演练Functional Exercise☆ Simulates a specific function or component of the emergency plan (e.g., EOC operations, communications). Involves simulated inputs and actual response coordination. No physical deployment of equipment.模拟应急计划的特定功能或组成部分（如EOC运作、通信）。涉及模拟输入和实际响应协调。不实际部署设备。 Medium / 中
全面演练Full-Scale Exercise☆ Simulates a real event as closely as possible. Deploys actual personnel, equipment, and resources. Involves multiple agencies, role players acting as victims, and real-time response. Most complex and expensive.尽可能真实地模拟真实事件。部署实际人员、设备和资源。涉及多个机构、扮演受害者的角色演员和实时响应。最复杂、最昂贵。 High / 高

Exercise Cycle / 演练周期

  1. Design & Development / 设计与开发: Define objectives, develop scenario, create exercise plan.定义目标、制定情景、创建演练计划。
  2. Conduct / 执行: Execute the exercise with controllers, evaluators, and participants.由控制员、评估员和参与者执行演练。
  3. Evaluation / 评估: Evaluators document observations against objectives.评估员根据目标记录观察结果。
  4. Improvement Planning (After-Action Report / AAR) / 改进计划: Document findings, develop an Improvement Plan (IP) with corrective actions, responsible parties, and deadlines. Track until completion.记录调查结果，制定改进计划（IP），包括纠正措施、负责人和截止日期。跟踪直至完成。
⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
Tabletop = discussion only. Functional = simulated function, no deployment. Full-scale = real deployment. The After-Action Report (AAR) is a key output — a drill without an AAR and Improvement Plan is incomplete. 桌面 = 仅讨论。功能 = 模拟功能，不部署。全面 = 实际部署。事后报告（AAR）是关键产出——没有AAR和改进计划的演练是不完整的。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MEDIUM
典型问法 / Typical question: "Which exercise deploys actual personnel and equipment?"
答题要点 / Key point: 演练三型：桌面(仅讨论、资源低)→功能(模拟某功能、不部署)→全面(实际部署、多机构、最高成本)。演练周期：设计→执行→评估→改进(AAR)。AAR+改进计划是演练关键产出，无AAR的演练不完整。
⚖️ 易混淆对比 / Common Confusions:
Tabletop 桌面演练Functional 功能演练Full-scale 全面演练
仅口头讨论、不部署模拟特定功能、模拟输入、不实际部署实际部署人员设备、多机构、实时响应
复杂度/成本递增：桌(低)→功能(中)→全面(高)。AAR与改进计划是每一级的收尾关键

12.7 危机沟通规划 · Crisis Communication Planning☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.29 (Emergencies and Security) · Brauer Ch.31 (Human Behavior and Performance)

Effective crisis communication ensures accurate, timely information reaches stakeholders during an emergency. Key principles: 有效的危机沟通确保在紧急情况期间准确、及时的信息传递给利益相关者。关键原则：

  • Designated Spokesperson / 指定发言人: One (or a small number) of trained individuals authorized to speak for the organization. All others refer media inquiries to the spokesperson.一个（或少数几个）受过培训的、被授权代表组织发言的个人。所有其他人员将媒体询问转交给发言人。
  • Message Templates / 消息模板: Pre-drafted holding statements and key messages for likely scenarios.为可能的情景预先起草的暂持声明和关键信息。
  • Audience Identification / 受众识别: Employees, families, media, regulators, community, customers, investors — each may need different information through different channels.员工、家属、媒体、监管机构、社区、客户、投资者——每个群体可能需要通过不同渠道获取不同信息。
  • Golden Hour / 黄金一小时: The first 60 minutes after an incident are critical. Acknowledge the situation quickly, even if full details are not yet known. Say what you know, what you are doing, and when you will provide the next update.事件发生后最初的60分钟至关重要。迅速确认情况，即使细节尚不清楚。说明你了解什么、正在做什么、以及何时提供下一次更新。
  • Internal Communication / 内部沟通: Employees should learn about an incident from the company, not from the news media. Establish rapid internal notification systems.员工应从公司而非新闻媒体那里了解事件。建立快速内部通知系统。
  • Social Media Monitoring / 社交媒体监控: Monitor for rumors, misinformation, and emerging concerns. Respond appropriately.监控社交媒体上的谣言、错误信息和新兴关注点。适当回应。
⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
Never say "no comment" — it is interpreted as admission of guilt. Acknowledge the situation, share what is known, state what is being done, and set a time for the next update. Silence creates a vacuum filled by rumors. 绝不说"无可奉告"——它被解读为认罪。确认情况、分享已知信息、说明正在采取的行动、并设定下次更新的时间。沉默会造成真空，被谣言填补。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MEDIUM
典型问法 / Typical question: "What is the 'golden hour' in crisis communication?"
答题要点 / Key point: 黄金一小时=事件后最初60分钟，须迅速确认情况（即使细节未知）：说明已知、正在做什么、下次更新何时。铁律：绝不说"no comment"（被解读为认罪）；指定单一发言人；员工应先于媒体获知；预置消息模板；监控社交媒体谣言。
📝 模拟情景题 / Practice Scenario
※ 模拟情景题，根据考点编写，非历年真题 / Practice scenario based on exam topics, not a past real question
"Within an hour of an incident, media ask questions before all facts are known. What is the BEST response?"
In the first hour (the "golden hour") of a crisis, the priority is to acknowledge the situation through a designated spokesperson, share confirmed facts, describe the response actions being taken, and commit to a time for the next update. "No comment" is read as an admission of fault, and silence creates a vacuum that rumor fills. Releasing unverified detail such as names or medical conditions may violate privacy obligations and is not required.

危机发生后的第一小时（"黄金一小时"）优先事项是：通过指定发言人确认情况、公布已确认的事实、说明正在采取的响应行动，并承诺下次更新的时间。"无可奉告"会被解读为认错，沉默则形成谣言滋生的真空。公布未经核实的姓名或医疗状况可能违反隐私义务，也非必要。

12.8 危险废物作业和应急响应 · HAZWOPER — 29 CFR 1910.120☆

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1910.120 (HAZWOPER) · Brauer Ch.27 (Hazardous Waste) · Yates Ch.25 (Hazardous Materials Management)

The Hazardous Waste Operations and Emergency Response (HAZWOPER) standard covers three distinct groups: (1) cleanup operations at uncontrolled hazardous waste sites, (2) operations at RCRA TSD (Treatment, Storage, Disposal) facilities, and (3) emergency response to hazardous substance releases. 危险废物作业和应急响应（HAZWOPER）标准涵盖三个不同的群体：(1) 无控制危险废物场所的清理作业，(2) RCRA TSD（处理、储存、处置）设施的作业，以及(3) 对危险物质泄漏的应急响应。

Emergency Response Training Levels / 应急响应培训级别

Level / 级别Role / 角色Training / 培训
第一响应人——认知级First Responder — Awareness☆ Recognize a hazardous release, notify authorities, take no further action识别危险品泄漏，通知当局，不采取进一步行动 Sufficient to demonstrate competency (no fixed hours)
第一响应人——操作级First Responder — Operations☆ Defensive actions: contain release from a safe distance without stopping the release防御性行动：在安全距离控制泄漏而不阻止泄漏 8 hours minimum / 至少8小时
危险物质技术员Hazardous Materials Technician☆ Offensive actions: approach the point of release to plug, patch, or stop it进攻性行动：接近泄漏点进行堵塞、修补或阻止 24 hours minimum / 至少24小时
危险物质专家Hazardous Materials Specialist☆ Advanced Technician — technical support; liaises with government authorities高级技术员——技术支持；与政府当局联络 24 hours minimum (plus specialized competencies)
事故指挥官（现场）Incident Commander (On-Scene)☆ Assumes control of the incident scene; implements ICS; knows each level's risks and capabilities接管事故现场控制权；实施ICS；了解每个级别的风险和能力 24 hours minimum (at least Operations level, plus IC competencies)
⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
Memorize the key hours: Awareness = competency (no hours), Operations = 8h, Technician = 24h, Specialist = 24h+, Incident Commander = 24h. Also: annual refresher training is required for all HAZWOPER-trained personnel. Offensive (stop the release) = Technician; defensive (contain from distance) = Operations. 记住关键时长：认知级 = 能力证明（无固定小时），操作级 = 8小时，技术员 = 24小时，专家 = 24小时以上，事故指挥官 = 24小时。另外：所有HAZWOPER培训人员需要年度复训。进攻性（阻止泄漏）= 技术员；防御性（从远处控制）= 操作级。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "How many hours of training are required for a Hazmat Technician?"
答题要点 / Key point: 培训级别与时长：认知级(仅识别通知、无固定小时)→操作级(防御性、8小时)→技术员(进攻性阻止泄漏、24小时)→专家(24h+)→事故指挥官(24h+)。全部需年度复训。进攻=技术员、防御=操作级。
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: "认0-操8-技24" — Awareness无固定小时 / Operations 8h / Technician 24h（Specialist & IC 24h+）。中文"认知零、操作八、技术二十四"。进攻=技、防御=操；人人年度复训。

12.9 Business Impact Analysis & Emergency Plan Structure业务影响分析与应急计划结构

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.29 (Emergencies and Security) · ISO 22301 (Business Continuity)

业务影响分析 · Business Impact Analysis (BIA)☆

A BIA identifies and quantifies the impacts of disruptions on critical business functions. It determines: which functions are time-critical, maximum tolerable downtime (MTD), recovery priorities, resource requirements, and the financial/operational consequences of each function being unavailable. BIA识别并量化中断对关键业务职能的影响。它确定：哪些职能是时间关键的、最大可容忍停机时间（MTD）、恢复优先级、资源需求，以及每个职能不可用时的财务/运营后果。

  • MTD (Maximum Tolerable Downtime) / 最大可容忍停机时间: The longest a business function can be down before serious harm occurs.业务职能在严重损害发生前可停机的最长时间。
  • RTO ≤ MTD / 恢复时间目标 ≤ 最大可容忍停机时间: The recovery time objective must be set within the maximum tolerable downtime.恢复时间目标必须设定在最大可容忍停机时间之内。
  • RPO / 恢复点目标: Defines backup frequency to limit acceptable data loss.定义备份频率以限制可接受的数据丢失。

Emergency Plan Hierarchy / 应急计划层级

  • Emergency Action Plan (EAP) / 应急行动计划: Immediate response — evacuate, alarm, account for people.即时响应——疏散、报警、清点人员。
  • Emergency Operations Plan / 应急运作计划: Coordinates the response organization (ICS), resources, and recovery for larger incidents.协调响应组织（ICS）、资源和更大事故的恢复。
  • Business Continuity Plan (BCP) / 业务连续性计划: Keeps critical business functions running during a disruption.在中断期间保持关键业务职能运行。
  • Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP) / 灾难恢复计划: Restores IT systems and infrastructure.恢复IT系统和基础设施。
⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
The relationship RTO ≤ MTD is commonly tested. A BIA drives the RTO/RPO values; the ERP then provides the response structure. These are building blocks of emergency management, not interchangeable terms. RTO ≤ MTD 的关系常被测试。BIA驱动RTO/RPO值；ERP随后提供响应结构。这些是应急管理的构建模块，不是可互换的术语。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MEDIUM
典型问法 / Typical question: "What is the relationship between RTO and MTD?"
答题要点 / Key point: RTO ≤ MTD（恢复时间目标须在最大可容忍停机时间内）。MTD=严重损害前可停机最长时间。BIA确定时间关键职能/MTD/恢复优先级，驱动RTO/RPO。计划层级：EAP(即时响应)→EOP(协调ICS)→BCP(业务连续)→DRP(IT恢复)。BIA是BCP的基础。
⚖️ 易混淆对比 / Common Confusions:
BIA 业务影响分析BCP 业务连续性计划DRP 灾难恢复计划
分析：识别关键职能、MTD、恢复优先级计划：中断期间保持业务运行计划：恢复IT系统/基础设施
BIA是"分析先行"，产出RTO/RPO/MTD；BCP/DRP是"计划落地"。RTO≤MTD是核心关系
📝 模拟情景题 / Practice Scenario
※ 模拟情景题，根据考点编写，非历年真题 / Practice scenario based on exam topics, not a past real question
"An organization wants to test its incident command, communications, and decision-making under realistic stress without deploying field personnel and equipment. Which exercise type is MOST appropriate?"
Exercises build in complexity: drills test one operation (for example, a fire evacuation); tabletop exercises are discussion-based and low-stress, useful for validating plans and roles; functional exercises stress command, coordination, and communications in a simulated environment such as an emergency operations center, without deploying field resources; full-scale exercises add actual personnel, equipment, and field operations. Testing command and decision-making under stress without field deployment is the defining purpose of a functional exercise.

演练类型复杂度递增：单项演练测试单一动作（如火灾疏散）；桌面演练为讨论式、低压力，适合验证计划与角色；功能演练在模拟环境（如应急指挥中心）中检验指挥、协调与通信，但不部署现场资源；全面演练则动用真实人员、装备和现场行动。在不部署现场力量的情况下检验压力下的指挥与决策，正是功能演练的核心目的。
