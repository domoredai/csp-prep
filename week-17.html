17.1 1. 噪声——监管标准 · Noise — Regulatory Standards☆

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1910.95 (Noise) · NIOSH Criteria 98-126 · Brauer Ch.23 (Noise and Vibration) · Yates Ch.8 (Noise)

Occupational noise exposure is regulated under OSHA 29 CFR 1910.95 (Occupational Noise Exposure). NIOSH also publishes recommended criteria (Publication No. 98-126, 1998). 职业噪声暴露受OSHA 29 CFR 1910.95（职业噪声暴露）监管。NIOSH也发布了推荐标准（第98-126号出版物，1998年）。

OSHA与NIOSH主要差异 / Key Differences: OSHA vs. NIOSH

参数 / ParameterOSHA (1910.95)NIOSH (1998)
PEL / REL (TWA)90 dBA (8-hr TWA)85 dBA (8-hr TWA)
交换率（倍增率） / Exchange Rate5 dB（能量每5dB翻倍）3 dB（能量每3dB翻倍）
行动水平 / Action Level85 dBA（触发HCP，50%剂量）N/A (REL即为85 dBA)
阈值（低于此被忽略） / Threshold90 dBA（HCP可选80 dBA）80 dBA（频率20-16,000 Hz）
最大峰值（脉冲） / Maximum peak140 dB（峰值声压）140 dBA（A加权，快速响应）
应用 / Application可强制执行的法规 / Enforceable regulation推荐的最佳实践 / Recommended best practice
⚠ 关键CSP考试概念 / Critical CSP Exam Concept:
The 5-dB vs. 3-dB exchange rate is one of the most frequently tested noise concepts. OSHA uses 5 dB (meaning a 5 dB increase halves allowed exposure time). NIOSH uses 3 dB (more protective — a 3 dB increase halves allowed time because sound energy truly doubles every 3 dB). 5-dB与3-dB交换率是最常考的噪声概念之一。OSHA使用5 dB（意味着增加5 dB将允许暴露时间减半）。NIOSH使用3 dB（更具保护性——增加3 dB将允许时间减半，因为声能确实每3 dB翻倍）。

噪声控制的层级——源头工程控制 / Hierarchy of Noise Controls — Engineering at the Source

The most effective noise control is engineering control at the source, applied in this order of preference: (1) eliminate/replace the noisy process or equipment; (2) engineering controls — buy quiet equipment, isolate/vibrate-mount the machine, and enclose the source in an acoustic enclosure or sound barrier, or build a sound-isolating wall between the source and the worker; (3) administrative controls — rotate workers to limit exposure time; (4) PPE (hearing protectors) — the last line of defense. Engineering controls protect all workers without relying on individual behavior, so they are preferred over PPE. 最有效的噪声控制是在源头的工程控制，按优先级排序：(1) 消除/替代噪声工艺或设备；(2) 工程控制——采购低噪设备、对机器做隔振安装、将噪声源封闭在隔声罩/隔声屏障中，或在声源与工人之间设置隔声墙；(3) 管理控制——轮岗以限制暴露时间；(4) PPE（听力防护器）——最后一道防线。工程控制不依赖个人行为即可保护所有工人，因此优于PPE。

17.2 2. 噪声计算 · Noise Calculations☆

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1910.95 (Noise) · Brauer Ch.23 (Noise and Vibration) · Yates Ch.8 (Noise)

允许暴露时间 (T) / Allowable Exposure Time (T)

OSHA公式 / OSHA Formula:
T (hours) = 8 / 2((L - 90) / 5)

NIOSH公式 / NIOSH Formula:
T (hours) = 8 / 2((L - 85) / 3)

Where L = 声级 dBA / sound level in dBA

示例：OSHA下95 dBA的允许时间 / Example: OSHA Allowable Time at 95 dBA

T = 8 / 2((95 - 90) / 5)
T = 8 / 2(5/5) = 8 / 21 = 8 / 2 = 4 hours / 4小时

噪声剂量 (D) / Noise Dose (D)

D (%) = 100 × Σ(Ci / Ti)

Where / 其中:
Ci = 噪声级i的实际暴露时间 / Actual exposure time at noise level i
Ti = 噪声级i下的允许暴露时间 / Allowable exposure time at noise level i

D > 100% (或TWA > 90 dBA OSHA) = 过度暴露 / Overexposure
D = 50% = 行动水平（相当于OSHA下85 dBA TWA）/ Action Level (85 dBA TWA equivalent under OSHA)

多日/多班次剂量的平均 / Averaging Dose Over Multiple Days

平均剂量 = (D₁ + D₂ + … + Dn) / n

When exposures span more than one day (or sampling is split across days), the individual daily doses are averaged arithmetically. Example: D₁ = 40%, D₂ = 60% → average = (40 + 60) / 2 = 50% dose. A daily dose ≥100% (or an average ≥100% across days) is an overexposure. NOTE: the individual day doses are added first if they belong to the SAME 8-hour day; averaging applies only when combining separate days. 当暴露跨越一天以上（或将采样分在多天进行）时，对各天的剂量取算术平均。示例：D₁=40%、D₂=60% → 平均 =（40+60）/2 = 50%剂量。单日剂量≥100%（或跨日平均≥100%）即为过度暴露。注意：若各段属于同一个8小时工作日内，应先将它们相加；只有当合并不同的日期时才取平均。

TWA换算 / TWA Conversion

TWA (dBA) = 16.61 × log10(D / 100) + 90
Converts a noise dose percentage into the equivalent 8-hour TWA in dBA (OSHA 5-dB exchange). 将噪声剂量百分比转换为等效8小时TWA dBA值（OSHA 5-dB交换率）。

合并噪声源 / Combining Noise Sources

两个相等声源 / For two equal sources: 单源声级加 3 dB

多声源（通用公式）/ For multiple sources:
Ltotal = 10 log10(Σ 10(Li / 10))

听力防护——NRR（噪声降低等级） / Hearing Protection — NRR (Noise Reduction Rating)

降噪法 / Derating Method公式 / Formula
OSHA（A加权—7 dB校正）估计暴露 (dBA) = TWA (dBA) — (NRR — 7) / 2
NIOSH — 耳罩 / Earmuffs估计暴露 (dBA) = TWA (dBA) — (NRR — 7) / 2
NIOSH — 泡沫耳塞 / Foam earplugs估计暴露 (dBA) = TWA (dBA) — (NRR — 7) / 4
NIOSH — 双重防护（耳塞+耳罩） / Dual protection使用较高的NRR + 5 dB，然后应用耳罩公式，再额外减3 dB
考试提示 / Exam Tip:
Memorize: 90 dBA for 8 hrs = 100% dose. 85 dBA for 8 hrs = 50% dose (Action Level). 95 dBA for 8 hrs = 200% dose. Every +5 dB under OSHA DOUBLES the dose. 记住：90 dBA 8小时 = 100%剂量。85 dBA 8小时 = 50%剂量（行动水平）。95 dBA 8小时 = 200%剂量。OSHA下每+5 dB剂量翻倍。
📝 模拟情景题 / Practice Scenario
※ 模拟情景题，根据考点编写，非历年真题 / Practice scenario based on exam topics, not a past real question
"A worker is exposed to 95 dBA for 2 hours and 90 dBA for 6 hours. What is the noise dose under the OSHA standard?"
Dose = 100 × Σ(Ci/Ti). At 95 dBA the allowed time is 4 h, so 2/4 = 0.5; at 90 dBA the allowed time is 8 h, so 6/8 = 0.75. Dose = 100 × (0.5 + 0.75) = 125%, which exceeds 100% — the worker is overexposed and the employer must implement feasible engineering/administrative controls and provide hearing protection. Remember the OSHA 5-dB exchange rate: each 5 dBA increase halves the allowed time.

剂量 = 100 × Σ(Ci/Ti)。95 dBA允许4小时，故2/4 = 0.5；90 dBA允许8小时，故6/8 = 0.75。剂量 = 100 × (0.5 + 0.75) = 125%，超过100%——工人过度暴露，雇主必须实施可行的工程/行政控制并提供听力防护。记住OSHA 5 dB交换率：每增加5 dBA，允许时间减半。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: Two identical machines each produce 88 dBA. What is the combined level? 两台各88 dBA的机器同时运行时总声级？
答题要点 / Key point: Two equal sources add 3 dB → 91 dBA. Decibels are logarithmic — never add directly. 两个相等声源加3 dB。记住90 dBA×8h=100%剂量，85 dBA×8h=50%行动水平。

17.3 3. 听力保护计划 · Hearing Conservation Program (HCP)☆

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1910.95 (Hearing Conservation) · Brauer Ch.23 (Noise and Vibration) · Yates Ch.8 (Noise)

Triggered when employee noise exposure equals or exceeds the Action Level of 85 dBA TWA (8-hr). Required elements: 当员工噪声暴露等于或超过TWA（8小时）85 dBA行动水平时触发。必要要素：

  1. Noise Monitoring / 噪声监测 — Area and personal dosimetry. Repeated whenever changes in production, process, or controls increase exposure.区域和个人剂量测量。当生产、工艺或控制措施的变化增加暴露时应重复进行。
  2. Audiometric Testing / 听力测试 — Baseline within 6 months of first exposure (1 year if mobile test van). Annual audiograms thereafter. Standard Threshold Shift (STS) = change of ≥10 dB average at 2000, 3000, and 4000 Hz in either ear (age-adjusted allowed) — triggers retest within 30 days.首次暴露后6个月内建立基线（如有移动检测车则为1年）。之后每年一次。标准阈值偏移（STS） = 2000、3000和4000 Hz的频率上，任意一耳平均变化≥10 dB（允许年龄校正）——触发30天内重测。
  3. Hearing Protection / 听力防护 — Must be provided at no cost at 85 dBA (mandatory at 90 dBA). Must attenuate to <90 dBA TWA (or <85 dBA for those with STS). At least one type of each (earplugs and earmuffs).在85 dBA时必须免费提供（在90 dBA时强制使用）。必须将噪声衰减至<90 dBA TWA（或有STS的<85 dBA）。每种类型至少提供一种（耳塞和耳罩）。
  4. Training Program / 培训计划 — Annual training on effects of noise, purpose of hearing protectors, selection/fit/use/care, audiometric testing purpose and procedures.关于噪声影响、听力保护器的目的、选择/适配/使用/保养、听力测试目的和程序的年度培训。
  5. Recordkeeping / 记录保存 — Noise exposure records (2 years), audiometric test records (duration of employment plus 30 years — per OSHA 1910.1020).噪声暴露记录（2年），听力测试记录（雇佣期加30年——依据OSHA 1910.1020）。

噪声效应 / Noise Effects

  • Noise-Induced Hearing Loss (NIHL) / 噪声性听力损失: Typically bilateral, most severe at 4,000 Hz initially, then spreads to lower frequencies. Permanent and irreversible.通常双侧，最严重于4,000 Hz，随后扩散到较低频率。永久且不可逆。
  • Tinnitus / 耳鸣: Ringing in the ears; can be caused by noise exposure, ototoxic chemicals, or both.耳内鸣响；可由噪声暴露、耳毒性化学品或两者共同引起。
  • Acoustic trauma / 声创伤: Sudden hearing loss from impulse noise (explosions, gunfire) exceeding 140 dB.因脉冲噪声（爆炸、枪击）超过140 dB导致的突发性听力损失。
  • Ototoxic chemicals / 耳毒性化学品: Toluene, styrene, xylene, lead, mercury, CO, HCN can damage hearing — especially when combined with noise exposure.甲苯、苯乙烯、二甲苯、铅、汞、CO、HCN可损害听力——尤其与噪声暴露叠加时。

确认STS后雇主的职责 / Employer Duties After a Confirmed STS

When an annual audiogram confirms a Standard Threshold Shift (29 CFR 1910.95(g)(8)), the employer must: (1) ensure the employee is fitted or refitted with hearing protectors, trained in their use, and required to wear them; (2) refer the employee for an audiological/medical evaluation if a medical pathology of the ear is suspected (and inform the employee); and (3) record the STS in the employee's audiometric test record. Hearing protector use becomes mandatory for an employee with a confirmed STS. 当年度听力图确认标准阈值偏移时（29 CFR 1910.95(g)(8)），雇主必须：(1) 确保为员工配好或重新配好听力保护器、培训其使用方法并要求其佩戴；(2) 若怀疑耳部医学病理，转介员工进行听力学/医学评估（并告知员工）；(3) 将STS记入员工的听力测试记录。对于确认有STS的员工，佩戴听力保护器成为强制要求。

答题要点 / Key point: Trigger = 85 dBA TWA (action level). STS = ≥10 dB average shift at 2,000, 3,000, 4,000 Hz → retest within 30 days. Recordkeeping: noise records 2 yrs; audiograms employment + 30 yrs. 记住STS频率与重测时限。

ALARA原则 · ALARA Principle☆:
All radiation doses should be kept As Low As Reasonably Achievable, taking into account economic and social factors. The three basic protective measures: Time (minimize), Distance (maximize — inverse square law), Shielding (appropriate material). 所有辐射剂量应保持合理可达到的尽量低水平，考虑经济和社会因素。三项基本防护措施：时间（最小化）、距离（最大化——平方反比定律）、屏蔽（使用适当材料）。

氡——α衰变子体与肺癌 / Radon — Alpha-Decay Progeny & Lung Cancer

Radon (Rn-222) is a naturally occurring radioactive noble gas produced by the decay of uranium in soil and rock. Radon itself emits alpha particles, but the main health hazard comes from its short-lived alpha-emitting decay progeny (polonium-218, polonium-214), which attach to airborne dust and are inhaled deep into the lung. There the alpha particles deliver a concentrated dose to lung tissue, making radon the second leading cause of lung cancer after smoking (and the leading cause among never-smokers). Radon risk is greatest in basements and confined spaces where it accumulates, and it is exacerbated by smoking (synergistic). 氡（Rn-222）是土壤和岩石中铀衰变产生的天然放射性惰性气体。氡本身发射α粒子，但主要健康危害来自其短寿命的α衰变子体（钋-218、钋-214），它们附着在空气尘埃上并被吸入肺部深处，在那里α粒子向肺组织释放集中剂量，使氡成为仅次于吸烟的第二大肺癌原因（在从不吸烟者中则是首位）。氡在积聚的地下室和密闭空间风险最高，且吸烟会加剧（协同作用）。

平方反比定律 / Inverse Square Law: I2 = I1 × (D1² / D2²)
Doubling the distance from a point source reduces intensity to 1/4. Halving the distance quadruples the intensity. 距点源距离加倍，强度降至1/4。距离减半，强度增至4倍。
📝 模拟情景题 / Practice Scenario
※ 模拟情景题，根据考点编写，非历年真题 / Practice scenario based on exam topics, not a past real question
"A radiation source produces 400 mrem/hr at 1 meter. What is the intensity at 2 meters (inverse square law)?"
Inverse square law: I2 = I1 × (D1²/D2²) = 400 × (1²/2²) = 400 × 1/4 = 100 mrem/hr. Doubling the distance quarters the intensity. ALARA uses Time, Distance, and Shielding — distance is the cheapest and most effective of the three precisely because of this squared relationship.

平方反比定律：I2 = I1 × (D1²/D2²) = 400 × (1²/2²) = 400 × 1/4 = 100 mrem/hr。距离加倍，强度降至1/4。ALARA三要素为时间、距离、屏蔽——正是由于平方关系，距离是三者中最经济有效的手段。

激光眼危害 / Laser Eye Hazard:
The eye lens focuses visible and near-IR laser light onto the retina, amplifying irradiance by ~100,000×. This is why even low-power lasers can cause retinal burns. Nominal Hazard Zone (NHZ) = area where beam irradiance exceeds the Maximum Permissible Exposure (MPE). 眼睛晶状体将可见和近红外激光聚焦到视网膜上，将辐照度放大约100,000倍。这就是为什么即使是低功率激光也能造成视网膜灼伤。标称危害区（NHZ）= 光束辐照度超过最大允许暴露（MPE）的区域。

17.6 6. 毒理学指标与进入途径 · Toxicological Metrics & Routes of Entry☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.24 (Chemicals) · Yates Ch.5 (Toxicology) · 29 CFR 1910.1020 (Exposure Records)

关键毒理学指标 / Key Toxicological Metrics

  • LD50（半数致死剂量）/ LD50 (Lethal Dose 50%): The single dose of a substance that causes death in 50% of a test animal population, expressed in mg/kg body weight. Lower LD50 = more toxic.在试验动物群体中导致50%死亡的单一剂量，以mg/kg体重表示。LD50越低 = 毒性越强。
  • LC50（半数致死浓度）/ LC50 (Lethal Concentration 50%): The concentration of a substance in air (or water) that causes death in 50% of test animals over a specified exposure period (typically 4 hours for inhalation), expressed as mg/L or ppm.在规定暴露期内（吸入通常为4小时）导致试验动物50%死亡的物质在空气（或水）中的浓度，以mg/L或ppm表示。
  • LD50/LC50分类（近似）/ Toxicity Classification (approximate): 超毒 / Super toxic (LD50 <5 mg/kg), 极毒 / Extremely toxic (5-50 mg/kg), 剧毒 / Very toxic (50-500 mg/kg), 中等毒性 / Moderately toxic (500-5,000 mg/kg), 微毒 / Slightly toxic (5,000-15,000 mg/kg), 基本无毒 / Practically non-toxic (>15,000 mg/kg).

17.7 7. 剂量-效应关系与联合效应 · Dose-Response Relationships & Combined Effects☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.24 (Chemicals) · Yates Ch.5 (Toxicology)

剂量-效应关系 / Dose-Response Relationships

  • Threshold model / 阈值模型: Assumes a dose below which no adverse effect occurs. Applies to most non-carcinogenic effects. Key values: NOEL, NOAEL, LOAEL.假设存在一个剂量，低于该剂量不会发生不良效应。适用于大多数非致癌效应。关键值：NOEL（无观察效应水平）、NOAEL（无观察不良效应水平）、LOAEL（最低观察不良效应水平）。
  • Non-threshold (linear) model / 非阈值（线性）模型: Assumes any dose, no matter how small, carries some risk of adverse effect. Used for carcinogens (conservative regulatory assumption). No "safe" dose — only acceptable risk levels (e.g., 10⁻⁶ excess cancer risk).假设任何剂量，无论多小，都带有一定的不良效应风险。用于致癌物（保守的监管假设）。没有"安全"剂量——只有可接受的风险水平（如：10⁻⁶超额癌症风险）。
术语 / Term全称 / Full Name含义 / Meaning
NOEL无观察效应水平 / No Observed Effect LevelHighest dose with NO effect at all无任何效应的最高剂量
NOAEL无观察不良效应水平 / No Observed Adverse Effect LevelHighest dose with no ADVERSE effect无不良效应的最高剂量
LOAEL最低观察不良效应水平 / Lowest Observed Adverse Effect LevelLowest dose producing an ADVERSE effect产生不良效应的最低剂量

不确定因子 / Uncertainty Factors (Safety Factors)

Regulators derive a safe exposure limit from animal data by dividing the NOAEL (or the LOAEL if no NOAEL exists) by uncertainty factors, each typically a factor of 10 (10×10 = 100): (1) 10× for animal-to-human extrapolation (interspecies); (2) 10× for human individual variability (intraspecies/sensitive subpopulations); often an additional 10× for subchronic-to-chronic extrapolation, and up to 10× more for database deficiencies. Example: an animal NOAEL of 100 mg/kg ÷ 100 = a reference dose of 1 mg/kg for humans. 监管机构通过将NOAEL（若无NOAEL则用LOAEL）除以不确定因子，从动物数据推导安全暴露限值，每个因子通常为10（10×10=100）：(1) 10×用于动物到人的外推（种间）；(2) 10×用于人群个体差异（种内/敏感亚群）；通常再加10×用于亚慢性到慢性外推，数据库不足时最多再乘10×。示例：动物NOAEL为100 mg/kg ÷ 100 = 人类参考剂量1 mg/kg。

联合效应 / Combined Effects

  • Additive / 加和: Combined effect equals sum of individual effects (2 + 3 = 5). Basis for the OSHA mixture formula. Quantified by the mixture formula: Em = C₁/L₁ + C₂/L₂ + … + Cn/Ln. If Em > 1.0, the combined exposure exceeds the limit (overexposure), even if each individual concentration is below its own limit.联合效应等于各单独效应之和（2 + 3 = 5）。OSHA混合物公式的基础。用混合物公式量化：Em = C₁/L₁ + C₂/L₂ + … + Cn/Ln。若Em > 1.0，则联合暴露超过限值（过度暴露），即使各单一浓度均低于其各自的限值。
  • Synergistic / 协同: Combined effect is GREATER than sum of individual effects (2 + 3 = 8). Example: asbestos exposure + smoking → lung cancer risk 50-90 times higher.联合效应大于各单独效应之和（2 + 3 = 8）。示例：石棉暴露+吸烟→肺癌风险高出50-90倍。
  • Antagonistic / 拮抗: Combined effect is LESS than sum of individual effects (2 + 3 = 3). Example: atropine antagonizes organophosphate pesticide toxicity.联合效应小于各单独效应之和（2 + 3 = 3）。示例：阿托品拮抗有机磷农药毒性。
  • Potentiation / 增强: One substance (not toxic to the target organ) increases the effect of another toxic substance.一种物质（对靶器官无毒）增强另一种有毒物质的作用。
Synergism is the most dangerous combined effect / 协同是最危险的联合效应
It explains why mixture calculations can fail to protect workers. Always consider: are workers exposed to substances that interact? OSHA requires consideration of additive effects for substances acting on the same target organ, but synergistic effects can be far more severe. 这解释了为什么混合物计算可能无法保护工人。始终要考虑：工人是否暴露于会产生相互作用的物质？OSHA要求考虑作用于同一靶器官物质的加和效应，但协同效应可能严重得多。

📝 模拟情景题 / Practice Scenario
※ 模拟情景题，根据考点编写，非历年真题 / Practice scenario based on exam topics, not a past real question
"Asbestos workers who also smoke have a lung cancer risk how many times higher than non-smoking asbestos workers?"
Asbestos exposure plus smoking produces a synergistic (multiplicative) interaction — lung cancer risk is roughly 50–90 times that of non-smoking asbestos workers, far greater than the sum of the individual risks. However, smoking does NOT increase the risk of mesothelioma, which is caused by asbestos alone. Dose-response questions test exactly this distinction between additive and synergistic combined effects.

石棉暴露与吸烟存在协同（倍增）相互作用——肺癌风险约为不吸烟石棉工人的50–90倍，远大于单独风险之和。但吸烟不会增加间皮瘤风险，后者仅由石棉引起。剂量-效应题目正是考查加和与协同效应的这一区别。

热应激 / Heat Stress

WBGT (Wet Bulb Globe Temperature) is the index used to assess heat stress. It combines: WB (wet bulb — humidity), GT (globe — radiant heat), DB (dry bulb — air temperature). Indoor/no-sun: WBGT = 0.7×WB + 0.3×GT. Outdoor/sun: WBGT = 0.7×WB + 0.2×GT + 0.1×DB. WBGT（湿球黑球温度）是评估热应激的指标。它结合：WB（湿球——湿度）、GT（黑球——辐射热）、DB（干球——气温）。室内/无阳光：WBGT = 0.7×WB + 0.3×GT。室外/有阳光：WBGT = 0.7×WB + 0.2×GT + 0.1×DB。
