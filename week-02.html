2.1 工艺安全管理概述 · Overview of Process Safety Management (29 CFR 1910.119)☆

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1910.119 · Brauer Ch.24

工艺安全管理Process Safety Management (PSM)☆ is an OSHA standard designed to prevent or minimize the consequences of catastrophic releases of toxic, reactive, flammable, or explosive chemicals. Unlike most OSHA standards that focus on worker injuries, PSM focuses on process-related major incidents — events that can cause multiple fatalities, facility destruction, and community impact. 工艺安全管理（PSM）是OSHA的一项标准，旨在预防或最小化有毒、活性、易燃或易爆化学品灾难性泄漏的后果。与大多数关注工人伤害的OSHA标准不同，PSM关注的是与工艺相关的重大事故——可能导致多人死亡、设施损毁和社区影响的事件。

关键概念 / Key Concept:
PSM is a management system standard, not a specification standard. It tells employers what management systems to have in place, not exactly how to build them. This is analogous to ISO management system standards. PSM是一项管理体系标准，而非规范标准。它告诉雇主需要建立哪些管理体系，而非具体如何建立。这与ISO管理体系标准类似。

Origin and history / 起源与历史: PSM was issued in 1992 in response to catastrophic incidents including the Bhopal disaster (1984, methyl isocyanate release killed thousands), the Phillips 66 explosion (1989, Pasadena TX, 23 deaths), and the Union Carbide/Institute WV incident (1985). The standard codifies the lessons from these events into a comprehensive management system. 起源与历史：PSM于1992年颁布，是对一系列灾难性事故的回应，包括博帕尔惨案（1984年，异氰酸甲酯泄漏致数千人死亡）、Phillips 66爆炸（1989年，德克萨斯州帕萨迪纳，23人死亡）和Union Carbide/西弗吉尼亚州Institute事故（1985年）。该标准将这些事件的教训固化为综合的管理体系。

PSM 适用范围 / PSM Applicability

A facility is covered by PSM if it has a process involving a highly hazardous chemical (HHC) at or above the threshold quantity. Specifically: 如果设施中包含涉及高度危险化学品（HHC）且达到或超过阈限量的工艺，则该设施适用PSM。具体如下：

  • Listed chemicals (Appendix A): Contains any of 137+ listed toxic or reactive chemicals at or above the threshold quantity (TQ) specified in the standard. Examples: ammonia (≥ 10,000 lbs), chlorine (≥ 1,500 lbs), formaldehyde (≥ 1,000 lbs), methyl isocyanate (≥ 250 lbs). 列名化学品（附录A）：含有标准中列出的137种以上有毒或活性化学品中的任何一种，且达到或超过规定的阈限量（TQ）。例如：氨（≥10,000磅）、氯气（≥1,500磅）、甲醛（≥1,000磅）、异氰酸甲酯（≥250磅）。
  • Flammable liquids and gases: Flammable liquids or gases in a quantity of 10,000 pounds (4,535 kg) or more in a single location (with some exceptions for hydrocarbon fuels used solely as a fuel for workplace consumption, and retail facilities). 易燃液体和气体：在单一位置的易燃液体或气体数量达到10,000磅（4,535公斤）或以上（某些例外情况包括：仅作为工作场所燃料使用的碳氢燃料，以及零售设施）。
  • Explosives and blasting agents: Covered under separate OSHA standards (1910.109). 爆炸物和爆破剂：适用单独的OSHA标准（1910.109）。
⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
Remember the 10,000 lb threshold. If a question mentions "a process involving flammable liquids ≥ 10,000 lbs," PSM applies. Also remember that PSM applies at the process level — a facility may have multiple covered processes. And learn the individual TQs for the most-tested chemicals (ammonia 10,000; chlorine 1,500; formaldehyde 1,000; MIC 250 lbs). 记住10,000磅的门槛。如果题目提到"涉及易燃液体≥10,000磅的工艺"，则PSM适用。还要记住PSM适用于工艺层面——一个设施可能有多个适用工艺。并记住最常考化学品的各自阈限量（氨10,000磅；氯气1,500磅；甲醛1,000磅；MIC 250磅）。

PSM 覆盖范围的例外情况 / Exceptions to PSM Coverage

  • Retail facilities 零售设施
  • Oil and gas well drilling or servicing operations 石油和天然气钻井或服务作业
  • Normally unoccupied remote facilities 通常无人值守的偏远设施
  • Hydrocarbon fuels used solely for workplace consumption as fuel (e.g., propane for forklifts), provided such fuels are not part of a process containing another HHC 仅作为工作场所燃料使用的碳氢燃料（如叉车用丙烷），前提是这些燃料不属于含有其他HHC的工艺
  • Flammable liquids stored in atmospheric tanks or transferred that are kept below their normal boiling point without benefit of chilling or refrigeration 在常压储罐中储存或输送的易燃液体，且保持在正常沸点以下，无需冷却或制冷
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "Does the PSM rule apply to this process?" — 判断适用性。
答题要点 / Key point: 两条触发线：①含附录A列名化学品≥TQ；②易燃液体/气体单一位置≥10,000磅。常考TQ：氨10,000 / 氯气1,500 / 甲醛1,000 / 异氰酸甲酯(MIC)250磅。例外（不适用）：零售设施、油气钻井/服务作业、通常无人值守的偏远设施、燃料用途碳氢、常压低温易燃液体。

2.2 PSM的14个要素 · The 14 Elements of PSM

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1910.119 · Brauer Ch.24

OSHA 1910.119 organizes PSM into 14 interconnected elements. All 14 must work together — a weakness in any one element can compromise the entire system. OSHA 1910.119将PSM组织为14个相互关联的要素。所有14个要素必须协同运作——任何一个要素的弱点都可能危及整个系统。

1. Employee Participation / 员工参与 — Written plan for employee involvement in PSM; employees must be represented in PHA teams, incident investigations, and consulted on the development of process hazard analyses. 制定员工参与PSM的书面计划；员工必须参与PHA团队、事故调查，并就工艺危害分析的制定进行咨询。
2. Process Safety Information (PSI) / 工艺安全信息 — Compile written information on: hazards of the highly hazardous chemicals used/produced; technology of the process (block flow diagrams, process chemistry, maximum intended inventory, safe upper/lower limits); equipment in the process (materials of construction, P&IDs, electrical classification, relief system design basis, ventilation system design, safety systems). 编制关于以下内容的书面信息：使用/生产的高度危险化学品的危害；工艺技术（方框流程图、工艺化学、最大预期存量、安全上下限）；工艺中的设备（构造材料、P&ID、电气分类、泄压系统设计依据、通风系统设计、安全系统）。
3. Process Hazard Analysis (PHA) / 工艺危害分析 — Thorough, organized, systematic approach to identify, evaluate, and control hazards of the process. Must be updated/revalidated at least every 5 years. Accepted methods: HAZOP, What-If, Checklist, What-If/Checklist, FMEA, Fault Tree Analysis. 全面、有组织、系统化的方法来识别、评估和控制工艺危害。必须至少每5年更新/重新验证一次。可接受的方法包括：HAZOP、What-If、检查表、What-If/检查表、FMEA、故障树分析。
4. Operating Procedures / 操作规程 — Written instructions for each operating phase: initial startup, normal operations, temporary operations, emergency shutdown, emergency operations, normal shutdown, startup following a turnaround. Must include operating limits, consequences of deviation, and steps to correct deviations. 各操作阶段的书面指导：初始启动、正常运行、临时操作、紧急停车、紧急操作、正常停车、检修后启动。必须包括操作限值、偏差的后果以及纠正偏差的步骤。
5. Training / 培训 — Initial training for all employees involved in a process; refresher training at least every 3 years (or more frequently as determined by the employer); documentation of training required. 所有参与工艺的员工的初始培训；至少每3年一次的复训（或由雇主确定更频繁的培训）；需要培训记录。
6. Contractors / 承包商 — Screen contractors for safety performance; inform contractors of known fire, explosion, or toxic release hazards; contractors must train their employees on the hazards; maintain contractor injury/illness log. 筛选承包商的安全表现；告知承包商已知的火灾、爆炸或有毒泄漏危害；承包商必须对其员工进行危害培训；保存承包商的伤害/疾病记录。
7. Pre-Startup Safety Review (PSSR) / 启动前安全审查 — Required for new facilities AND for modified facilities when the modification is significant enough to require a change in process safety information. Must confirm: construction/equipment meets design specs; safety, operating, maintenance, and emergency procedures are in place; a PHA has been performed and recommendations resolved; training is complete. 新设施以及需要更改工艺安全信息的重大改造设施均需进行。必须确认：施工/设备符合设计规范；安全、操作、维护和应急程序已到位；PHA已完成且建议已解决；培训已完成。
8. Mechanical Integrity / 机械完整性 — Written procedures to maintain ongoing integrity of process equipment including: pressure vessels/storage tanks, piping systems, relief and vent systems/devices, emergency shutdown systems, controls (monitoring devices, sensors, alarms), pumps. Inspections and tests must follow recognized and generally accepted good engineering practices (RAGAGEP). 维护工艺设备持续完整性的书面程序，包括：压力容器/储罐、管道系统、泄压和排放系统/装置、紧急停车系统、控制装置（监控设备、传感器、报警器）、泵。检验和测试必须遵循公认和普遍接受的优良工程实践（RAGAGEP）。
9. Hot Work Permit / 动火作业许可证 — Required for welding, cutting, brazing, grinding, or other spark-producing operations on or near a covered process. Must be issued before work begins and specify: the date, the object to be worked on, duration of permit validity. 在适用工艺上或附近进行焊接、切割、钎焊、打磨或其他产生火花的作业时需要。必须在工作开始前签发，并注明：日期、作业对象、许可证有效期。
10. Management of Change (MOC) / 变更管理 — Written procedures to manage changes to process chemicals, technology, equipment, procedures, and facilities (except "replacements in kind"). Must address: technical basis for change, impact on safety and health, modifications to operating procedures, time period for the change, authorization requirements. 管理工艺化学品、技术、设备、程序和设施变更的书面程序（"同类替换"除外）。必须涉及：变更的技术依据、对安全与健康的影响、对操作规程的修改、变更时间段、授权要求。
11. Incident Investigation / 事故调查 — Investigate each incident that resulted in or could reasonably have resulted in a catastrophic release. Investigation must begin within 48 hours of the incident. Team must include at least one person knowledgeable in the process and a contract employee if a contractor was involved. Report must be retained for 5 years. 调查每起已导致或可能合理导致灾难性泄漏的事故。调查必须在事故发生后48小时内开始。调查团队必须包括至少一名了解该工艺的人员，若涉及承包商则包括合同员工。报告必须保存5年。
12. Emergency Planning and Response / 应急计划与响应 — Develop and implement an emergency action plan per 1910.38(a) for the entire plant; procedures for handling small releases. This element also requires coordination with local emergency responders. 制定并实施全厂应急行动计划（依据1910.38(a)）；处理小型泄漏的程序。该要素还要求与当地应急响应机构协调。
13. Compliance Audits / 合规审核 — At least every 3 years, certify that the employer has evaluated compliance with PSM. Audit team must include at least one person knowledgeable in the process. A report of findings must be developed, and the two most recent audit reports must be retained. 至少每3年认证一次雇主已评估PSM合规性。审核团队必须包括至少一名了解该工艺的人员。必须编制发现报告，并保留最近两份审核报告。
14. Trade Secrets / 商业秘密 — Employers must make all information necessary to comply with PSM available to those responsible for compiling PSI, conducting PHAs, developing operating procedures, incident investigations, emergency planning, and compliance audits — regardless of trade secret status. Those receiving trade secret information may be required to sign confidentiality agreements. 雇主必须将所有PSM合规所需信息提供给负责编制PSI、开展PHA、制定操作规程、事故调查、应急计划和合规审核的人员——无论是否涉及商业秘密。接受商业秘密信息的人员可能需要签署保密协议。
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: "5-3-3-48-5-2" 核心周期数：PHA复验每5年；培训复训每3年；合规审核每3年（保留最近2份报告）；事故调查48小时内启动，报告保存5年。中文："PH五、培训三、审核三、事故四八、报告五年"。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "How often must a PHA be revalidated?" / "Which element requires a permit for welding?"
答题要点 / Key point: PHA每5年复验（方法：HAZOP、What-If、Checklist、FMEA、FTA）；培训3年复训；合规审核3年一次、保留最近2份报告；事故调查48小时内启动、报告留5年。动火=要素9(Hot Work Permit)；启动前审查=PSSR(要素7)。14要素按(a)-(o)子条款对应。

2.3 关键PSM概念详解 · Key PSM Concepts in Detail

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1910.119 · Brauer Ch.24

工艺危害分析 · Process Hazard Analysis (PHA)☆ — 要素 3 / Element 3

The PHA is the analytical core of PSM. It must be performed by a team with expertise in engineering and process operations, and must include at least one employee with experience and knowledge specific to the process being evaluated, and one member knowledgeable in the specific PHA methodology being used. PHA是PSM的分析核心。必须由具有工程和工艺操作专业知识的团队执行，且必须包括至少一名具有被评估工艺特定经验和知识的员工，以及一名了解所使用特定PHA方法的成员。

The PHA must address: PHA必须涉及以下内容：

  • Hazards of the process 工艺的危害
  • Previous incidents with potential for catastrophic consequences 可能造成灾难性后果的以往事故
  • Engineering and administrative controls applicable to the hazards 适用于危害的工程和行政控制
  • Consequences of failure of engineering and administrative controls 工程和行政控制失效的后果
  • Facility siting 设施选址
  • Human factors 人因工程
  • Qualitative evaluation of possible safety and health effects of failure of controls on employees 控制失效对员工可能造成的安全与健康影响的定性评估

变更管理 · Management of Change (MOC)☆ — 要素 10 / Element 10

MOC is widely considered one of the most critical PSM elements because uncontrolled change is a major cause of process incidents. Key points: MOC被广泛认为是最关键的PSM要素之一，因为不受控制的变更是工艺事故的主要原因。关键要点：

  • Applies to changes in process chemicals, technology, equipment, procedures, and facilities 适用于工艺化学品、技术、设备、程序和设施的变更
  • Does NOT apply to "replacements in kind" (replacing something with an identical or functionally equivalent item) 不适用于"同类替换"（用相同或功能等同的物品替换）
  • Must review and authorize the change BEFORE it is implemented 必须在实施之前审查并批准变更
  • Employees affected by the change must be informed and trained before startup 受变更影响的员工必须在启动前得到通知和培训
  • PSI and operating procedures must be updated if the change makes them inaccurate 如果变更使PSI和操作规程不准确，必须予以更新
  • Controlling the change: A change is considered "minor" if it does not affect process safety information or operating procedures; these may be handled by a simplified process. Any change that alters PSI or operating procedures is "major" and requires the full MOC procedure. 变更分级：如果变更不影响工艺安全信息或操作规程，则为"次要"变更，可采用简化流程处理。任何改变PSI或操作规程的变更为"重大"变更，需要完整的MOC程序。
⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
A common exam scenario: "A maintenance supervisor replaces a valve with a different type because the original was not in stock. Does MOC apply?" Answer: YES — this is not a replacement in kind. MOC applies to any change not functionally identical to the original. Also note that changing a MATERIAL OF CONSTRUCTION (e.g., carbon steel gasket to a different material) triggers MOC even if dimensions are identical. 常见考题场景："维修主管因原件缺货，更换了一个不同类型的阀门。MOC是否适用？"答案：是——这不是同类替换。MOC适用于任何功能上与原件不完全相同的变更。还要注意：更换构造材料（如碳钢垫片改为其他材料）即使尺寸完全相同，也触发MOC。
📋 实际案例 / Case Study (MOC failure):
In the 2005 BP Texas City refinery explosion (15 deaths, 180+ injuries), the process startup sequence deviated from the written procedures during turnaround, and there was no effective MOC review for the changed startup procedure. The incident illustrates how failures in operating procedures and MOC — not just engineering — can cause catastrophic releases. 在2005年BP德克萨斯城炼油厂爆炸（15人死亡，180余人受伤）中，检修期间的工艺启动顺序偏离了书面程序，且对变更后的启动程序没有进行有效的MOC审查。该事件说明操作规程和MOC的失效——不仅是工程——也可能造成灾难性泄漏。

启动前安全审查 · Pre-Startup Safety Review (PSSR)☆ — 要素 7 / Element 7

The PSSR applies to new facilities AND modified facilities when the modification requires a change in process safety information. It is the final check before introducing hazardous chemicals to a process. A PSSR is also required before restarting a process that has been shut down after a major incident. PSSR适用于新设施以及需要更改工艺安全信息的改造设施。它是在向工艺引入危险化学品之前的最后检查。在重大事故停产后重新启动工艺前也需要PSSR。

2.4 PHA与HAZOP深入 · PHA & HAZOP in Depth

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1910.119(e) · Brauer Ch.24 · Brauer Ch.36

HAZOP 原理 / HAZOP Methodology

HAZOP (Hazard and Operability Study) is the most rigorous and most frequently used PHA method. It uses guide words applied to process parameters (flow, pressure, temperature, level, composition, reaction) at each node (a section of the P&ID) to identify possible deviations and their causes and consequences. HAZOP（危害与可操作性研究）是最严格、最常用的PHA方法。它在每个节点（P&ID的一段）上，将引导词应用于工艺参数（流量、压力、温度、液位、组成、反应），识别可能的偏差及其原因和后果。

引导词 / Guide Word含义 / Meaning示例偏差 / Example Deviation
NONE / 无No flow, no parameterNo flow in a line (blocked valve)
MORE OF / 过量Higher than designHigher flow, higher pressure, higher temperature
LESS OF / 不足Lower than designLower level, lower pressure, low flow
REVERSE / 反向Opposite directionBackflow through a pump

For each deviation, the team evaluates causes, consequences, existing safeguards, and recommended actions, and assigns a risk rating. Actions must be tracked to closure and documented in the PHA report. 对每个偏差，团队评估原因、后果、现有防护措施和推荐行动，并赋予风险等级。行动项必须跟踪至关闭，并记录在PHA报告中。

LOPA — 保护层分析 / Layer of Protection Analysis

保护层分析LOPA☆ is a semi-quantitative risk analysis that evaluates the independent protection layers (IPLs) between a cause and a consequence. Typical layers, from innermost to outermost: 保护层分析（LOPA）是一种半定量风险分析，评估原因与后果之间的独立保护层（IPL）。典型保护层由内到外：

  1. Inherent safety design / 本质安全设计 — design the hazard out (ISD strategies). 通过设计消除危害（ISD策略）。
  2. Basic process control system (BPCS) / 基础工艺控制系统 — normal control loops and interlocks. 正常控制回路和联锁。
  3. Alarms and operator intervention / 报警与操作员干预. 报警与操作员干预。
  4. Safety instrumented functions (SIF) / 安全仪表功能 — SIS trips that shut down the process. 安全仪表系统（SIS）停车功能。
  5. Physical protection / 物理保护 — relief valves, rupture discs, dikes, blast walls. 泄压阀、爆破片、围堤、防爆墙。
  6. Plant emergency response / 工厂应急响应, and finally community emergency response / 社区应急响应. 工厂应急响应，最后是社区应急响应。

A layer is "independent" only if it is not affected by the initiating event and is separate from other layers. LOPA is used to determine whether the number and reliability of IPLs is adequate — often to size Safety Instrumented Functions. 只有当保护层不受初始事件影响且与其他层相互独立时才是"独立的"。LOPA用于判断保护层的数量和可靠性是否充分——通常用于确定安全仪表功能的规模。

2.5 机械完整性与泄压 · Mechanical Integrity & Pressure Relief

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1910.119(j) · Brauer Ch.24 · Brauer Ch.19

机械完整性 — 要素 8 / Mechanical Integrity — Element 8

Mechanical integrity (MI) ensures that process equipment remains in a fit-for-service condition. It is a systematic preventive maintenance program, not a one-time inspection. 机械完整性（MI）确保工艺设备保持可服役状态。它是系统性的预防性维护计划，而非一次性检查。

  • Covered equipment: Pressure vessels, storage tanks, piping, relief and vent systems, emergency shutdown systems, controls (monitoring devices, sensors, alarms), pumps. 涵盖设备：压力容器、储罐、管道、泄压和排放系统、紧急停车系统、控制装置（监控设备、传感器、报警器）、泵。
  • Requirements: Written procedures, training of MI personnel, inspections and tests per RAGAGEP, documentation of defects and corrections, and quality assurance for new/repaired equipment. 要求：书面程序、MI人员培训、依据RAGAGEP进行的检查和测试、缺陷及纠正记录、新购/维修设备的质量保证。
  • RAGAGEP / 公认优良工程实践: Recognized and Generally Accepted Good Engineering Practices — e.g., ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code, API 510 (pressure vessel inspection), API 570 (piping inspection), API 653 (storage tanks), NBIC. RAGAGEP：公认和普遍接受的优良工程实践——如ASME锅炉压力容器规范、API 510（压力容器检验）、API 570（管道检验）、API 653（储罐）、NBIC。
⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
Mechanical integrity is the element that MOST directly prevents equipment failure. A classic question: "Which PSM element ensures process equipment is inspected and tested to prevent deterioration?" → Mechanical Integrity (Element 8). Distinguish it from MOC (managing changes) and PHA (identifying hazards). 机械完整性是最直接防止设备失效的要素。经典题目："哪个PSM要素确保工艺设备被检查和测试以防止劣化？"→机械完整性（要素8）。要将其与MOC（管理变更）和PHA（识别危害）区分开。

泄压系统 · Pressure Relief Systems

Pressure relief is a critical layer of protection in process safety. Key exam points: 泄压是工艺安全中的关键保护层。考试要点：

  • Relief devices must be sized for worst-case credible scenarios (external fire, runaway reaction, blocked outlet, thermal expansion, utility failure). 泄压装置必须按照最坏可信情景（外部火灾、失控反应、出口堵塞、热膨胀、公用工程失效）确定尺寸。
  • Relief system design basis is required PSI documentation (Element 2). Relief device mechanical integrity falls under Element 8 — must be inspected/tested per RAGAGEP. 泄压系统设计依据是要求的PSI文件（要素2）。泄压装置的机械完整性属于要素8——必须按照RAGAGEP进行检查/测试。
  • Relief discharge must be routed to a safe location (flare, scrubber, safe atmosphere). 泄放物必须排放到安全位置（火炬、洗涤器、安全大气）。
  • Safety relief valve (SRV): A spring-loaded, automatic pressure-relieving device actuated by the static pressure in the vessel. Recloses when pressure returns below the set point. 安全泄压阀（SRV）：由容器内静压促动的弹簧式自动泄压装置。压力回落到设定点以下时重新关闭。
  • Rupture disc: A non-reclosing device that bursts at a set pressure. Faster response than an SRV but must be replaced after activation. Often installed upstream of an SRV to isolate it from process contaminants. 爆破片：在设定压力下爆破的不可复位装置。响应比SRV快，但启动后必须更换。常安装在SRV上游，使其与工艺污染物隔离。

2.6 化学相容性与构造材料 · Chemical Compatibility & Materials of Construction

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1910.119(d) · Brauer Ch.24

化学相容性 · Chemical Compatibility

Chemical compatibility is a fundamental process safety consideration — incompatible materials can cause violent reactions, accelerated corrosion, or catastrophic equipment failure. 化学相容性是工艺安全的基本考量——不相容物质可能导致剧烈反应、加速腐蚀或灾难性设备失效。

  • Materials of construction must be compatible with process chemicals throughout the full range of operating temperatures and pressures. 构造材料必须在整个操作温度和压力范围内与工艺化学品相容。
  • Chemical compatibility data is part of the hazard information required in PSI (Element 2). 化学相容性数据是PSI（要素2）中要求的危害信息的一部分。
  • Inadvertent mixing scenarios must be evaluated during the PHA. MOC review must re-assess compatibility whenever chemicals, materials, or operating conditions change. 意外混合情景必须在PHA期间评估。当化学品、材料或操作条件发生变更时，MOC审查必须重新评估相容性。
  • Even a "replacement in kind" of a gasket, seal, or valve component must consider chemical compatibility — if the substitute material differs chemically, it triggers MOC. 即使是垫片、密封件或阀门组件的"同类替换"，也必须考虑化学相容性——如果替代材料的化学成分不同，则触发MOC。

危险反应性 / Hazardous Reactivity

Reactive chemicals can release energy violently when exposed to heat, shock, contamination, or mixing. The CSP exam tests common incompatibility pairs: 活性化学品在受热、冲击、污染或混合时可能剧烈释放能量。CSP考试考查常见的相容性禁忌配对：

构造材料 · Materials of Construction

Materials of construction data is explicitly required as PSI under Element 2. It is closely linked to both chemical compatibility (above) and mechanical integrity (Element 8). 构造材料数据是要素2明确要求的PSI。它与化学相容性（上述）和机械完整性（要素8）密切相关。

  • Materials must be specified considering: corrosion resistance, temperature limits, pressure ratings, and compatibility with all process fluids (including cleaning and utility fluids). 材料规格必须考虑：耐腐蚀性、温度限制、压力等级以及与所有工艺流体（包括清洗和公用工程流体）的相容性。
  • When a material of construction is changed in kind (e.g., carbon steel to stainless steel, or a different gasket material), MOC is triggered — even if the physical dimensions are identical. 当构造材料发生同类变更时（如碳钢改为不锈钢，或不同的垫片材料），触发MOC——即使物理尺寸完全相同。
  • Common metals / 常见金属: Carbon steel (strong, cheap, corrodes), stainless steel (corrosion-resistant, costlier), aluminum, copper, titanium (exotic, high cost). Non-metallics: PTFE, PVC, fiberglass-reinforced plastic (FRP). 常见金属：碳钢（强度高、便宜、易腐蚀）、不锈钢（耐腐蚀、较贵）、铝、铜、钛（稀有、高成本）。非金属：PTFE、PVC、玻璃钢（FRP）。

答题要点 / Key point: 相容性数据属于PSI（要素2）的危害信息部分；意外混合情景须在PHA中评估。陷阱：改变构造材料（如碳钢→不锈钢、换垫片材料）即使尺寸相同也触发MOC。选材须考虑耐腐蚀、温度、压力、与全部工艺流体相容。

📋 实际案例 / Case Study:

工艺安全信息的内容 / Contents of Process Safety Information

PSI (Element 2) is the foundation that all other PSM elements depend on. It has three major blocks: PSI（要素2）是所有其他PSM要素依赖的基础。它包含三大块：

  1. Information on the hazards of the chemicals: toxicity, permissible exposure limits, physical data (boiling point, flash point), reactivity data, corrosivity, thermal/chemical stability, hazardous effects of inadvertent mixing. 化学品危害信息：毒性、允许暴露限值、物理数据（沸点、闪点）、反应性数据、腐蚀性、热/化学稳定性、意外混合的危害影响。
  2. Information on the technology of the process: block flow diagrams, process chemistry, maximum intended inventory, safe upper and lower limits for temperatures, pressures, flows, and compositions. 工艺技术信息：方框流程图、工艺化学、最大预期存量、温度、压力、流量和组成的安全上下限。
  3. Information on the equipment in the process: materials of construction, P&IDs, electrical classification, relief system design basis, ventilation system design, design codes and standards employed, safety systems (e.g., interlocks, detection or suppression systems). 工艺设备信息：构造材料、P&ID、电气分类、泄压系统设计依据、通风系统设计、采用的设计规范和标准、安全系统（如联锁、检测或抑制系统）。
