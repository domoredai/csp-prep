📝 月度考试4：环境、健康与培训 / Monthly Exam 4: Environment, Health & Training

领域5、6和7 / Domains 5, 6 & 7 — 综合评估 / Comprehensive Assessment (占考试26%) · 24 题 / 24 Questions
领域5：环境管理 (6%) / Domain 5: Environmental Management 领域6：职业健康 (10%) / Domain 6: Occupational Health 领域7：培训/教育 (10%) / Domain 7: Training / Education

考试说明 / Exam Instructions

This monthly exam covers Domains 5 through 7, which together comprise approximately 26% of the CSP11 exam blueprint. The 24 questions are distributed as follows: 本月度考试涵盖领域5至7，共占CSP11考试蓝图约26%。24道题分布如下：

  • Domain 5 — Environmental Management (~7 questions): GHS pictograms, SDS 16-section knowledge, RCRA generator categories (VSQG/SQG/LQG), CERCLA RQ and NPL, Clean Air Act / Clean Water Act, EPCRA Tier II / TRI reporting, and SPCC requirements. 领域5——环境管理（约7题）：GHS象形图、SDS 16节知识、RCRA产生者类别（VSQG/SQG/LQG）、CERCLA RQ和NPL、《清洁空气法》/《清洁水法》、EPCRA Tier II / TRI报告以及SPCC要求。
  • Domain 6 — Occupational Health (~10 questions): AREC framework, TWA calculations, personal vs. area sampling, noise (OSHA vs. NIOSH, dose/TWA/combining noise calculations), PEL vs. TLV vs. REL, toxicology (LD50, routes of entry, carcinogens/mutagens/teratogens), NIOSH Lifting Equation (RWL/LI), ventilation (Q = VA), and epidemiology basics (cohort vs. case-control, RR vs. OR). 领域6——职业健康（约10题）：AREC框架、TWA计算、个人采样vs.区域采样、噪声（OSHA vs. NIOSH、剂量/TWA/组合噪声计算）、PEL vs. TLV vs. REL、毒理学（LD50、进入途径、致癌物/致突变物/致畸物）、NIOSH提举方程（RWL/LI）、通风（Q = VA）和流行病学基础（队列研究vs.病例对照、RR vs. OR）。
  • Domain 7 — Training (~5 questions): ADDIE model phases, Kirkpatrick evaluation levels, Knowles's andragogy principles, OSHA training hour requirements (HAZWOPER 40-hr), and VARK learning styles. 领域7——培训（约5题）：ADDIE模型阶段、Kirkpatrick评估层级、Knowles成人教育原则、OSHA培训小时要求（HAZWOPER 40小时）和VARK学习风格。

Select the best answer for each question. Your score and detailed feedback will be shown upon completion. Retake the exam as many times as needed. 为每个问题选择最佳答案。完成后将显示你的分数和详细反馈。可以无限次重考。
