6.1 领先指标与滞后指标 · Leading vs. Lagging Indicators☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.35 · Brauer Ch.37 · Yates Ch.2

Safety performance measurement uses two complementary types of indicators. The CSP exam expects you to distinguish between them and know when to use each. 安全绩效测量使用两类互补的指标。CSP考试要求能够区分它们并知道何时使用每一种。

特性 / Feature领先指标 / Leading Indicators滞后指标 / Lagging Indicators
性质 / Nature Preventive, proactive, predictive预防性、主动性、预测性 Reactive, after-the-fact, retrospective反应性、事后、回顾性
测量什么 / What It Measures Inputs and activities that drive future safety performance驱动未来安全绩效的输入和活动 Outcomes — the results of past safety performance结果——过去安全绩效的产出
示例 / Examples Number of safety inspections completed, training hours delivered, near-miss reports submitted, safety meeting attendance, hazard assessments conducted, corrective actions closed on time已完成安全检查的数量、已交付培训时数、提交的未遂事件报告、安全会议出席率、已进行的危害评估、按时关闭的纠正行动 TRIR, DART, LTIFR, fatality count, workers' compensation costs, lost workdaysTRIR、DART、LTIFR、死亡人数、工伤保险费用、损失工作日
时间取向 / Time Orientation Forward-looking前瞻性 Backward-looking回顾性
优点 / Strengths Allows intervention before incidents occur; drives proactive behavior允许在事故发生前进行干预；推动主动行为 Objective, verifiable, allows trending and benchmarking客观、可验证、允许趋势分析和基准比较
局限 / Limitations May not directly correlate with outcomes; can be "gamed"可能与结果不直接相关；可能被"钻空子" Tells you what happened but not why; low-frequency events can create misleading trends告诉你发生了什么，但不告诉你原因；低频事件可能产生误导性趋势

滞后指标的问题 / The Problem with Lagging Indicators

  • They are counter-cyclical to effort: a strong safety program pushes incidents down, so the metric (e.g., TRIR) gets "worse" statistically as small samples fluctuate — managers may fear that being proactive makes numbers look bad. 它们与努力反周期：强大的安全项目会压低事故数，因此在小样本下指标（如TRIR）在统计上会"变差"——管理者可能担心主动作为反而让数字难看。
  • Low frequency in a small site makes rates unstable: one single incident can swing a small company's TRIR dramatically. 小场所的低频率使比率不稳定：一起事故就能让小型公司的TRIR大幅波动。
  • They measure failures, not the health of the program's inputs (training, inspections, hazard corrections). 它们衡量失败，而不衡量项目输入（培训、检查、危害纠正）的健康程度。
CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
A balanced safety scorecard uses BOTH leading and lagging indicators. Leading indicators drive improvement; lagging indicators validate results. Neither alone provides a complete picture of safety performance. When asked "which metric is most useful for early warning," choose a leading indicator. 平衡的安全计分卡同时使用领先指标和滞后指标。领先指标推动改进；滞后指标验证结果。单独使用任何一种都无法完整反映安全绩效。当被问"哪个指标对早期预警最有用"时，选领先指标。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "Which is a LEADING indicator of safety performance?"
答题要点 / Key point: 领先=事前、过程、可预见风险（培训完成率、隐患关闭率、安全观察次数、JHA完成数）；滞后=事后、结果（TRIR、DART、LTIR、死亡数）。问"早期预警"选领先指标。TRIR/失时工伤率等滞后指标在小场所会因一次事故剧烈波动。
⚖️ 易混淆对比 / Common Confusions:
Leading Indicator 领先指标Lagging Indicator 滞后指标关键区别 / Key Difference
过程/输入：培训时数、隐患关闭、安全观察、JSA数量结果/输出：TRIR、DART、LTIR、fatalities领先推动改进（事前预测）；滞后验证结果（事后确认）。问"预警/预防"→领先；问"结果/绩效"→滞后

6.2 关键安全指标与公式 / Key Safety Metrics & Formulas

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.8 · Brauer Ch.37 · Yates Ch.13

These formulas are essential for the CSP exam. Know the numerator, denominator, and multiplier for each. 以下公式对CSP考试至关重要。要掌握每个公式的分子、分母和乘数。

TRIR (Total Recordable Incident Rate / 总可记录事故率)
TRIR = (N × 200,000) / 总工时 / total hours worked
N = OSHA可记录伤害/疾病数量 / N = number of OSHA-recordable injuries/illnesses
DART (Days Away, Restricted, or Transferred / 缺勤、受限或转岗天数率)
DART = (缺勤/受限/转岗案例数 × 200,000) / 总工时 / DART = (cases with days away/restricted/transfer × 200,000) / total hours worked
仅计算导致损失工时、限制性岗位或岗位调动的案例 / Only count cases that result in lost time, restricted duty, or job transfer
LTIFR (Lost Time Injury Frequency Rate / 损失工时伤害频率率)
LTIFR = (损失工时伤害数 × 1,000,000) / 总工时 / LTIFR = (lost time injuries × 1,000,000) / total hours worked
美国以外常用；使用1,000,000工时基数，而TRIR/DART使用200,000 / Commonly used outside the U.S.; uses 1,000,000-hour base vs. 200,000 for TRIR/DART
严重度 / Severity Rate
严重度 = (损失工作日 × 200,000) / 总工时 / Severity Rate = (lost workdays × 200,000) / total hours worked
衡量损失工时事件的严重程度（每损失的工作日数）
平均严重度 / Average Severity = 总损失工作日 / 损失工时案例数 / Total lost workdays ÷ Number of lost-time cases
每个损失工时案例的平均损失工作日数
为什么是200,000？/ Why 200,000?
200,000 hours = 100 full-time workers × 50 weeks × 40 hours/week. This normalizes rates to a per-100-worker basis, making comparisons fair across organizations of different sizes. LTIFR uses 1,000,000 (= 500 workers), the international base. 200,000小时 = 100名全职工人 × 50周 × 40小时/周。这将比率标准化为每100名工人的基数，使不同规模的组织之间能够公平比较。LTIFR使用1,000,000（=500名工人），这是国际基数。

计算示例 / Worked Example

示例 / Example: 一家公司有6起可记录伤害，其中2起为损失工时伤害，1起为受限工作伤害。总工时400,000。
TRIR = (6 × 200,000) / 400,000 = 3.00
DART = (3 × 200,000) / 400,000 = 1.50
LTIFR = (2 × 1,000,000) / 400,000 = 5.00
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "A company has 4 recordable injuries and 200,000 hours. What is the TRIR?"
答题要点 / Key point: TRIR=(案例数×200,000)/总工时；DART用天数受限+损失工时案例（DART=(DART案例×200,000)/总工时）；LTIFR用1,000,000基数。200,000=100名工人×50周×40小时。示例：6起可记录/400,000h → TRIR 3.0；DART 1.5；LTIFR 5.0。
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: "200,000 = 100×50×40" — 每100名工人的基数（200,000小时）；LTIFR用1,000,000（国际/500工人）。TRIR的DART分子只看"损失工时+受限工作"案例。中文："TRIR二十万、DART算严重、LTIFR一百万"。
⚖️ 易混淆对比 / Common Confusions:
TRIRDART关键区别 / Key Difference
所有可记录案例（含医疗处理、轻伤）仅损失工时(LTI)+受限工作(RTW)案例DART是TRIR的"更严重子集"——DART只统计需要离岗或改岗的案例。数值上DART ≤ TRIR

6.3 OSHA记录判定与记录保存 / OSHA Recordability Determination & Recordkeeping

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1904 · Brauer Ch.8 · Yates Ch.2

Knowing whether a case is OSHA-recordable is a tested skill. An injury or illness is recordable if it is work-related, is a new case, and meets one of the general recording criteria. 判断一个案例是否可记录是考试技能。若伤害或疾病与工作相关、是新案例、且满足一般记录标准之一，则必须记录。

记录判定流程 / Recordability Decision Flow

工作相关? / Work-related? → 新案例? / New case? → 满足记录标准? / Meets criteria? → 记录 / Record on 300 Log

一般记录标准 / General Recording Criteria

标准 / Criterion说明 / Explanation
死亡 / Death Any work-related fatality must be recorded and reported to OSHA within 8 hours.任何与工作相关的死亡必须记录，并在8小时内报告给OSHA。
损失工时 / Days Away from Work Days the employee could not work at all due to the injury/illness.员工因伤害/疾病完全无法工作的天数。
工作限制 / Restricted Work Employee is assigned to a job they cannot perform fully or a less physically demanding task.员工被分配到无法完全胜任的岗位或体力要求较低的任务。
转岗 / Job Transfer Employee is assigned to a completely different job on a temporary/permanent basis.员工被临时/永久地分配到完全不同的岗位。
医疗处理 / Medical Treatment Treatment beyond first aid (prescription medication, sutures, surgery, hospitalization).超出急救范围的治疗（处方药、缝合、手术、住院）。
意识丧失 / Loss of Consciousness Any work-related loss of consciousness is recordable.任何与工作相关的意识丧失都是可记录的。
确诊职业病 / Significant Diagnosed Injury/Illness A significant injury/illness diagnosed by a physician or other licensed health care professional, even if not resulting in the above.由医生或其他持照医疗保健专业人士诊断的重大伤害/疾病，即使未导致上述情况。

急救（不记录）vs 医疗处理（记录）/ First Aid (Not Recordable) vs. Medical Treatment (Recordable)

急救 — 不记录 / First Aid — Not Recordable医疗处理 — 记录 / Medical Treatment — Recordable
  • Non-prescription medication (OTC)非处方药（OTC）
  • Bandages / cold compress / ice packs绷带 / 冷敷 / 冰袋
  • Cleaning minor cuts with hydrogen peroxide用过氧化氢清洗轻微伤口
  • Use of eye patches / irrigation for minor injuries对轻微伤害使用眼罩 / 冲洗
  • Heat therapy / massage (no prescription)热疗 / 按摩（无处方）
  • Drilling a fingernail / removing splinters钻指甲 / 取出木刺
  • Prescription medication (Rx)处方药（Rx）
  • Sutures / surgical closure缝合 / 手术闭合
  • Hospitalization / surgery住院 / 手术
  • Physical therapy / chiropractic (by licensed provider)物理治疗 / 脊椎按摩（由持照专业人员）
  • X-rays / diagnostic testsX光 / 诊断检查
  • Antibiotics / IV fluids抗生素 / 静脉输液

OSHA记录表格 / OSHA Recording Forms

表格 / Form目的 / Purpose关键细节 / Key Details
OSHA 300 工作相关伤害与疾病日志 / Log of Work-Related Injuries and Illnesses Each recordable case gets one line. Includes: date, employee name, job title, description, classification (injury, skin disorder, respiratory, etc.), days away, days restricted. Must be kept for 5 years following the end of the calendar year. Cases must be logged within 7 calendar days of knowledge. 每项可记录案例占一行。包括：日期、员工姓名、职务、描述、分类（伤害、皮肤病、呼吸系统等）、缺勤天数、受限天数。必须在日历年结束后保留5年。案例须在知悉后7个日历日内登记。
OSHA 300A 工作相关伤害与疾病摘要 / Summary of Work-Related Injuries and Illnesses Annual summary showing totals (not individual cases). Must be certified by a company executive. Posted from February 1 through April 30 each year, covering the previous calendar year. 年度摘要，显示总数（非个案）。必须由公司高管认证。每年2月1日至4月30日张贴，涵盖上一日历年。
OSHA 301 伤害与疾病事件报告 / Injury and Illness Incident Report Detailed report for each individual recordable case. Equivalent to a Workers' Compensation "First Report of Injury." Must be completed within 7 calendar days. 每项可记录案例的详细报告。等同于工伤保险"伤害首次报告"。必须在7个日历日内完成。

强制上报（与记录不同）/ Mandatory Reporting (Distinct from Recording)

  • Fatality: must report to OSHA within 8 hours. 死亡：必须在8小时内报告OSHA。
  • In-patient hospitalization: must report within 24 hours. 住院治疗：必须在24小时内报告。
  • Amputation / loss of an eye: must report within 24 hours. 截肢 / 眼球丧失：必须在24小时内报告。
  • Distinguish reporting (to OSHA) from recording (on the 300 Log): reporting is immediate and time-bound; recording is the annual log discipline. 区分报告（向OSHA，即时且有时限）与记录（在300日志上，年度日志纪律）。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "When must a fatality be reported to OSHA?"
答题要点 / Key point: 强制报告（向OSHA）：死亡8小时内；住院、截肢、眼球丧失24小时内。表单：300日志(逐案)、300A摘要(2/1–4/30张贴、高管认证)、301个案报告(7天)。记录判定三要件：工作相关+新案例+符合一般标准（医疗处理/限制/损失工作日）。
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: "8死24住院" — 死亡8小时内报告；住院/截肢/失明24小时内报告。表单口诀："300记、300A贴、301填"。中文"八小时死亡、二十四住院"。

6.4 基准比较 / Benchmarking

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.35 · Brauer Ch.37 · BLS

基准比较Benchmarking is the process of comparing an organization's safety performance against a reference point to identify gaps and opportunities for improvement. 基准比较是将组织的安全绩效与参考标准进行比较，以识别差距和改进机会的过程。

类型 / Type描述 / Description示例 / Example
内部基准比较 / Internal Benchmarking Comparing performance across different sites, departments, or time periods within the same organization 比较同一组织内不同地点、部门或时间段的绩效 Comparing TRIR between Plant A and Plant B; tracking year-over-year improvement at one facility 比较A厂与B厂的TRIR；追踪一个设施的年同比改进
竞争基准比较 / Competitive Benchmarking Comparing performance against direct competitors in the same industry 与同行业直接竞争对手比较绩效 A chemical manufacturer comparing DART rate against other chemical companies in the same NAICS code 化工制造商与相同NAICS代码的其他化工公司比较DART率
功能基准比较 / Functional Benchmarking Comparing a specific function or process against best-in-class organizations, even from different industries 将特定功能或流程与最佳组织进行比较，甚至跨行业 Comparing your lockout/tagout program against that of an organization known for LOTO excellence, regardless of industry 将您的上锁挂牌项目与以LOTO卓越著称的组织比较，无论其行业
通用基准比较 / Generic Benchmarking Comparing general processes against universal best practices applicable across all industries 将通用流程与适用于所有行业的通用最佳实践比较 Comparing safety training program design against adult learning best practices used across all sectors 将安全培训项目设计与所有行业通用的成人学习最佳实践比较
数据来源 / Data Sources:
BLS (Bureau of Labor Statistics) publishes industry incidence rates by NAICS code; OSHA uses these for targeting. Industry averages should be compared within the same NAICS code and employment size class to be meaningful. 劳工统计局（BLS）按NAICS代码发布行业事故率；OSHA用这些数据确定检查重点。行业平均值应在相同NAICS代码和就业规模等级内比较才有意义。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MEDIUM
典型问法 / Typical question: "A company compares its TRIR against other companies in the same NAICS code. This is what type of benchmarking?"
答题要点 / Key point: 四类：内部(自己厂间)/竞争(同行)/功能(跨行业最佳实践)/通用(全行业通用最佳实践)。BLS按NAICS发布行业事故率；比较须同NAICS+同规模等级才有意义。

6.5 安全文化测量 / Safety Culture Measurement

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.35 · Brauer Ch.31 · Yates Ch.19

Bradley曲线 (杜邦Bradley曲线) / The Bradley Curve (DuPont Bradley Curve)

The Bradley曲线Bradley Curve☆ describes the journey of safety culture maturity through four stages: Bradley曲线描述了安全文化成熟度的四个阶段旅程：

⚠
反应型 / Reactive
Safety by natural instinct; "accidents happen"; blame culture; no ownership 凭本能维护安全；"事故不可避免"；责备文化；无主人翁意识
📋
依赖型 / Dependent
Safety by rules & supervision; management commitment; fear/discipline driven 凭规章制度与监管维护安全；管理层承诺；恐惧/纪律驱动
👤
独立型 / Independent
Safety by personal knowledge & commitment; self-regulation; personal standards 凭个人知识和承诺维护安全；自我管理；个人标准
🤝
互助型 / Interdependent
Safety as a team value; "I am my brother's keeper"; collective ownership; helping others 安全是团队价值观；"我是兄弟的守护者"；集体主人翁意识；互相帮助

The curve plots injury rates against culture maturity: as the culture matures from Reactive to Interdependent, incident rates decline, with the steepest drop occurring as organizations move from Dependent to Independent. The goal is to help organizations shift the responsibility for safety from management enforcement (Dependent) to personal commitment (Independent) and ultimately collective care (Interdependent). 该曲线将伤害率与安全文化成熟度对应绘制：随着文化从反应型成熟到互助型，事故率下降，其中从依赖型向独立型转变时下降最陡。目标是帮助组织将安全责任从管理层强制执行（依赖型）转向个人承诺（独立型），最终达到集体关怀（互助型）。

安全文化成熟度模型 / Safety Culture Maturity Model

Another framework describing five levels: 另一个描述五个层级的框架：

  1. Pathological / 病态型: Safety is a problem caused by workers; hide incidents 安全是工人造成的问题；隐瞒事故
  2. Reactive / 反应型: Safety is important; act after incidents occur 安全很重要；事故发生后采取行动
  3. Calculative / 计算型: Safety is managed through systems and data collection 安全通过系统和数据收集进行管理
  4. Proactive / 主动型: Safety is a core value; anticipate problems before they occur 安全是核心价值观；在问题发生之前进行预判
  5. Generative / 生成型: Safety is fully integrated into everything the organization does; chronic unease about risk 安全完全融入组织的一切活动；对风险保持持续关注

测量安全文化的方法 / Methods to Measure Safety Culture

  • Safety climate surveys: Anonymous questionnaires measuring workers' perceptions of management commitment, communication, and reporting climate. 安全氛围调查：匿名问卷，测量员工对管理层承诺、沟通和报告氛围的感知。
  • Behavioral observations: Structured observation of safe/unsafe behaviors and at-risk practices. 行为观察：对安全/不安全行为和冒险实践进行结构化观察。
  • Near-miss reporting rates: High rates typically indicate a "just culture" where workers report without fear — a strong signal of culture health. 未遂事件报告率：高报告率通常表明存在"公正文化"，工人无忧报告——这是文化健康的强信号。
  • Focus groups and interviews: Qualitative insight into barriers and drivers of safe behavior. 焦点小组与访谈：定性了解安全行为障碍和驱动因素。

6.6 安全分析的统计基础 / Statistical Basics for Safety Analysis

📖 来源 / Sources: Yates Ch.13 · Brauer Ch.37
指标 / Measure定义 / Definition在安全中的应用 / Use in Safety
均值 / Mean (μ or x̄) Arithmetic average of all values所有数值的算术平均数 Average incident rate, average exposure level平均事故率、平均暴露水平
中位数 / Median Middle value when data is ordered数据排序后的中间值 More robust than mean when data has outliers (e.g., one catastrophic loss skewing the mean)数据有异常值时比均值更稳健（如一次灾难性损失使均值偏斜）
众数 / Mode Most frequently occurring value出现频率最高的值 Most common type of injury, most frequent root cause最常见的伤害类型、最频繁的根本原因
标准差 / Standard Deviation (σ or s) Measure of dispersion/spread around the mean测量均值周围的分散/扩散程度 Variability in exposure measurements, consistency of safety performance暴露测量的变异性、安全绩效的一致性
方差 / Variance Average squared deviation from the mean; σ² for population, s² for sample与均值之差的平方的平均值；总体为σ²，样本为s² Foundation for standard deviation; used in ANOVA and regression标准差的基础；用于方差分析和回归
正态分布 / Normal Distribution Bell-shaped symmetric distribution; 68-95-99.7 rule (68% within ±1σ, 95% within ±2σ, 99.7% within ±3σ)钟形对称分布；68-95-99.7法则（68%在±1σ内，95%在±2σ内，99.7%在±3σ内） Modeling exposure data, setting exposure limits, control charts暴露数据建模、设限、控制图

计算示例 / Worked Examples

均值 / Mean: 月度事故数 4, 5, 5, 6, 8, 10 → 均值 = 38/6 = 6.33
中位数 / Median: 排序后 4,5,5,6,8,10 → 中间两值 (5+6)/2 = 5.5
众数 / Mode: 出现最多的值是 5

样本标准差 / Sample SD (n=5): 数据 4,6,8,10,12，均值=8
s = √[ Σ(xᵢ−x̄)² / (n−1) ] = √[ (16+4+0+4+16)/4 ] = √(40/4) = √10 = 3.16
注意：样本标准差用 n−1（自由度）；总体标准差用 n。
异常值的影响 / Effect of Outliers:
Consider the data 4, 5, 5, 6, 8, 100 (one catastrophic claim). Mean = 21.3 but median = 5.5. The median is far more robust — it does not change with extreme outliers. In safety, when presenting cost or severity data with rare catastrophic events, the median is often a better central measure. 考虑数据4、5、5、6、8、100（一次灾难性索赔）。均值为21.3，但中位数为5.5。中位数稳健得多——不受极端异常值影响。在安全领域，当呈现包含罕见灾难性事件的成本或严重度数据时，中位数通常是更好的中心度量。

6.7 置信区间与t分布 / Confidence Intervals & the t-Distribution

📖 来源 / Sources: Yates Ch.13 · Brauer Ch.37

A 置信区间confidence interval (CI)☆ gives a range of plausible values for a population parameter (e.g., the true mean exposure) based on a sample. It expresses sampling uncertainty. 置信区间基于样本给出总体参数（如真实平均暴露）的合理取值范围。它表达抽样不确定性。

置信区间公式 / Confidence Interval Formula

均值的95%置信区间 / 95% Confidence Interval for the Mean
CI = x̄ ± Z × (σ / √n)   (总体σ已知或n≥30)
CI = x̄ ± t × (s / √n)   (总体σ未知且n<30)
对于95% CI，Z = 1.96；对于90% CI，Z = 1.645；对于99% CI，Z = 2.576。
当使用t分布时，自由度 df = n − 1。

计算示例 / Worked Example

Z分布示例: 36个噪声测量值，样本均值x̄ = 85 dB，总体标准差σ = 3 dB（已知）。
95% CI = 85 ± 1.96 × (3/√36) = 85 ± 1.96 × 0.5 = 85 ± 0.98 → [84.0, 86.0]

t分布示例: 25个测量值，样本均值x̄ = 50，样本标准差s = 5（σ未知）。
df = 24，t(0.025, 24) ≈ 2.064
95% CI = 50 ± 2.064 × (5/√25) = 50 ± 2.06 → [47.9, 52.1]
关键区分 / Key Distinction:
Use Z-score (normal distribution) when population standard deviation (σ) is known OR when sample size n ≥ 30. Use t-distribution when population σ is unknown AND sample size n < 30. Degrees of freedom (df) = n − 1 for t-distribution. The t-distribution has wider tails, giving wider (more conservative) intervals for small samples. 当总体标准差(σ)已知或样本量n≥30时使用Z分数（正态分布）。当总体σ未知且样本量n<30时使用t分布。t分布的自由度(df) = n − 1。t分布尾部更宽，为小样本给出更宽（更保守）的区间。

6.8 帕累托分析与数据展示 / Pareto Analysis & Data Presentation

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.3 · Yates Ch.13 · Yates Ch.2

帕累托分析（80/20法则）/ Pareto Analysis (80/20 Rule)

帕累托分析Pareto Analysis is based on the principle that roughly 80% of effects come from 20% of causes. In safety, this means: 帕累托分析基于约80%的结果来自20%的原因这一原则。在安全领域，这意味着：

  • 80% of injuries come from 20% of hazards 80%的伤害来自20%的危害
  • 80% of incidents stem from 20% of root causes 80%的事故源于20%的根本原因
  • A Pareto chart is a combined bar chart (descending frequency) and cumulative line graph 帕累托图是柱状图（按频率降序排列）与累积折线图的组合
  • Use it to prioritize which hazards or causes to address first for maximum impact 用于优先确定应首先处理哪些危害或原因以达到最大效果

常用数据展示工具 / Common Data Presentation Tools

工具 / Tool用途 / Use
帕累托图 / Pareto Chart Rank-ordered bars with cumulative line; prioritize the "vital few" causes降序柱状图加累积折线；确定"关键少数"原因优先级
直方图 / Histogram Distribution of a continuous variable (e.g., exposure levels)连续变量的分布（如暴露水平）
控制图 / Control Chart Track a process over time against control limits (typically ±3σ); detect special-cause variation随时间跟踪过程并与控制限（通常±3σ）比较；检测特殊原因变异
散点图 / Scatter Plot Relationship between two variables (e.g., training hours vs. incident rate)两个变量之间的关系（如培训时数vs事故率）
趋势图 / Run/Trend Chart Incident rates over time; detect trends and seasonality随时间的事故率；检测趋势和季节性
因果图 / Cause-and-Effect (Fishbone) Organize potential causes of a problem into categories将问题的潜在原因分类组织

选择中心趋势指标 / Choosing a Central Tendency Measure

  • Use the mean when data is symmetric and you want the arithmetic average (e.g., average exposure). 使用均值：数据对称且需要算术平均时（如平均暴露）。
  • Use the median when data is skewed or contains outliers (e.g., cost data with rare catastrophic claims). 使用中位数：数据偏斜或含异常值时（如含罕见灾难性索赔的成本数据）。
  • Use the mode for categorical data — the most frequent category (e.g., most common injury type). 使用众数：用于分类数据——最常见的类别（如最常见的伤害类型）。
