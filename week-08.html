8.1 安全预算 / Safety Budgeting

📖 来源 / Sources: Yates Ch.18 · Brauer Ch.35

Safety professionals must be able to justify safety expenditures and manage budgets effectively. Three common budgeting approaches are used: 安全专业人员必须能够证明安全支出的合理性并有效管理预算。常用的三种预算方法：

预算方法 / Budgeting Method描述 / Description优点 / Advantages缺点 / Disadvantages
零基预算Zero-Based Budgeting (ZBB)☆ Every expense must be justified from scratch for each new period. Starts from a "zero base" — nothing is carried over from previous budgets. 每项支出在每个新期间都必须从零开始论证。从"零基础"开始——不沿用任何上期预算。 Eliminates waste; forces re-evaluation of all programs; aligns spending with current priorities 消除浪费；强制重新评估所有项目；使支出与当前优先事项对齐 Time-consuming; requires significant effort to justify every line item; may overlook essential ongoing programs 耗时；每笔支出都需大量论证工作；可能忽视必要的持续性项目
增量预算Incremental Budgeting☆ Uses the previous period's budget as a baseline, then adjusts up or down by a percentage or specific increments. 以上期预算为基线，然后按百分比或特定增量上调或下调。 Simple and fast; stable and predictable; minimal administrative burden 简单快捷；稳定可预测；行政管理负担最小 Perpetuates inefficiencies; assumes past spending was optimal; discourages innovation; may not reflect current needs 延续低效率；假定过去的支出是最优的；阻碍创新；可能不反映当前需求
作业基础预算Activity-Based Budgeting (ABB)☆ Budget is built around specific activities, programs, and their costs. Each activity (training, inspections, PPE, ventilation maintenance) is budgeted based on its actual resource requirements. 围绕特定活动、项目及其成本构建预算。每项活动（培训、检查、PPE、通风维护）根据实际资源需求编制预算。 Links spending directly to safety activities; transparent; supports cost-benefit analysis; aligns resources with objectives 将支出与安全活动直接挂钩；透明；支持成本效益分析；将资源与目标对齐 More complex to develop; requires detailed activity cost data; may be difficult for new or changing programs 开发更复杂；需要详细的活动成本数据；对新项目或变化中的项目可能较困难

预算场景选择 / Choosing a Budgeting Approach

  • Activity-based budgeting is generally most appropriate for safety programs because it directly ties spending to safety activities and objectives, and supports cost-benefit justification. 作业基础预算通常最适合安全项目，因为它将支出与安全活动和目标直接挂钩，并支持成本效益论证。
  • Zero-based budgeting is useful when the organization needs to fundamentally re-evaluate its safety spending or when budgets have become bloated. 零基预算在组织需要从根本上重新评估安全支出或预算膨胀时有用。
  • Incremental budgeting is simplest but perpetuates historical inefficiencies and may not fund new safety initiatives. 增量预算最简单，但延续历史低效，可能无法为新安全举措提供资金。
CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:

8.2 投资回报率、回收期与成本效益分析 / ROI, Payback Period & Cost-Benefit Analysis

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.34 · Brauer Ch.35 · Yates Ch.18

投资回报率 / Return on Investment (ROI)

ROI / 投资回报率 = (投资收益 − 投资成本) / 投资成本 × 100%
ROI = (Gain from Investment − Cost of Investment) / Cost of Investment × 100%

Example: A machine guarding project costs $50,000 and is projected to prevent injuries costing $200,000 over 5 years. ROI = ($200,000 − $50,000) / $50,000 = 300%. 示例：一个机械防护项目成本为50,000美元，预计在5年内可预防造成200,000美元损失的伤害。ROI = ($200,000 − $50,000) / $50,000 = 300%。

回收期 / Payback Period

回收期 / Payback Period = 投资成本 / 年化收益
Payback = Initial Investment / Annual Savings
示例：项目成本$80,000，年节省$40,000 → 回收期 = $80,000 / $40,000 = 2年

ROI vs 回收期 / ROI vs. Payback

指标 / Metric衡量什么 / What It Measures使用场景 / Use
ROI Total return relative to investment over the project life (percentage)项目寿命期内相对于投资的总体回报（百分比） Comparing the profitability of competing projects比较竞争项目的盈利能力
Payback Period Time required to recover the initial investment收回初始投资所需的时间 Assessing liquidity/risk — shorter payback is safer评估流动性/风险——回收期越短越安全

冰山模型 — 直接成本与间接成本 / The Iceberg Model — Direct vs. Indirect Costs

The 冰山模型Iceberg Model☆ illustrates that the visible (direct) costs of an incident are only a small fraction of the total cost. The hidden (indirect) costs below the surface are typically 3–10 times greater than direct costs. 冰山模型说明，事故的可见（直接）成本仅占总成本的一小部分。表面之下的隐藏（间接）成本通常为直接成本的3–10倍。

直接成本（可见——水面之上）/ DIRECT COSTS (Visible — Above Water)
Medical treatment · Indemnity/wage replacement · Property damage repair · Insurance deductibles · Legal fees/fines 医疗救治 · 工伤补偿/工资替代 · 财产损失修复 · 保险免赔额 · 法律费用/罚款
▼ 水面 / WATER LINE ▼
间接成本（隐藏——水面之下）/ INDIRECT COSTS (Hidden — Below Water)
Lost productivity of injured worker and coworkers · Investigation time · Hiring & training replacement workers · Overtime to cover absences · Damaged equipment downtime · Reduced morale · Reputation damage · Increased insurance premiums · Administrative burden · Lost business opportunities
受伤工人和同事的生产力损失 · 调查时间 · 替代工人的招聘与培训 · 弥补缺勤的加班 · 受损设备的停机时间 · 士气下降 · 声誉受损 · 保险费上涨 · 行政管理负担 · 业务机会损失
成本类型 / Cost Type示例 / Examples典型比例 / Typical Ratio

间接成本 / Indirect Costs Lost productivity, investigation time, replacement labor, morale, reputation生产力损失、调查时间、替代劳动力、士气、声誉 ~70–90% 的总事故成本（3–10倍直接成本）/ of total incident cost (3–10× direct costs)
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MEDIUM
典型问法 / Typical question: "Which is an indirect (hidden) cost of an incident?"
答题要点 / Key point: 直接=医疗/工伤赔付/财产损失/罚款（可见）；间接=生产力损失/调查/替代工/士气/声誉（隐藏）。间接占70–90%，为直接的3–10倍。冰山模型：水下部分更大。论证预防投资用总成本。

8.3 与安全相关的管理理论 / Management Theories Relevant to Safety

📖 来源 / Sources: Yates Ch.19 · Brauer Ch.31

X理论与Y理论 (麦格雷戈) / Theory X vs. Theory Y (McGregor)

Theory X: Workers inherently dislike work, need close supervision, and are motivated by punishment/reward. Leads to authoritarian management.

Theory Y: Workers are self-motivated, seek responsibility, and find satisfaction in work. Leads to participative management.

Safety implication: Theory Y organizations typically have better safety cultures — workers are trusted to identify hazards and participate in safety decisions. X理论：工人天性厌恶工作，需要密切监督，由惩罚/奖励驱动。导致专制式管理。

Y理论：工人自我激励，寻求责任，在工作中获得满足感。导致参与式管理。

安全启示：Y理论组织通常拥有更好的安全文化——工人被信任去识别危害并参与安全决策。

马斯洛需求层次理论 / Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs

Five levels (bottom to top):
1. Physiological (food, water, shelter)
2. Safety (security, health, stable employment)
3. Love/Belonging (team, relationships)
4. Esteem (recognition, respect)
5. Self-Actualization (fulfilling potential)

Safety implication: Safety is a foundational need — if workers feel unsafe, they cannot focus on higher-level needs like teamwork and quality. A safe workplace enables all higher-order motivation. 五个层次（从下到上）：
1. 生理需求（食物、水、住所）
2. 安全需求（安全、健康、稳定就业）
3. 爱与归属（团队、关系）
4. 尊重（认可、尊重）
5. 自我实现（发挥潜能）

安全启示：安全是基础需求——如果工人感到不安全，就无法专注于团队合作和质量等更高层次的需求。安全的工作场所为所有更高层次的激励创造条件。

赫茨伯格双因素理论 / Herzberg's Two-Factor Theory

Hygiene Factors (Dissatisfiers): Company policies, supervision, working conditions, salary, job security. Their absence causes dissatisfaction but their presence does NOT motivate.

Motivators (Satisfiers): Achievement, recognition, meaningful work, responsibility, advancement, personal growth. These drive satisfaction and motivation.

Safety implication: A safe workplace is a hygiene factor — it prevents dissatisfaction. To truly motivate workers toward safety excellence, provide recognition, responsibility, and meaningful involvement. 保健因素（不满足因素）：公司政策、监督、工作条件、薪资、工作保障。其缺失会导致不满足，但其存在并不激励人。

激励因素（满足因素）：成就、认可、有意义的工作、责任、晋升、个人成长。这些驱动满足感和激励。

安全启示：安全的工作场所是保健因素——它防止不满足。要真正激励工人追求安全卓越，需要提供认可、责任和有意义的参与。

三种理论对比 / Comparing the Three Theories

理论 / Theory核心观点 / Core Idea安全应用 / Safety Application
McGregor X/Y Assumptions managers hold about workers shape management style管理者对工人的假设塑造管理风格 Y-assumption → participative safety programsY假设→参与式安全项目
Maslow Needs are hierarchical; lower needs must be met first需求有层次；必须先满足低层需求 Safety is a Level-2 foundational need安全是第2层基础需求
Herzberg Hygiene factors prevent dissatisfaction; motivators drive satisfaction保健因素防止不满足；激励因素驱动满足 Safe workplace = hygiene; recognition = motivator安全工作场所=保健；认可=激励
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MEDIUM
典型问法 / Typical question: "In Herzberg's theory, salary is classified as what?"
答题要点 / Key point: Herzberg：保健因素（薪资/工作条件/政策/监督/安全）=缺失致不满、存在不激励；激励因素（成就/认可/责任/成长）=驱动满意。Maslow：安全是第2层（生理之上）。McGregor X/Y：Y假设→参与式安全。安全=保健因素，认可=激励因素。

8.4 安全领导力与安全管理 / Safety Leadership vs. Safety Management

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.35 · Brauer Ch.31
维度 / Dimension安全管理 / Safety Management安全领导力 / Safety Leadership
关注点 / Focus Systems, processes, compliance, rules系统、流程、合规、规则 Vision, culture, inspiration, values愿景、文化、激励、价值观
方法 / Approach Plan, organize, control, measure计划、组织、控制、衡量 Influence, inspire, empower, model影响、激励、赋能、示范
提出的问题 / Questions Asked "Are we in compliance?" "What is our TRIR?""我们合规了吗？" "我们的TRIR是多少？" "Do our people feel safe speaking up?" "What does safety excellence look like?""我们的人敢于表达吗？" "安全卓越是什么样的？"
关键活动 / Key Activities Writing procedures, conducting audits, tracking metrics, enforcing rules编写程序、开展审核、追踪指标、执行规则 Communicating vision, walking the floor, recognizing safe behavior, removing barriers, building trust传达愿景、现场巡视、认可安全行为、消除障碍、建立信任
与规则的关系 / Relationship to Rules Enforce compliance with rules强制执行规则遵守 Create shared values where people follow rules because they believe in them, not because they fear consequences建立共同价值观，人们因信仰规则而遵守，非因恐惧后果而遵守
核心见解 / Key Insight:
Both management and leadership are necessary. Management without leadership produces compliance without commitment. Leadership without management produces vision without execution. World-class safety requires both: the systems and discipline of management + the vision and influence of leadership. 管理和领导力缺一不可。没有领导力的管理产生的是没有承诺的服从。没有管理的领导力产生的是没有执行的愿景。世界级安全需要两者兼具：管理的系统和纪律+领导力的愿景和影响力。

提高管理层参与 / Improving Management Commitment

  • Visible leadership — managers walk the floor, participate in safety activities, and lead by example (management by walking around). 可见的领导力——管理者现场巡视、参与安全活动、以身作则（走动式管理）。
  • Include safety performance in managers' goals and performance reviews. 将安全绩效纳入管理者的目标与绩效评估。
  • Provide resources and remove barriers that impede safe work. 提供资源并消除阻碍安全工作开展的障碍。
  • Recognize and reward safe behavior and reporting, not just outcome metrics. 认可并奖励安全行为和报告，而不仅仅是结果指标。
⚖️ 易混淆对比 / Common Confusions:
Safety Management 安全管理Safety Leadership 安全领导力关键区别 / Key Difference
系统/流程/合规/规则：写程序、做审核、追TRIR、执行规则愿景/文化/激励：走动巡视、认可行为、建信任、引导价值观管理=计划-组织-控制-衡量；领导=影响-激励-赋能-示范。无领导的管→无承诺的服从；无管理的领导→无执行的愿景
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MEDIUM
典型问法 / Typical question: "Which activity reflects safety LEADERSHIP rather than management?"
答题要点 / Key point: 领导力关键词：愿景、激励、走动式管理、认可安全行为、建信任；管理关键词：合规、程序、审核、指标、规则执行。世界级安全=两者兼具。管理层承诺手段：目标纳入绩效、提供资源、以身作则、奖励报告行为。

8.5 项目管理 — RACI图与项目时间线 · Project Management — RACI Charts & Project Timelines☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.35 · Yates Ch.2

The CSP11 blueprint (Domain 2, item 13) explicitly names RACI charts and project timelines as project management tools. Safety professionals use these to plan and execute safety initiatives — from implementing a new safety program to rolling out equipment upgrades. CSP11蓝图（领域2，第13项）明确将RACI图和项目时间线列为项目管理工具。安全专业人员使用这些工具来规划和执行安全举措——从实施新的安全项目到推出设备升级。

RACI图（责任分配矩阵） · RACI Chart (Responsibility Assignment Matrix)

A RACI图RACI chart☆ is a matrix that clarifies who does what in a project or process. Each task or deliverable is assigned one of four roles: RACI图是一个矩阵，明确项目中谁做什么。每个任务或交付件被分配四种角色之一：

角色 / Role含义 / Meaning安全项目示例 / Safety Project Example
R — Responsible / 负责执行 The person who does the work to complete the task. There must be at least one R per task. This is the "doer." 实际执行任务完成工作的人。每项任务至少有一个R。这是"执行者"。 Safety engineer who writes the new LOTO procedure编写新LOTO程序的安全工程师
A — Accountable / 最终负责 The person ultimately answerable for the correct and thorough completion of the task. Only ONE A per task. This person signs off / approves. The A is the "owner." 对任务正确完整完成的最终负责人。每项任务只有一个A。此人签核/审批。A是"责任人"。 Safety manager who approves the LOTO procedure审批LOTO程序的安全经理
C — Consulted / 征询意见 People whose opinions are sought, typically subject matter experts. Two-way communication. Consult before the task is done. 被征询意见的人，通常是主题专家。双向沟通。在执行任务前征询。 Maintenance supervisors and equipment operators providing input on the LOTO procedure提供LOTO程序意见的维修主管和设备操作员
I — Informed / 知悉 People kept up-to-date on progress and decisions. One-way communication. Informed after the task or decision. 持续了解进展和决策的人。单向沟通。在任务或决策后告知。 All affected employees in the area, HR department for training records该区域所有受影响员工、负责培训记录的人事部门
RACI关键规则 / RACI Key Rules:
Every task has exactly ONE "A" (Accountable). Every task has at least ONE "R" (Responsible). The same person can be both A and R, but avoid it when possible for segregation of duties. No task should have zero R or zero A. Avoid too many C's (bogged down) and too many I's (information overload). 每项任务恰好一个"A"（最终负责）。每项任务至少一个"R"（负责执行）。同一个人可以同时是A和R，但为了职责分离应尽可能避免。任何任务不得没有R或A。避免太多C（陷入僵局）和太多I（信息过载）。

项目时间线（甘特图） · Project Timelines (Gantt Charts)

项目时间线Project timelines, typically displayed as 甘特图Gantt charts, are bar charts showing tasks, durations, dependencies, milestones, and progress over time. For safety professionals, project timelines are essential for planning initiatives like: 项目时间线，通常以甘特图显示，是展示任务、持续时间、依赖关系、里程碑和随时间进展的条形图。对安全专业人员而言，项目时间线对于规划以下举措至关重要：

  • ISO 45001 implementation or certification (typically 12–18 months) ISO 45001实施或认证（通常12-18个月）
  • Corrective action implementation after an incident investigation 事故调查后的纠正措施实施
  • Annual safety program rollout (training, inspections, audits) 年度安全项目推出（培训、检查、审核）
  • Capital projects: ventilation upgrade, machine guarding retrofit, fire protection system installation 资本项目：通风升级、机器防护改造、消防系统安装

Key Gantt chart elements: Tasks (work breakdown), durations, start/end dates, dependencies (predecessor/successor links), milestones (zero-duration markers for key events), critical path (longest path through dependencies — determines minimum project duration), resource assignment, and percent complete tracking. 甘特图关键元素：任务（工作分解）、持续时间、开始/结束日期、依赖关系（前任/后继链接）、里程碑（关键事件的零持续时间标记）、关键路径（通过依赖关系的最长路径——决定最短项目工期）、资源分配和完成百分比跟踪。

CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
The CSP exam may present a scenario requiring role clarification on a safety project — use RACI to identify gaps (e.g., "Who is the A for this task? The description is unclear."). For timelines, the CSP may test critical path analysis — the longest sequence of dependent tasks that determines the minimum project completion time. Any delay on the critical path delays the entire project. CSP考试可能呈现一个需要明确安全项目角色的场景——使用RACI识别差距（如"这项任务的A是谁？描述不明确。"）。对于时间线，CSP可能考查关键路径分析——确定最短项目完成时间的最长依赖任务序列。关键路径上的任何延迟都会延迟整个项目。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MEDIUM
典型问法 / Typical question: "Who is the Accountable (A) party in this RACI chart?"
答题要点 / Key point: RACI铁律：每任务恰一个A（最终审批）、至少一个R（执行）。A=签核owner；R=doer；C=事前征询（双向）；I=事后告知（单向）。甘特图关键路径=最长依赖序列=最短工期，其延迟=整个项目延迟。
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: "RACI = 1A多R" — 每任务一个Accountable、至少一个Responsible；C=Consulted事前问、I=Informed事后知。中文"A负责拍板、R负责干活、C先问、I后知"。关键路径=最长路径=最短工期。

记录与医疗记录访问 / Access to Exposure & Medical Records

  • Employee access (29 CFR 1910.1020): Workers have the right to access their own exposure and medical records. 员工访问权（29 CFR 1910.1020）：工人有权访问自己的暴露记录和医疗记录。
  • Trade secrets: Records containing trade secrets must still be disclosed, but may be under a confidentiality agreement; the identity of confidential informants can be protected. 商业机密：含商业机密的记录仍须披露，但可在保密协议下进行；保密举报人的身份可受到保护。
  • OSHA access: OSHA may require that records be provided for inspection; records must be retained even during facility closure or sale (transferred or retained for the required period). OSHA访问权：OSHA可要求提供记录供检查；即使在设施关闭或出售时也必须保留记录（转移或保留至规定期限）。
CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
The two longest retention periods are frequently tested: Exposure records = 30 years. Medical records = duration of employment + 30 years. This means medical records for a 20-year employee must be kept for 50 years total. 两个最长的保留期限经常被考查：暴露记录=30年。医疗记录=雇佣期间+30年。这意味着为一位工龄20年的员工保留的医疗记录需要总共保留50年。

8.7 自愿保护计划 (VPP) / Voluntary Protection Programs (VPP)

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.38 · OSHA VPP

OSHA自愿保护计划OSHA's Voluntary Protection Programs (VPP)☆ recognize employers and workers who have implemented effective safety and health management systems and maintain injury and illness rates below national Bureau of Labor Statistics averages for their respective industries. OSHA的自愿保护计划（VPP）认可那些实施了有效的安全健康管理体系，并将伤害和疾病率保持在劳工统计局各行业全国平均水平以下的雇主和工人。

计划 / Program描述 / Description
星级计划 / Star Program For exemplary worksites with comprehensive, successful safety and health management systems. Must have rates below industry average. On-site evaluation required. Recertified every 3–5 years. 适用于拥有全面、成功的安全健康管理体系的模范工作场所。比率必须低于行业平均水平。需要现场评估。每3–5年重新认证。
优秀计划 / Merit Program For worksites with good safety and health management systems that have demonstrated the potential and commitment to achieve Star status. Serves as a stepping stone to Star. 适用于拥有良好安全健康管理体系，且已展现出达到星级水平的潜力和承诺的工作场所。作为通往星级的跳板。
示范计划 / Demonstration Program For worksites that provide Star-quality worker protection but use approaches that differ from current VPP requirements, serving as test cases to determine if alternatives are effective. 适用于提供星级质量的工人保护，但采用与现行VPP要求不同方法的工作场所，作为测试案例以确定替代方案是否有效。

VPP sites are removed from OSHA programmed inspection lists (they are not subject to routine scheduled inspections), though OSHA still investigates fatalities, catastrophes, and employee complaints. VPP participation is voluntary and requires management commitment, employee involvement, and a comprehensive safety and health management system. VPP场所从OSHA计划检查列表中移除（不接受常规计划检查），但OSHA仍会调查死亡、灾难和员工投诉。VPP参与是自愿的，需要管理层承诺、员工参与和全面的安全健康管理体系。

CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
VPP is voluntary and recognition-based — it does NOT exempt a site from OSHA enforcement. OSHA can still investigate complaints, fatalities, and catastrophes. Also know that VPP is distinct from SHARP (the Safety and Health Achievement Recognition Program), which is for small businesses in states with approved consultation programs. VPP是自愿性和基于认可的——它不免除现场的OSHA执法。OSHA仍可调查投诉、死亡和灾难。同时要知道VPP不同于SHARP（安全健康成就认可计划），后者面向拥有经批准咨询计划的州的州的小企业。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MEDIUM
典型问法 / Typical question: "Does VPP participation exempt a site from OSHA enforcement?"
答题要点 / Key point: VPP=自愿认可计划，不豁免OSHA执法——仍可调查投诉/死亡/灾难。从常规计划检查名单移除。与SHARP（面向小企业，州咨询计划）区分。参与需管理层承诺+员工参与+全面安全健康管理体系。
⚖️ 易混淆对比 / Common Confusions:
VPP 自愿保护计划SHARP关键区别 / Key Difference
任何规模组织，基于认可，不免除执法仅小企业、须州批准咨询计划VPP规模不限；SHARP限小企业。两者都是自愿、都不豁免OSHA执法（投诉/死亡/灾难仍查）

8.8 法律、道德与职业操守 / Law, Ethics & Professional Conduct

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.4 · Brauer Ch.5 · Yates Ch.2 · BCSP Code of Ethics

BCSP道德准则 / BCSP Code of Ethics

The Board of Certified Safety Professionals (BCSP) Code of Ethics governs the conduct of CSP holders. Key principles: 注册安全专业人员委员会（BCSP）道德准则规范CSP持证人的行为。主要原则：

  • Hold paramount the safety and health of people, protection of the environment, and protection of property 将以下置于最高地位：人员安全与健康、环境保护和财产保护
  • Be honest, fair, and impartial in all professional relationships 在所有职业关系中保持诚实、公平和公正
  • Issue public statements only in an objective and truthful manner 仅以客观和真实的方式发表公开声明
  • Act as faithful agents or trustees for each employer or client 作为忠实代理或受托人为每位雇主或客户服务
  • Maintain confidentiality of privileged information 对保密信息保持机密
  • Practice only in areas of competence; maintain competence through continuing education 仅在能力范围内执业；通过持续教育维持能力
  • Avoid conflicts of interest and disclose them when they exist 避免利益冲突，并在存在冲突时予以披露

法律概念 / Legal Concepts

概念 / Concept定义 / Definition
尽职调查Due Diligence☆ The level of judgment, care, prudence, and activity that a person would reasonably be expected to do under particular circumstances. In safety, it means taking all reasonable steps to prevent harm. It is a defense against charges of negligence. 一个人在特定情况下被合理期望应尽的判断、注意、审慎和行动水平。在安全领域，指采取一切合理措施预防伤害。它是针对过失指控的辩护理由。
过失Negligence☆ Failure to exercise the care that a reasonably prudent person would exercise in similar circumstances. Four elements: (1) duty of care existed, (2) duty was breached, (3) breach caused the injury (causation), (4) actual damages resulted. 未能尽到一个合理谨慎的人在类似情况下应尽的注意义务。四个要素：(1) 存在注意义务，(2) 义务被违反，(3) 违反导致伤害（因果关系），(4) 造成实际损害。
严格责任Strict Liability Liability without fault — a party is liable regardless of negligence (e.g., certain abnormally dangerous activities). No proof of fault needed. 无过错责任——不论是否存在过失，当事方均须负责（如某些异常危险活动）。无需证明过错。
替代责任Vicarious Liability An employer is liable for the acts of its employees performed within the scope of employment. 雇主对其雇员在雇佣范围内实施的行为承担责任。

OSHA违规分类 / OSHA Violation Classifications

类型 / Type定义 / Definition处罚背景 / Penalty Context
故意违规 / Willful Employer intentionally and knowingly commits a violation, or is aware a hazardous condition exists and makes no reasonable effort to eliminate it 雇主故意且明知地实施违规行为，或意识到存在危险条件但未做出合理努力消除之 Most serious; up to $161,323 per violation (2024); can carry criminal penalties if a worker death results 最严重；每项违规最高$161,323（2024年）；若导致工人死亡可追究刑事责任

违规分类测试技巧 / Classification Test Tip:
Test questions often pair the scenario with the classification: "condition with a substantial probability of death or serious harm, and the employer knew or should have known" = Serious. "Intentional and knowing" = Willful. "Would probably not cause death or serious harm but affects safety" = Other-than-serious. "Substantially similar violation on re-inspection" = Repeat. 考题常将场景与分类配对："存在死亡或严重伤害的重大可能性，且雇主知道或应该知道" = 严重。"故意且明知" = 故意。"可能不会导致死亡或严重伤害但影响安全" = 非严重。"复检时发现实质性相似违规" = 重复。

8.9 商业机密、保密与举报人保护 / Trade Secrets, Confidentiality & Whistleblower Protection

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1910.1200(i) · OSH Act §11(c) · Yates Ch.2 · BCSP Code of Ethics

Safety professionals frequently handle sensitive information. The CSP must understand the boundaries of confidentiality, trade secrets, and whistleblower protections under the OSH Act. 安全专业人员经常处理敏感信息。CSP必须理解《职业安全卫生法》下保密、商业机密和举报人保护的边界。

商业机密 / Trade Secrets

  • OSHA's access: OSHA may require employers to reveal trade secrets and confidential business information during inspections; this information is protected from public disclosure under 18 U.S.C. 1905 (criminal penalties for unauthorized disclosure). OSHA的访问权：OSHA可要求雇主在检查期间透露商业机密和机密商业信息；该信息依据18 U.S.C. 1905（未经授权披露的刑事处罚）免于公开披露。
  • Chemical identity: OSHA Hazard Communication allows withholding specific chemical identities as trade secrets ONLY if: (a) the identity is not disclosed on the SDS, (b) the claim is valid, and (c) the identity is disclosed to health professionals in emergencies (29 CFR 1910.1200(i)). 化学物质身份：OSHA危险沟通允许仅在以下条件下将特定化学物质身份作为商业机密保留：(a) 该身份未在SDS上披露，(b) 主张有效，且(c) 紧急情况下向医疗专业人员披露（29 CFR 1910.1200(i)）。
  • Trade secret does NOT override employee/OSHA safety rights: An employer cannot refuse to disclose information needed for safety protection by invoking trade secrets. 商业机密不凌驾于员工/OSHA安全权利之上：雇主不能以商业机密为由拒绝披露安全防护所需的信息。

举报人保护 / Whistleblower Protection

法律 / Law保护内容 / Protection
OSH Act 第11(c)条 / Section 11(c) Prohibits retaliation against employees who report safety/health hazards, file complaints, or participate in OSHA proceedings. Employees have 30 days to file a retaliation complaint.禁止对报告安全/健康危害、提出投诉或参与OSHA程序的员工进行报复。员工有30天时间提出报复投诉。
其他举报人法规 / Other Whistleblower Statutes OSHA administers whistleblower provisions of 20+ statutes (e.g., Sarbanes-Oxley, the Consumer Product Safety Act) covering financial, transportation, nuclear, and environmental whistleblowing.OSHA管理20多项法规的举报人条款（如萨班斯-奥克斯利法案、消费品安全法），涵盖金融、运输、核能和环境举报。

专业保密 / Professional Confidentiality

  • The BCSP Code of Ethics requires CSPs to maintain confidentiality of privileged information (e.g., client processes, proprietary data). BCSP道德准则要求CSP对保密信息（如客户工艺、专有数据）保密。
  • Confidentiality must NOT prevent disclosure required to protect people or comply with law — safety of people remains paramount. 保密不得妨碍为保护人员或遵守法律所需的披露——人员安全仍为最高优先。
  • Recognize conflicts of interest: e.g., a CSP should not accept compensation from two parties with conflicting interests on the same matter without disclosure. 识别利益冲突：如CSP未经披露不得在同一事项上接受利益冲突双方的报酬。
CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
Three rules to remember: (1) Trade secrets must be disclosed to OSHA and to health professionals in emergencies. (2) The OSH Act's anti-retaliation provision (Section 11(c)) has a 30-day filing deadline for the employee. (3) "Confidentiality" of client information never overrides the paramount duty to protect people and the environment. 记住三条规则：(1) 商业机密必须向OSHA和紧急情况下的医疗专业人员披露。(2) OSH法案的反报复条款（第11(c)条）规定员工有30天的投诉期限。(3) 对客户信息的"保密"绝不凌驾于保护人员和环境这一最高职责之上。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "An employee is fired for reporting a safety hazard. What is the relevant protection and deadline?"
答题要点 / Key point: OSH Act 第11(c)条禁止报复，员工30天内提出投诉。商业机密：向OSHA、紧急时向医疗专业人员披露；不得凌驾于员工/OSHA安全权利。保密不凌驾于保护人员与环境。利益冲突须披露。
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: "11(c) 30天" — OSH Act第11(c)条禁止报复、员工30天内投诉。商业机密"三必须"：向OSHA披露、紧急向医疗披露、不得凌驾安全权利。中文"秘密让位安全、保密不遮人命"。
