5.1 ISO 45001:2018 — 职业健康安全管理体系 · ISO 45001:2018 — Occupational Health & Safety Management System☆

📖 来源 / Sources: ISO 45001:2018 · Brauer Ch.35 · Yates Ch.2

ISO 45001:2018ISO 45001:2018☆ is the international standard for occupational health and safety (OH&S) management systems. It provides a framework for organizations to manage risks and improve OH&S performance. It replaced OHSAS 18001 and follows the Annex SL high-level structure common to all ISO management system standards (ISO 9001, ISO 14001, etc.). First published in March 2018, it is the world's first ISO standard for OH&S management systems. ISO 45001:2018是职业健康安全（OH&S）管理体系的国际标准。它为组织提供了管理风险和改善OH&S绩效的框架。它取代了OHSAS 18001，并遵循所有ISO管理体系标准（ISO 9001、ISO 14001等）通用的Annex SL高层结构。2018年3月首次发布，是全球首个OH&S管理体系ISO标准。

从OHSAS 18001到ISO 45001的演变 / Evolution from OHSAS 18001 to ISO 45001

BS 8800 (1996) → OHSAS 18001 (1999/2007) → ISO 45001:2018
  • Key changes: ISO 45001 adds "Context of the Organization" (Clause 4) and strengthens leadership (Clause 5) with worker consultation and participation. It shifts from a procedure-based approach (OHSAS 18001) to a process-based, risk-based approach. 关键变化：ISO 45001增加了"组织环境"（条款4），并通过员工协商与参与强化了领导力（条款5）。它从以程序为基础的方法（OHSAS 18001）转向以过程为基础、以风险为导向的方法。
  • Terminology change: "Preventive and corrective action" (OHSAS) became "nonconformity and corrective action" (Clause 10.2); "document control" became "documented information" (Clause 7.5). 术语变化："预防与纠正措施"（OHSAS）变为"不符合与纠正措施"（条款10.2）；"文件控制"变为"文件化信息"（条款7.5）。
  • Annex SL: A unified high-level structure, identical core text, and common terms shared by all ISO management system standards — enables easy integration of quality, environmental, and OH&S systems. Annex SL：所有ISO管理体系标准共享的统一高层结构、相同核心文本和通用术语——便于质量、环境和OH&S体系的整合。

ISO 45001中的PDCA循环 / The PDCA Cycle in ISO 45001

ISO 45001 is built on the 策划-实施-检查-改进Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA)☆ cycle of continuous improvement. PDCA is the universal problem-solving and improvement loop; in ISO 45001 the entire clause structure maps onto it. ISO 45001建立在持续改进的PDCA循环之上。PDCA是通用的解决问题与改进循环；在ISO 45001中，整个条款结构与之对应。

策划 / PLAN
Clauses 4–6: context, leadership, planning; establish objectives, identify hazards, assess risks, determine controls
条款4–6：组织环境、领导力、策划；建立目标，识别危害，评估风险，确定控制措施
实施 / DO
Clauses 7–8: support and operation; implement processes, provide resources, ensure competence & awareness
条款7–8：支持与运行；实施流程，提供资源，确保能力与意识
检查 / CHECK
Clause 9: performance evaluation; monitor, measure, evaluate performance, conduct audits, management review
条款9：绩效评价；监控、测量、评估绩效，开展审核，管理评审
改进 / ACT
Clause 10: improvement; take corrective actions, investigate incidents, continually improve, update the system
条款10：改进；采取纠正措施，调查事故，持续改进，更新体系

ISO 45001:2018条款（Annex SL结构）/ ISO 45001:2018 Clauses (Annex SL Structure)

Clause 4 组织环境 / Context of the Organization Understand internal/external issues, needs of workers & interested parties, define scope of OH&S management system 理解内外部问题、员工与相关方需求，界定OH&S管理体系范围
Clause 5 领导力与员工参与 / Leadership & Worker Participation Top management accountability, OH&S policy, roles & responsibilities, consultation & participation of workers 最高管理层问责，OH&S方针，角色与职责，员工协商与参与
Clause 6 策划 / Planning Hazard identification, risk assessment, legal requirements, OH&S objectives & planning to achieve them 危害识别，风险评估，法律法规要求，OH&S目标与实现目标的策划

CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
Remember the clause structure: C-L-P-S-O-P-I = "Context, Leadership, Planning, Support, Operation, Performance, Improvement." Clause 9 (Performance Evaluation) covers internal audits and management review — two heavily tested areas. Management of change sits in Clause 8 (Operation); incident investigation and corrective action sit in Clause 10 (Improvement). 记住条款结构：C-L-P-S-O-P-I = "组织环境、领导力、策划、支持、运行、绩效评价、改进"。条款9（绩效评价）涵盖内部审核和管理评审——两个高频考查领域。变更管理在条款8（运行）；事故调查和纠正措施在条款10（改进）。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "In which ISO 45001 clause is Management of Change addressed?"
答题要点 / Key point: 条款框架：8.1.2变更管理（Operation）；9.2内部审核、9.3管理评审（Performance）；10.2不符合与纠正措施、10.3持续改进（Improvement）。高管承诺与工人参与（5条）常考。PDCA隐含在条款结构里。核心：Clause 8=Do、Clause 9=Check、Clause 10=Act。
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: C-L-P-S-O-P-I = Context-Leadership-Planning-Support-Operation-Performance-Improvement。中文"环-领-划-支-运-评-改"。8=运(含变更管理)、9=评(审核+管理评审)、10=改(事故调查+纠正)。

5.2 ANSI/ASSP Z10.0 — 美国OH&S管理体系标准 · ANSI/ASSP Z10.0 — OH&S Management System (U.S. Standard)☆

📖 来源 / Sources: ANSI/ASSP Z10.0 · Brauer Ch.35 · Yates Ch.2

ANSI/ASSP Z10.0ANSI/ASSP Z10.0 is the American national standard for occupational health and safety management systems, developed by the American National Standards Institute (ANSI) and the American Society of Safety Professionals (ASSP). While ISO 45001 is international, Z10.0 is the primary U.S. consensus standard. Both share the PDCA framework, but Z10.0 has a stronger, explicit emphasis on the hierarchy of controls as the primary risk-reduction method. Z10.0 is a voluntary consensus standard — it is NOT a federal regulation. ANSI/ASSP Z10.0是美国职业健康安全管理体系的国家标准，由美国国家标准协会（ANSI）和美国安全专业人士协会（ASSP）制定。ISO 45001是国际标准，而Z10.0是美国主要的共识标准。两者都有PDCA框架，但Z10.0更明确地强调控制层级作为降低风险的主要方法。Z10.0是自愿性共识标准——不是联邦法规。

Both standards share the goal of continual improvement — not just compliance, but systematic reduction of workplace risks over time. The hierarchy of controls (elimination → substitution → engineering controls → administrative controls → PPE) is tested heavily; remember that elimination is the most effective and PPE is the least. 两项标准都有持续改进的共同目标——不仅是合规，而是系统性地减少工作场所风险。控制层级（消除→替代→工程控制→行政控制→PPE）是高频考点；记住消除最有效、PPE效果最差。

CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
Know which standard is international (ISO 45001) vs. U.S.-national (Z10.0). Z10.0's explicit, prescriptive use of the hierarchy of controls is its distinguishing feature. Expect questions pairing a control approach with its place in the hierarchy. 区分国际标准（ISO 45001）与美国国家标准（Z10.0）。Z10.0明确、规定性地使用控制层级是其标志性特征。预期会有将控制方法与层级位置配对考查的题目。

🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "Which OH&S management system standard is certifiable?"
答题要点 / Key point: ISO 45001可认证；Z10.0为美国自愿性标准（不可认证）。两者都要求管理评审、员工参与、持续改进；都内嵌控制层级（Z10.0尤为明确）。Z10.0区分"政策/P"与"计划/计划性改进"结构。高管的组织领导力是两大标准的共同核心。

5.3 ISO 19011 — 管理体系审核指南 · ISO 19011 — Guidelines for Auditing Management Systems☆

📖 来源 / Sources: ISO 19011:2018 · Yates Ch.2

ISO 19011ISO 19011 provides guidance on auditing management systems, including OH&S. It covers audit principles, auditor competence, and the audit process. It is NOT a requirements standard — it provides guidance on how to conduct audits. The current edition (ISO 19011:2018) applies to the entire ISO management system family (9001, 14001, 45001, etc.). ISO 19011为管理体系审核提供指导，包括OH&S。它涵盖审核原则、审核员能力和审核过程。它不是要求标准——它为如何进行审核提供指导。现行版本（ISO 19011:2018）适用于整个ISO管理体系家族（9001、14001、45001等）。

七项审核原则 / Seven Audit Principles

  1. Integrity / 诚信: Auditors must be honest, ethical, and fair. 审核员必须诚实、道德和公平。
  2. Fair Presentation / 公正表达: Audit findings and conclusions must be truthful and accurate, reporting significant obstacles encountered. 审核发现和结论必须真实准确，并报告遇到的重大障碍。
  3. Due Professional Care / 应有的职业素养: Auditors must exercise sound judgment and diligence. 审核员必须运用合理的判断和勤勉。
  4. Confidentiality / 保密: Audit information must be protected and not misused. 审核信息必须受到保护，不得被滥用。
  5. Independence / 独立性: Auditors must be impartial and free from bias/conflict of interest. Auditors should not audit their own work. 审核员必须公正，不受偏见/利益冲突影响。审核员不应审核自己的工作。
  6. Evidence-Based Approach / 基于证据的方法: Audit conclusions must be based on verifiable objective evidence (interviews, documents, observations). 审核结论必须基于可验证的客观证据（访谈、文件、观察）。
  7. Risk-Based Approach / 基于风险的方法: Audit planning and execution should consider risks and opportunities, focusing resources on higher-risk areas. 审核策划和执行应考虑风险和机遇，将资源集中在高风险领域。

审核过程（依据ISO 19011）/ Audit Process (Per ISO 19011)

1. 启动 / Initiation → 2. 准备 / Preparation → 3. 实施 / Conducting → 4. 报告 / Reporting → 5. 跟踪 / Follow-up
阶段 / Stage活动 / Activities
启动 / Initiation Establish audit objectives, scope, criteria; select audit team; confirm feasibility of audit; make initial contact with auditee 确定审核目标、范围、准则；选择审核团队；确认审核可行性；与受审核方初步联系
准备 / Preparation Review documents; develop audit plan; prepare checklists and working documents; assign auditor roles 审查文件；制定审核计划；准备检查表和工作文件；分配审核员角色
实施 / Conducting Opening meeting; collect evidence through interviews, observation, document review; document findings (conformities & nonconformities); closing meeting 首次会议；通过访谈、观察、文件审查收集证据；记录发现（符合与不符合）；末次会议
报告 / Reporting Prepare written audit report with findings, conclusions, and recommendations; distribute to management 编制书面审核报告，包含发现、结论和建议；分发至管理层
跟踪 / Follow-up Verify corrective actions are implemented and effective; close out audit findings; learn from the audit 验证纠正措施已实施且有效；关闭审核发现；从审核中学习

审核发现分类 / Audit Finding Classification

类型 / Type定义 / Definition
符合 / Conformity Objective evidence shows the audited requirement is met.客观证据表明受审要求已得到满足。
不符合 / Nonconformity Objective evidence shows a requirement is NOT met. May be classified as major (systemic, affects effectiveness) or minor (isolated, low impact).客观证据表明要求未得到满足。可分为严重不符合（系统性、影响有效性）或轻微不符合（孤立、影响小）。
改进机会 / Opportunity for Improvement (OFI) A potential improvement observed; NOT a nonconformity and requires no mandatory corrective action.观察到的潜在改进；不是不符合，无需强制纠正措施。

审核员能力 / Auditor Competence

Auditors must possess: 审核员必须具备：

  • Knowledge / 知识: Audit principles and techniques, management system standards, organizational context, applicable legal requirements 审核原则与技术、管理体系标准、组织环境、适用法律法规要求
  • Skills / 技能: Communication, interviewing, observation, analytical thinking, report writing 沟通、访谈、观察、分析思维、报告撰写
  • Personal Attributes / 个人素质: Ethical, open-minded, diplomatic, observant, perceptive, tenacious, decisive, self-reliant 道德、开放思维、有外交手腕、观察力强、敏锐、坚韧、果断、自主
  • Independence requirement: Auditors must NOT audit their own work — auditing your own department's programs creates a conflict of interest and violates the independence principle. 独立性要求：审核员不得审核自己的工作——审核自己部门的项目会产生利益冲突并违反独立性原则。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MEDIUM
典型问法 / Typical question: "What distinguishes a nonconformity from an opportunity for improvement?"
答题要点 / Key point: 不符合=客观证据表明要求未满足（重大=系统性/影响有效性；轻微=孤立/影响小）；OFI=潜在改进、不强制纠正。审核员能力三块：知识+技能+个人素质。独立性铁律：审核员不得审核自己的工作（利益冲突）。

审核类型选择指南 / Choosing an Audit Type

  • Internal (first-party): Routine self-check before external audits; cost-effective; may lack objectivity if done by people with vested interest in results. 内部（一方）：外部审核前的例行自查；经济高效；若由与结果有利害关系的人员执行可能缺乏客观性。
  • Second-party: Evaluate vendors/contractors before awarding or renewing contracts; ensures the supply chain meets safety expectations. 二方：在授予或续签合同前评估供应商/承包商；确保供应链符合安全期望。
  • Third-party: Certification audits by accredited bodies (surveillance and recertification cycles); OSHA and EPA regulatory inspections. 三方：由认可机构进行的认证审核（监督和再认证周期）；OSHA和EPA监管检查。
关键区别 / Key Distinction:
An audit is a systematic, independent, documented process for obtaining objective evidence. It is NOT the same as an inspection (physical walkthrough checking conditions) or an assessment (broader evaluation that may include judgment and qualitative analysis). Audits evaluate the management system; inspections evaluate physical conditions. 审核是一个系统化、独立、文件化的获取客观证据的过程。它与检查（现场巡视检查状况）或评估（可能包括判断和定性分析的更广泛评价）不同。审核评估管理体系；检查评估物理状况。

5.5 管理评审 / Management Review

📖 来源 / Sources: ISO 45001:2018 Clause 9.3 · ANSI/ASSP Z10.0 · Brauer Ch.35

管理评审Management Review is a formal, planned evaluation by top management of the OH&S management system's continuing suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness. It is required by both ISO 45001 (Clause 9.3) and Z10.0. Note the three criteria: suitability (still appropriate for the context), adequacy (sufficient in scope), effectiveness (achieving intended results). 管理评审是最高管理层对OH&S管理体系持续适宜性、充分性和有效性的正式、有计划的评价。ISO 45001（条款9.3）和Z10.0都要求进行管理评审。注意三个标准：适宜性（是否仍适用于环境）、充分性（范围是否足够）、有效性（是否达到预期结果）。

管理评审输入 / Management Review Inputs

  • Status of actions from previous management reviews 以往管理评审措施的落实状况
  • Changes in internal/external issues (context, interested parties, legal requirements) 内外部问题的变化（环境、相关方、法律法规要求）
  • OH&S performance data: incident statistics, near misses, audit results, monitoring results OH&S绩效数据：事故统计、未遂事件、审核结果、监测结果
  • Adequacy of resources (staffing, budget, equipment) 资源的充分性（人员配置、预算、设备）
  • Results of worker consultation and participation 员工协商与参与的结果
  • Risk and opportunity assessments 风险与机遇评估
  • Recommendations for improvement 改进建议

管理评审输出（决策与行动）/ Management Review Outputs (Decisions & Actions)

  • Conclusions on continuing suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness of the OH&S system 关于OH&S体系持续适宜性、充分性和有效性的结论
  • Decisions on improvement opportunities 关于改进机遇的决策
  • Decisions on any need for changes to the OH&S system 关于OH&S体系变更需求的决策
  • Resource needs (budget, personnel, technology) 资源需求（预算、人员、技术）
  • Actions when OH&S objectives have not been met OH&S目标未达成时的措施

CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
Management review is NOT the same as an audit. An audit checks conformity; management review evaluates strategic direction and resource allocation. Management review is top management's job — not the safety manager's job alone. The safety professional provides inputs; top management makes decisions. 管理评审与审核不同。审核检查符合性；管理评审评估战略方向和资源分配。管理评审是最高管理层的工作——不仅仅是安全管理者的工作。安全专业人员提供输入；最高管理层做出决策。

5.6 审核、检查与评估 — 关键区别 / Audit, Inspection, and Assessment — Key Differences

📖 来源 / Sources: ISO 19011:2018 · Brauer Ch.35
活动 / Activity关注点 / Focus方法 / Method结果 / Outcome
审核 / Audit Management system — policies, procedures, records 管理体系——方针、程序、记录 Systematic review of documents, interviews, verification of conformity to standards 系统化审查文件、访谈、验证标准符合性 Findings of conformity/nonconformity; opportunities for improvement 符合/不符合发现；改进机会
检查 / Inspection Physical conditions — equipment, workplace, practices 物理状况——设备、工作场所、实践 Walkthrough observation, checklists, measurements 巡视观察、检查表、测量 Identification of hazards, unsafe conditions, or regulatory violations 识别危害、不安全状况或违规
评估 / Assessment Broader evaluation — may combine audit + inspection + analysis 更广泛的评价——可结合审核+检查+分析 Comprehensive review including risk analysis, performance evaluation 综合审查，包括风险分析、绩效评价 Overall evaluation/judgment; may include prioritization and recommendations 总体评价/判断；可包括优先级排序和建议

A simple way to remember: Audit = checks the system (are we following our procedures?). Inspection = checks the conditions (is the workplace safe?). Assessment = evaluates overall status (how effective is our program?). 简单记忆法：审核=检查体系（我们是否在遵守程序？）。检查=检查状况（工作场所安全吗？）。评估=评价总体状态（我们的计划效果如何？）。

场景识别 / Scenario Recognition:
A worker observes a missing machine guard during a weekly walkthrough → this is an inspection finding. An auditor finds the organization lacks a documented LOTO procedure → this is an audit nonconformity. A consultant completes a full review of the program's strengths and gaps with recommendations → this is an assessment. 工人每周巡视时发现机器防护罩缺失 → 这是检查发现。审核员发现组织缺少文件化的LOTO程序 → 这是审核不符合项。顾问完成对项目优缺点和建议的全面审查 → 这是评估。

整合管理体系 (IMS) / Integrated Management System (IMS)

Because ISO 9001 (quality), ISO 14001 (environment), and ISO 45001 (OH&S) all follow the Annex SL high-level structure with identical clause numbering and core text, organizations increasingly combine them into a single 整合管理体系Integrated Management System (IMS)☆ with one set of policies, procedures, audits, and management reviews. 由于ISO 9001（质量）、ISO 14001（环境）和ISO 45001（OH&S）都遵循Annex SL高层结构，条款编号和核心文本相同，组织越来越多地将它们整合为一个整合管理体系（IMS），共用一套方针、程序、审核和管理评审。

  • Shared elements: Documented information control, internal audit (Clause 9.2), management review (Clause 9.3), corrective action (Clause 10.2), competence and awareness (Clause 7.2–7.3). 共享要素：文件化信息控制、内部审核（条款9.2）、管理评审（条款9.3）、纠正措施（条款10.2）、能力与意识（条款7.2-7.3）。

5.8 系统安全分析技术 · System Safety Analysis Techniques☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.36 · Brauer Ch.34 · Yates Ch.24

The CSP11 blueprint (Domain 2, item 6) explicitly names system safety analysis techniques: FTA, FMEA, Safety Case approach, and risk summation. FTA and FMEA are covered in other sections (incident investigation and PHA). This section focuses on the Safety Case approach and risk summation per the exam blueprint. CSP11蓝图（领域2，第6项）明确列出系统安全分析技术：FTA、FMEA、安全案例方法和风险汇总。FTA和FMEA在其他部分（事故调查和PHA）有覆盖。本节根据考试蓝图重点介绍安全案例方法和风险汇总。

安全案例方法 · Safety Case Approach

A 安全案例Safety Case☆ is a structured, documented argument that a system is acceptably safe for a given application in a given operating environment. Unlike a prescriptive checklist, it requires the operator to demonstrate — through evidence — that risks have been reduced to ALARP (As Low As Reasonably Practicable). 安全案例是一个结构化的文件化论证，证明系统在特定运行环境中对特定应用是可接受安全的。与规定性检查表不同，它要求操作者通过证据证明风险已降低至ALARP（最低合理可行水平）。

  • Origin: UK HSE — became mandatory for offshore installations after the Piper Alpha disaster (1988). Now common in nuclear, rail, and major hazard industries worldwide. 起源：英国HSE——Piper Alpha灾难（1988年）后成为海上装置的强制性要求。现已在全球核能、铁路和重大危害工业中普及。
  • Three pillars: (1) Hazard identification — what can go wrong? (2) Risk assessment — how likely and severe? (3) Risk management — risks reduced to ALARP? Each pillar needs objective evidence. 三大支柱：(1) 危害识别——什么可能出错？(2) 风险评估——概率和严重度？(3) 风险管理——风险降低至ALARP？每个支柱需要客观证据。
  • ALARP principle: Risk is reduced until the cost of further reduction is grossly disproportionate to the benefit gained. The cost/benefit test distinguishes ALARP from absolute "zero risk." ALARP原则：将风险降低到进一步降低的成本与所得收益严重不成比例为止。成本/收益检验将ALARP与绝对"零风险"区分开来。
  • Living document: Must be updated after major modifications, new knowledge, or incidents. 活性文件：重大改造、新知识或事故后必须更新。

风险汇总 · Risk Summation

风险汇总Risk summation is the aggregation of multiple individual hazards and their risks into a total risk profile. Individual hazards may each appear acceptable, but their combined risk may exceed acceptability thresholds. 风险汇总是将多个单独危害及其风险汇总为总风险轮廓。单独评估时每个危害可能都可接受，但其组合风险可能超过可接受性门槛。

  • Used in quantitative risk assessment (QRA), facility siting studies, and portfolio risk management. Supports decisions on whether cumulative risk is tolerable. 用于定量风险评估(QRA)、设施选址研究和组合风险管理。支持关于累积风险是否可容忍的决策。
  • Care must be taken to avoid double-counting shared consequences or correlated failure modes. 必须注意避免重复计算共享的后果或关联的失效模式。
  • Related techniques: FTA (fault tree, top-down Boolean logic) and FMEA (failure modes and effects, bottom-up inductive) complement the Safety Case and risk summation approaches. 相关技术：FTA（故障树，自顶向下布尔逻辑）和FMEA（失效模式与影响，自底向上归纳）对安全案例和风险汇总方法起补充作用。
