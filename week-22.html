22.1 领域5 — 关键EPA法规汇总 / Domain 5 — Key EPA Regulations Summary

📖 来源 / Sources: RCRA 40 CFR 261 · CERCLA · CAA · CWA · TSCA · EPCRA · Brauer Ch.27 (Hazardous Waste) · Yates Ch.34

The following table summarizes the major U.S. environmental regulations that are tested on the CSP exam. Understanding the primary purpose, year enacted, and key provisions of each regulation is essential. 下表总结了CSP考试中涉及的主要美国环境法规。理解每项法规的主要目的、颁布年份和关键条款至关重要。

法规 / Regulation 名称 / Name 年份 / Year 主要目的 / Primary Purpose 关键条款 / Key Provisions
RCRA Resource Conservation and Recovery Act / 资源保护和回收法 1976 Cradle-to-grave management of hazardous waste (current operations) / 危险废物从产生到处置的全过程管理（当前运营） Subtitle C (hazardous waste); Subtitle D (solid waste); Subtitle I (USTs). Generator categories: LQG, SQG, CESQG/VSQG. Manifest system. TSDF permitting.
CERCLA Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation, and Liability Act (Superfund) / 综合环境响应、赔偿和责任法（超级基金） 1980 Cleanup of abandoned/uncontrolled hazardous waste sites (past disposal) / 清理废弃/失控危险废物场地（历史处置） Retroactive, strict, joint and several liability. Four classes of PRPs. National Priorities List (NPL). Reportable Quantities (RQs) for releases. Hazard Ranking System (HRS).
CWA Clean Water Act / 清洁水法 1972 Protect surface water quality; regulate pollutant discharges / 保护地表水质；监管污染物排放 NPDES permits for point source discharges. Section 404 (dredge/fill). SPCC plans for oil storage >1,320 gal. TMDLs. Water quality standards.
CAA Clean Air Act / 清洁空气法 1970 (amended 1990) Protect air quality; regulate air emissions / 保护空气质量；监管大气排放 NAAQS (6 criteria pollutants — see list below). NESHAPs for HAPs (187 listed). Title V operating permits. NSR/PSD for new sources. SIPs.
TSCA Toxic Substances Control Act / 有毒物质控制法 1976 Regulate chemical substances before they enter commerce / 在化学品进入商业流通前进行监管 PMN for new chemicals. Existing chemicals inventory. Significant New Use Rules (SNURs). Asbestos, PCBs, lead-based paint regulations under TSCA.
EPCRA Emergency Planning and Community Right-to-Know Act / 应急规划和社区知情权法 1986 Community emergency planning and public access to chemical hazard information / 社区应急规划和公众获取化学品危害信息 Title III of SARA. Sections 302 (EHS notification), 304 (release reporting), 311/312 (Tier II inventory), 313 (TRI Form R). LEPCs and SERC. 10,000 lb threshold (or 500 lb for EHS).
FIFRA Federal Insecticide, Fungicide, and Rodenticide Act / 联邦杀虫剂、杀菌剂和灭鼠剂法 1947 (amended) Regulate pesticide registration, distribution, sale, and use / 监管农药的注册、分销、销售和使用 Pesticide labels carry force of law. General-use vs. restricted-use classification. Worker Protection Standard (WPS).
RCRA vs. CERCLA / RCRA与CERCLA对比:
RCRA = "cradle to grave" for CURRENT waste operations. CERCLA = cleanup of PAST/abandoned sites. Both establish liability frameworks but for different timeframes. CERCLA liability is retroactive, strict, joint and several. RCRA = 针对当前废物运营的"从摇篮到坟墓"管理。CERCLA = 清理过去/废弃的场地。两者都建立了责任框架，但针对不同的时间段。CERCLA责任具有追溯性、严格性、连带性。

概念A概念B关键区别
RCRA（1976）当前废物CERCLA（1980）历史场地RCRA = cradle-to-grave for current operations; CERCLA = cleanup of past/abandoned sites (retroactive, strict, joint & several).
CWA 水（NPDES）CAA 空气（NAAQS）CWA = surface water, point-source permits; CAA = air, 6 criteria pollutants, Title V permits.
六种标准污染物 / Six Criteria Pollutants (NAAQS — Clean Air Act):
CO (carbon monoxide 一氧化碳), Pb (lead 铅), NO₂ (nitrogen dioxide 二氧化氮), O₃ (ozone 臭氧), PM (particulate matter — PM₁₀ and PM₂.₅ 颗粒物), SO₂ (sulfur dioxide 二氧化硫). Memory: "CO-Pb-NO₂-O₃-PM-SO₂". Carbon dioxide (CO₂) is a greenhouse gas but is NOT one of the six criteria pollutants. CO（一氧化碳）、Pb（铅）、NO₂（二氧化氮）、O₃（臭氧）、PM（颗粒物——PM₁₀和PM₂.₅）、SO₂（二氧化硫）。记忆："CO-Pb-NO₂-O₃-PM-SO₂"。二氧化碳（CO₂）是温室气体，但不是六种标准污染物之一。
CERCLA 泄漏报告 —— NRC 通知规则 / CERCLA Release Reporting — NRC Notification:
CERCLA (Superfund) assigns a Reportable Quantity (RQ) to each hazardous substance. When a release equals or exceeds the RQ within any 24-hour period, the responsible party must immediately notify the National Response Center (NRC) (1-800-424-8802), the single federal point of contact. If the substance is also an Extremely Hazardous Substance (EHS) under EPCRA, EPCRA Section 304 additionally requires prompt notification to the State Emergency Response Commission (SERC) and the Local Emergency Planning Committee (LEPC). RQ is a mass threshold (typically in pounds) — compare the released mass (volume × density) against the RQ, not the volume alone. Notification delays are not permitted. CERCLA（超级基金）为每种危险物质规定了可报告量（RQ）。当在任意24小时内泄漏达到或超过RQ时，责任方必须立即通知国家响应中心（NRC）（1-800-424-8802），这是唯一的联邦联络点。如果该物质同时属于EPCRA下的极度危险物质（EHS），EPCRA第304条还要求及时通知州应急响应委员会（SERC）和地方应急规划委员会（LEPC）。RQ是质量阈值（通常以磅计）——应将泄漏质量（体积×密度）与RQ比较，而非仅比较体积。不允许拖延报告。
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: 年份：76-80-72-70-76-86：RCRA 1976、CERCLA 1980、CWA 1972、CAA 1970(1990修订)、TSCA 1976、EPCRA 1986。 / Key environmental statute years.

22.3 废物产生者分类 / Waste Generator Classification

📖 来源 / Sources: RCRA 40 CFR 261/262 · Brauer Ch.27 (Hazardous Waste) · Yates Ch.34

RCRA classifies hazardous waste generators into three categories based on the quantity of waste generated per calendar month. RCRA根据每个日历月产生的废物量将危险废物产生者分为三类。

产生者类别 / Generator Category 月限量 / Monthly Limit 累计时间限制 / Accumulation Time Limit 关键要求 / Key Requirements
LQG
Large Quantity Generator / 大量产生者		 ≥ 1,000 kg/mo hazardous waste
OR > 1 kg/mo acute hazardous waste		 90 days / 90天 EPA ID number, manifest, biennial report, contingency plan, training program, emergency coordinator. Full compliance.
SQG
Small Quantity Generator / 小量产生者		 100 – 1,000 kg/mo
AND ≤ 1 kg/mo acute hazardous		 180 days
(270 if > 200 mi to TSDF)		 EPA ID number, manifest, basic training. Reduced requirements compared to LQG. Emergency coordinator not required but recommended.
VSQG
Very Small Quantity Generator / 极少量产生者
(formerly CESQG)		 ≤ 100 kg/mo
AND ≤ 1 kg/mo acute hazardous		 No limit if ≤ 1,000 kg accumulated Must identify waste, ensure delivery to permitted facility. Minimal federal requirements but check state rules (may be stricter).
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: A facility generates 150 kg of hazardous waste per month plus <1 kg of acute waste. What is its generator classification? 产生者分类？
答题要点 / Key point: SQG (100-1,000 kg/mo). LQG = ≥1,000 kg OR >1 kg acute. VSQG = ≤100 kg. SQG accumulates 180 days (270 if TSDF >200 mi); LQG = 90 days. 记住100/1,000 kg阈值与累积时间。
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: 90-180-270：LQG累积90天；SQG 180天（TSDF>200英里则270天）。 / Generator accumulation times.

22.4 领域6 — 关键工业卫生公式 / Domain 6 — Key Industrial Hygiene Formulas

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1910.95 (Noise) · 29 CFR 1910.1000 (PELs) · Yates Ch.6 (IH Air Sampling) · Yates Ch.8 (Noise) · Brauer Ch.23 · Brauer Ch.24

The following formulas are essential for the CSP exam. You must be able to calculate TWA exposures, noise dose, ventilation flow rates, and NIOSH lifting limits. 以下公式对CSP考试至关重要。你必须能够计算TWA暴露、噪声剂量、通风流量和NIOSH提举限值。

公式 / Formula 应用 / Application
TWA = Σ(Ci × Ti) / Total Time Calculate 8-hour time-weighted average exposure. Ci = concentration during time interval i; Ti = duration of interval i. / 计算8小时时间加权平均暴露。Ci = 时间段i的浓度；Ti = 时间段i的持续时间。
Noise Dose % = 100 × (C1/T1 + C2/T2 + …) Noise dose where C = actual exposure time at a noise level and T = reference duration for that level. For OSHA (5 dB exchange): T = 8 / [2^((L-90)/5)].
TWA (dBA) = 16.61 × log10(D/100) + 90 Convert noise dose percentage to 8-hr TWA in dBA using OSHA 5-dB exchange rate. / 使用OSHA 5dB交换率将噪声剂量百分比转换为8小时TWA（dBA）。
RWL = LC × HM × VM × DM × AM × FM × CM NIOSH Lifting Equation — Recommended Weight Limit. LI = Load Weight / RWL. LI > 1 = increased risk; LI > 3 = significant risk. / NIOSH提举方程——推荐重量限值。LI = 负荷重量 / RWL。LI > 1 = 风险增加；LI > 3 = 显著风险。
Q = V × A Volumetric flow rate (ventilation). Q = flow rate (cfm or m³/s), V = velocity (fpm or m/s), A = cross-sectional area (ft² or m²). / 体积流量（通风）。Q = 流量，V = 流速，A = 截面积。
Dilution Ventilation: Q = (q × K × 10⁶) / (C - C₀) q = generation rate of contaminant; K = safety factor; C = target concentration (PEL/TLV); C₀ = incoming air concentration. / q = 污染物产生率；K = 安全系数；C = 目标浓度；C₀ = 进风浓度。
Mixture: Em = Σ(Ci/Li) Additive combined exposure for substances with similar target organ effects. Em > 1 = exceeds the limit. / 相似靶器官效应物质的加和综合暴露。Em > 1 = 超过限值。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: A worker is exposed to a solvent at 80 ppm for 4 hours and 30 ppm for 4 hours. What is the 8-hour TWA? 8小时TWA计算？
答题要点 / Key point: TWA = (80×4 + 30×4)/8 = 440/8 = 55 ppm. 必须掌握：TWA、噪声剂量、通风Q=VA、NIOSH提举RWL、混合物公式Em=Σ(Ci/Li)。

22.5 PEL vs. TLV vs. REL — 暴露限值对比 / Exposure Limits Comparison

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1910.1000 (PELs) · ACGIH TLVs · NIOSH RELs · Brauer Ch.24 (Chemicals) · Yates Ch.6

Understanding the differences between PELs, TLVs, and RELs is critical for the CSP exam. Each has a different issuing body, legal status, and update frequency. 理解PEL、TLV和REL之间的区别对CSP考试至关重要。它们各有不同的发布机构、法律地位和更新频率。

属性 / Attribute PEL TLV REL
全称 / Full NamePermissible Exposure Limit / 允许暴露限值Threshold Limit Value / 阈限值Recommended Exposure Limit / 推荐暴露限值
发布机构 / Issuing BodyOSHA (regulatory) / OSHA（监管机构）ACGIH (professional organization) / ACGIH（专业组织）NIOSH (research agency) / NIOSH（研究机构）
法律地位 / Legal StatusLegally enforceable / 具有法律强制力Guidelines — not legally enforceable / 指南——不具法律强制力Recommendations — not directly enforceable / 建议——不直接强制执行
更新频率 / Update FrequencyRarely updated (many date to 1970s) / 很少更新（许多可追溯到20世纪70年代）Updated annually / 每年更新Updated as research warrants / 根据研究需要更新
保守程度 / ConservatismLeast conservative (most permissive) / 最不保守（最宽松）More conservative than PELs / 比PEL更保守Generally the most conservative (most protective) / 通常最保守（最具保护性）

CSP 考试技巧 / CSP Exam Tip:
If asked what limit to use for a safety program design, TLVs are the best practice choice (ACGIH updates annually). If asked the legal minimum, PELs are the answer. If asked the most health-protective, RELs. PELs are often outdated but are the only legally enforceable limit. 如果问安全程序设计应使用哪个限值，TLV是最佳实践选择（ACGIH每年更新）。如果问法律最低要求，PEL是答案。如果问最具健康保护性的限值，选择REL。PEL通常已过时，但却是唯一具有法律强制力的限值。

22.6 领域7 — ADDIE + Kirkpatrick 整合 / Domain 7 — ADDIE + Kirkpatrick Integration

📖 来源 / Sources: ANSI/ASSP Z490.1 · Yates Ch.17 (Training) · Brauer Ch.32 (Procedures, Rules, and Training)

The ADDIE instructional design model and Kirkpatrick's evaluation levels work together to create effective safety training programs. ADDIE教学设计模型和Kirkpatrick评估层级共同作用，创建有效的安全培训项目。

ADDIE阶段 / ADDIE Phase 关键活动 / Key Activities Kirkpatrick关联 / Kirkpatrick Connection
分析 / AnalysisTNA (organizational, task, person); learner analysis; context/constraints / TNA（组织、任务、人员）；学习者分析；背景/约束Define what Level 3 and 4 results are desired — these drive the training need / 定义期望的3级和4级结果——这些驱动培训需求
设计 / DesignSMART objectives using Bloom's taxonomy; assessment strategy; content sequencing / 使用Bloom分类学的SMART目标；评估策略；内容排序Design Level 2 assessments (pre/post tests, skills checks); plan for Level 1 survey / 设计2级评估（前/后测、技能检查）；规划1级调查
开发 / DevelopmentCreate materials; pilot test; revise; finalize facilitator/participant guides / 创建材料；试点测试；修订；完成讲师/学员指南Validate that assessments actually measure against objectives (Level 2 alignment) / 验证评估确实针对目标进行测量（2级对齐）
实施 / ImplementationDeliver training; manage logistics; attendance records / 交付培训；管理后勤；出勤记录Collect Level 1 data (reaction/satisfaction) during and immediately after delivery / 在交付期间和交付后立即收集1级数据（反应/满意度）
评估 / EvaluationAssess effectiveness; measure against objectives; continuous improvement / 评估有效性；对照目标衡量；持续改进Measure Levels 1-4: satisfaction, learning gains, on-the-job behavior change, organizational results. Level 5 (ROI) if warranted. / 衡量1-4级：满意度、学习收益、工作行为改变、组织结果。如有必要可衡量5级（ROI）。

成人学习原则回顾 / Adult Learning Principles Recap

  • Knowles's 6 Assumptions: Need to Know, Self-Concept, Experience, Readiness, Orientation (problem-centered), Motivation (intrinsic) Knowles六项假设：需要知道、自我概念、经验、准备度、导向（以问题为中心）、动机（内在的）
  • Kolb's Cycle: CE → RO → AC → AE → (back to CE) Kolb循环：具体体验 → 反思观察 → 抽象概念化 → 主动实验 →（回到具体体验）
  • VARK Modalities: Visual, Auditory, Read/Write, Kinesthetic VARK模式：视觉、听觉、读写、动觉
  • Toolbox Talks: Short (~15 min), focused, single-topic safety meetings — ideal for adult learners' attention spans 工具箱谈话：简短（约15分钟）、专注、单一主题的安全会议——非常适合成人学习者的注意力持续时间
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: Which Kirkpatrick level corresponds to ADDIE's Evaluation phase measuring actual work-behavior change after training? 衡量行为改变的柯氏级别？
答题要点 / Key point: Level 3 (Behavior) — transfer of learning to the job. ADDIE Analysis defines desired Level 3/4 results; Evaluation measures Levels 1-4 (+ Level 5 ROI if warranted). 记住ADDIE与柯氏映射。

22.7 跨领域整合 / Cross-Domain Integration

📖 来源 / Sources: 综合 / Consolidated: Brauer Ch.34 (Risk) · Brauer Ch.35 (Safety Management) · Yates Ch.24 · Yates Ch.19

The CSP exam often tests your ability to connect concepts across domains. Understanding these connections is critical for the scenario-based questions. CSP考试经常测试你跨领域连接概念的能力。理解这些联系对场景题至关重要。

整合 / Integration 说明 / Explanation 示例 / Example
风险评估 (D3) → 培训需求 (D7) / Risk Assessment → Training Needs Risk assessments identify hazards and controls; training needs flow directly from these findings. Higher risk = more training priority. / 风险评估识别危害和控制措施；培训需求直接从这些发现中产生。风险越高 = 培训优先级越高。 A PHA (D3) identifies a chemical reaction hazard → operators need HAZWOPER awareness training (D7) on the specific process hazards. / PHA（D3）识别出化学反应危害 → 操作员需要针对特定工艺危害的HAZWOPER意识培训（D7）。
环境危害 (D5) → 职业健康 (D6) / Environmental Hazards → Occupational Health Environmental releases often create occupational exposures. The same chemical can be both an environmental and occupational health concern. / 环境释放通常会造成职业暴露。同一种化学品可能同时是环境和职业健康问题。 A solvent spill (D5 — CWA/CERCLA reporting) creates airborne concentrations that require IH monitoring (D6 — air sampling, PEL/TLV comparison) and respiratory protection (D2). / 溶剂泄漏（D5——CWA/CERCLA报告）产生空气浓度，需要IH监测（D6——空气采样、PEL/TLV比较）和呼吸防护（D2）。
应急规划 (D4) → 风险评估 (D3) / Emergency Planning → Risk Assessment Emergency plans should be based on identified risks. The worst-case and more-likely scenarios from risk assessments drive emergency preparedness. / 应急计划应基于已识别的风险。风险评估中最坏情况和更可能场景驱动应急准备。 A Bow-Tie analysis (D3) identifies the top event "chlorine release" → EAP (D4) must address evacuation routes, shelter-in-place procedures, and coordination with LEPC. / Bow-Tie分析（D3）识别出顶事件"氯气释放" → EAP（D4）必须涉及疏散路线、就地避难程序以及与LEPC的协调。
IH监测 (D6) → 培训 (D7) / IH Monitoring → Training Exposure monitoring results determine what training is needed (e.g., above Action Level triggers hearing conservation training). / 暴露监测结果决定需要什么培训（例如，超过行动水平触发听力保护培训）。 Noise monitoring shows TWA = 87 dBA (D6) → >85 dBA Action Level → hearing conservation training required annually (D7 per 1910.95). / 噪声监测显示TWA = 87 dBA（D6）→ >85 dBA行动水平 → 需要每年进行听力保护培训（D7，依据1910.95）。
工程控制 (D1) → 环境 (D5) / Engineering Controls → Environmental Engineering controls for occupational safety (ventilation, enclosures) also serve as environmental controls (emission reduction). / 职业安全的工程控制（通风、封闭）也可作为环境控制（减少排放）。 Local exhaust ventilation (D1/D6) captures welding fume at source, reducing both worker exposure (D6) and facility air emissions (D5 — CAA Title V). / 局部排风（D1/D6）在源头捕获焊接烟尘，减少工人暴露（D6）和设施空气排放（D5——CAA Title V）。
📝 模拟情景题 / Practice Scenario
※ 模拟情景题，根据考点编写，非历年真题 / Practice scenario based on exam topics, not a past real question
"A solvent spill (D5) creates airborne concentrations requiring IH monitoring and respiratory protection. Which cross-domain connections apply?"
A single event can cross domains. A solvent spill is first an environmental release (D5) governed by CWA/CERCLA reporting — an RQ exceedance requires notification of the National Response Center. The same release creates airborne concentrations that must be evaluated by industrial hygiene (D6) — air sampling compared against the PEL/TLV — and, if exposure limits cannot be met by engineering controls, respiratory protection (D2) under 1910.134. Emergency planning (D4) may also be triggered. The correct answer is the chain of connections, not a single domain.

单一事件可跨多个领域。溶剂泄漏首先是环境释放（D5），受CWA/CERCLA报告要求管辖——超过RQ必须通知国家响应中心（NRC）。同一泄漏会产生空气浓度，须由工业卫生（D6）评估——空气采样与PEL/TLV比较——若工程控制无法满足暴露限值，则需按1910.134使用呼吸防护（D2）。还可能需要应急计划（D4）。正确答案是这一连串的跨领域联系，而非单一领域。

1910.134 呼吸防护要求 / 29 CFR 1910.134 Respiratory Protection Requirements:
Where respirators are required, the employer must establish a written respiratory protection program (1910.134(c)) that includes: (1) procedures for selecting respirators based on the hazard and exposure level; (2) medical evaluation before fit testing or respirator use; (3) fit testing (qualitative or quantitative) — at initial assignment and at least annually thereafter, and whenever facial changes could affect the seal; (4) procedures for proper use in routine and reasonably foreseeable emergency situations; (5) cleaning, disinfection, storage, and maintenance; (6) training — at least annually on why the respirator is needed, its limitations, donning/doffing, and maintenance; and (7) program evaluation. Air-purifying respirators may not be used above IDLH or the maximum use concentration (MUC = APF × PEL); atmosphere-supplying respirators (SCBA/SAR) are required above IDLH. 在需要呼吸器的情况下，雇主必须建立书面呼吸防护计划（1910.134(c)），其中包括：(1) 根据危害和暴露水平选择呼吸器的程序；(2) 在适合性检验或使用呼吸器之前的医学评估；(3) 适合性检验（定性或定量）——初次上岗时以及此后至少每年一次，且面部变化可能影响密封时应重新检验；(4) 常规和可合理预见的紧急情况下的正确使用程序；(5) 清洁、消毒、储存和维护；(6) 培训——至少每年一次，内容涵盖为何需要呼吸器、其局限性、穿戴/脱卸和维护；以及(7) 计划评估。空气净化式呼吸器不得在超过IDLH或最大使用浓度（MUC = APF × PEL）时使用；超过IDLH必须使用供气式呼吸器（SCBA/SAR）。
ESG 定义 / ESG Defined:
E — Environmental（环境）: emissions, waste, energy, water, climate impact. S — Social（社会）: workforce health and safety (TRIR, DART), labor practices, diversity, equity, inclusion, community relations, human rights. G — Governance（治理）: board oversight, executive accountability, ethics, compliance, transparency, risk management. For safety professionals, worker health and safety metrics (TRIR/DART) are Social (S) indicators, while board-level safety oversight and executive accountability are Governance (G). E — 环境：排放、废物、能源、水、气候影响。S — 社会：员工健康与安全（TRIR、DART）、劳动实践、多元化、公平、包容、社区关系、人权。G — 治理：董事会监督、高管问责、道德、合规、透明度、风险管理。对安全专业人员而言，工人健康与安全指标（TRIR/DART）属于社会（S）指标，而董事会对安全的监督和高管问责属于治理（G）。
