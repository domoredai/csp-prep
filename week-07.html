7.1 事故调查的目的与基本概念 / Incident Investigation — Purpose & Basic Concepts

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.37 · Yates Ch.20

Incident investigation is a systematic process to identify the root causes of incidents so that effective corrective actions can prevent recurrence. The goal is NOT to assign blame, but to find and fix system weaknesses. 事故调查是一个系统化过程，旨在识别事故的根本原因，以便采取有效的纠正措施防止再发生。目标不是追究责任，而是发现并修复系统弱点。

术语定义 / Key Terminology

术语 / Term定义 / Definition
事故Incident☆ An unplanned, undesired event that results in (or has the potential to result in) injury, illness, or damage. "Incident" is the modern preferred term covering all severity levels.一个计划外、非预期的事件，导致（或可能导致）伤害、疾病或损失。现代更倾向用"事件"一词涵盖所有严重程度。
事故（狭义）Accident An incident that actually results in injury, illness, or damage. Some professionals avoid "accident" because it implies chance/unpreventable.实际导致伤害、疾病或损失的事件。有些专业人士避免用"事故"，因为该词暗示偶然/不可预防。
未遂事件Near Miss☆ An incident that could have resulted in injury or damage but did not, by chance or timely intervention.本可能导致伤害或损失但（因偶然或及时干预）并未发生的事件。
直接原因Immediate Cause The most obvious cause directly associated with the event — typically an unsafe act or unsafe condition (e.g., "worker removed guard").与事件最直接相关的明显原因——通常是不安全行为或不安全状况（如"工人取下防护罩"）。
基本原因Basic/Root Cause☆ The underlying system deficiency (management system, training, culture, design) that allowed the immediate cause to exist (e.g., "no LOTO procedure for this equipment").使直接原因得以存在的潜在系统缺陷（管理体系、培训、文化、设计）（如"该设备没有LOTO程序"）。

OSHA报告与记录要求 / OSHA Reporting vs. Recordkeeping

Do not confuse reporting with recording. Reporting means notifying OSHA directly, and it is time-bound: a fatality must be reported within 8 hours, and an in-patient hospitalization, amputation, or loss of an eye within 24 hours. Recording means entering the case on the OSHA 300 Log, which must be done within 7 calendar days of learning of the recordable case. The OSHA 300A Summary is a posted annual summary (February 1 – April 30), not a reporting form. Near-misses are neither reported nor recorded because no injury/illness occurred. 不要把报告（reporting）与记录（recording）混为一谈。报告指直接通知OSHA，且有严格时限：死亡须在8小时内报告，住院治疗、截肢或眼球丧失须在24小时内报告。记录指在OSHA 300日志上登记，须在知悉可记录病例后7个日历日内完成。OSHA 300A摘要是年度张贴摘要（2月1日–4月30日），不是报告表格。未遂事件既不报告也不记录，因为未发生伤害/疾病。

为什么调查事故？/ Why Investigate?

  • Identify root causes so corrective actions prevent recurrence (primary purpose). 识别根本原因，使纠正措施防止再发生（主要目的）。
  • Comply with legal/regulatory obligations (OSHA, internal policy). 满足法律/法规义务（OSHA、内部政策）。
  • Gather facts for workers' compensation, insurance, and liability defense. 为工伤保险、保险和责任辩护收集事实。
  • Identify trends and systemic weaknesses across the organization. 识别整个组织的趋势和系统性弱点。
  • Demonstrate management commitment and build a reporting (just) culture. 展示管理层承诺并建立报告（公正）文化。
核心原则 / Core Principle:
Investigations focus on facts and system causes, not blame. A blame-oriented investigation suppresses reporting and hides root causes. The investigator asks "What failed in the system?" not "Who is guilty?" 调查聚焦于事实和系统原因，而非追究责任。以追责为导向的调查会抑制报告并掩盖根本原因。调查者问"系统哪里失效了？"而非"谁有罪？"
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "What is the primary purpose of an incident investigation?"
答题要点 / Key point: 目的=识别系统/根本原因并防止复发（不是追究个人责任）。调查聚焦事实与系统原因、非追责（blame-free）。追责导向会抑制报告、掩盖根因。注意区分近因(immediate cause)与根本原因(root cause)。
📝 模拟情景题 / Practice Scenario
※ 模拟情景题，根据考点编写，非历年真题 / Practice scenario based on exam topics, not a past real question
"After a serious injury, management demands to know which employee is at fault before the investigation begins. What is the BEST response?"
The purpose of an incident investigation is to prevent recurrence by finding facts and system causes — not to assign blame. Focusing on fault up front suppresses witness reporting and hides the systemic deficiencies that actually allowed the incident. The investigator should collect facts, determine immediate and root causes, and only then consider human factors and corrective actions. A blame culture is one of the greatest threats to a mature safety program and is a common distractor answer on the exam.

事故调查的目的是通过查明事实和系统原因来防止再次发生，而不是追究责任。一开始就聚焦追责会抑制证人报告并掩盖真正导致事故的系统缺陷。调查者应先收集事实、确定直接原因和根本原因，之后才考虑人的因素和纠正措施。追责文化是成熟安全计划的最大威胁之一，也是考试中常见的干扰项。 答题要点：看到“先追责/先处罚”即为错误选项——调查是fact-finding，不是fault-finding。

7.2 事故调查七步流程 / The Seven-Step Investigation Process

📖 来源 / Sources: Yates Ch.20 · Brauer Ch.37

The investigation must begin immediately after the incident. The first priority is always medical care and scene safety. Memorize the sequence: 调查必须在事故发生后立即开始。首要任务始终是医疗救助和现场安全。牢记顺序：

1. 保护现场 / Secure Scene → 2. 收集证据 / Collect Evidence → 3. 访谈证人 / Interview Witnesses → 4. 分析数据 / Analyze Data → 5. 确定根本原因 / Determine Root Causes → 6. 建议纠正措施 / Recommend Corrective Actions → 7. 跟踪 / Follow-Up
步骤 / Step活动 / Activities关键考虑 / Key Considerations
1. 保护现场 / Secure the Scene Provide medical care; eliminate immediate hazards; preserve evidence; control access; take photos/video 提供医疗救助；消除即时危害；保护证据；控制进入；拍照/录像 Scene preservation is critical — do NOT disturb evidence except to prevent further injury or property damage. Use barricade tape, lock doors, station a guard. 现场保护至关重要——除非为防止进一步伤害或财产损失，否则不要扰动证据。使用警戒带、锁门、派驻守卫。
2. 收集证据 / Collect Evidence Gather physical evidence (equipment, tools, PPE); take photographs, measurements, and sketches; collect documents (procedures, training records, maintenance logs, JSA) 收集实物证据（设备、工具、PPE）；拍照、测量和绘制草图；收集文件（程序、培训记录、维修日志、JSA） Evidence includes physical items, documents, and digital data (CCTV, access logs, SCADA data). Chain of custody matters for serious incidents. 证据包括实物、文件和数字数据（闭路电视、进出日志、SCADA数据）。对于严重事故，证据链很重要。
3. 访谈证人 / Interview Witnesses Interview injured worker(s), eyewitnesses, and those with relevant knowledge; conduct interviews promptly (memory fades); interview separately to avoid groupthink 访谈受伤员工、目击者和有相关知识的人员；及时进行访谈（记忆会消退）；分开访谈以避免从众思维 Use open-ended questions. Put the witness at ease. Avoid leading questions. Do NOT blame. Take notes or record (with consent). Re-interview if needed. 使用开放式问题。让证人感到放松。避免诱导性问题。不要责备。做笔记或录音（需征得同意）。必要时重新访谈。
4. 分析数据 / Analyze Data Use root cause analysis techniques (5 Whys, Fishbone, Barrier Analysis, Change Analysis); create timeline/sequence of events; distinguish direct causes from root causes 使用根源分析技术（5Why法、鱼骨图、屏障分析、变更分析）；制作时间线/事件序列；区分直接原因与根本原因 Multiple causes typically contribute. Look beyond the obvious direct cause. Ask "why" repeatedly until you reach system-level causes. 通常有多种原因共同作用。超越显而易见的直接原因。反复问"为什么"直到找到系统层面的原因。
5. 确定根本原因 / Determine Root Causes Identify underlying system deficiencies: inadequate training, poor hazard identification, missing procedures, design flaws, lack of supervision, production pressure 识别潜在的系统缺陷：培训不足、危害识别不良、程序缺失、设计缺陷、监督缺失、生产压力 Root causes are system-level, not person-level. "Worker error" is rarely the full answer — ask WHY the error was possible. 根本原因是系统层面的，而非个人层面的。"工人失误"很少是完整答案——要问为什么失误可能发生。
6. 建议纠正措施 / Recommend Corrective Actions Develop specific, measurable corrective actions addressing each root cause; apply hierarchy of controls; assign responsibility and due dates 制定针对每个根本原因的具体、可衡量的纠正措施；应用控制层级；分配责任和截止日期 Corrective actions should eliminate or reduce the hazard, not just retrain. Use SMART criteria: Specific, Measurable, Assignable, Realistic, Time-bound. 纠正措施应消除或减少危害，而不仅仅是重新培训。使用SMART标准：具体、可衡量、可分配、现实、有时限。
7. 跟踪 / Follow-Up Verify corrective actions are implemented; evaluate effectiveness (did the hazard actually decrease?); share lessons learned across the organization 验证纠正措施已实施；评估有效性（危害是否实际减少了？）；在整个组织内分享经验教训 Without follow-up, corrective actions often fade. Schedule verification at defined intervals. Close the loop. 没有跟踪，纠正措施往往会逐步消失。按规定的间隔安排验证。形成闭环。
CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
The investigation must begin immediately after the incident. The first priority is always medical care and scene safety. Only after the scene is secured do you begin evidence collection. Memorize the sequence: Secure → Collect → Interview → Analyze → Root Causes → Corrective Actions → Follow-Up. 调查必须在事故发生后立即开始。首要任务始终是医疗救助和现场安全。只有在现场安全得到保障后，才能开始收集证据。牢记顺序：保护→收集→访谈→分析→根本原因→纠正措施→跟踪。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "What is the FIRST step in an incident investigation?"
答题要点 / Key point: 第一步永远是医疗救助与现场安全（现场隔离保护），然后才收集证据。七步顺序：Secure→Collect→Interview→Analyze→Root Cause→Corrective→Follow-Up。纠正措施用SMART（具体/可衡量/可分配/现实/有时限），且按控制层级选（不止重新培训）。
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: "S-C-I-A-R-C-F" — Secure, Collect, Interview, Analyze, Root cause, Corrective, Follow-up。中文"护-采-访-析-根-纠-跟"。纠正措施用SMART（S-M-A-R-T）。

7.3 证据收集与证据链 / Evidence Collection & Chain of Custody

📖 来源 / Sources: Yates Ch.20

Evidence is the foundation of an investigation. Collect it systematically and document everything. 证据是调查的基础。系统收集并记录一切。

证据类型 / Types of Evidence

类型 / Type示例 / Examples
实物证据 / Physical Evidence Damaged equipment, tools, PPE, machine parts, materials, product samples受损设备、工具、PPE、机器零件、材料、产品样本
文件证据 / Documentary Evidence Procedures, work instructions, JSA/JHA, training records, maintenance logs, inspection reports, shift logs程序、作业指导书、JSA/JHA、培训记录、维修日志、检查报告、交接班日志
数字证据 / Digital Evidence CCTV footage, access-control logs, SCADA/PCS data, machine data logs, emails, photos闭路电视录像、门禁日志、SCADA/PCS数据、机器数据日志、电子邮件、照片
证人证词 / Testimonial Evidence Witness statements, interviews, expert opinions证人陈述、访谈、专家意见
测量数据 / Measurement Data Noise, chemical exposure, illumination, distances, temperatures, video timelines噪声、化学暴露、照度、距离、温度、视频时间线

证据链 / Chain of Custody

  • Definition: A documented, unbroken record of who collected, handled, transferred, and stored each piece of evidence, from collection to final disposition. 定义：一份文件化、不间断的记录，记载每项证据从收集到最终处置过程中由谁收集、处理、转移和保管。
  • Purpose: Ensures evidence is authentic and has not been altered, supporting legal and regulatory defensibility. 目的：确保证据真实且未被篡改，支持法律和法规层面的可辩护性。
  • Key elements: Unique identifier for each item, date/time of collection, collector's signature, every transfer documented, secure storage, original condition preservation. 关键要素：每件物品的唯一标识符、收集日期/时间、收集人签名、每次移交都记录、安全保管、保持原始状况。
  • Photograph the scene before moving anything; mark evidence locations on a sketch. 在移动任何物品前拍照记录现场；在草图上标记证据位置。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MEDIUM
典型问法 / Typical question: "What is the purpose of a chain of custody?"
答题要点 / Key point: 证据链=不间断记录每件证据由谁收集/处理/转移/保管，确保真实未被篡改（法律可辩护性）。证据类型五类：实物/文件/数字/证词/测量。铁律：移动任何物品前先拍照，草图标记位置。

7.4 证人访谈技巧 / Witness Interviewing Techniques

📖 来源 / Sources: Yates Ch.20

Witnesses are interviewed promptly, separately, and with open-ended questions. Interviews are conversations to gather facts, not interrogations. 证人应被及时、单独地访谈，使用开放式问题。访谈是收集事实的对话，而非审讯。

有效访谈原则 / Effective Interviewing Principles

原则 / Principle说明 / Explanation
及时 / Timeliness Interview as soon as possible — memories fade rapidly, and witnesses talk to each other.尽快访谈——记忆会迅速消退，证人之间会相互交流。
单独 / Separate Interview witnesses one at a time to avoid groupthink and contamination of accounts.逐一访谈以避免从众思维和陈述相互污染。
开放式问题 / Open-Ended Questions Use "What happened then?" not "Did he remove the guard?" Open questions elicit full narratives.用"接下来发生了什么？"而非"他是否取下了防护罩？"开放式问题引出完整叙述。
避免诱导 / Avoid Leading Do not suggest answers; leading questions bias the account.不要暗示答案；诱导性问题会歪曲陈述。
不责备 / No Blame Assure the witness the purpose is prevention, not punishment. Fear suppresses facts.向证人保证目的是预防而非惩罚。恐惧会压制事实。
记录 / Recording Take notes; record audio/video only with consent; have the witness review their statement.做笔记；仅在征得同意后录音/录像；请证人复核其陈述。

访谈流程 / Interview Flow

  1. Put them at ease: Introduce yourself, explain the purpose, and set a non-threatening tone. 让对方放松：自我介绍、说明目的、营造非威胁性氛围。
  2. Let them tell their story: Ask open-ended questions, listen actively, minimize interruptions. 让其讲述经过：提出开放式问题、积极倾听、尽量减少打断。
  3. Clarify and probe: Ask follow-up questions to clarify details and fill gaps in the timeline. 澄清与追问：提出追问以澄清细节并填补时间线空白。
  4. Close and document: Summarize key points, thank the witness, and document the account. 收尾与记录：总结要点、感谢证人、记录陈述。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MEDIUM
典型问法 / Typical question: "Which question is most appropriate when interviewing a witness?"
答题要点 / Key point: 六原则：及时（记忆快速消退）、单独（避免从众污染）、开放式问题（"发生了什么？"）、避免诱导、不责备（恐惧压制事实）、征得同意才录音+请证人复核。访谈四步：放松→讲述→澄清→收尾记录。
📝 模拟情景题 / Practice Scenario
※ 模拟情景题，根据考点编写，非历年真题 / Practice scenario based on exam topics, not a past real question
"An investigator asks a witness: 'Did the operator remove the guard before the accident?' Is this appropriate?"
Asking does the operator remove the guard? suggests the expected answer and primes the witness, biasing the account. Interviews should use open-ended questions (for example, please describe what you observed before, during, and after the event) and let the witness volunteer information. The goal is to collect facts, not to confirm a preconceived conclusion. Closed and leading questions should be reserved for clarifying specific factual details already volunteered.

问“操作员是不是拆掉了防护罩？”暗示了预期答案并引导证人，污染证词。访谈应使用开放式问题（如“请描述事故前后你看到的经过”），让证人主动提供信息。目的是收集事实，而非印证预设结论。封闭式和诱导式问题只应留作对已主动提供事实细节的澄清。 答题要点：诱导性问题＝污染证据；先开放式叙述，后封闭式澄清。

7.5 根源分析方法 / Root Cause Analysis Methods

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.36 · Yates Ch.20

5Why法 / 5 Whys

5Why法5 Whys☆ is a simple but powerful technique: ask "why?" repeatedly (typically five times) until you reach the root cause. Each answer becomes the basis for the next "why?" 5Why法是一种简单但强大的技术：反复问"为什么？"（通常五次），直到找到根本原因。每个答案都成为下一个"为什么"的基础。

Example: Worker slipped and fell. Why? Floor was wet. Why? Pipe leaked. Why? Gasket failed. Why? No preventive maintenance schedule. Why? Maintenance budget was cut. → Root cause: inadequate resource allocation for maintenance. 示例：工人滑倒摔伤。为什么？地面湿滑。为什么？管道泄漏。为什么？密封垫失效。为什么？没有预防性维护计划。为什么？维护预算被削减。→根本原因：维护资源分配不足。

  • "Five" is a guideline, not a fixed number — keep asking until you reach a system-level cause where a corrective action can prevent recurrence. "五"是指导数字而非固定数目——一直问到达到系统层面、可通过纠正措施防止再发生的原因为止。
  • Limitation: single-chain analysis may miss multiple contributing causes; best combined with other methods (e.g., Fishbone). 局限：单链条分析可能遗漏多个促成因素；最好与其他方法结合（如鱼骨图）。

鱼骨图 / 石川图 / Fishbone / Ishikawa Diagram

The 鱼骨图Fishbone Diagram☆ (also called Cause-and-Effect Diagram or Ishikawa Diagram) organizes potential causes into categories. The six classic categories (6Ms): 鱼骨图（也称因果图或石川图）将潜在原因组织为不同类别。六个经典类别（6M）包括：

类别 / Category要问的问题 / Questions to Ask
人员 / People Was training adequate? Fatigue? Communication? Experience level?培训是否充分？疲劳？沟通？经验水平？
设备 / Equipment Was equipment fit for purpose? Maintained? Guards functional? Design flaws?设备是否适合用途？是否维护？防护装置功能是否正常？设计缺陷？
材料 / Materials Were materials as specified? Substitutions? Contaminants? Handling requirements?材料是否符合规格？是否有替代品？污染物？处理要求？
方法 / Methods Were procedures clear, current, and followed? Was there a procedure? Workarounds?程序是否清晰、最新且被遵守？是否有程序？是否有变通做法？
环境 / Environment Lighting? Temperature? Noise? Housekeeping? Weather? Layout?照明？温度？噪声？整洁？天气？布局？
测量 / Measurement Were inspections adequate? Monitoring? Calibration? Performance metrics driving behavior?检查是否充分？监测？校准？绩效指标是否驱动行为？

其他根源分析方法 / Other RCA Methods

  • 屏障分析Barrier Analysis☆: Identifies the barriers (controls) that should have prevented the incident and analyzes why each barrier failed. Useful for process safety incidents. 识别本应防止事故发生的屏障（控制措施），并分析每个屏障为何失效。适用于工艺安全事故。
  • 变更分析Change Analysis☆: Compares the situation before and after a change (new equipment, procedure, personnel, material) to identify what changed and whether it contributed to the incident. Closely linked to Management of Change (MOC). 比较变更前后的情况（新设备、程序、人员、材料），以识别发生了什么变化以及它是否促成了事故。与变更管理(MOC)密切相关。
  • 故障树分析Fault Tree Analysis (FTA)☆: Top-down, deductive analysis using Boolean logic (AND/OR gates) to identify combinations of failures that lead to a top event. 自上而下的演绎分析，使用布尔逻辑（与/或门）识别导致顶事件的故障组合。
  • 事件因果图表法Events & Causal Factors Charting (ECFA): Builds a timeline of the event sequence and causal factors, mapping conditions that contributed. Widely used in process safety (e.g., after the BP Texas City incident). 构建事件序列和因果因素的时间线，标绘促成条件。广泛用于工艺安全（如BP德州城事故后）。
  • 因果树 / 前因分析MORT (Management Oversight & Risk Tree): A comprehensive, structured analysis using a pre-defined tree of management system elements to identify why controls were absent or inadequate. 使用预定义的管理体系要素树进行全面的结构化分析，以识别控制措施为何缺失或不足。

7.6 未遂事件报告与海因里希三角 / Near-Miss Reporting & Heinrich's Triangle

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.3 · Yates Ch.20

未遂事件报告Near-miss reporting☆ is the reporting of incidents that could have resulted in injury or damage but did not (by chance). A strong near-miss reporting culture is a hallmark of a mature safety program. 未遂事件报告是对本可能导致伤害或损失但（因偶然）并未发生的事件的报告。强大的未遂事件报告文化是成熟安全项目的标志。

海因里希三角 (1931) / Heinrich's Triangle (1931)

1 重大伤害/死亡 / 1 Major Injury / Fatality
29 轻微伤害 / 29 Minor Injuries
300 无伤害事故/财产损失 / 300 No-Injury Incidents / Property Damage
（底层：不安全行为与条件——数千）/ (Underlying: Unsafe Acts & Conditions — Thousands)

Key insight: For every major injury, there are many more minor injuries and near-misses. By investigating and correcting the causes of near-misses and minor incidents, you can prevent the major ones. Near-misses are free learning opportunities — no one was hurt, but the same causes could lead to a serious incident next time. 核心见解：每一起重大伤害背后，有更多轻微伤害和未遂事件。通过调查和纠正未遂事件和轻微事故的原因，可以预防重大事故。未遂事件是免费的学习机会——没有人受伤，但同样的原因下次可能导致严重事故。

现代观点 / Modern perspective:
Heinrich's exact ratios (1:29:300) are debated, but the principle is sound. Frank Bird updated the ratio to 1:10:30:600 (serious : minor : property damage : near-miss). The takeaway: addressing near-misses and minor incidents prevents serious ones. Organizations with high near-miss reporting rates typically have lower serious incident rates. Note that near-misses are NOT OSHA-recordable (no injury/illness occurred). 海因里希的精确比例（1:29:300）存在争议，但原则是正确的。弗兰克·伯德将比例更新为1:10:30:600（严重:轻微:财产损失:未遂事件）。要点：处理未遂事件和轻微事故可预防严重事故。未遂事件报告率高的组织通常严重事故率较低。注意：未遂事件不属于OSHA可记录范围（未发生伤害/疾病）。

未遂事件报告的好处 / Benefits of Near-Miss Reporting

  • Identifies hazards before they cause harm — free lessons. 在危害造成伤害前识别——免费教训。
  • High reporting volume provides statistically better data than rare serious incidents. 高报告量比罕见的严重事故提供统计上更好的数据。
  • Signals a "just culture" — workers report without fear of blame. 表明存在"公正文化"——工人不惧追责地报告。
  • Enables proactive corrective action before a serious event. 在严重事件发生前实现主动的纠正措施。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MEDIUM
典型问法 / Typical question: "What does a high near-miss reporting rate most likely indicate?"
答题要点 / Key point: 高未遂报告率=健康信号：表明公正文化（just culture）、工人无惧追责地报告；提供统计上更好的数据；实现事前主动纠正。海因里希三角（1:29:300）也需记忆。低报告率常是坏事（文化恐惧）而非"没事故"。

7.7 纠正措施与预防措施 / Corrective Actions vs. Preventive Actions

📖 来源 / Sources: ISO 45001:2018 Clause 10.2 · Brauer Ch.36
特性 / Feature纠正措施 / Corrective Action预防措施 / Preventive Action
定义 / Definition Action to eliminate the cause of a detected nonconformity or incident and prevent its RECURRENCE 消除已发现的不符合或事故原因并防止其再发生的措施 Action to eliminate the cause of a POTENTIAL nonconformity or incident and prevent its OCCURRENCE 消除潜在不符合或事故原因并防止其发生的措施
触发条件 / Trigger An incident or nonconformity has already occurred 事故或不符合已经发生 A potential problem is identified (risk assessment, near-miss trend, audit finding) 识别出潜在问题（风险评估、未遂事件趋势、审核发现）
关注点 / Focus Reactive — fix what went wrong 反应性——修复出了什么问题 Proactive — prevent what could go wrong 主动性——预防可能出问题的地方
示例 / Example After a fall, install guardrails where the fall occurred 发生坠落后，在坠落处安装护栏 During a risk assessment, identify unprotected edges and install guardrails before anyone falls 在风险评估中识别未防护边缘，在有人坠落前安装护栏
ISO 45001注意 / ISO 45001 Note:
In ISO 45001 Clause 10.2, the standard requires corrective action for nonconformities (reactive). The concept of "preventive action" as a separate clause was absorbed into risk-based planning (Clause 6.1) — addressing risks and opportunities BEFORE they become problems. This is the ISO 9001:2015/45001:2018 evolution. 在ISO 45001条款10.2中，标准要求对不符合采取纠正措施（反应性）。"预防措施"作为单独条款的概念已被吸收进基于风险的策划（条款6.1）——在问题发生前处理风险和机遇。这是ISO 9001:2015/45001:2018的演变。
⚖️ 易混淆对比 / Common Confusions:
Corrective Action 纠正措施Preventive Action 预防措施关键区别 / Key Difference
反应性：针对已发生的问题/不符合（事故后装护栏）主动性：在问题发生前消除潜在风险（风险评估时装护栏）纠正=修复已出问题的；预防=消除可能出问题的。ISO 45001中"预防措施"已并入基于风险的策划（条款6.1）
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MEDIUM
典型问法 / Typical question: "Which action is preventive rather than corrective?"
答题要点 / Key point: 纠正=反应（针对已有问题）；预防=主动（针对潜在风险）。ISO 45001：纠正措施在10.2；"预防"并入6.1基于风险的策划。示例：坠落后才装护栏=纠正；风险评估时先装护栏=预防。

7.8 变更管理 (MOC) / Management of Change (MOC)

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1910.119(l) · Brauer Ch.24 · Brauer Ch.36

变更管理Management of Change (MOC)☆ is a systematic process to evaluate and control risks associated with changes to facilities, operations, equipment, personnel, or procedures. In the context of incident investigation, MOC is critical because many incidents occur during periods of change. The CSP11 blueprint (Domain 2, item 5) specifically tests MOC in three phases: before, during, and after the change. 变更管理(MOC)是一个系统化过程，用于评估和控制与设施、操作、设备、人员或程序变更相关的风险。在事故调查中，MOC至关重要，因为许多事故发生在变更期间。CSP11蓝图（领域2，第5项）专门考查MOC的三个阶段：变更前、变更中和变更后。

MOC三阶段流程 / MOC Three-Phase Process

阶段 / Phase关键活动 / Key Activities常见失效 / Common Failures
变更前 (PRIOR) / 变更前 Define the change scope clearly; identify hazards and assess risks; determine controls using hierarchy; obtain multi-discipline approval BEFORE implementation; update PSI and operating procedures provisionally; develop pre-startup safety review (PSSR) plan; communicate pending change to affected workers and train them. 明确定义变更范围；识别危害并评估风险；使用控制层级确定控制措施；在实施前获得多专业审批；临时更新PSI和操作规程；制定启动前安全审查(PSSR)计划；向受影响员工传达即将发生的变更并进行培训。 Rushing approval under production pressure; underestimating scope ("just a small change"); skipping hazard review for "minor" changes; failure to involve all disciplines. 因生产压力仓促审批；低估范围（"只是小改动"）；对"微小"变更跳过危害审查；未能让所有专业参与。
变更中 (DURING) / 变更中 Execute the change according to approved plan and procedures; maintain continuous supervision; verify that actual installation matches design specifications; conduct ongoing monitoring for unexpected conditions; update affected personnel in real time; manage concurrent operations safely; document as-built conditions. 按照已批准的计划和程序执行变更；保持持续监督；验证实际安装符合设计规范；持续监控意外情况；实时更新受影响人员；安全管理同步操作；记录竣工状况。 Deviation from approved plan without re-approval; field modifications ("we'll just do this differently"); loss of communication between shifts; unmanaged interactions with ongoing operations. 未经重新批准偏离已批准计划；现场修改（"我们就这么做不同的"）；班次间沟通丢失；与正在进行的操作的不受控交互。
变更后 (AFTER) / 变更后 Complete PSSR before introducing hazardous materials/energy; verify all training is complete; update all affected documents permanently (PSI, P&IDs, operating procedures, training materials, maintenance schedules); verify controls are effective under actual operating conditions; conduct post-implementation review after a defined period; close out MOC with formal sign-off; retain records. 在引入危险物料/能量之前完成PSSR；验证所有培训已完成；永久更新所有受影响的文件（PSI、P&ID、操作规程、培训材料、维护计划）；验证控制措施在实际操作条件下有效；在规定的期限后进行实施后审查；以正式签核关闭MOC；保留记录。 Skipping PSSR because "it's already running"; failure to update documentation (creating future hazard from outdated info); declaring success before verifying sustained safe operation; loss of MOC records over time. 跳过PSSR因为"它已经在运行"；未能更新文件（因信息过时造成未来危害）；在验证持续安全运行之前宣布成功；MOC记录随时间长丢失。
  • Changes requiring MOC / 需要MOC的变更: New equipment, modified processes, different materials/chemicals, revised procedures, organizational restructuring, new contractors, facility modifications 新设备、修改的工艺、不同的材料/化学品、修订的程序、组织重组、新承包商、设施改造
  • MOC process / MOC流程: (1) Define the change, (2) Assess hazards and risks, (3) Determine controls, (4) Obtain approvals before implementation, (5) Communicate and train affected workers, (6) Implement, (7) Verify controls are effective post-implementation, (8) Update documentation (1) 定义变更，(2) 评估危害和风险，(3) 确定控制措施，(4) 实施前获得批准，(5) 沟通并培训受影响员工，(6) 实施，(7) 实施后验证控制措施有效，(8) 更新文件
  • OSHA PSM standard (29 CFR 1910.119(l)) requires MOC for covered processes OSHA PSM标准（29 CFR 1910.119(l)）要求对适用工艺进行MOC
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 常考 MEDIUM
典型问法 / Typical question: "What must be completed before a change is implemented?"
答题要点 / Key point: MOC八步：定义→评估危害→定控制→实施前批准→沟通培训→实施→验证有效→更新文件。变更后引入危险物料前须完成PSSR。PSM工艺强制（1910.119(l)）；同类替换可豁免。三阶段陷阱：实施前仓促审批/跳过危害审查、实施中偏离批准计划、实施后跳过PSSR/不更新文件。
📝 模拟情景题 / Practice Scenario
※ 模拟情景题，根据考点编写，非历年真题 / Practice scenario based on exam topics, not a past real question
"A maintenance supervisor plans to swap a gasket material on a PSM-covered process 'just for cost.' Must MOC apply?"
Replacement in kind means a part is replaced with one that is functionally and materially identical — same specifications, same materials of construction. Substituting a different gasket material is not a replacement in kind even if the dimensions are identical, so it triggers the Management of Change element of PSM under 29 CFR 1910.119(l). MOC requires a technical basis for the change, evaluation of safety and health impacts, authorization, updates to PSI and procedures, and — if the change is significant enough to alter process safety information — a pre-startup safety review before hazardous chemicals are introduced.

“同类替换”指用功能与材质完全相同（同规格、同构造材料）的备件替换。更换不同的垫片材料即使尺寸相同也不属于同类替换，因此触发29 CFR 1910.119(l)的PSM变更管理要素。MOC要求变更的技术依据、对安全与健康影响的评估、授权批准、更新PSI和程序，且若变更重大到需更改工艺安全信息，在引入危险化学品前还需进行启动前安全审查。 答题要点：维度相同不等于同类替换——材质变更是变更；“不兼容材料”是常见失效根因。

7.9 事故成本：直接与间接 / Incident Costs: Direct & Indirect

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.3 · Yates Ch.20

Understanding incident costs is essential for justifying corrective actions and safety investments to management. The total cost of an incident far exceeds the visible costs. 理解事故成本对于向管理层论证纠正措施和安全投资的合理性至关重要。事故总成本远超可见成本。

成本类型 / Cost Type示例 / Examples比例 / Ratio
直接成本 / Direct Costs Medical treatment, workers' compensation payments, disability payments, property damage repairs, insurance deductibles, OSHA fines, legal fees医疗救治、工伤保险赔付、伤残赔付、财产损失修复、保险免赔额、OSHA罚款、法律费用 Small portion of total — the "tip of the iceberg"占总成本的小部分——"冰山一角"
间接成本 / Indirect Costs Lost productivity (injured worker and coworkers), investigation time, hiring and training replacement workers, overtime to cover absences, equipment downtime, reduced morale, reputation damage, increased insurance premiums, administrative burden, lost business opportunities生产力损失（受伤工人和同事）、调查时间、替代工人的招聘与培训、弥补缺勤的加班、设备停机、士气下降、声誉受损、保险费上涨、行政管理负担、业务机会损失 3–10 times the direct costs (studies range from 4x to 10x)为直接成本的3–10倍（研究范围4x–10x）
总事故成本 / Total Incident Cost ≈ 直接成本 + 间接成本
典型估算：间接成本 = 直接成本 × 3 至 10
示例：直接成本$10,000 → 总成本可能为$40,000–$110,000（以4–10倍估算）
CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
The classic CSP exam figure: indirect costs are 3 to 10 times direct costs. When justifying a corrective action to management, present the TOTAL cost (direct + indirect) — this is the strongest argument for investment in prevention. CSP经典考点：间接成本为直接成本的3至10倍。向管理层论证纠正措施时，呈现总成本（直接+间接）——这是投资于预防的最有力论据。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "Indirect costs of an incident are approximately how many times the direct costs?"
答题要点 / Key point: 间接成本=直接成本的3–10倍（冰山一角：直接是看得见的、间接是水下的）。直接：医疗、工伤赔付、财产损失、罚款；间接：生产力损失、调查时间、士气、声誉。向管理层论证预防投资时用总成本（直接+间接）。
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: "冰山3-10倍" — 直接成本=冰山一角（看得见）；间接成本=冰山水下部分，是直接的3–10倍。中文"三到十倍、水下冰山"。向管理层算账：总成本=直接×(4~11)。
