1.1 控制层级 · Hierarchy of Controls☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.9 · Yates Ch.29

The 控制层级Hierarchy of Controls☆ is the fundamental framework for managing workplace hazards, ranked from most effective to least effective. OSHA（职业安全与健康管理局）OSHA, NIOSH（国家职业安全与健康研究所）NIOSH, and ANSI/ASSP Z10.0（职业健康安全管理体系标准）ANSI/ASSP Z10.0 all endorse this framework. 控制层级是管理工作场所危害的基本框架，按从最有效到最不有效的顺序排列。OSHA、NIOSH和ANSI/ASSP Z10.0均认可此框架。

各层级详解 / Each Level in Detail

层级 Level含义 What It Means示例 Examples
消除 / Elimination
☆ 加入词汇本		 Physically remove the hazard so it no longer exists. This is the ONLY level that completely removes risk. 物理移除危害，使其不复存在。这是唯一能完全消除风险的层级。 Automate a manual lifting task; remove a toxic chemical from the process; redesign so working at height is unnecessary. 自动化人工搬运任务；从工艺中移除有毒化学品；重新设计使高空作业不再必要。
替代 / Substitution
☆ 加入词汇本		 Replace a hazardous material, process, or equipment with a less hazardous alternative. The hazard still exists but with reduced severity or likelihood. 用危害较小的替代品替换危险的材料、工艺或设备。危害仍然存在，但严重性或可能性降低。 Replace solvent-based paint with water-based paint; use a less toxic cleaning chemical; replace manual heavy lifting with a vacuum lift assist. 用水性漆替代溶剂型漆；使用毒性较低的清洁剂；用真空辅助吊具替代人工搬运。
工程控制 / Engineering Controls
☆ 加入词汇本		 Design physical changes to the workplace that isolate workers from the hazard. Does NOT rely on worker behavior — it works passively. 设计物理改造，将工人与危害隔离。不依赖工人行为——被动工作。 Machine guards, interlocks, 局部排风local exhaust ventilation (LEV)☆, sound enclosures, guardrails, trench shoring. 机械防护罩、联锁装置、局部排风（LEV）、隔音罩、护栏、沟槽支护。
行政控制 / Administrative Controls
☆ 加入词汇本		 Change work procedures, schedules, or policies to reduce exposure. RELIES on worker compliance and can fail when workers do not follow the rules. 改变工作程序、排班或政策以减少暴露。依赖工人遵守，工人不遵守规则即失效。 Job rotation to reduce ergonomic strain, safety training, warning signs, 上锁挂牌lockout/tagout (LOTO)☆ procedures, rest breaks, pre-task briefings. 岗位轮换以减轻人因工程压力、安全培训、警示标识、上锁挂牌程序、休息时间、作业前简报。
个人防护装备 / PPE
☆ 加入词汇本		 个人防护装备Personal Protective Equipment — the LAST line of defense. Protects only the individual wearing it; does NOT reduce the hazard at the source. Fails if not worn, worn improperly, or damaged. 最后一道防线。仅保护佩戴者个人；不会从源头减少危害。不佩戴、佩戴不当或损坏即失效。 Hard hats, safety glasses, gloves, respirators, hearing protection, steel-toe boots, 个人防坠系统fall arrest harnesses☆. 安全帽、护目镜、手套、呼吸器、听力防护、钢头靴、防坠安全带。
⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
When asked "What is the BEST control?", always work DOWN the hierarchy: Elimination > Substitution > Engineering > Administrative > PPE. PPE is only correct when higher-level controls are not feasible or are already in place. 当题目问"最佳控制措施是什么"，始终按层级从上到下选择：消除 > 替代 > 工程控制 > 行政控制 > PPE。只有当更高层级的控制不可行或已经到位时，PPE才是正确答案。

为什么控制层级很重要 / Why the Hierarchy Matters

  • Effectiveness decreases as you go down. Elimination and substitution address the hazard at its source; PPE only protects the worker and fails if not worn. 效果逐级递减。消除和替代从源头解决危害；PPE仅保护工人，且不佩戴则失效。
  • Reliability differs. 工程控制Engineering controls work passively (a guardrail works 24/7). Administrative controls require ongoing human compliance. 可靠性不同。工程控制是被动的（护栏24小时不间断工作）。行政控制需要持续的人为遵守。
  • OSHA expects employers to use higher-level controls first before resorting to PPE. Many OSHA standards explicitly require engineering controls before PPE (e.g., 29 CFR 1910.95 for noise, 1910.134 for respiratory protection). OSHA要求雇主优先使用高层级控制措施，然后才能依赖PPE。许多OSHA标准明确要求先采用工程控制再使用PPE（如29 CFR 1910.95噪声标准、1910.134呼吸防护标准）。
  • Multiple controls are often layered. The hierarchy is not "pick one" — a robust program uses defense-in-depth, applying several levels simultaneously. CSP questions may ask which ADDITIONAL control to add, not which single one to use. 常采用多层控制。控制层级不是"二选一"——健全的计划采用纵深防御，同时应用多个层级。CSP题目可能问要额外添加哪种控制，而不是只用哪一种。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "Which is the MOST effective control?" / "将X控制归入层级中的哪一级？"
答题要点 / Key point: Always answer DOWN the hierarchy — Elimination > Substitution > Engineering > Administrative > PPE. "BEST" always means the highest feasible level; PPE is correct only when higher levels are infeasible or already in place. Elimination is the ONLY level that completely removes risk.
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: ESEA-P — Elimination, Substitution, Engineering, Administrative, PPE — 中文"一消二代三工四管五护"：消除 → 替代 → 工程控制 → 行政控制 → 个人防护装备。记住"消替工行护"从上到下。

📝 模拟情景题 / Practice Scenario
※ 模拟情景题，根据考点编写，非历年真题 / Practice scenario based on exam topics, not a past real question
"A safety manager at a distribution center wants to reduce back injuries from manual palletizing. What is the BEST course of action?"
The BEST answer is the highest feasible level of the hierarchy. Automating the task removes the manual-lifting hazard entirely, so it is elimination — the most effective level, because the hazard no longer exists. A vacuum lift assist is an engineering control (a lower level), while training and rotation are administrative controls and back belts are PPE. Whenever a scenario asks for the BEST control, work down the hierarchy: Elimination > Substitution > Engineering > Administrative > PPE.

最佳答案是层级中最高的可行层级。自动化任务完全消除了人工搬运危害，属于消除——最有效的层级，因为危害不复存在。真空辅助吊具属于工程控制（较低层级），培训与轮岗属于行政控制，护腰带属于PPE。凡问"最佳"控制措施，始终按层级从上到下选择：消除 > 替代 > 工程控制 > 行政控制 > PPE。

1.2 消除与替代 · Elimination & Substitution in Depth

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.9 · Yates Ch.29

These top two levels are the only ones that address the hazard at the source. They are the most effective because they reduce or remove the hazard itself, not merely the exposure to it. 这最顶层两级是仅有的从源头解决危害的层级。它们最有效，因为它们减少或移除危害本身，而不只是减少暴露。

消除 / Elimination

  • Definition: Permanently removing the hazard so it can no longer cause harm. Example: automating a process so no worker is exposed; discontinuing a hazardous product line. 定义：永久移除危害，使其不再造成伤害。示例：自动化工艺使工人不再暴露；停产危险产品线。
  • Exam distinction: Elimination means the hazard is GONE. Substitution means the hazard is CHANGED to a lesser one. If a scenario says the hazard "no longer exists" → Elimination; if it says "replaced with a less hazardous version" → Substitution. 考试区分：消除意味着危害消失。替代意味着危害变为较小的危害。如果场景说危害"不再存在"→消除；如果说"替换为危害较小的版本"→替代。
  • Elimination is not always possible — the task may be essential (e.g., can't eliminate welding if the product must be welded). This is why the hierarchy is ordered; each level is used when the higher level is infeasible. 消除并非总是可行——任务可能是必需的（例如产品必须焊接则无法消除焊接）。这就是层级排序的原因；当更高层级不可行时使用下一层级。

替代 / Substitution

  • Definition: Replacing a hazardous material, process, or equipment with a less hazardous one. The hazard is reduced in severity or likelihood but NOT removed. 定义：用危害较小的材料、工艺或设备替换危险的。危害在严重性或可能性上降低，但并未移除。
  • Examples: Water-based paint instead of solvent-based; non-asbestos gaskets; low-VOC adhesives; less toxic metalworking fluid; smaller gauge electrical system where feasible. 示例：水性漆替代溶剂型漆；无石棉垫片；低VOC胶粘剂；毒性较低的金属加工液；可行的低压电气系统。
  • Key exam caution: Substitution may introduce new hazards. Example: replacing a flammable solvent with a more toxic-but-nonflammable one trades an explosion risk for a health risk. A proper risk assessment must evaluate the substitute. 考试提醒：替代可能引入新的危害。示例：用毒性更大但不燃的溶剂替代易燃溶剂，是用健康风险换取爆炸风险。必须对替代品进行适当的风险评估。
📋 实际案例 / Case Study:
A metal parts manufacturer replaced a chlorinated solvent degreaser (perchloroethylene, an OSHA-listed carcinogen) with an aqueous alkaline cleaner. The substitution eliminated the carcinogen exposure, reduced regulatory obligations (Hazard Communication, ventilation permits), and simplified waste disposal. However, a chemical compatibility review was required because the aqueous cleaner was incompatible with some aluminum alloys, causing corrosion failures that later triggered a Management of Change review. 一家金属零件制造商用碱性水基清洗剂替代氯化溶剂脱脂剂（四氯乙烯，OSHA列出的致癌物）。替代消除了致癌物暴露，减少了法规义务（危害沟通、通风许可），并简化了废物处理。但由于水基清洗剂与某些铝合金不相容，造成腐蚀失效，随后触发了变更管理审查——因此必须进行化学相容性审查。

Elimination 消除Substitution 替代关键区别 / Key Difference
危害被永久移除 / hazard permanently removed危害被换成危害较小的替代品 / hazard replaced by a lesser one消除=危害完全消失；替代=危害仍存在，仅降低严重性或可能性，且可能引入新危害
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "Replace solvent-based paint with water-based paint — is this elimination or substitution?"
答题要点 / Key point: 更换材料=替代（hazard remains, reduced）。消除需要危害完全不存在（如自动化取消人工搬运）。常考陷阱：替代引入新危害（用健康风险换火灾风险），替代品本身必须进行风险评估。
📝 模拟情景题 / Practice Scenario
※ 模拟情景题，根据考点编写，非历年真题 / Practice scenario based on exam topics, not a past real question
"A plant proposes replacing a highly flammable degreasing solvent with a less flammable but more toxic solvent. What should the safety professional require before the change is accepted?"
Substitution reduces but does not remove the hazard, and it can introduce new hazards — a less flammable but more toxic solvent swaps a safety (fire) hazard for a health (toxicity) hazard. The substitute itself must be risk-assessed (toxicity, exposure potential, compatibility) before it is accepted. This is still a valid substitution and still ranks above engineering, administrative, and PPE controls — but it is not automatically "safe" just because the fire risk is lower.

替代降低但不消除危害，且可能引入新危害——不易燃但毒性更大的溶剂是以安全（火灾）危害换取健康（毒性）危害。接受前必须对替代品本身进行风险评估（毒性、暴露可能性、相容性）。这仍是一种有效的替代，层级上仍高于工程、行政和PPE——但不能因为火灾风险降低就自动视为"安全"。

1.3 工程控制 · Engineering Controls in Depth

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.9 · 29 CFR 1910.212 · Yates Ch.29

Engineering controls are physical modifications to the workplace that isolate workers from hazards. Their defining characteristic is that they work passively — they do not depend on the worker to do anything. A guardrail protects a worker who is not even paying attention; a training program does not. 工程控制是对工作场所的物理改造，将工人与危害隔离。其决定性特征是被动工作——不依赖工人做任何事。护栏能保护一个根本没注意的工人；培训计划则不能。

常见工程控制类型 / Common Types

类型 / Type描述 / Description示例 / Examples
机器防护
Machine Guarding		 Physical barriers at the point of operation, power transmission, and moving parts. Required by 29 CFR 1910.212 (general industry). 在操作点、动力传输和运动部件处的物理屏障。29 CFR 1910.212（一般工业）要求。 Fixed guards, interlocked guards, presence-sensing devices (light curtains), two-hand trip controls, pull-back devices. 固定防护罩、联锁防护罩、光电保护装置（光幕）、双手控制装置、拉回装置。
通风与粉尘控制
Ventilation & Dust Control		 Capture or dilute airborne contaminants. Local exhaust ventilation (LEV) captures at the source; general (dilution) ventilation reduces concentrations. 捕集或稀释空气污染物。局部排风（LEV）在源头捕集；全面（稀释）通风降低浓度。 Welding fume extractors, laboratory fume hoods, canopy hoods, dust collectors, pressurization/depressurization systems. 焊接烟尘抽排器、实验室通风橱、伞形罩、除尘器、正压/负压系统。
噪声控制

Required before hearing protection per 1910.95. 在源头、传播路径或接收端降低噪声。依据1910.95，先于听力防护实施。 Sound enclosures, mufflers on exhausts, vibration isolation mounts, sound-absorbing panels, quieter equipment. 隔音罩、排气管消音器、隔振支架、吸音板、更安静的设备。
隔离与防护屏障

坠落防护作为工程控制 / Fall Protection as an Engineering Control

Fall protection is a standard application of engineering controls. Guardrail systems are passive engineering controls: a barrier that prevents a worker from reaching an unprotected edge without any worker action. By contrast, a personal fall arrest system (PFAS) — anchorage, full-body harness, and connector (lanyard or self-retracting lifeline) — is active protection that depends on the worker donning and using it correctly, and on a warning line + safety monitor arrangement, which is administrative. 坠落防护是工程控制的典型应用。护栏系统是被动的工程控制：屏障阻止工人接近未防护边缘，无需工人做任何动作。相比之下，个人防坠系统（PFAS）——锚固点、全身式安全带和连接器（系索或自缩式救生索）——是主动防护，依赖工人正确穿戴和使用；而警示线+安全监视员则属于行政控制。

  • Hierarchy of fall protection: guardrails (passive engineering) > safety nets > personal fall arrest systems (active) > warning lines / safety monitors (administrative). Choose the highest feasible level. 坠落防护层级：护栏（被动工程）> 安全网 > 个人防坠系统（主动）> 警示线/安全监视员（行政）。选择可行的最高层级。
  • Guardrail specs (29 CFR 1926.502): top rail height 42 in ± 3 in, must withstand a 200-lb force applied within 2 in of the top edge without deflecting below 39 in; midrail at mid-height; intermediate vertical members ≤ 19 in apart; toeboard ≥ 3.5 in high. 护栏规格（29 CFR 1926.502）：顶部横杆高42英寸±3英寸，须在顶部边缘2英寸内承受200磅力且受力时不偏斜至39英寸以下；中间横杆位于中间高度；中间垂直构件间距≤19英寸；踢脚板高≥3.5英寸。
  • PFAS specs: anchorage strength 5,000 lbs per attached employee (or a 2× safety-factor design with certification); maximum free-fall distance 6 ft; maximum deceleration distance 3.5 ft. Body belts are PROHIBITED for fall arrest. PFAS规格：锚固点强度为每名连接员工5,000磅（或经认证采用2倍安全系数设计）；最大自由坠落距离6英尺；最大减速距离3.5英尺。防坠禁止使用身体腰带。
  • Why guardrails are preferred: as a passive/engineering control, a guardrail protects 24/7 without depending on worker behavior — the same reason engineering controls outrank administrative controls and PPE in the general hierarchy. 为何首选护栏：作为被动/工程控制，护栏24小时不间断防护，不依赖工人行为——这正是通用控制层级中工程控制高于行政控制和PPE的原因。
⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
On the exam, an engineering control is always a physical/mechanical change: a guard, a barrier, ventilation, an interlock, a sound enclosure, a machine modification. If the answer describes a behavior, schedule, sign, or procedure, it is an administrative control — even if it sounds "engineering-ish." Remember: a light curtain is engineering; a training class is administrative. 考试中，工程控制始终是物理/机械改造：防护罩、屏障、通风、联锁、隔音罩、机器改装。如果答案描述的是行为、排班、标识或程序，则是行政控制——即使听起来"像工程"。记住：光幕是工程控制；培训课是行政控制。

📝 模拟情景题 / Practice Scenario
※ 模拟情景题，根据考点编写，非历年真题 / Practice scenario based on exam topics, not a past real question
"A fabrication shop wants to control welders' exposure to welding fume. Which of the following is an ENGINEERING control?"
A local exhaust ventilation (LEV) system captures fumes at the source through a physical modification to the workplace — that is an engineering control, and it works passively without depending on worker behavior. Respirators are PPE, rotation is administrative, and switching filler metal is substitution. Under 29 CFR 1910.134, feasible engineering controls must be used before respirators. If a control depends on worker behavior it is administrative, no matter how technical it sounds.

局部排风（LEV）系统通过对工作场所的物理改造在源头捕集烟尘——属于工程控制，且被动工作、不依赖工人行为。呼吸器是PPE，轮岗是行政控制，更换焊丝是替代。依据29 CFR 1910.134，必须先采用可行的工程控制，才允许使用呼吸器。凡依赖工人行为的控制都是行政控制，无论听起来多专业。

1.4 行政控制与PPE · Administrative Controls & PPE

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.9 · 29 CFR 1910.132 · Yates Ch.2

行政控制 / Administrative Controls

Administrative controls reduce exposure by changing how and when people work. They are effective only as long as workers consistently follow them. 行政控制通过改变人们工作方式和时间来减少暴露。只有当工人持续遵守时才有效。

  • Work practices / 作业实践: Safe work procedures, pre-task hazard briefings (JSA/JHA), safe lifting techniques, housekeeping. 安全工作程序、作业前危害简报（JSA/JHA）、安全提举技巧、5S现场管理。
  • Scheduling / 排班: Job rotation, limiting time on a task, scheduling hazardous work when fewer people are present, breaks to reduce fatigue. 岗位轮换、限制任务时间、在人员较少时安排危险作业、安排休息减少疲劳。
  • Warning systems / 警示系统: Warning signs, alarms, 上锁挂牌lockout/tagout☆, warning lines, safety monitors, buddy systems. 警示标识、报警器、上锁挂牌、警示线、安全监视员、结伴作业制度。
  • Training / 培训: Hazard communication, specific task training, supervisor enforcement. 危害沟通培训、特定任务培训、主管监督执行。
  • Medical surveillance / 医学监护: Audiometric testing in hearing conservation programs, biological monitoring, medical exams. 听力保护计划中的听力测试、生物监测、医学检查。

个人防护装备 PPE — 29 CFR 1910.132(d)

OSHA's PPE standard (Subpart I, 29 CFR 1910 Subpart I) is explicit about when PPE may be used. Employers must not default to PPE while higher-level controls are still feasible. OSHA的PPE标准（Subpart I，29 CFR 1910 Subpart I）明确规定了何时可以使用PPE。当更高层级控制仍可行时，雇主不得直接依赖PPE。

  • 1910.132(d) Hazard Assessment: The employer must conduct a written (certified) hazard assessment of the workplace to determine if hazards are present that require PPE, and certify in writing that the assessment has been performed. 1910.132(d)危害评估：雇主必须对工作场所进行书面的（认证的）危害评估，确定是否存在需要PPE的危害，并书面认证评估已完成。
  • Selection: PPE must be selected based on the specific hazards identified, and properly fitted. 选型：必须根据识别出的具体危害选择PPE，并适当贴合。
  • Free of charge: Employers must provide PPE at no cost to employees (with limited exceptions for ordinary items like steel-toe boots and prescription safety glasses). 免费提供：雇主必须免费向员工提供PPE（少数例外，如钢头鞋和处方安全眼镜等日常物品）。
  • Training: Employees must be trained on when PPE is necessary, what PPE is necessary, how to properly don/doff, limitations, and care/maintenance. 培训：员工必须接受培训，了解何时需要PPE、需要何种PPE、如何正确穿戴/脱下、局限性以及保养维护。
  • Exam point: PPE training must be documented and employees must demonstrate understanding. Retraining is required when workplace conditions change or when the PPE is not used correctly. 考试要点：PPE培训必须记录，员工必须展示理解。当工作场所条件变化或PPE使用不当时需要复训。
⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
The phrase "after an assessment of the workplace and the job" in 1910.132(d) is frequently tested. A common trap question states the employer bought PPE for all workers "without performing a hazard assessment" — the correct answer is that this violates 1910.132(d). PPE cannot be the first or only control; it must follow an assessment that higher controls are infeasible or insufficient. 1910.132(d)中"在对工作场所和岗位进行评估之后"的表述是高频考点。常见陷阱题：雇主"未进行危害评估"就为所有工人购买了PPE——正确答案是这违反了1910.132(d)。PPE不能是第一或唯一控制；必须在评估确认更高层级控制不可行或不足之后使用。

📝 模拟情景题 / Practice Scenario
※ 模拟情景题，根据考点编写，非历年真题 / Practice scenario based on exam topics, not a past real question
"An employer buys hard hats and safety glasses for every worker but never conducts a written hazard assessment. What is the safety professional's BEST recommendation?"
29 CFR 1910.132(d) requires the employer to conduct and certify in writing a hazard assessment of the workplace before selecting PPE. Purchasing PPE without that assessment violates the standard — and without it the employer cannot know whether the PPE type is appropriate, whether it fits, or whether higher-level controls are feasible. Raising PPE to a higher control level is not the fix either; the missing element is the documented assessment that must precede PPE selection.

29 CFR 1910.132(d)要求雇主在选择PPE之前对工作场所进行危害评估并书面认证。未评估就采购PPE违反标准——没有评估，雇主无法判断PPE类型是否恰当、是否贴合，也无法判断更高层级控制是否可行。把PPE直接换成更高层级控制也不是答案；缺失的是PPE选型之前必须完成的书面评估。

层级在单个标准中的应用：噪声 / The Hierarchy Applied: Noise (29 CFR 1910.95)

The noise standard is the clearest single-standard illustration of the hierarchy: 噪声标准是层级在单一标准中最清晰的示例：

  1. PEL = 90 dBA (8-hour TWA). When exposure exceeds the PEL, the employer MUST implement feasible engineering or administrative controls. This is not optional. PEL = 90 dBA（8小时TWA）。当暴露超过PEL时，雇主必须实施可行的工程或行政控制。这是强制性的。
  2. Action level = 85 dBA. At or above 85 dBA TWA, the employer must establish a Hearing Conservation Program (HCP): noise monitoring, audiometric testing, hearing protection made available, training. 行动水平 = 85 dBA。TWA达到或超过85 dBA时，雇主必须建立听力保护计划（HCP）：噪声监测、听力测试、提供听力防护、培训。
  3. Exchange rate = 5 dB (OSHA): every 5 dBA increase halves the allowed exposure time. 90 dBA→8h; 95 dBA→4h; 100 dBA→2h; 105 dBA→1h. 交换率 = 5 dB（OSHA）：每增加5 dBA，允许暴露时间减半。90 dBA→8小时；95 dBA→4小时；100 dBA→2小时；105 dBA→1小时。
噪声剂量与TWA计算 / Noise Dose & TWA Calculation (OSHA 1910.95)

剂量（%）/ Dose (%) = 100 × (C₁/T₁ + C₂/T₂ + … + Cₙ/Tₙ)
  其中 C = 实际暴露时间 / actual time at a level; T = 该水平允许时间 / allowed time at that level

8小时TWA = 90 + 16.61 × log₁₀(Dose% / 100)

示例 / Example: 工人以90 dBA暴露4小时，以95 dBA暴露4小时。
  Dose = 100 × (4/8 + 4/4) = 100 × (0.5 + 1.0) = 150%
  TWA = 90 + 16.61 × log₁₀(1.5) = 90 + 16.61 × 0.1761 ≈ 92.9 dBA
  由于超过90 dBA，需要工程/行政控制 / Since > 90 dBA, engineering/administrative controls are required.
📋 实际案例 / Case Study:
A metal stamping plant had 38 workers exposed to press noise of 98 dBA. Initially the program was "hearing plugs for everyone." An OSHA inspection cited the plant for failing to attempt engineering controls per 1910.95(b)(1). The plant then: (1) enclosed the presses with sound enclosures (reduced to 88 dBA), (2) installed vibration-isolation mounts, (3) rotated operators (administrative), and only then (4) maintained hearing protection as a supplement. After enclosure, only 6 workers remained above 85 dBA and required audiometric testing. 一家金属冲压厂有38名工人暴露于98 dBA的冲床噪声。最初方案是"给所有人发耳塞"。OSHA检查以未按1910.95(b)(1)尝试工程控制为由处罚了该厂。工厂随后：(1) 用隔音罩封闭冲床（降至88 dBA），(2) 安装隔振支架，(3) 操作员轮换（行政控制），最后才(4) 保留听力防护作为补充。封闭后仅6名工人仍高于85 dBA，需要听力测试。

典型问法 / Typical question: "At what dBA is a Hearing Conservation Program required? At what dBA are engineering controls mandatory?"
答题要点 / Key point: 死记四个数：85 dBA=行动水平→听力保护计划（监测、听力测试、提供听力防护）；90 dBA=PEL→强制工程/行政控制；交换率5 dB（95→4h，100→2h，105→1h）。呼吸防护（1910.134）：工程控制优先，工程不可行时才允许呼吸器。

1.6 预防性设计 · Prevention through Design (PtD)☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.1 (Designing to Achieve Safety) · Brauer Ch.9 · Yates Ch.29

预防性设计Prevention through Design (PtD), also called "安全设计Safety by Design," is the practice of addressing hazards during the design phase of facilities, processes, equipment, tools, and work organization — rather than retrofitting controls after the hazard already exists. It applies the hierarchy of controls earliest, when it is cheapest and most effective. 预防性设计（PtD），也称"安全设计"，是指在设施、工艺、设备、工具和工作组织的设计阶段就解决危害问题——而非在危害产生后再加装控制措施。它在最早的阶段应用控制层级，此时成本最低、效果最好。

PtD 核心原则 / Core PtD Principles

  1. Anticipate hazards early — during the design and planning phase, not after construction or implementation. 尽早预判危害——在设计和规划阶段，而不是在建造或实施之后。
  2. Apply the hierarchy of controls at the design stage — elimination and substitution are most feasible and cost-effective during design. 在设计阶段应用控制层级——消除和替代在设计阶段最可行、成本效益最高。
  3. Lifecycle thinking / 全生命周期思维 — consider safety during construction, operation, maintenance, renovation, and eventual decommissioning. 考虑建设、运营、维护、改造和最终退役全过程的安全。
  4. Engage cross-functional teams — involve safety professionals, engineers, operators, and maintenance staff in design reviews. 跨职能团队参与——让安全专业人员、工程师、操作员和维护人员参与设计评审。
  5. Document decisions / 记录决策 — retain the hazard-analysis and design-decision records so future modifications can be evaluated against the original safety basis. 保留危害分析和设计决策记录，以便未来的改造可对照原始安全依据进行评估。

💡 PtD 的商业案例 — "1-10-100 法则" / Business case for PtD — the "1-10-100 Rule":
The cost to correct a hazard increases exponentially as a project progresses. A safety fix that costs $1 during design might cost $10 during construction, $100 after commissioning, and $1,000+ after an incident. This is called the "safety cost escalation curve." 纠正危害的成本随项目推进呈指数级增长。设计阶段花费1美元的安全修复，在施工阶段可能需要10美元，投产之后需要100美元，事故发生之后则需要1000美元以上。这被称为"安全成本升级曲线"。
📋 实际案例 / Case Study:
A pharmaceutical plant specified — at the design stage — all roof access via a parapet wall with integrated anchor points, and all above-ground piping routed at floor level behind barriers. During commissioning, the design review identified that the HVAC maintenance walkway had a 7-ft drop on one side. Because the design was still on paper, adding a second guardrail cost $1,200 (the "$1 design cost"). Retrofitting after construction would have cost an estimated $18,000 in demolition and rework — a 15× multiplier, illustrating the 1-10-100 escalation. 一家制药厂在设计阶段就规定：所有屋顶通道设女儿墙并集成锚点，所有地上管道在楼层后屏障处布置。投产时设计审查发现HVAC维护走道一侧有7英尺落差。由于设计仍在图纸上，增加第二道护栏仅花费1,200美元（"1美元设计成本"）。若在施工后再改造，估计需18,000美元的拆除和返工——15倍乘数，印证了1-10-100的升级规律。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "Which statement best describes Prevention through Design?"
答题要点 / Key point: PtD=在设计阶段解决危害（主动/Proactive），而非危害产生后加装控制（改造/retrofit）。关键词：early, design phase, lifecycle thinking, cross-functional teams。与"事故后调查""采购PPE"区分。常考"1-10-100法则"：设计$1 → 施工$10 → 投产$100 → 事故后$1000+。
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: "1-10-100" — 设计阶段$1 / 施工$10 / 投产$100（事故后$1000+）。中文："设计一、施工十、投产百"——越晚改越贵，安全成本升级曲线（safety cost escalation curve）。

1.7 本质安全设计Inherently Safer Design (ISD)☆

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.9 · Yates Ch.29

Closely related to PtD, ISD eliminates or reduces hazards by changing what is in the process, not by adding protective layers. A hazard that does not exist cannot cause an accident. The four strategies spell the mnemonic M-S-M-S. 与PtD密切相关，ISD通过改变工艺中有什么来消除或减少危害，而非增加保护层。不存在的危害不会引发事故。四项策略的助记符为M-S-M-S。

策略 / Strategy别名 / Also Called含义 / Meaning示例 / Example
Minimize / 最小化 Intensification / 强化 Use smaller quantities of hazardous substances. 使用更少量的危险物质。 Reduce inventory of a toxic gas; buy chemicals in smaller containers; "just-in-time" delivery. 减少有毒气体库存；购买小容器化学品；"准时制"供应。
Substitute / 替代 — Replace hazardous materials with less hazardous ones. 用危害较小的材料替代危险材料。 Replace ammonia refrigerant with a less toxic refrigerant; water-based coatings. 用毒性较小的制冷剂替代氨制冷剂；水性涂料。
Moderate / 缓和 Attenuation / 弱化 Use less hazardous process conditions (lower temperature, lower pressure, lower concentration, dilute form). 使用危害较小的工艺条件（较低温度、较低压力、较低浓度、稀释形态）。 Store a chemical below its boiling point; use dilute acid rather than concentrated; operate at lower pressure. 将化学品储存在沸点以下；使用稀酸而非浓酸；在较低压力下操作。
Simplify / 简化 Error-tolerance / 容错 Design processes less prone to human error (fewer steps, clearer labeling, error-proofing / poka-yoke, forgiving designs). 设计不易发生人为错误的工艺（更少的步骤、更清晰的标签、防错设计、宽容性设计）。 Design incompatible connections so they cannot be connected; color-code and key different gas lines. 设计不相容的接口使其无法连接；对不同的气体管线进行颜色编码和钥匙互锁。
⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
Distinguish Substitute (ISD) from the control-hierarchy Substitution — they are the same concept; ISD is just its application in process design. More important: don't confuse Minimize (reduce quantity) with Moderate (reduce severity of conditions). "Lower temperature/pressure/concentration" = Moderate. "Less inventory" = Minimize. 区分替代（ISD）与控制层级的Substitution——它们是同一概念；ISD只是其在工艺设计中的应用。更重要的是：不要混淆最小化（减少数量）与缓和（降低条件严重性）。"降低温度/压力/浓度"=缓和。"减少库存"=最小化。

ISD 在设计评审中的应用 / ISD in Design Reviews

When evaluating a proposed design, the safety professional should ask ISD questions in order: 评估拟议设计时，安全专业人员应按顺序提问ISD问题：

  1. Can the hazardous material or operation be eliminated? 危险物料或操作能否消除？
  2. Can it be minimized in quantity? 能否在数量上最小化？
  3. Can a less hazardous substitute be used? 能否使用危害较小的替代品？
  4. Can conditions be moderated (lower T/P/concentration)? 能否缓和条件（降低温度/压力/浓度）？
  5. Can the design be simplified to reduce human-error potential? 能否简化设计以减少人为错误可能？
  6. Only after exhausting ISD: what active and passive protective layers (relief, interlocks, containment) are needed? 只有在穷尽ISD之后：需要哪些主动和被动的保护层（泄压、联锁、围护）？
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "Reducing chemical inventory from 5,000 to 500 gallons is which ISD strategy?"
答题要点 / Key point: ISD四策略M-S-M-S：Minimize(数量最小化) / Substitute(替代材料) / Moderate(缓和条件=降T/P/浓度) / Simplify(简化=防错)。口诀区分：数量→Minimize；温度压力浓度→Moderate；换材料→Substitute；减少人为错误→Simplify。
🧠 记忆口诀 / Mnemonic: M-S-M-S — Minimize, Substitute, Moderate, Simplify。中文"量料条错"："减量、换料、降条件、防错"。别与Substitution混淆——ISD中的Substitute就是控制层级里的替代在工艺设计中的应用。

1.8 运用层级选择控制措施 / Applying the Hierarchy: Selecting Controls

📖 来源 / Sources: Brauer Ch.9 · Yates Ch.29

控制措施选择的系统化方法 / A Systematic Approach to Control Selection

When a hazard is identified, the safety professional should apply this decision logic — which mirrors exactly how CSP scenario questions should be answered: 识别危害后，安全专业人员应应用以下决策逻辑——这与CSP情景题的作答逻辑完全一致：

  1. Identify the hazard and its energy source. 识别危害及其能量来源。
  2. Eliminate the hazard if possible (can the task be redesigned away?). 如有可能消除危害（任务能否通过重新设计取消？）。
  3. Substitute — a less hazardous material/process/equipment. 替代——使用危害较小的材料/工艺/设备。
  4. Engineering controls — isolate the worker from the hazard (guards, ventilation, enclosures). 工程控制——将工人与危害隔离（防护罩、通风、封闭）。
  5. Administrative controls — change procedures, rotation, training, signs. 行政控制——改变程序、轮换、培训、标识。
  6. PPE — only when higher levels are infeasible or as a supplement while engineering controls are installed. PPE——仅当更高层级不可行时，或作为工程控制安装期间的补充。

常见失误与考试陷阱 / Common Errors & Exam Traps

陷阱 / Trap正确理解 / Correct Understanding
Choosing PPE as the "first" answer to a hazard scenario. 面对危害场景第一反应选PPE。 PPE is the LAST line of defense. Unless the scenario states higher controls are infeasible, the best answer is a higher-level control. PPE是最后一道防线。除非场景说明更高层级控制不可行，否则最佳答案是更高层级的控制。
Confusing an administrative control (training, rotation, sign) with an engineering control. 把行政控制（培训、轮换、标识）误认为工程控制。 If no physical change to the workplace occurs, it is administrative. Look for the physical/mechanical modification. 如果工作场所没有物理改变，就是行政控制。寻找物理/机械改造。
Picking "elimination" when the scenario only replaces the material. 场景只是更换材料却选"消除"。 Replacement of material = substitution. Elimination requires the hazard to be gone entirely. 更换材料=替代。消除要求危害完全消失。
Forgetting that administrative controls still require PPE when exposure remains above limits. 忘记当暴露仍超限时，行政控制之后仍需PPE。 Layering is normal: engineering + administrative + PPE often coexist. Questions about "additional" control may expect a supplementary level. 分层是正常的：工程+行政+PPE常并存。问"额外"控制的题目可能期望补充一个层级。
📌 本节点睛 / Key Takeaway:
Hierarchy of Controls is the single most-tested concept in Domain 1. You must be able to: (1) rank controls, (2) classify an example into a level, (3) identify what is required by a specific OSHA standard before PPE is allowed, and (4) apply the decision logic to a scenario. Master this page and the associated OSHA threshold numbers before moving on. 控制层级是领域1中考得最多的概念。你必须能够：(1) 给控制排序，(2) 将示例归类到某一层级，(3) 识别特定OSHA标准在允许PPE之前的要求，(4) 将决策逻辑应用于情景。先掌握本页及相关OSHA阈值数值，再继续学习。
