3.1 电气安全 — NFPA 70E概述 · Electrical Safety — NFPA 70E Overview☆

📖 来源 / Sources: NFPA 70E · Brauer Ch.12

NFPA 70E：工作场所电气安全标准NFPA 70E: Standard for Electrical Safety in the Workplace☆ is the primary consensus standard for electrical safety, referenced by OSHA and widely tested on the CSP exam. It addresses electrical hazards including shock, arc flash, and arc blast. NFPA 70E是电气安全的主要共识标准，被OSHA引用并在CSP考试中广泛考查。它涉及电击、弧闪和弧爆等电气危害。

三种电气危害 / The Three Electrical Hazards

危害 / Hazard机制 / Mechanism主要后果 / Primary Consequence
Shock / 电击电击 Current passes through the body — severity depends on current magnitude, path, duration, and frequency电流通过人体——严重性取决于电流大小、路径、时长和频率 Ventricular fibrillation, burns, muscle contraction, death心室颤动、烧伤、肌肉收缩、死亡
Arc Flash / 弧闪弧闪 Extreme heat and light from an electrical fault that ionizes the air into a conducting arc电气故障产生的极热和强光使空气电离形成导电电弧 Severe burns (incident energy), ignition of clothing, eye damage严重烧伤（入射能量）、衣物点燃、眼睛损伤
Arc Blast / 弧爆弧爆 Rapid expansion of superheated gas creates a pressure wave过热气体的快速膨胀产生压力波 Blunt trauma, hearing damage, flying debris钝性创伤、听力损伤、飞溅碎片

合格人员与不合格人员 / Qualified vs. Unqualified Persons

合格人员 / Qualified Person不合格人员 / Unqualified Person
A person who has skills and knowledge related to the construction and operation of electrical equipment and installations, and has received safety training to identify and avoid the hazards involved. 具备与电气设备和装置的构造及操作相关的技能和知识，并接受过安全培训能够识别和避免相关危害的人员。 A person who is NOT a qualified person. Unqualified persons must not approach exposed energized conductors or circuit parts closer than the limited approach boundary unless accompanied by a qualified person. 不是合格人员的人。不合格人员不得接近裸露带电导体或电路部件超过限制接近边界，除非有合格人员陪同。

弧闪边界 (NFPA 70E) / Arc Flash Boundaries (NFPA 70E)

NFPA 70E establishes three approach boundaries for shock protection and one for arc flash protection. These boundaries determine who may approach, what PPE is required, and what precautions must be taken. NFPA 70E为电击防护设立了三个接近边界，为弧闪防护设立了一个边界。这些边界决定了谁可以接近、需要什么PPE以及必须采取哪些预防措施。

边界 / Boundary定义 / Definition谁可以进入？/ Who Can Cross?
限制接近边界Limited Approach Boundary☆ The distance from an exposed energized conductor or circuit part within which a shock hazard exists. Typically the outermost boundary. 距裸露带电导体或电路部件的距离，在此范围内存在电击危害。通常是最外层边界。 Qualified persons OR unqualified persons accompanied by a qualified person. Unqualified persons must be advised of the hazards. 合格人员或有合格人员陪同的不合格人员。不合格人员必须被告知相关危害。
限制区域边界Restricted Approach Boundary☆ The distance from an exposed energized conductor or circuit part within which there is an increased likelihood of electric shock due to arc-over combined with inadvertent movement. 距裸露带电导体或电路部件的距离，在此范围内由于电弧跨越加上无意移动，电击可能性增加。 Only qualified persons with an approved, documented work plan and appropriate PPE. No unqualified persons. 仅限具有经批准的书面工作计划和适当PPE的合格人员。无不合格人员。
禁止接近边界Prohibited Approach Boundary☆ The distance from an exposed energized conductor or circuit part within which work is considered the same as making direct contact with the live part. 距裸露带电导体或电路部件的距离，在此范围内的工作被视为与直接接触带电部件等同。 Only qualified persons with the same protection as if they were making direct contact with the live part. Requires a documented energized electrical work permit in many cases. 仅限具有如同直接接触带电部件般的保护的合格人员。多数情况下需要书面的带电电气工作许可证。
弧闪边界Arc Flash Boundary☆ The distance from a prospective arc source at which the incident energy equals 1.2 cal/cm² (the onset of a second-degree burn). This is a separate boundary from the shock boundaries. 距潜在电弧源的距离，在此处入射能量等于1.2 cal/cm²（二级烧伤的起始点）。这是独立于电击边界的单独边界。 Anyone crossing this boundary must wear arc-rated PPE appropriate for the calculated incident energy level. 进入此边界的任何人必须穿戴与计算的入射能量水平相适应的防弧PPE。

弧闪PPE类别 / Arc Flash PPE Categories

NFPA 70E uses PPE categories based on incident energy levels. The CSP exam expects familiarity with the concept and what the categories represent. NFPA 70E使用基于入射能量水平的PPE类别。CSP考试要求熟悉这一概念及其各类别代表的含义。

类别 / Category入射能量 / Incident Energy典型PPE / Typical PPE
类别 1 / Category 1 ≥ 1.2 cal/cm² (最低4 cal/cm²评级) Arc-rated shirt and pants or coverall, hard hat, safety glasses, leather gloves防弧衬衫和裤子或连体工作服、安全帽、护目镜、皮革手套
类别 2 / Category 2 ≥ 4 cal/cm² (最低8 cal/cm²评级) Category 1 + arc-rated face shield or arc flash hood, hearing protection类别1 + 防弧面罩或弧闪头罩、听力防护
类别 3 / Category 3 ≥ 8 cal/cm² (最低25 cal/cm²评级) Arc flash suit jacket and pants, arc flash hood, Category 2 underlayers弧闪防护服外套和裤子、弧闪头罩、类别2内层
类别 4 / Category 4 ≥ 25 cal/cm² (最低40 cal/cm²评级) Arc flash suit jacket and pants, arc flash hood, Category 2 underlayers — highest protection level弧闪防护服外套和裤子、弧闪头罩、类别2内层——最高防护等级
⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
The arc flash boundary is defined by the 1.2 cal/cm² threshold (second-degree burn onset). If the calculated incident energy exceeds 40 cal/cm², equipment must be de-energized — no PPE category exists for that level. 弧闪边界由1.2 cal/cm²阈限值（二级烧伤起始点）定义。如果计算的入射能量超过40 cal/cm²，设备必须断电——没有适用于该水平的PPE类别。

3.2 电气安全工作条件与许可证 · Electrically Safe Work Conditions & Permits

📖 来源 / Sources: NFPA 70E · 29 CFR 1910.147 · Brauer Ch.12

建立电气安全工作条件 / Establishing an Electrically Safe Work Condition

NFPA 70E's preferred approach is to establish an electrically safe work condition (de-energize) before performing work. The process is: NFPA 70E的首选方法是先建立电气安全工作条件（断电）再进行作业。流程是：

  1. Identify all power sources. 识别所有电源。
  2. Disconnect all power sources (open breakers, pull disconnect switches). 断开所有电源（断开断路器、拉开隔离开关）。
  3. Apply LOTO — place locks and tags on the energy-isolating devices (per 1910.147). 施加LOTO——在能量隔离装置上施加锁具和标签（依据1910.147）。
  4. Test for the absence of voltage with an adequately rated voltage tester, checking phase-to-phase and phase-to-ground. 测试无电压——使用额定电压足够的电压测试仪，检查相间和相对地。
  5. Ground — install temporary protective grounds where required (e.g., overhead lines). 接地——在需要处安装临时保护接地（如架空线）。

带电电气工作许可证 / Energized Electrical Work Permit

Work performed on energized conductors within the limited approach boundary, or that requires crossing the restricted approach boundary, requires an energized electrical work permit (subject to listed exceptions such as testing/troubleshooting and thermographic inspection). 在限制接近边界内对带电导体进行作业，或需要跨越限制区域边界的作业，需要带电电气工作许可证（有例外情况，如测试/故障排除和热成像检查）。

Justification for energized work must document that: (1) de-energizing introduces additional or increased hazards, or (2) the task is infeasible to perform in a de-energized state (e.g., voltage testing is inherently energized). 带电作业的正当理由必须记录：(1) 断电会引入额外或更大的危害，或(2) 任务在断电状态下不可行（如电压测试本质上就是带电的）。

关键电气安全原则 / Key Electrical Safety Principles

  • Establish an electrically safe work condition (de-energize) before work begins whenever possible. This is the preferred approach. 在工作开始前建立电气安全工作条件（断电），尽可能这样做。这是首选方法。
  • Energized electrical work permit required when: (a) energized work is justified (infeasible to de-energize, or de-energizing creates greater hazard), (b) work is within the restricted approach boundary. 需要带电电气工作许可证的情况：(a) 带电工作有充分理由（不可行断电，或断电造成更大危害），(b) 工作在限制区域边界内。
  • 接地故障断路器Ground-fault circuit interrupters (GFCI)☆ required on construction sites for all 120V, single-phase, 15A and 20A receptacle outlets not part of permanent wiring. 建筑工地上所有非永久布线的120V、单相、15A和20A插座都需配备GFCI。
  • 确保设备接地导体计划Assured equipment grounding conductor program (AEGCP)☆ — alternative to GFCI on construction sites; requires daily visual inspections of cord sets and equipment connected by cord and plug, plus quarterly continuity tests. 建筑工地上GFCI的替代方案；需要对电源线组及通过插头连接的设备进行每日目视检查，加上每季度一次连续性测试。
  • Current vs. voltage: Severity of shock is governed by CURRENT (amperage), not voltage alone. Even 120V can be lethal. Body resistance drops when wet (from ~100,000 Ω dry to ~1,000 Ω wet), greatly increasing current. 电流与电压：电击严重性由电流（安培）决定，而非仅电压。即使120V也可能致命。人体电阻在潮湿时大幅下降（从干燥约100,000Ω降至约1,000Ω），使电流大幅增加。
📋 实际案例 / Case Study:
A maintenance electrician in a food plant de-energized a panel but did not test for absence of voltage before touching the conductors. A back-fed circuit kept one bus energized, resulting in a shock and arc flash that caused second-degree burns. Root causes: failure to apply LOTO, failure to test for absence of voltage, and no energized-work permit. The corrective program emphasized the electrically safe work condition sequence and verifying a "prove-test-prove" voltage tester procedure. 一家食品厂的电工给配电盘断电后，未测试无电压就触摸导体。一个反供电回路使某段母线仍带电，导致电击和弧闪，造成二度烧伤。根本原因：未施加LOTO、未测试无电压、无带电作业许可证。纠正计划强调电气安全工作条件的流程和"验证-测试-再验证"电压测试仪程序。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "What steps are required to establish an electrically safe work condition?"
答题要点 / Key point: 顺序：断电(把负荷切断) → 上锁挂牌(LOTO) → 用"验证-测试-再验证"(prove-test-prove)验证无电压 → 验电时若发现带电须使用PPE。带电作业许可证仅当"不可断电"或"断电更危险"时需要。工地120V插座需GFCI或AEGCP（AEGCP：每日目视+季度连续性测试）。

3.3 坠落防护 — 29 CFR 1926 Subpart M · Fall Protection — 29 CFR 1926 Subpart M☆

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1926 Subpart M · 29 CFR 1926 Subpart R · 29 CFR 1910.28 · Brauer Ch.11 · Yates Ch.23

坠落防护Fall protection is consistently one of the most frequently cited OSHA standards. The CSP exam tests trigger heights, system requirements, and the hierarchy of fall protection. Falls are a leading cause of occupational fatalities — in construction, falls are the #1 "Fatal Four" cause of death. 坠落防护一直是OSHA最常引用的标准之一。CSP考试考查触发高度、系统要求和坠落防护的层级。坠落是职业致死的主要原因之一——在建筑行业，坠落是"致命四项"中排名第一的死因。

Construction — General / 建筑 — 一般 6 英尺 / 6 feet 29 CFR 1926.501(b)(1)
Scaffolds / 脚手架 10 英尺 / 10 feet 29 CFR 1926.451(g)
Steel Erection — General activities / 钢结构安装 — 一般活动 15 英尺 / 15 feet 29 CFR 1926.760(a)

⚠ CSP 考试技巧 / Exam Tip:
Memorize the general industry vs. construction trigger height distinction. General industry = 4 ft. Construction = 6 ft. This is a classic exam distinction. Also remember: scaffolds = 10 ft (construction), steel erection = 15 ft / 30 ft. 记住一般工业与建筑的触发高度区别。一般工业=4英尺。建筑=6英尺。这是经典的考试区分。还要记住：脚手架=10英尺（建筑），钢结构安装=15英尺/30英尺。

坠落防护系统（按层级优先级排序）/ Fall Protection Systems (Hierarchy Preferred)

系统 / System描述 / Description关键规格 / Key Specs & Notes
护栏系统Guardrail Systems☆ Passive fall protection — a barrier that prevents a worker from reaching a fall hazard. 被动坠落防护——防止工人接近坠落危害的屏障。 Top rail: 42 inches (±3 in) high. Must withstand 200 lbs force applied within 2 inches of the top edge, in any outward or downward direction. Top rail must not deflect below 39 inches under load. Midrails (required when no wall or parapet ≥ 21 inches high): installed at a height midway between top rail and walking surface. Intermediate vertical members (balusters): spacing ≤ 19 inches apart. Toeboard: minimum 3.5 inches high, must withstand 50 lbs force. Guardrails are PREFERRED because they are passive (no worker action required). 顶部横杆：高42英寸（±3英寸）。必须能在顶部边缘2英寸内、在任意向外或向下方向承受200磅力。受力时顶部横杆不得偏斜至39英寸以下。中间横杆（当没有高度≥21英寸的墙体或护墙时需要）：安装高度位于顶部横杆与行走表面中间。中间垂直构件（栏杆柱）：间距≤19英寸。踢脚板：最低3.5英寸高，必须承受50磅力。护栏是首选，因为它们是被动的（无需工人采取行动）。
安全网系统Safety Net Systems☆ Nets installed as close as practicable under the walking/working surface, but no more than 30 feet below. 尽可能靠近行走/工作表面下方安装的网，但不超过下方30英尺。 Must extend outward from the edge per OSHA tables. Must be drop-tested or certified. Maximum mesh size: 6 inches × 6 inches. 必须按OSHA表格从边缘向外延伸。必须进行跌落测试或获得认证。最大网孔尺寸：6英寸×6英寸。
个人防坠系统Personal Fall Arrest System (PFAS)☆ Active fall protection consisting of: anchorage, full-body harness, and connector (lanyard or SRL). 主动坠落防护，包括：锚固点、全身式安全带和连接器（系索或自缩式救生索SRL）。 Anchorage must support 5,000 lbs per employee attached (or 2× safety factor with certification). Maximum free fall distance: 6 feet. Maximum deceleration distance: 3.5 feet. Body belts are PROHIBITED for fall arrest (may only be used for positioning). 锚固点必须支撑每名连接的员工5,000磅（或经认证有2倍安全系数）。最大自由坠落距离：6英尺。最大减速距离：3.5英尺。防坠保护禁止使用身体腰带（仅可用于定位）。
定位装置系统Positioning Device Systems☆ Allows a worker to be supported on an elevated vertical surface with both hands free. 允许工人在高处垂直表面上得到支撑，双手自由作业。 Maximum free fall: 2 feet. Anchorage must support 3,000 lbs (or 2× safety factor). 最大自由坠落：2英尺。锚固点必须支撑3,000磅（或2倍安全系数）。
警示线系统Warning Line Systems☆ Barriers erected on a roof to warn workers they are approaching an unprotected edge. Used only for roofing work on low-slope roofs. 在屋顶上设立的屏障，警告工人正在接近未防护边缘。仅用于低坡度屋顶的屋面作业。 Must be erected at least 6 feet from the roof edge (mechanical equipment on roof: 6 ft from edge; non-mechanical: 10 ft from edge). Must be flagged every 6 feet. Lines must withstand 16 lbs of force. Requires a safety monitor. 必须设立在距屋顶边缘至少6英尺处（屋顶有机械设备：距边缘6英尺；无机械：距边缘10英尺）。必须每6英尺挂旗标识。绳索必须承受16磅力。需要安全监视员。
安全监视系统Safety Monitoring System☆ A competent person monitors workers for fall hazards. Used in conjunction with warning line systems. 由合格人员监视工人的坠落危害。与警示线系统配合使用。 Monitor must be on the same walking/working surface, in visual/verbal range, and have no other responsibilities. May only be used where no mechanical equipment is in use. 监视员必须在同一行走/工作表面上，在视觉/语言范围内，且不得有其它职责。仅可在无机械设备使用的情况下使用。

3.4 坠落间隙与PFAS计算 / Fall Clearance & PFAS Calculations

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1926.502(d) · Yates Ch.23

坠落间隙计算 / Fall Clearance Calculation

When using a PFAS, sufficient clearance below the worker must exist to prevent striking the lower level or an obstruction. The total fall distance is calculated as: 使用PFAS时，工人下方必须有足够的间隙以防止撞击下层或障碍物。总坠落距离计算如下：

所需总坠落间隙 / Total Fall Clearance Required = 自由坠落距离(FFD) + 减速距离(DD) + 安全带拉伸(HS) + D型环滑动(DS) + 安全系数(SF)

典型计算（6英尺系索带减速装置）/ Typical Calculation (6-ft lanyard with deceleration device):
= 6 英尺(FF) + 3.5 英尺(Decel) + 1 英尺(H.Stretch) + 1 英尺(D-Ring) + 2 英尺(Safety)
= 13.5 英尺最低间隙 / 13.5 feet minimum clearance

保守估算（CSP考试）/ Conservative Estimate (CSP Exam): 17.5–18.5 英尺 / 17.5–18.5 feet — 考试通常使用较保守的数字以考虑锚点高度、系索长度变化和最坏情况。

自缩式救生索 (SRL) / Self-Retracting Lifelines (SRLs)

An SRL is a connector that automatically limits free fall distance and reduces the required clearance. Key points for the exam: 自缩式救生索（SRL）是一种自动限制自由坠落距离并减少所需间隙的连接器。考试要点：

  • SRLs limit free fall to roughly 2 feet (or less), versus 6 feet for a standard 6-ft lanyard. SRL将自由坠落限制在约2英尺（或更少），而标准6英尺系索为6英尺。
  • If the SRL is anchored at the feet (e.g., tied to the back D-ring while on a ladder), free fall increases; anchorage should be at or above the dorsal D-ring when possible. 如果SRL锚固在脚部（如在梯子上连接到背部D型环），自由坠落会增加；锚固应尽可能在背D型环处或以上。
  • SRLs require a fall clearance typically of 3 to 8.5 feet depending on model and anchorage position — much less than a lanyard. SRL通常需要3至8.5英尺的坠落间隙，取决于型号和锚固位置——远少于系索。
  • Sharp-edge testing: lanyards and SRLs used where sharp edges (e.g., steel beams) could cut the line must be certified for sharp-edge conditions or protected from abrasion. 锐边测试：在可能切割绳索的锐边（如钢梁）处使用的系索和SRL必须通过锐边认证或用防护套防磨损。
自由坠落距离判定 / Free Fall Distance Logic

自由坠落距离 = 锚固点到作业面的垂直距离 + 连接器长度
Free fall distance = vertical distance from anchorage to work surface + connector length

判定规则 / Rule: 若自由坠落距离 > 6英尺，则PFAS不符合要求，必须降低锚固点或更换连接器。
If free fall > 6 ft, the PFAS does not comply — lower the anchorage or change the connector.

示例 / Example: 锚固点位于作业面上方2英尺，使用6英尺系索。
Anchorage is 2 ft above the work surface, using a 6-ft lanyard.
  自由坠落 = 2 + 6 = 8 英尺 / 8 ft → 超过6英尺限制，需降低锚固点或改用更短系索。
  Free fall = 2 + 6 = 8 ft → exceeds the 6-ft limit; lower the anchorage or use a shorter lanyard.
📋 实际案例 / Case Study (free-fall limit):
A worker tied off to an anchor 4 feet above his D-ring with a 6-ft lanyard. His free fall was 10 feet — well over the 6-ft limit. When he slipped, he fell 10 feet, the energy absorber deployed fully, and he struck the concrete below (no clearance). The incident highlighted the exam-critical relationship between anchorage height, connector length, and clearance. Corrective fix: anchor at shoulder height or above, and verify clearance ≥ 13.5 ft. 一名工人用6英尺系索连接到其D型环上方4英尺的锚点上。自由坠落达10英尺——远超6英尺限制。当他滑倒时坠落了10英尺，吸能器完全展开，但仍撞到下方混凝土（无净空）。该事件凸显了锚点高度、连接器长度和净空之间的关键关系。纠正措施：锚固在肩部或以上，并核实净空≥13.5英尺。

3.5 附加坠落防护要求 / Additional Fall Protection Requirements

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1926 Subpart M · Yates Ch.23
  • Hole covers: Must support 2× the expected load, must be secured, must be marked "HOLE" or "COVER." 孔洞盖板：必须支撑2倍预期负载，必须固定，必须标记"HOLE"或"COVER"。
  • Stairways with 4+ risers (construction): Must have a handrail and stair railing. 4级及以上台阶的楼梯（建筑）：必须设有扶手和楼梯栏杆。
  • PFAS inspection: Harnesses and lanyards must be inspected before each use by the user. Annual competent person inspection is a best practice (not an OSHA requirement per se, but often tested). PFAS检查：安全带和系索必须在每次使用前由使用者检查。年度合格人员检查是最佳实践（本身不是OSHA要求，但常被考查）。
  • Rescue plan: Employers must provide for prompt rescue of employees in the event of a fall (post-fall suspension trauma can occur in under 15 minutes). 救援计划：雇主必须为坠落事件中员工的及时救援做好准备（坠落后悬挂创伤可在15分钟内发生）。
  • Training: Every employee who might be exposed to fall hazards must be trained in the nature of the hazards, the correct procedures for erecting/maintaining/dismantling fall protection systems, and the proper use of PFAS. 培训：每个可能暴露于坠落危害的员工必须接受培训，了解危害性质、架设/维护/拆除坠落防护系统的正确程序以及PFAS的正确使用。

坠落危害区标记 / Leading Edge Work

前沿作业Leading edge work☆ — work at the advancing edge of a surface — requires a controlled decking zone (CDZ) for steel deckers. In a CDZ, employees are allowed to work without a PFAS but must be protected by a safety monitor, and the CDZ must be at least 2 feet wide and no more than 90 feet wide, with a warning line at 90 ft. 前沿作业——在结构表面的推进边缘作业——对钢结构铺板工要求设置受控铺板区（CDZ）。在CDZ内，员工可不穿戴PFAS作业，但必须由安全监视员保护，CDZ宽度至少2英尺且不超过90英尺，在90英尺处设置警示线。

屋顶作业常用组合 / Common Roofing Combinations

For low-slope roofing work, OSHA permits warning lines combined with a safety monitoring system as an alternative to guardrails, PFAS, or nets — but only where no mechanical equipment is in use. If mechanical equipment (e.g., a roof decking machine) is used, the roofers must also use a PFAS or a safety net/guardrail system. 对于低坡度屋面作业，OSHA允许使用警示线与安全监视系统组合，替代护栏、PFAS或安全网——但仅限不使用机械设备时。若使用机械设备（如铺板机），屋面工还必须使用PFAS或安全网/护栏系统。

3.6 脚手架与梯子安全 · Scaffold & Ladder Safety

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1926 Subpart L · 29 CFR 1926 Subpart X · 29 CFR 1910.23 · Yates Ch.23

脚手架 — 29 CFR 1926 Subpart L / Scaffolds — 29 CFR 1926 Subpart L

Fall protection (guardrail or PFAS) is required when working on a scaffold 10 feet or higher above the lower level. 在距下层10英尺或更高的脚手架上作业时，需要坠落防护（护栏或PFAS）。

  • Platform requirements / 平台要求: Platform must be at least 18 inches wide (unless using a narrower platform for a specific trade). Scaffold platforms must be fully planked or decked. 平台至少宽18英寸（除非特定工种使用更窄的平台）。脚手架平台必须满铺脚手板。
  • Maximum gap / 最大间隙: No more than 1 inch gap between scaffold planks. 脚手板之间间隙不超过1英寸。
  • Maximum platform deflection / 最大平台挠度: Planks may not deflect more than 1/60 of their span. 脚手板挠度不得超过跨度的1/60。
  • Erection/dismantling / 架设/拆除: Performed by a competent person; a competent person inspects the scaffold before each work shift and after any occurrence that could affect structural integrity. 架设/拆除由合格人员执行；合格人员在每个工作班次前以及任何可能影响结构完整性的情况发生后检查脚手架。
  • Distance from wall / 距墙距离: Scaffold platform must be within 14 inches horizontally of the work face (30 inches if outriggers are used). 脚手架平台距作业面水平距离必须在14英寸以内（使用悬挑架时为30英寸）。
  • Supported scaffold base / 支撑式脚手架底座: Must sit on base plates and mud sills (or other firm foundations), not on unstable objects like barrels or boxes. 支撑式脚手架底座必须放在底座板和垫木（或其他牢固基础上），不得放在油桶或箱子等不稳固物体上。

梯子 — 29 CFR 1926 Subpart X & 1910.23 / Ladders — 29 CFR 1926 Subpart X & 1910.23

  • Three-point contact / 三点接触: Face the ladder and maintain three points of contact when climbing. 攀爬时面向梯子并保持三点接触。
  • Rung spacing / 梯级间距: Rungs must be spaced 10 to 14 inches apart. 梯级间距必须为10至14英寸。
  • Side rails / 侧轨: Must extend at least 3 feet (36 inches) above the landing surface when used for access to an elevated platform. 用于进入高处平台时，侧轨必须伸出着陆面至少3英尺（36英寸）。
  • Portable ladder angle / 便携梯角度: Set at approximately a 4:1 ratio (one foot of base distance out for every 4 feet of height). 便携梯角度约为4:1（每4英尺高度，底部外移1英尺）。
  • Ladder integrity / 梯子完整性: No broken rungs, cracked rails, or missing safety feet. Damaged ladders must be tagged and removed from service. 梯子完好性：不得有断裂梯级、开裂侧轨或缺失防滑脚。损坏的梯子必须挂牌并停用。
  • Overload / 超载: Never load a ladder beyond its maximum intended load. 绝不使梯子超过其最大预期载荷。

3.7 坠落防护层级与选择 · Fall Protection Hierarchy & Selection

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1926 Subpart M · Brauer Ch.9 · Brauer Ch.11

坠落防护层级 / The Fall Protection Hierarchy

When selecting fall protection, apply the hierarchy (mirroring the general Hierarchy of Controls): 选择坠落防护时，应用层级（与一般控制层级对应）：

  1. Eliminate / 消除: Design the hazard away (work at ground level, remote inspection). 通过设计消除危害（在地面作业、远程检查）。
  2. Passive systems / 被动系统: Guardrails, covers, nets — protect without worker action. 护栏、盖板、安全网——无需工人行动即保护。
  3. Active systems / 主动系统: PFAS, positioning devices, travel restraints — require worker action and correct use. PFAS、定位装置、行进限制系统——需要工人行动和正确使用。
  4. Administrative systems / 行政系统: Warning lines, safety monitors, controlled access zones, training. 警示线、安全监视员、受控进入区、培训。
📌 考试决策逻辑 / Exam Decision Logic:
OSHA's fall protection preference: guardrails > safety nets > personal fall arrest systems. For roofs: guardrails/parapets preferred; warning lines + monitor only for low-slope roofing without mechanical equipment; PFAS where no other option. For scaffolds >10 ft: guardrail or PFAS. When asked the "best" fall protection, pick the passive/engineering option unless the scenario justifies a lower option. OSHA的坠落防护优先级：护栏 > 安全网 > 个人防坠系统。对于屋顶：首选护栏/女儿墙；警示线+监视员仅用于无机械设备的低坡度屋面；无其他选择时用PFAS。对于脚手架>10英尺：护栏或PFAS。当被问"最佳"坠落防护时，选择被动/工程选项，除非场景证明更低选项合理。
🎯 高频考点 / Exam Hotspot 必考 HIGH
典型问法 / Typical question: "Which is the BEST fall protection for this task?"
答题要点 / Key point: 优先级：护栏 > 安全网 > PFAS（被动优先）。屋顶：首选护栏/女儿墙。脚手架>10英尺：护栏或PFAS。警示线+监视员仅限无机械的低坡屋面。题目问"最佳"选被动/工程选项，除非场景限定。

※ 模拟情景题，根据考点编写，非历年真题 / Practice scenario based on exam topics, not a past real question
"Workers regularly service equipment 12 ft from an unprotected roof edge several times a week. What is the BEST fall protection?"
最佳答案 / Best answer: A fixed guardrail or parapet around the roof perimeter. 解析：这是高频维护作业，护栏是被动/工程控制、持续有效且无需工人操作，优于PFAS（依赖穿戴正确）、警示线或安全网。按"护栏 > 安全网 > PFAS"优先级选择。

3.9 CSP11常见工作场所危害 · CSP11 Common Workplace Hazards☆

📖 来源 / Sources: 29 CFR 1910.212 · Yates Ch.2 · Yates Ch.23

The CSP11 exam blueprint (Domain 1, item 3) lists these common workplace hazards: electrical, falls, confined spaces, LOTO, working around water, caught in/between, struck by, and excavation. Electrical and falls are covered above; confined spaces, LOTO, and excavation are covered in Week 4. This section covers working around water, caught-in, and struck-by hazards. CSP11考试蓝图（领域1，第3项）列出了以下常见工作场所危害：电气、坠落、密闭空间、LOTO、水中作业、卷入/挤压、撞击和挖掘。电气和坠落已在上方覆盖；密闭空间、LOTO和挖掘在第4周覆盖。本节涵盖水中作业、卷入/挤压和撞击危害。

水中作业安全 · Working Around Water

Working on, over, or adjacent to water presents drowning and hypothermia hazards. Key requirements: 在水上、水面上方或水体附近作业存在溺水和体温过低危害。关键要求：

  • Personal Flotation Devices (PFDs): US Coast Guard-approved Type I, II, III, or V PFDs must be provided and worn by employees working over or near water where drowning risk exists. 个人浮力装置(PFD)：必须为在水上或水体附近作业、有溺水风险的员工提供并穿戴美国海岸警卫队认证的I型、II型、III型或V型PFD。
  • Ring buoys (life rings): At least one Coast Guard-approved 30-inch ring buoy with 90 feet of line must be available at work sites over or adjacent to water. 救生圈：在水上或水体附近的工作场所，必须至少配备一个美国海岸警卫队认证的30英寸救生圈，附带90英尺绳索。
  • Rescue skiff/boat: Required in certain situations when employees are working over or adjacent to water; must be immediately available with a competent operator. 救援小船：在某些情况下，当员工在水上或水体附近作业时需要；必须配备合格操作员，随时可用。
  • Fall protection over water: When working over water, fall protection may still be required; nets or PFAS over water must consider that a fall into water itself is a hazard. 水上坠落防护：在水上作业时仍可能需要坠落防护；水上安全网或PFAS必须考虑落入水中本身即是危害。

卷入/挤压危害 · Caught-In / Between Hazards

Caught-in/between hazards are one of OSHA's "Fatal Four" construction hazards. A worker is caught, crushed, squeezed, or pinched between objects or parts of an object. 卷入/挤压危害是OSHA建筑施工"致命四项"之一。工人被夹在两个或多个物体之间或被物体的一部分挤压、压碎。

  • Trench/excavation cave-ins (most common caught-in fatality — covered in depth in Week 4). 沟槽/挖掘坍塌（最常见的卷入致死类型——第4周有详细覆盖）。
  • Unguarded machinery: rotating shafts, belts, pulleys, gears, chain drives — all must be guarded per 1910.212. 无防护机器：旋转轴、皮带、滑轮、齿轮、链传动——均须依据1910.212进行防护。
  • Pinned between equipment and fixed structures (e.g., heavy equipment operator pinned against a wall). 被夹在设备与固定结构之间（如重型设备操作员被挤在墙上）。
  • Prevention: training, machine guarding, barricading, LOTO, trench protective systems, competent person inspections. 预防：培训、机器防护、路障、LOTO、沟槽防护系统和合格人员检查。

撞击危害 · Struck-By Hazards

Struck-by hazards are another of OSHA's "Fatal Four." A worker is hit by a flying, falling, swinging, or rolling object. 撞击危害是OSHA"致命四项"的另一项。工人被飞行、坠落、摆动或滚动的物体击中。

  • Flying objects: Grinding/cutting particles, ejected parts. Mitigation: machine guards, safety glasses, face shields. 飞行物体：打磨/切割颗粒、弹出零件。缓解措施：机器防护、安全眼镜、面罩。
  • Falling objects: Tools/materials from elevated work. Mitigation: toeboards, debris nets, hard hats, barricading below. 坠落物体：高处作业掉落的工具/物料。缓解措施：踢脚板、防坠网、安全帽、下方路障。
  • Swinging objects: Crane loads, excavator buckets. Mitigation: tag lines, exclusion zones, signal persons. 摆动物体：起重机负载、挖掘机铲斗。缓解措施：牵引绳、禁入区域、信号员。
  • Rolling objects: Vehicles, mobile equipment on incline. Mitigation: traffic control, spotters, backing alarms, chocks. 滚动物体：车辆、坡道上的移动设备。缓解措施：交通控制、观察员、倒车警报、轮挡。
